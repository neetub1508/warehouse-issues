## The meter is the whole billing engine; everything else in P5 is a report over it

R4 `F-011` states it in one line and it governs this phase: *"the billable-event meter is the whole
billing engine; everything else is a report over it."* Nobody computes a bill by querying the
operational tables at month end, because operational tables get corrected, re-received, cancelled and
re-picked — and the bill has to reflect **what happened**, not what is currently true.

**And the granularity reaches back into base.** `PC-42` fixed the event vocabulary in v1 *for this task*:
a design that emits only `order.shipped` makes per-line handling billing — **how every audited 3PL
prices** — permanently unavailable for the past, because the finer facts were never emitted and cannot
be reconstructed. This is the one place where an event-catalogue decision is **irreversible rather than
additive** (`F-012`). If `P0-11` shipped a coarse outbox, this task cannot be built correctly and the
defect is in `P0-11`, not here — **verify the vocabulary before writing a line of this task.**

## Scope

- **`V530030` — `wh3_billable_events`** (`W3-05`): `event_code`, client, owner, warehouse, charge code,
  `occurred_at`, `posting_date`, signed `quantity`, `uom_code`, **`subject_type` / `subject_id`** (the
  generic pair, `DATA-MODEL.md` §1.9), `source_system`,
  **`source_event_key` with `uk(source_system, source_event_key)`**, `outbox_cursor`,
  `rate_card_line_id` (null until rated), `unit_rate`, `rated_amount`, `currency_code`, `billing_run_id`,
  `status` ∈ {`METERED`,`RATED`,`BILLED`,`REVERSED`,`EXCLUDED`}, `reversal_of_event_id`,
  `exclusion_reason_code`, `updated_at`, `updated_by`.
- **Append-only, stated once.** An `UPDATE` may touch only the allowlist `rate_card_line_id`,
  `unit_rate`, `rated_amount`, `status`, `exclusion_reason_code`, `billing_run_id`, `updated_at` and
  `updated_by`. Everything else is protected by a `to_jsonb`-diff trigger with that explicit allowlist —
  the same shape as `I-2` — so a column added by a later `ALTER TABLE` is protected automatically rather
  than being silently mutable.

> **Round-3 fold — `RF-008` (`GAP-REGISTER-R3.md` §2.6, §4.2).** **The meter is specified once, in
> `DATA-MODEL.md` §2.3, and §2.3 takes this task's columns.** Before this fold the two specifications
> disagreed on eleven columns, on the rate-card status and on whether the meter could be updated at all.
> The three conflicts are resolved:
> - **`source_event_key`, not `idempotency_key`.** It is the unique index, and it is the key the outbox
>   delivers.
> - **The generic `subject_type`/`subject_id` pair**, not eight nullable subject FKs. It is the house
>   style. The item, lot or LPN is reached through the subject, and the typed dimensions ride on the
>   outbox row (`RL-002`).
> - **`SUPERSEDED`, not `EXPIRED`**, for a rate card (`P5-02`).
>
> The rating columns and the `status` ladder land in `V530030` itself. Added after the first billed
> period, they would leave every historical event with NULL rating data and every historical dispute
> unanswerable. Because the allowlisted columns do change, **`wh3_billable_events` carries
> `updated_at`/`updated_by`**, and `DATA-MODEL.md`'s *No `updated_at` / `updated_by`* row no longer lists
> the meter. `is_reversed` is retired; `status = REVERSED` carries it.
- **A correction is a reversing row**, never a delete — the same rule as `L-2`/`L-3` on the stock ledger.
  A cancelled pick adds a reversing event; a re-run of the period then produces the same number twice.
- **`EXCLUDED` carries a reason code and an author.** A credit decision is a **recorded act**, not a
  deleted row — that is what makes a dispute resolvable without archaeology.
- **The consumer**, subscribing to `whb_outbox` by cursor: `pick.line.confirmed`, `carton.packed`,
  `receipt.line.confirmed`, `putaway.task.completed`, `task.completed`, `return.line.dispositioned`,
  `work_order.completed`, `owner.changed`, `count.variance.posted`. Delivery is **at-least-once**, so the
  handler **deduplicates on `(subscriber_code, sequence_no)`** *and* the meter's own
  `uk(source_system, source_event_key)` is the second line of defence (`PC-44`).
  **The consumer reads `event_version` and the dimensions** — `company_id`, `item_id`, `lot_id`,
  `serial_id` and the rest of `PC-36`'s typed columns — because the event *is* those columns and there is
  no payload to parse in v1 (`RL-002`). Its subscription pins `accepted_event_version`, so the first
  `event_version = 2` never reaches the meter unannounced (`RL-002`).
- **WS-160 is read-only plus Reverse.** Log-style: no audit columns, and therefore none in the export.

## Traps

- **The outbox has no precedent in this repository.** `grep -rli "outbox" --include=*.java --include=*.sql .`
  → **0 files**, computed 2026-09-01 (`PC-35`). If `P0-11` under-budgeted it as *"a table"*, the missing
  pieces surface **here**: the gapless cursor, the publisher job, retry with backoff, the dead-letter grid
  and replay-from-cursor. `@Async` is not a substitute and the repo's own memory records
  `reference_async_notification_lazyinit` as a live trap.
- **The cursor deliberately blocks on a poison message** (`PC-47`). A dead letter on the 3PL subscription
  stops the meter advancing — which is correct and visible, and must be alerted on, because a silently
  skipped event is an unbillable month nobody notices.
- **There is no payload and no `payload_ref` in v1 — the event is its typed columns** (`RL-002`, `PC-37`,
  CLAUDE.md DATABASE CONVENTIONS); a consumer wanting more calls the lineage `GET` (`RL-002`). An
  outbox that carries an opaque blob is *"the shape a billing meter cannot aggregate over"*.
- **Never trust `recorded_at` for billing.** `occurred_at` is producer-supplied and is the billing date;
  a gate-out at 22:00 synced at 09:00 dates itself wrong and bills the wrong month (`L-13`, R7 §6 item 4).
  `posting_date` is the third column and is the accounting date. All three exist; use the right one.
- **`quantity` is signed and `DECIMAL(18,4)`**; `unit_rate` is `DECIMAL(19,6)`; `rated_amount` is
  `DECIMAL(15,2)`, rounded once `HALF_UP` (user ruling, W3 fix round — supersedes `OD-7`'s `DECIMAL(19,4)`;
  CLAUDE.md's `DECIMAL(19,4)` carve-out is the four accounting modules only).
- **Owner segregation applies to this grid too.** Every repository method touching `wh3_billable_events`
  takes an owner-set parameter; `P5-08`'s contract test fails the build if one does not.
- **A filter-aware statistics strip gets no cache name.** `CacheConfiguration.java:190-196` records that
  exact mistake (classic#790, #791; R1 `C-043`). And an *unregistered* name throws
  `IllegalArgumentException` on the **first call, not at startup** (`CacheConfiguration.java:375-376`).
- `occurredFrom`/`To` are two **`date`** filters; `postingDateFrom`/`To` are two **`dateOnly`** filters
  (`filterUtils.ts:49-56`) — the timestamp type moves the lower bound back a day east of UTC.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Posting the same `(source_system, source_event_key)` twice creates **one** event and returns the original — proved by replaying an outbox range through the subscriber
- [ ] An `UPDATE` to any column outside the allowlist (`rate_card_line_id`, `unit_rate`, `rated_amount`, `status`, `exclusion_reason_code`, `billing_run_id`, `updated_at`, `updated_by`) is refused by the trigger **and** by the service first, with a field-level error (`RF-008`)
- [ ] `DATA-MODEL.md` §2.3's `wh3_billable_events` row lists exactly `V530030`'s columns — `source_event_key`, `subject_type`/`subject_id`, the rating columns, `status` and `updated_at`/`updated_by` — and no `idempotency_key` or `is_reversed` (`RF-008`)
- [ ] A `DELETE` on a metered event is refused by every path including direct SQL
- [ ] Cancelling a shipment produces a reversing event linked by `reversal_of_event_id`; the original row is unchanged and `status = REVERSED` on both — **amended (W3 gate F4):** in P5-03 the reversing row is written by WS-160's manual **Reverse**; the automatic half waits for `P0-11` to emit the reversal linkage (`shipment.confirmed` emitter, the reversed event's cursor/subject on `stock.movement.reversed`) and `P5-05` to consume it
- [ ] Excluding an event requires a reason code and an author and leaves the row in place
- [ ] **Re-rating November twice against the same card version returns byte-identical totals** — the reproducibility test, and the reason the meter exists
- [ ] A `pick.line.confirmed` event carries `quantity`, `item_id` and `location` — if it does not, **stop and fix `P0-11`**; per-line handling billing is unavailable for the past otherwise
- [ ] The subscriber reads `event_version` and the typed dimension columns, parses no payload, and its subscription pins `accepted_event_version` (`RL-002`)
- [ ] The subscriber deduplicates on `(subscriber_code, sequence_no)` — asserted by delivering the same sequence twice
- [ ] **Mobile:** `none` — a billing meter is a desk surface (`D-13` declaration)
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-234`

## Decisions recorded at build
W3 gate round (reviewer + functional-reviewer, `.claude/tasks/reports/warehouse-p5-W3/p503-func.md`), ratified by the driver:
- **WS-160's permission group ships in `wh3plPermissions.ts`** (F1): `BILLABLE_EVENTS { VIEW, EXPORT, REVERSE }` = `wh3_billable_events:view|export|reverse` (`V531000`).
- **The meter meters every event whose owner maps to a `wh3_clients` row, whatever the client's status or go-live date at delivery** (F2). The lane's LIVE/TERMINATING + go-live gate is removed: read at delivery time it dropped a backdated go-live, a backlog and a replay for good, because the cursor moves past a skip. **Obligation on `P5-05`:** the run decides billability in the period (go-live, suspension, offboarding) against each event's `posting_date`, and excludes what is not billable with a recorded reason — never by the meter skipping it. Own stock (no client row) is still skipped; every skip (unmapped type, duplicate cursor, no client) is logged at INFO with its cursor and reason.
- **`rated_amount` stays `DECIMAL(15,2)`** (F3, user ruling: money `DECIMAL(15,2)`, rounded once `HALF_UP`). The Traps line is amended.
- **`WH-SC-234`'s cancellation half is a manual Reverse in P5-03** (F4). No operational event produces a reversal until `P0-11`/`P5-05` emit and consume the reversal linkage; the acceptance line is amended.
- **`V530101` enforces the status ladder in the guard** (F6): `METERED → RATED | REVERSED | EXCLUDED`, `RATED → BILLED | REVERSED | EXCLUDED` (re-rating RATED→RATED stays open), `BILLED → BILLED` only with `rate_card_line_id`, `unit_rate`, `rated_amount` and `billing_run_id` frozen (`BILLABLE_EVENT_BILLED`), REVERSED/EXCLUDED terminal; an original may become REVERSED only once its reversing row exists (`BILLABLE_EVENT_STATUS_TRANSITION`). `RATED → METERED` (un-rating) and `BILLED → REVERSED` are refused; if `P5-05`'s run needs either, P5-05 widens the guard in its own migration. `V530101` is the first number of §7.5's correction reserve (`V530031` is `W3-06`'s).
- **A named warehouse outside the caller's branch tier is 403, never an empty grid** (F7): `scopedCriteria` calls `requireReadableSite(warehouseId)`, so grid, statistics and export refuse alike (`BILLABLE_EVENT_SITE_NOT_PERMITTED`).
- **Only `:view:all` sees site-less (client-level) events** (F8). A `:view:branch` user's grid, statistics and export leave them out, and opening one is 403; `P5-04`'s client-level true-ups are reviewed by an all-sites user.
- **The cursor dedupe is scoped to `source_system = 'WHB_OUTBOX'`** (F9), so a later writer (P5-04/P5-06) that records a row carrying an outbox cursor under its own source never shadows the subscriber's event.
- **Inherited `JpaRepository` methods stay** (F10). `WhbOwnerScopeContractTest` scans warehouse-base's repository directory only and does not demand narrowing; the service uses `saveAndFlush`. Every read path through `Wh3plBillableEventRepositoryCustom` binds the owner set, and `findByIdForUpdate` is refused by name after the unscoped lock.
- **Exclude is endpoint-only and rides `wh3_billable_events:reverse`** (F11). `POST /{id}/exclude` has no WS-160 button; it is consumed by `P5-05`'s run review. A separate permission is not introduced.
- **`count.variance.posted` is metered with its signed quantity** (F12). Whether `ACC_COUNT_VARIANCE` rates on `abs(quantity)` or per event is `P5-05`'s rating rule to record — a negative variance must not become a client credit by default.
- **Recorded, not fixed here:** F5 (DB-level proof — a repository integration test through `ci-gate.sh` for replay idempotency, trigger refusals incl. the new ladder, and site 403 across grid/statistics/export) is carried to the tester; `shipment.confirmed`, the `:view:all`/`:view:branch` tiers, the `wh3_billable_events:view → wh3_clients:view` dependency, the four seeded charge codes and four `BILLING_EXCLUSION` reasons are in code and in `V530030`, and this section is their contract row.
