## Scope

- **`wh_pack_sessions`** (`V510101`, `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.3 WH-91). The carton tables already exist at
  `V510042` from `P2-11`; this task adds the **session**, not a second carton model.
- **The pack session is an operator flow, not arithmetic** (`FR-190`, `P-037`). The whole
  specification, and every clause is load-bearing:
  - **one active carton at a time**
  - the **first carton auto-created on session start**
  - the **next carton auto-created on seal, and only while units remain**
  - a **quantity field on scan**
  - **repeat scans of one SKU merged into the existing pack line**, never N rows of one
  - the **change-package control directly above the scanner**
  - **no loose packing option** — loading consumes only sealed cartons
  - the result is *scan × N, seal × cartons, complete × 1*
- **Scan verification as configuration per task type** (`FR-192`): requires location scan, item
  scan, lot scan, serial scan, quantity entry — five independent flags on the task type, **honoured
  identically by web and mobile**. A confirm button produces about 98% pick accuracy; scan
  verification produces above 99.9%, and that difference is the entire business case for the
  handheld.

## What the handheld does when the network drops

WS-233 is **online-only and says so**. It is one of only two RF screens that is (`P3-01`), and the
reason is specific: a pack session's carton weight and photograph come from the device at the moment
of sealing, and a queued seal replayed an hour later attaches a weight to a carton that has already
left. The screen blocks and tells the packer to wait, rather than buffering something it cannot
honour.

## Traps

- **"Auto-create the next carton on seal, *only while units remain*."** Auto-creating
  unconditionally leaves an empty carton on every completed session, which then appears on the
  manifest as a package that does not exist and is billed by the carrier.
- **Repeat scans merge into the existing pack line.** Twelve scans of one SKU is one line with
  quantity 12, not twelve lines of 1. The prior art got this wrong and it is `P-037`'s named defect;
  it is also what makes the packing slip readable.
- **No loose packing option, anywhere.** `FR-190` is explicit and `WH-SC-232` asserts it: loading
  consumes only sealed cartons. A "ship without packing" escape hatch means the mandatory package
  count on the carrier manifest has nothing to count.
- **Scan verification is five flags on the task type, not one boolean.** Requiring a location scan
  and requiring a serial scan are different decisions for different clients, and collapsing them
  means the strictest client sets the rule for everyone.
- **Web and mobile must honour the five flags identically** (`FR-192`, `D-13`). A web pack screen
  that lets a packer type a serial while the handheld demands a scan is two accuracy rates in one
  warehouse and the audit cannot tell which was used.
- **The carton tables are `P2-11`'s at `V510042`.** Do not create a second carton table in
  `V510101`; add the session and reference them.
- **Every quantity and weight is computed on the backend.** There is no decimal library on the
  frontend (`PLATFORM-DEPENDENCIES.md` §2.11 — one `package.json`, 38 dependencies, no
  `decimal.js`/`big.js`), so dimensional weight, carton totals and fill percentages are `BigDecimal`
  on the server and formatted strings on the wire.
- **The change-package control sits directly above the scanner.** This reads like a design note and
  is not: a packer changing carton type has one hand and cannot reach a control at the top of a form.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Starting a session auto-creates carton 1 with no user action
- [ ] Sealing carton 1 with units remaining auto-creates carton 2; sealing the last carton creates none
- [ ] Scanning one SKU twelve times produces **one** pack line of quantity 12
- [ ] There is no loose-packing path on web or handheld, and loading refuses an unsealed carton
- [ ] A task type configured to require a lot scan refuses a typed lot on **both** the web screen and WS-233
- [ ] All five verification flags are independently settable and independently honoured
- [ ] WS-233 states it is online-only and blocks rather than queueing a seal
- [ ] Carton totals and weights are computed on the backend
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-232`
