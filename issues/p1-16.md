## Scope

- **`V510018`** — `wh_receipt_reversals`, `wh_receipt_reversal_lines`.
- **Reversal is an action, not a data fix** (`FR-131`): it generates a **`REVERSAL` movement linked to
  the original**, **decrements the PO line's received quantity**, and **leaves both documents
  visible**. There is no delete, no edit and no "correct the GRN".
- **The reversal is blocked once the stock has moved on.** If any of the received units have been put
  away, picked, status-changed or have left the site — any posted, unreversed movement after the receipt
  that took them out of the receiving location or out of their received status, so **a completed putaway
  alone blocks** (`WH-SC-082`'s *"120 relocated"*) — the reversal is refused with a message naming what
  blocks it.
- **Ratified 2026-09-14** (`docs/contracts/receipt-qc-putaway.contract.md`): a reversal covers **the
  whole GRN** — one `wh_receipt_reversal_lines` row per GRN line carrying its full received quantity; a
  partial correction is an adjustment (`L-3`, `RQP-OPEN-20`). Its Post also recomputes the PO header from
  its lines and cancels the GRN lines' open (`CREATED`/`ASSIGNED`) putaway tasks in the same transaction;
  an `APPROVED` reversal whose Post is refused leaves by **Reject** (`H4`).

## Why this is a separate task from `P1-13`

Because the tempting implementation is an `UPDATE` on the GRN and a compensating write to the
position — and **both are forbidden**. `L-2` makes posted lines immutable in three layers, `L-3`
makes correction a reversal, and `FR-436` allows exactly one writer. A reversal screen that exists
before anyone needs one is what prevents a support engineer inventing the data fix.

## Traps

- **There is no edit modal for a posted GRN, and there must not be one** (`P1-13`). The moment one
  exists, correction stops being reversal and `L-3` is dead in practice while still true in the
  contract.
- **The reversal is a movement, not a compensating position write.** It goes through the single
  writer service, mirrors the original lines, sets `reversal_of_movement_id` and marks the original
  reversed (`FR-005`, `FR-035`). `whb_stock_positions` is a cache and is updated as a consequence,
  never directly.
- **Reversing a reversal is refused** (`FR-035`). The `/reverse` endpoint enforces it; this screen
  must not offer a path around it.
- **The PO line's `received_quantity` must be decremented in the same transaction.** If it is not,
  the PO shows a receipt that no longer exists, and the reorder logic and the three-way match both
  read the wrong number.
- **Blocking conditions must be *named*, not summarised.** *"Cannot reverse: 12 EA of `OF-1120` lot
  `L-2609` have been picked on shipment `SH-0042`"* is actionable; *"stock has moved"* is not.
- **A reversal into a `CLOSED` period is refused, including this one** (`FR-020`, `L-8`). A soft-close
  override is the sanctioned path and it is recorded (`P0-07`).
- **The approver may not be the requester** (`FR-408`), and the dependency row is
  `(wh_receipt_reversals:approve → wh_receipt_reversals:view)` — **the reverse row must not exist**
  (`P0-15`).
- **`cascade = CascadeType.ALL` on `wh_receipt_reversals → _lines` will look natural and is the
  wrong mapping** for anything that fronts ledger rows — 105 occurrences of that trap repo-wide
  (R1 §8 trap `T-1`).
- **One grid × two platform files**; `reasonCodeId` fetches from `whb_reason_codes` and must not be
  re-closed as a TypeScript union (`OD-5`, `FR-380`).
