Part of __P5__ · Module **`warehouse-3pl`** · Migrations **V530031** · Screens **Storage Billing Periods (WS-161)** · `V530031` gains `free_days_applied`, `days_charged` and `is_prorated` on `wh3_storage_billing_lines`, and `method` gains `SPLIT_MONTH` (`RF-007`)

## Scope

- **`V530031` — `wh3_storage_billing_periods` + `wh3_storage_billing_lines`** (`W3-06`): client, period
  from/to, method, basis, computed units, charge amount, status; lines log-style with the arithmetic
  shown.
- **Five methods**, and the contract names one per client:
  | Method | What it computes |
  |---|---|
  | `PERIOD_END` | occupancy on the last day of the period |
  | `PERIOD_START` | occupancy on the first day |
  | **`ANNIVERSARY`** | each pallet billed for a **storage month starting on its own receipt date** |
  | `AVERAGE_DAILY` | the mean of the daily snapshots across the period |
  | `SPLIT_MONTH` | a unit received on or before the 15th bills the full period; one received after it bills half (`RF-007`) |
- **Every one of the five reads `whb_stock_position_snapshots`** — `P2-28`'s persisted daily rows, one per
  date × client × owner × warehouse × basis × identity, carrying the **oldest receipt date**.
  **None of them recomputes occupancy from the current ledger.** Recomputing gives a different answer
  every time the ledger changes, and re-rating a closed period would not reproduce.
- **The minimum monthly charge posts a metered true-up event** on the seeded `MINIMUM_TRUEUP` charge code
  from `P5-02`, **with the arithmetic shown** — not a hidden invoice line. It is visible in the meter, in
  the run and in the client portal, and a dispute against it has something to point at. It is the single
  most common source of leaked 3PL revenue when it is a manual adjustment (`F-017`).
- Bases: pallet · location · unit · weight · cubic · square feet occupied · square feet allocated, with
  free days, a minimum billable quantity and aged-inventory surcharge bands.

> **Round-3 fold — `RF-007` (`GAP-REGISTER-R3.md` §2.6, §4.2).** **The methods and bases this task names
> are the ones that will be built, and each modifier has a column.** `FR-288` and this task promised
> more than any table held, so the data is extended rather than the requirement cut:
> - **`method` gains `SPLIT_MONTH`.**
> - **`basis` is `P5-02`'s enumerated whitelist**: `PALLET` · `LOCATION` · `UNIT` · `WEIGHT` · `CUBIC`
>   · `SQFT_OCCUPIED` · `SQFT_ALLOCATED`. `DATA-MODEL.md` §2.3's four-value `PALLET/SQFT/CBM/UNIT` is
>   retired in the same edit.
> - **Free days, the minimum billable quantity and the rounding** read `wh3_rate_card_lines`'
>   `free_quantity`, `minimum_charge`, `maximum_charge` and `rounding_mode` (`P5-02`, `V530021`).
> - **The ageing surcharge** reads `wh3_storage_aging_bands` by the line's `age_days`.
> - **The client minimum** reads `wh3_clients.minimum_monthly_charge` and `minimum_scope` (`P5-01`,
>   `V530010`).
> - `V530031` gives `wh3_storage_billing_lines` `free_days_applied` (`INTEGER`), `days_charged`
>   (`INTEGER`) and `is_prorated`, so the arithmetic a client disputes is stored, not re-derived.

## Traps

- **⚠ `PNR-3` governs what is billable at all.** `DATA-MODEL.md` `WHB-45` marks `V500045`'s snapshot job
  start date a **point of no return**: *"occupancy on each past day cannot be cheaply reconstructed, so
  storage billing, ageing, days-on-hand and obsolescence all begin on the day the job was switched on."*
  **A period before the job started cannot be billed by any method** and the screen must say so rather
  than returning zero. Assert the earliest snapshot date and refuse a period that starts before it, with
  a field-level error naming the date.
- **Anniversary is the method that cannot be computed from a month-end balance**, and it is the clearest
  single argument for `FR-100`'s `whb_lpns.received_at`. Verify that column exists and is populated
  **before** building this task; if it is not, anniversary billing is unavailable for the past, forever.
- **The true-up is a charge code, not a branch in the biller** (`F-013`). If you find yourself writing
  `if (total < minimum)` inside the invoice renderer, the design has gone wrong: the shortfall is a
  metered event on `MINIMUM_TRUEUP` and the renderer sums events like every other line.
- **Free days interact with anniversary in a way the other three methods do not** — a pallet received on
  the 28th with 7 free days has no billable storage month at all in that period. Assert this case
  explicitly; it is where the arithmetic is most often wrong.
- **Money `DECIMAL(19,4)`, per-unit `DECIMAL(19,6)`, quantities `DECIMAL(18,4)`, surcharge percentages
  `DECIMAL(9,6)`** (`OD-7`). `computed_units` for a square-feet basis is a quantity, not a percentage.
- **Recompute is permitted before approval and forbidden after** — the same freeze rule as `P5-05`'s run.
  Wire the guard to the run's state, not to the period's.
- The grid's statistics strip is filter-aware ⇒ **no `statistics.*` cache name** (R1 `C-043`,
  `CacheConfiguration.java:190-196`). `periodFromFrom`/`To` are two **`dateOnly`** filters.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Each of the five methods computes from `whb_stock_position_snapshots` — asserted by mutating the live ledger after the snapshot date and observing the charge **not** change
- [ ] Anniversary bills each pallet from its own `received_at`, and a pallet received mid-period is charged one storage month, not a pro-rata fraction, unless the contract says pro-rata
- [ ] A period whose start precedes the first snapshot row is **refused** with a field-level error naming the earliest billable date — it does not silently bill zero
- [ ] The minimum true-up appears as a **billable event on `MINIMUM_TRUEUP`** with a `computation_note` showing `minimum − metered = shortfall`, and is visible in WS-160, WS-162 and the portal
- [ ] A dispute can be raised against the true-up event (`P5-05`), which proves it is a real line and not a rendering
- [ ] Recompute is refused once a billing run holds any of the period's events; before that, only `:recompute` may recompute an APPROVED period, reversing its events. **Until `P5-05` ships nothing sets `billing_run_id` or BILLED, so every approved period stays recomputable — the freeze is inert until then** (W4 gate Deviation 1, amended)
- [ ] `SPLIT_MONTH` bills a unit received on the 15th for the full period and one received on the 16th for half, and every storage billing line stores `free_days_applied`, `days_charged` and `is_prorated` (`RF-007`)
- [ ] An aged unit picks up the surcharge of the `wh3_storage_aging_bands` row its `age_days` falls in, and a basis outside `P5-02`'s whitelist is refused (`RF-007`)
- [ ] **Mobile:** `none` — declared (`D-13`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-235` · `WH-SC-237`

## Decisions recorded at build
Build lane W4 (`classic` commit `58e0987fb9`, migration `V530031`), for the W4 gate to ratify:
- **The method is chosen at Compute** (with basis and STORAGE charge code). `wh3_clients` has no storage-method column, so the key is `uk(client_id, period_from, method)`; a per-client default method is a `P5-01` client-screen follow-up, not built here.
- **Recompute is wired to the run's state, not the period's** (Traps line). An APPROVED period whose events no billing run holds may be recomputed (escalated `:recompute`): its storage and true-up events are reversed through WS-160's `reverse()`, the period returns to COMPUTED and is recomputed. It is refused `409 STORAGE_PERIOD_BILLED` once any event carries `billing_run_id` or is BILLED. **This departs from the acceptance line "Recomputing an approved period is refused"** — the line is satisfied from the moment `P5-05`'s run takes the events; before that, refusing would leave a wrong approved charge with no correction path but a manual WS-160 Reverse. Compute on an APPROVED key is refused (`409 STORAGE_PERIOD_APPROVED`). The recompute actor also needs `wh3_billable_events` all-sites reading (P5-03 F8), because the events are site-less.
- **Money stays `DECIMAL(15,2)`, rounded once `HALF_UP`** (user ruling, as P5-03 F3); per-unit `DECIMAL(19,6)`, quantities `DECIMAL(18,4)`, fractions/percent `DECIMAL(9,6)`. A line's `charge` is display-rounded; the period total is rounded once.
- **Snapshot source only**: live, non-superseded, non-zero `whb_stock_position_snapshots` rows. The ANNIVERSARY/SPLIT_MONTH anchor is the snapshot's `oldest_receipt_date`, never live `whb_lpns`. A period must lie within `[MIN, MAX]` live `snapshot_date`: before the first is refused on `periodFrom` naming that date (`STORAGE_PERIOD_BEFORE_FIRST_SNAPSHOT`, PNR-3); after the last is refused on `periodTo`. At most 31 days.
- **Bases**: PALLET = distinct LPN (LPN-less rows skipped), LOCATION = distinct location, UNIT = quantity, WEIGHT = qty × `weight_gross_kg` (missing weight → 422), CUBIC = qty × `volume_cc` / 28316.846592 (cubic feet). **SQFT_OCCUPIED / SQFT_ALLOCATED are refused `422 STORAGE_BASIS_NOT_MEASURABLE`** — warehouse-base records no location floor area (open interface).
- **SPLIT_MONTH**: effective date = anchor + free days; on or before `period_from + 14` bills full, later bills half (`is_prorated`, `days_charged` = effective..to), past `period_to` bills nothing. **ANNIVERSARY**: storage months start at `(anchor + free days).plusMonths(k)` inside the period and the identity must be on hand that day (WH-SC-393: a 28th receipt with 7 free days bills nothing that month).
- **Rate modifiers from the P5-02 line** resolved on `period_to`: free days (lowest tier, then re-measured once with the resolved tier's), minimum quantity, rounding mode, min/max charge clamps, ageing band surcharge `[age_from, age_to)`, FLAT = the rate once.
- **Metering**: Approve records the storage charge (skipped when zero) and the true-up through `Wh3plBillableEventMeter.record`, born METERED and moved to RATED through the RF-008 allowlist; `source_system = WH3PL_STORAGE_BILLING`, subject `STORAGE_BILLING_PERIOD`, `warehouse_id` null (client-level). Approve refuses an overlapping APPROVED period of the same client (409).
- **True-up outcome is recorded** (`true_up_status`): NOT_APPLICABLE (no minimum), NOT_REQUIRED (met), POSTED (event on the `MINIMUM_TRUEUP` category code, qty 1 EA, rated at the shortfall), PENDING_RATING (unrated METERED events in the period — nothing is guessed), DEFERRED (WAREHOUSE-scope or non-MONTHLY minimum → `P5-05`). **Obligation on `P5-05`:** after rating, call `Wh3plMinimumTrueUpService.postTrueUp` for PENDING_RATING periods and apply DEFERRED minimums in the run.
- **`wh3_billable_events.computation_note VARCHAR(500)`** is added by `V530031` and shown on WS-160's Rating tab: `Minimum monthly charge X - metered Y = shortfall Z CUR (from to to)`; the storage event carries `METHOD BASIS from to to: units UOM x rate + surcharge S = charge CUR`. WS-162 and the portal read it from the event.
- **Site tier**: a period is client-level, so only ADMIN or `wh3_storage_billing_periods:view:all` read it; for a `:view:branch` user grid, statistics and export are empty and opening one is `403 STORAGE_PERIOD_SITE_NOT_PERMITTED`. Owner scope via `WhbOwnerScopeResolver` on every read.
- **Fresh DB**: `V530031` sorts before the applied `V531000` seed, so on a fresh DB its tier-permission and menu-leaf seeds skip with a NOTICE; a `V531xxx` reseed is needed (driver allocates). On the running stack it needs Flyway `outOfOrder` (it sorts below applied `V530101`).
- **Mobile:** none (FR-218, D-13). No scheduled job.

W4 fix round (`classic` commit `5de66c4f72`, migration `V531007`) — gate rulings on `p504-func` F1–F7:
- **Deviation 1 ACCEPTED with conditions**: acceptance bullet amended above (a); the freeze is inert until `P5-05` (b); the approved-branch recompute is covered by `Wh3plStorageBillingPeriodServiceTest` (c, F7).
- **Deviation 2 ACCEPTED**: money `DECIMAL(15,2)` rounded once `HALF_UP` — recorded as a deviation from Traps `OD-7` (which asks for 19,4), user ruling as P5-03 F3.
- **F1 (FR-290 amended)**: the minimum MONTHLY charge is judged only when `[periodFrom, periodTo]` is exactly one calendar month (1st to last day); any other period's true-up is `DEFERRED` to the run, so a half-month pair or a straddling window never charges the minimum twice.
- **F2 (PNR-3 Trap amended)**: every day the method reads — `period_to` for PERIOD_END, `period_from` for PERIOD_START, every day for AVERAGE_DAILY / SPLIT_MONTH / ANNIVERSARY — must have a completed snapshot at each warehouse where the owner holds stock in the period; otherwise `422 STORAGE_PERIOD_SNAPSHOT_DAY_MISSING` on `periodTo` (when the day is `period_to`) or `periodFrom`, naming the day and the warehouse. Evidence of a completed run is the site's live snapshot row on that day: `whb_job_runs` records the snapshot job install-wide, with no warehouse or snapshot date.
- **F3 → open interface on `P5-05`**: the run must re-judge POSTED true-ups before taking them (an event backdated into the month and metered after Approve raises "metered"; the posted shortfall is not reduced until then).
- **F4**: SPLIT_MONTH with no receipt date is refused `422 STORAGE_ANCHOR_MISSING` on `method`, like ANNIVERSARY — never billed as the full period.
- **F5 (T2 row for `:recompute` amended)**: `wh3_storage_billing_periods:recompute` **requires `wh3_billable_events:view:all`** (the reversed events are site-less). `V531007` grants the tier in `role_permissions` to every role holding `:recompute` (ADMIN already did — a no-op here) and records the `permission_dependencies` row; dependencies are documentation only at runtime, so any future grant of `:recompute` must grant the tier too.
- **F6**: the response carries `recomputable` (false once a live event of the period carries `billing_run_id` or is BILLED — `validateRecompute`'s test); the grid row action and the View modal's Recompute are hidden when it is false.
