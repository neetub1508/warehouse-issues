Part of __P5__ · Module **`warehouse-3pl`** · Migrations **V530031** · Screens **Storage Billing Periods (WS-161)**

## Scope

- **`V530031` — `wh3_storage_billing_periods` + `wh3_storage_billing_lines`** (`W3-06`): client, period
  from/to, method, basis, computed units, charge amount, status; lines log-style with the arithmetic
  shown.
- **Four methods**, and the contract names one per client:
  | Method | What it computes |
  |---|---|
  | `PERIOD_END` | occupancy on the last day of the period |
  | `PERIOD_START` | occupancy on the first day |
  | **`ANNIVERSARY`** | each pallet billed for a **storage month starting on its own receipt date** |
  | `AVERAGE_DAILY` | the mean of the daily snapshots across the period |
- **Every one of the four reads `whb_stock_position_snapshots`** — `P2-28`'s persisted daily rows, one per
  date × client × owner × warehouse × basis × identity, carrying the **oldest receipt date**.
  **None of them recomputes occupancy from the current ledger.** Recomputing gives a different answer
  every time the ledger changes, and re-rating a closed period would not reproduce.
- **The minimum monthly charge posts a metered true-up event** on the seeded `MINIMUM_TRUEUP` charge code
  from `P5-02`, **with the arithmetic shown** — not a hidden invoice line. It is visible in the meter, in
  the run and in the client portal, and a dispute against it has something to point at. It is the single
  most common source of leaked 3PL revenue when it is a manual adjustment (`F-017`).
- Bases: pallet · location · unit · weight · cubic · square feet occupied · square feet allocated, with
  free days, a minimum billable quantity and aged-inventory surcharge bands.

## Traps

- **⚠ `PNR-3` governs what is billable at all.** `DATA-MODEL.md` `WHB-45` marks `V500045`'s snapshot job
  start date a **point of no return**: *"occupancy on each past day cannot be cheaply reconstructed, so
  storage billing, ageing, days-on-hand and obsolescence all begin on the day the job was switched on."*
  **A period before the job started cannot be billed by any method** and the screen must say so rather
  than returning zero. Assert the earliest snapshot date and refuse a period that starts before it, with
  a field-level error naming the date.
- **Anniversary is the method that cannot be computed from a month-end balance**, and it is the clearest
  single argument for `FR-100`'s `whb_lpns.received_at`. Verify that column exists and is populated
  **before** building this task; if it is not, anniversary billing is unavailable for the past, forever.
- **The true-up is a charge code, not a branch in the biller** (`F-013`). If you find yourself writing
  `if (total < minimum)` inside the invoice renderer, the design has gone wrong: the shortfall is a
  metered event on `MINIMUM_TRUEUP` and the renderer sums events like every other line.
- **Free days interact with anniversary in a way the other three methods do not** — a pallet received on
  the 28th with 7 free days has no billable storage month at all in that period. Assert this case
  explicitly; it is where the arithmetic is most often wrong.
- **Money `DECIMAL(19,4)`, per-unit `DECIMAL(19,6)`, quantities `DECIMAL(18,4)`, surcharge percentages
  `DECIMAL(9,6)`** (`OD-7`). `computed_units` for a square-feet basis is a quantity, not a percentage.
- **Recompute is permitted before approval and forbidden after** — the same freeze rule as `P5-05`'s run.
  Wire the guard to the run's state, not to the period's.
- The grid's statistics strip is filter-aware ⇒ **no `statistics.*` cache name** (R1 `C-043`,
  `CacheConfiguration.java:190-196`). `periodFromFrom`/`To` are two **`dateOnly`** filters.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Each of the four methods computes from `whb_stock_position_snapshots` — asserted by mutating the live ledger after the snapshot date and observing the charge **not** change
- [ ] Anniversary bills each pallet from its own `received_at`, and a pallet received mid-period is charged one storage month, not a pro-rata fraction, unless the contract says pro-rata
- [ ] A period whose start precedes the first snapshot row is **refused** with a field-level error naming the earliest billable date — it does not silently bill zero
- [ ] The minimum true-up appears as a **billable event on `MINIMUM_TRUEUP`** with a `computation_note` showing `minimum − metered = shortfall`, and is visible in WS-160, WS-162 and the portal
- [ ] A dispute can be raised against the true-up event (`P5-05`), which proves it is a real line and not a rendering
- [ ] Recomputing an approved period is refused
- [ ] **Mobile:** `none` — declared (`D-13`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-235` · `WH-SC-237`
