## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `FR-447`, `U-005`.** **Delivery is confirmed, not inferred.** The shipment
> ladder ended at despatch and nothing in v1 recorded that the goods arrived, so *"was it delivered"*
> was answerable only by a carrier's website — and every downstream clock that starts at delivery
> (the return window, the claim window, the POD obligation) started at a date the system did not
> hold. `WS-105` gains a **Confirm delivery** action gated on `wh_shipments:confirm_delivery`,
> recording the delivery timestamp, the receiver and an optional document. **The driver-side ePOD
> stays `logistics` at v3** — this is the back-office confirmation, which is what a v1 warehouse
> actually has.

**`V510043` — WH-33 — `wh_shipments`, `wh_shipment_orders`.**
**`V510044` — WH-34 — `wh_carriers`, `wh_carrier_services`, `wh_carrier_accounts`.**

**The outbound object chain is shipment (ours) → consignment (transport, 1:1 and optional) →
manifest → trip** (`FR-193`), with **"add truck" living on the shipment** so a child consignment
cannot create siblings. *A third-party parcel simply has no consignment.* v1 ships the **shipment**;
`wh_consignments`, `wh_manifests` and `wh_handovers` are `V510104` / `P3`. The v1 obligation is that
the shipment's shape does not have to change when they arrive.

**Order → many shipments → many cartons is three tables in v1**, even though v1 ships one carton per
shipment in practice (`FR-179`). *A tracking-number column on the order is wrong within a month, and
retrofitting it changes every screen, export, tracking webhook and channel confirmation.*
`wh_shipment_orders` is a junction: **many orders per shipment and many shipments per order** —
**the cardinality is a one-way door**.

**Carrier, carrier service and carrier account masters, with `owner_id` nullable on the account**
(`FR-196`, `IRR-59`). **Null means the house account.** *That one nullable column is the whole of
"ship on the client's account", a standard 3PL contract clause* — and a schema change plus a
re-mapping of every existing account if it is added later. `credentials_ref` on the account is
**masked at the edge** per the platform admin-settings idiom: the response carries `hasValue`
(**`null`, not `undefined`**, when unset), never the value.

**The five relocatable objects are referenced by a stable code resolved through a service, never by
an FK from the referencing table** (`FR-199`): carriers, tracking events, NDR, RTO receipts and COD
remittances. *Moving them into a logistics module later is then a refactor and not a data
migration.* R4 names one movable boundary; **R7 `G-013` finds five and this design follows R7.**
Only **carriers** and **RTO receipts** exist in v1 — the rule applies to both from day one.

**Dispatch on WS-105 is the inventory-relief event** (`P2-09`, `FR-189`), and it is where
`P2-IN-04`'s e-way-bill precondition attaches on a Mode-D install (see that task's Traps —
`wh_handovers`, which `BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §5.1 names as the gate-pass host, is **v1.1**).
Since round 4 it is also **the dispatch of a transfer and of a supplier return** (`RJ-003`). For a
transfer the relief is into transit, so neither document has a dispatch of its own.

**A carrier is one-to-one with its party** (`RG-016`). `wh_carriers` carries `uk(counterparty_id)` in
`V510044`. This is a KEEP-SCALAR under `D-14`, because a carrier *is* a counterparty in a role. Per-site
scoping of a carrier account is `P5-11`'s v2 `wh_carrier_account_scopes`. v1 keeps the nullable
`owner_id` and adds no site column.

**The shipment ladder** (`RJ-004`). It is R24 Appendix A, adopted by `GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §2.3, in
§0.11's columns. *Mark loaded* is optional: dispatch is legal from `OPEN`.

| Table | From | To | Verb | Actor (permission) | Guard | Terminal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `wh_shipments` | — | `OPEN` | Add · *(created from a released demand order)* | `wh_shipments:create` | — | no |

| `wh_shipments` | `DISPATCHED` | `DELIVERED` | Confirm delivery | `wh_shipments:confirm_delivery` | not before dispatch, not twice (`FR-447`); for a transfer's shipment, set instead by the transfer's first *Receive* | **yes** |
| `wh_shipments` | `OPEN` · `LOADED` | `CANCELLED` | Cancel | `wh_shipments:cancel` | **only before `DISPATCHED`** (`RJ-006`); posts nothing, and the demand order keeps its reservations | **yes** |

**After dispatch a shipment is never cancelled** (`RJ-006`). Goods that come back are a return (`P2-12`)
or an RTO. Before round 4 *Cancel* was offered with no rule on whether it was legal after the relief
had posted.

`wh_shipments:confirm_delivery`.

## Traps

- **`IRR-59` — `owner_id` on the carrier account is nullable *from v1*.** `PNR-3`: adding it later
  is a schema change plus a re-mapping of every existing account.
- **The order↔shipment junction is many-to-many from v1.** A `shipment_id` on the order line, or an
  `order_id` on the shipment, is the one-way door `F-030` names. Ship `wh_shipment_orders` even
  though v1 practice is one order per shipment.
- **`FR-199` is a rule about references, not about tables.** `wh_carriers` is referenced by
  **stable code through a resolver service**, never by an FK from a relocatable table. Check both
  directions: a `wh_shipments.carrier_id` FK **is** legal (the shipment is not relocatable); a
  `wh_shipment_tracking_events.carrier_id` FK would not be.
- **`credentials_ref` is masked at the edge.** The response carries `hasValue` and it is **`null`,
  not `undefined`**, when unset — the platform admin-settings idiom. A `undefined` here renders as
  "configured" in the UI.
- **`T-18` — verify column names before native SQL.** The shipment grid joins platform `branches`
  through the warehouse; the column is **`branches.branch_name`**.
- **`T-4`** — `dispatched_at` / `delivered_at` from an `Object[]` native query can be any of four
  types; copy `platform/…/service/UserActivityLogQueryService.java:288-307`.
- **`T-11` — the frontend merge is last-write-wins across the whole `src/` tree**
  (`Dockerfile.frontend:80-177`). Every file here is `wh`-prefixed:
  `components/whShipment/…`, `services/api/whShipmentApi.ts`, `types/whShipment.ts`.
  `/warehouse/outbound/*` is `warehouse`'s exclusive route root (§0.8).
- **`T-7`** — three new `dropdown.*` cache names (carrier, carrier service, carrier account) go into
  `CacheConfiguration.java`; the shipment statistics strip gets **none**.
- **Precision.** `shipping_cost`, `declared_value` `DECIMAL(19,4)`; `total_weight_kg`
  `DECIMAL(12,3)`; `total_volume_cc` `DECIMAL(15,2)`.

## Acceptance
- [ ] One order splits across two shipments and each posts its own despatch movement; the junction carries both directions
- [ ] A carrier account with `owner_id IS NULL` is the house account and one with an owner is the client's, and dispatch on either works with no branch in the code
- [ ] `credentials_ref` never appears in any response; `hasValue` is `null` when unset
- [ ] No FK points **into** `wh_carriers` from a relocatable table; carrier references outside the shipment resolve through the code service
- [ ] The shipment shape does not need a column when `wh_consignments` lands in v1.1 — asserted by writing the P3 consignment FK against the existing `shipment_id`
- [ ] Three `dropdown.*` cache names registered; the statistics strip has none
- [ ] `mobile/src/screens/whShipment` dispatches, with `dispatchedWithin` as a dropdown
- [ ] `WS-105` has a **Confirm delivery** action gated on `wh_shipments:confirm_delivery`, recording timestamp, receiver and an optional document
- [ ] A shipment cannot be confirmed delivered before it is despatched, and cannot be confirmed twice
- [ ] The return window and the claim window compute from the confirmed delivery date, not from the despatch date
- [ ] No driver app, no signature capture and no geofence exists in this task's diff — the ePOD is `logistics` at v3
- [ ] The shipment ladder has no state without an inbound transition and no non-terminal state without an outbound one; *Cancel* is refused once the shipment is `DISPATCHED` (`RJ-004`, `RJ-006`)
- [ ] *Confirm delivery* and *Cancel* are each gated by their own verb, seeded in `V511204` with dependency rows in `V511234` (`RJ-005`)
- [ ] `wh_carriers` refuses a second carrier for the same `counterparty_id` (`RG-016`)
- [ ] Dispatching a shipment that carries a `TRANSFER` demand order moves the transfer to `IN_TRANSIT`, and one that carries a `VENDOR_RETURN` order moves the supplier return to `DISPATCHED` (`RJ-003`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-094` · `WH-SC-103` · `WH-SC-104`
