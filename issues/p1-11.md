## Traps

- **Do not touch `all_activity_history`.** It is **dealer-owned** (`dealer/V20412:354`,
  `dealer/V20885:85-177`) and `accessories` **explicitly declined to join it**
  (`accessories/V30379:7-9`). Redefining or joining it from warehouse creates a **dealer dependency
  in `warehouse-base`**, which `WarehouseBaseCouplingTest` fails and `D-7` forbids (`C-048`,
  `FR-428`, R1 §8 trap `T-14`). Warehouse owns `whb_activity_history` and stays out of the view.
- **`whb_transport_details` is polymorphic by `(document_type, document_id)` — and that is *not* an
  FK.** It attaches to a `wh_transfer_orders` row (app), a `whin_delivery_challans` row (India) or an
  issue, and **`warehouse-base` may not FK into any of them** (`FR-350`, §3.3's generic-reference
  rule). The document type resolves through the display-resolver registry (`FR-357`, `P0-04`).
- **The e-way-bill fields are in base and are not India-specific.** `D-8`: the core carries the
  *hooks* India needs because they cannot be added later; the *rules* live in `warehouse-india`.
  A reviewer will read `vehicle_number` + `distance` as India creep — it is the opposite.
- **The vehicle's dual identity must not become a master-slave relationship.** A location row and an
  asset row, joined through an external-ref table, **neither the other's master** (`FR-339`). Making
  the asset the master means stock cannot sit on a vehicle in a standalone install (`D-7`).
- **`OD-2` — does dealer's vehicle inventory migrate onto this ledger?** **Not in v1 or v2.** Model it
  as a documented future adapter and **prove the ledger on parts first** (`G-050`). This task builds
  the seam, not the migration.
- **`whb_channels` in base, not in app.** It is referenced by the ledger's source lineage and by
  `whb_item_identifiers` (marketplace listing codes, `FR-059`), so it cannot live in `warehouse`
  without base FK-ing upward.
- **The view is `whb_`-prefixed and in the base band** — `V500054` — and it creates no table, so its
  migration must be idempotent through `CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW`.
- **One grid × two platform files**: `WAREHOUSE_CHANNEL` in `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS` and
  `dropdown.whbChannel` in `CacheConfiguration.java`.
