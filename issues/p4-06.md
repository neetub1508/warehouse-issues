## Scope

- **`V540150`** — `whin_approval_dispatches`, `whin_approval_clocks`
  (`DATA-MODEL.md` §7.6 WIN-18).
- **Goods sent on approval / sale or return are a stock state at the customer that is still our
  asset** (`FR-322`): the goods sit at a location representing the customer's premises, with
  **`owner_id` unchanged**, on a **challan**, with a **deemed-supply clock**. It is **the same
  owner-and-location mechanism as consignment** (`WH-SC-276` models all six commercial arrangements
  this way) and as job work (`P4-02`).
- **The clock's anchor column shipped in wave 1** — `deemed_supply_due_date` and
  `expected_return_date` on `whin_delivery_challan_lines` (`P2-IN-03`). A movement recorded before
  the challan object existed can never acquire one.
- **The challan purpose is `APPROVAL`** on the **one** challan table (`FR-313`, `WH-SC-207`). This
  task creates no second challan.
- **Three outcomes, and all three must exist**: the customer keeps the goods (an ordinary sale,
  closing the clock), returns them (an ordinary receipt back into stock), or the clock expires (a
  **deemed supply** — an obligation raised, not a silent lapse).

## Traps

- **`owner_id` does not change.** `L-11`: ownership never changes silently and a title transfer is an
  explicit movement type with its own reason code. Goods on approval are **ours** at **their**
  location right up to the moment the customer keeps them — and the moment they do, that *is* the
  title transfer, and it is a movement.
- **One challan table.** `FR-313`: warehouse's delivery challan **is** the challan, with its own
  per-branch series from `whb_number_series`. `whin_approval_dispatches` references the challan; it
  does not carry its own numbering.
- **A partial outcome is the normal case.** A customer keeping 4 of 10 and returning 6 is one
  dispatch with three quantities on it; modelling the outcome as a status on the header loses the
  split.
- **The clock is a dated obligation and therefore needs its job** (`FR-165` — *a threshold column
  with no scheduled job that reads it is a defect at the moment it is merged*), with a `whb_job_runs`
  row (WS-064) and named notification recipients. The obligation is raised **before** the six months
  elapse, not after: an alert on the day of expiry has reported a tax liability, not an obligation.
- **Expiry is not automatic posting.** When the clock runs out the goods have become a deemed supply
  in law, but the **document** is accounting's to raise (`OD-9` — warehouse never computes tax, and
  warehouse never issues an invoice or a credit note, `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §1.6). Warehouse
  raises the **obligation** and hands over the facts.
- **`deemed_supply_due_date` is a SQL `DATE` with a `dateOnly` filter** (`FR-327`, `P-045`) — the
  prior art typed exactly these columns as `TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE` mapped to `OffsetDateTime`, so
  *"expired today"* shifted across a day boundary.
- **`dueWithinDays` is a dropdown of named horizons, not a date filter**, so the decision is
  mobile-carryable: `mobile/src/components/common/ListHeader.tsx:216` supports
  `type?: 'dropdown' | 'text'` and **no date type**.
- **This task depends on `P4-02`** for the ageing machinery, which is the same clock over a different
  challan purpose. Building a second ager is how the two purposes drift apart.
- **No `whb_`/`wh_` table gains a foreign key to a `whin_` table** (`D-11`).
