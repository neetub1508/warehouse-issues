## Scope

**`V500050`** — `whb_item_site_settings`, `whb_item_supplier_sources`, `whb_item_supersessions`.
**`DATA-MODEL.md` §2.1 is the authority on all three column sets and must be re-read immediately
before the migration is written.**

- **Reorder point, safety stock, min, max, reorder quantity and lead time on item × site** in v1
  (`FR-053`, `FR-252`). Item-level values are a **ten-branch dealer's worst answer**; item × location
  is v1.1 (`P3-12`).
- **Catch weight as a second, independent quantity** (`FR-065`): `is_catch_weight` on the item,
  `secondary_quantity` + `secondary_uom_code` on the movement line and the position — **those two
  ledger columns are `P0-02`'s and share `PNR-2`** (`IRR-35`). v1 writes null; the columns exist.
- **`tax_classification_code` as a string, never an FK into a tax master** (`FR-066`), **snapshotted
  onto every document and movement line** so a reclassification does not rewrite a filed return
  (`FR-318` — again `P0-02`'s `hsn_code`, `IRR-42`).
- **The hazmat block** (`FR-067`): UN number, class 1–9, packing group, proper shipping name,
  subsidiary classes — on the item, in v1.
- **Temperature / storage class on the item and a temperature zone on the location** (`FR-068`).
  A putaway into the wrong zone is **rejected by the port**, not warned about.
- **The three shelf-life columns** (`FR-069`): `shelf_life_days`, `min_shelf_life_receipt_pct`,
  `min_shelf_life_ship_pct` — v1 columns even though FEFO and the shelf-life enforcement points ship
  in `P2-05`.
- **ABC / velocity / count-frequency classes as v1 columns with a v3 job** (`FR-070`). `P6-02` owns
  the recompute.
- **Supersession chains with cycle detection** (`FR-071`): `chain_sequence`, `quantity_ratio`, a type
  (`REPLACES`/`INTERCHANGE`/`PARTIAL`), an effective date, and **`stock_treatment`**.
- **MRP, net content, country of origin and pack month/year on the lot, with item defaults**
  (`FR-320`) — the **lot** columns are `P1-07`'s; the item defaults are here.
- **The statutory unit-quantity code on the item, copied onto every line** (`FR-319`). The return
  carries the government's unit code, not our UoM name.
- **Every statutory, expiry, manufacture and count date is a SQL `DATE`, not a timestamp**
  (`FR-327`) — and therefore a **`dateOnly`** filter, not a `date` filter.

## Traps

- **`dateOnly`, not `date`, for every column in `FR-327`'s set.** `filterUtils.ts:49-56` documents
  that `date` shifts to UTC day boundaries for `TIMESTAMP` columns, *"which moves the 'from' bound to
  the previous calendar day for users east of UTC"*. An expiry filter that silently includes the
  previous day is a recall that misses a batch. The prior art hit this too — `P-045` is the
  date-typed-statutory-fields remediation.
- **There is no `daterange` filter type** (`filterUtils.ts:30` — eight types). Every from→to pair is
  **two keys**, and **both** go in the scope allowlist.
- **A class column with no job is a defect at merge** (`FR-165`): `abc_class`, `velocity_class` and
  `count_frequency_class` are v1 columns whose recompute is `P6-02`. Enter them in the
  **dated-obligation register** (`P0-13`) with their owning task, or the register is already
  incomplete on the day it ships.
- **Supersession cycle detection is not optional.** A → B → A is a real data-entry outcome and an
  un-detected cycle makes "resolve to the terminal item" an infinite loop in a request thread
  (`FR-071`).
- **`MERGE_STOCK` posts a movement.** It is not a data fix: merging the old part's stock into the new
  one moves quantity at the old part's cost layers and must go through the single writer service
  (`FR-436`). Gate it on its own permission.
- **No price on `whb_item_supplier_sources`.** `FR-119` keeps commercial terms out of
  `warehouse-base`; a price column here is how a second commercial master starts.
- **`tax_classification_code` must not become an FK.** `FR-066` is explicit, and the prior art's
  relational tax engine is `warehouse-india` v1.1 material (`P-043`), not a base FK target.
- **`is_catch_weight` without the two ledger columns is a dead flag.** `secondary_quantity` and
  `secondary_uom_code` are `P0-02`'s and share `PNR-2` — **verify they are in `V500030` before this
  task merges**, because after that they can never be added (`IRR-35`).
- **Three grids × two platform files**, and `belowReorderPoint` is a **backend-computed boolean
  filter** — a frontend `.filter()` over the rows is a CLAUDE.md CRITICAL #8 violation and is wrong
  under pagination anyway.
