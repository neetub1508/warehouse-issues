## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `H-005`.** **`wh_replenishment_tasks` is a 1:1 extension of the base task,
> not a second task table.** In the `V510108` scope bullet, state it: `task_id` is unique and FK
> to `whb_tasks` (`P0-10`), and the extension duplicates none of `whb_tasks`' lifecycle columns.
> The handheld queue reads the **base** table, so a replenishment interleaves with a pick and a
> count without a union query (`FR-255`, `FR-259`). The same sentence is due on `wh_count_tasks`
> in `P2-04`.

- **`whb_item_location_settings`** (`V500061`, `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.2 WHB-61) — reorder policy at
  **item × location**. v1 shipped item × **site** (`P1-03`, `FR-053`/`FR-252`); this is the follow-on
  half named in `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §8.2, and it is what makes a pick face addressable.
- **`wh_replenishment_tasks`** (`V510108`, `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.3 WH-98) — **pick-face replenishment
  tasks generated from location min/max**, with **priority against pick starvation** (`FR-255`).
- The `trigger` vocabulary is `MIN_MAX` · `SHORT_PICK` · `OPPORTUNISTIC` · `BREAK_CASE`. Only
  `MIN_MAX` and `SHORT_PICK` are v1.1; `OPPORTUNISTIC` and `BREAK_CASE` are `FR-259`, v2 — the column
  admits them so the task type is not re-keyed later.

## Traps

- **Priority against pick starvation is the requirement, not a nice-to-have.** A replenishment queued
  behind 200 picks arrives after the pick face is empty, and the picker who found it empty has
  already logged a short. The replenishment task's priority must beat an ordinary pick for the
  **same location**, and that is a rule in the `P3-02` pull query, not a number a planner types.
- **`SHORT_PICK` closes the loop with `wh_insufficient_stock_log`** (`P2-09`). A short pick that does
  not raise a replenishment leaves the next picker to discover the same empty bin.
- **Item × location does not replace item × site.** `FR-053`/`FR-252` shipped at the site grain in
  `P1-03` and both grains coexist: the site policy is the default and the location row is the
  override. Resolving them needs a stated ladder — the more specific row wins — or two planners set
  two numbers and neither knows which fired.
- **`whb_item_location_settings` is `warehouse-base` and `wh_replenishment_tasks` is `warehouse`.**
  The settings table is referenced by the replenishment generator in app; app depends on base and
  never the reverse (`D-1`).
- **Quantities are `DECIMAL(18,4)`** (`DATA-MODEL.md` §5.1). `min_quantity`, `max_quantity` and
  `replen_trigger_quantity` are quantities, not money and not per-unit figures.
- **The generator is a scheduled job and it needs a `whb_job_runs` row** (`FR-165`, WS-064): *a
  threshold column with no scheduled job that reads it is a defect at the moment it is merged*. A
  min/max pair with no generator is exactly that defect.
- **Generate in SQL over the position table, not by iterating items in Java.** 3,400 items at one
  site (`WH-SC-281`) with a `findById` per item is the N+1 CLAUDE.md CRITICAL 5 forbids.
- **The `trigger` column carries no `CHECK`** (`D-10`) — `OPPORTUNISTIC` and `BREAK_CASE` are v2
  values that must be insertable by migration without a DROP/ADD.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A pick-face location carries min, max and a trigger quantity, and `isPickFace` is settable
- [ ] Falling below the trigger generates a `MIN_MAX` replenishment task with a from-location and a to-location
- [ ] A short pick generates a `SHORT_PICK` replenishment for the same location
- [ ] A replenishment for a location outranks an ordinary pick against that location in the pull query
- [ ] Site-level and location-level policies resolve by a **stated** specificity ladder, and the grid shows which one applied
- [ ] The generator appears in `whb_job_runs` with records-read and records-written
- [ ] 3,400 items at one site generate in SQL, with no per-item query
- [ ] `trigger` has no `CHECK` constraint
- [ ] The replenishment task appears on WS-237 RF Task List
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-228` (jointly with `P3-02`, `P3-06`) — and `WH-SC-280` still passes
