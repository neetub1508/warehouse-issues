## Why a fifth module, said once

**`D-1`.** The brief's four modules carry a *vertical* axis and **no jurisdiction axis**. Every India
rule — e-way bill, delivery challan, job work and ITC-04, the Rule 56 stock account, MRP
declarations, bonded warehousing — would otherwise live either in `warehouse`, **making the core
product unsellable outside India**, or in `warehouse-adapter-dealer`, **making it invisible to a
pharma customer**. Accounting took the same decision and shipped `accounting-india`.

**`A-4` splits it into two waves.** **v1 / P2-IN** — the documents required to move goods legally.
**v2 / P4** — the statutory registers and filings. The line between them is **"can the truck
leave?"**

## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Q-006`.** `WH-SC-304` is the portal-unavailability rule, and it is a
> **product decision, not an error handler**: **master-data entry is never blocked by a government
> portal being down.** A GSTIN profile saves with verification `UNVERIFIED` when the taxpayer
> endpoint returns `502` or exceeds the environment's `timeout_ms`; every attempt writes a
> `whin_compliance_api_logs` row with `http_status`, `duration_ms` and `correlation_id`; the
> screen reads *"GSTIN not verified — the portal did not respond; saved and queued"*, **never
> *verified*, and never a bare `500` with the form's contents lost**. Retries are idempotent on
> the correlation id, and **the row never flips to verified without a portal response** — an
> expired `token_expires_at` produces the same shape, not a false pass.

**`V540010` — WIN-01 — `whin_gstin_profiles`, `whin_compliance_registrations`.**

`whin_gstin_profiles`: `gstin` **unique**, `company_id`, `branch_id`, `state_code`,
`registration_type` (**a catalogue FK, not a `CHECK`** — `REGULAR` · `COMPOSITION` · `SEZ_UNIT` ·
`SEZ_DEVELOPER` · `CASUAL` · `NON_RESIDENT` · `UNREGISTERED`), `legal_name`, `trade_name`,
`effective_from`, `effective_to` (**both `DATE`**), `is_active`, `certificate_document_id`.
**The GSTIN is read from the branch (`FR-079`) and profiled here — it is never duplicated onto the
warehouse.**

`whin_compliance_registrations`: the registration of a GSTIN profile with a compliance provider —
`gstin_profile_id`, `provider_id`, `environment`, `document_kinds`, `registered_at`, `status`,
`token_expires_at`. Its **`_credentials` and `_auth_sessions` children are `P2-IN-02`'s
`V540011`**, so **WS-175's *Rotate credentials* and *Test connection* actions cannot ship until
`P2-IN-02` lands** — build the grid and the Register action here and state the sequencing on the
screen.

**`V541000`–`V541049`** — wave-1 permissions, `permission_dependencies` (**inserted, never
created**), the menu tree with `menu_translations` **en / fr / hi** and `WHERE NOT EXISTS` guards,
grid configuration with **both** `default_columns` and `default_filters`, and `admin_settings`.

**Routes: `/warehouse/india/*` is this module's exclusive root** (§0.8). Files are `whin`-prefixed.

**Mobile.** `none` for WS-173 and WS-175 — *this module is a compliance desk*. The one mobile screen
in `warehouse-india` is `screens/whinEwayBill` (`P2-IN-04`), and it does **Fill Part-B and Update
vehicle only**. Stated as a decision, per `D-13`.

- **`OD-9` gates this task and it decides the module's size.** *Does `warehouse` carry a tax engine,
  or never compute tax?* `FR-325` has it carrying one; `FR-294` and R3 say it never does. **The
  answer is: warehouse never computes tax.** It captures the facts — HSN, place of supply,
  `is_taxable_supply` frozen at creation, taxable value — and hands them over.
  **This drops 6 conditional tables**: the India pack is **50 or 56** depending on the answer.
  Confirm it is recorded in `DECISIONS.md` §3 before writing `V540000`.
- **Two live divergences between the two authorities, carried and not silently resolved.**
  `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §1.4 states both and rules that **`DATA-MODEL.md` is the migration
  authority**:
  (a) `INDIA-LOCALISATION-PACK.md` §11.1 counts **14** wave-1 tables including three seed masters
  (`whin_gst_state_codes`, `whin_hsn_codes`, `whin_uqc_codes`); `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.6 puts the
  equivalents at **`WIN-12`, `V540110`, wave 2**, leaving **12** in wave 1.
  (b) the two documents name the registration table differently —
  `whin_gst_registrations` versus `whin_gstin_profiles` + `whin_compliance_registrations`.
  **Follow `DATA-MODEL.md`.** Filed in `DEFECTS-FOUND.md`.
- **The India pack's own migration blocks collide with `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.6 and must not be used.**
  §11.1 puts `whin_delivery_challans` at `V540100`–`V540119` and `whin_eway_bills` at
  `V540200`–`V540229`; `DATA-MODEL.md` puts them at **`V540020`** and **`V540030`** and reserves
  `V540100`+ for **wave 2** (`whin_eway_bill_vehicle_updates` is `V540100`). §11.2 additionally puts
  wave-2 group A at `V541000`–`V541099`, which is **exactly the band `DATA-MODEL.md` gives to
  wave-1 permissions and grids (`WIN-30`, `V541000`–`V541199`)**. Filed in `DEFECTS-FOUND.md`.
- **`warehouse-base` must not gain a foreign key into `warehouse-india`, in either direction**
  (`D-11`, `MODULE-INTEGRATION.md:889`). Therefore **`whb_warehouses.tax_registration_id` is a
  `UUID` with no `REFERENCES` clause**, resolved through a registry at read time — the same
  external-reference pattern the item and location masters use. In a non-India install it is simply
  null, and the `ArchitectureInvariantsTest` proving `warehouse-base` names no `whin_` table keeps
  it that way.
- **GSTIN is in three of our own tables today and they disagree by design** (census run 2026-09-01):
  `branches.gst_number VARCHAR(15)` (`platform/…/V182__Add_financial_columns_to_branches.sql:7`) is
  the per-state registration `CLAUDE.md:148` names; `companies.gstin`
  (`automotive/…/V10002__Create_companies_table.sql:19`) **contradicts that rule and was never
  dropped**; `dealers.gstin` (`dealer/…/V20000:11`) is a third candidate.
  **Read the branch.** Duplicating onto the warehouse is how the three-way disagreement happened.
- **There is no GST state-code master and no state-code column anywhere.**
  `grep -rn "gst_state_code\|state_code" --include="*.sql" .` excluding client/test/seed → **0**.
  `branches.state` is a free-text `VARCHAR(100)` (`platform/…/V149__create_branches_table.sql:33`),
  **not** the two-digit code Part A of an e-way bill requires. `whb_warehouses.state_code`
  (`P1-05`) is a **denormalised snapshot with a derivation rule** — the first two digits of a valid
  GSTIN — not a second source of truth. The state-code **master** is `WIN-12`, wave 2.
- **`D-3` — never name anything `wms_*` or `scc_*`.** `platform/…/V528:37,57,59` and `V663:17-19`
  still carry hardcoded `wms_*`/`scc_*`/`warehouse-*` **permission exclusions** from a deleted
  earlier module; those migrations have already run, so a new `wms_`-prefixed permission would
  inherit a decision nobody took.
- **`T-15` — do not copy `accounting-india`'s banding.** It is `V602000`–`V602999`, **inside**
  `accounting-base`'s `V600000`–`V609999` — a sub-band carve-out putting a dependent module's DDL
  numerically **below** its base's later migrations. `warehouse-india`'s band is **disjoint**.
- **`T-12`** — all module migrations are flattened into one directory at build time
  (`Dockerfile.backend:140-181`); a number outside the band is a **startup failure**.
- **`T-9`** — `WHERE NOT EXISTS` on every menu insert. **`T-10`** — `permission_dependencies` rows
  inserted, never created; columns `permission_id` / **`dependent_permission_id`**.
- **`T-8`** — `grid_preferences` needs **both** arrays as `'[…]'::jsonb`.
- **`P-045` / `FR-327`** — `effective_from`/`effective_to` are **`DATE`/`LocalDate`**, filtered
  **`dateOnly`**. The prior art shipped statutory dates as `TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE` mapped to
  `OffsetDateTime`, so *"expired today"* shifted across a day boundary.
- **`AF-2`** — en / fr / hi, in `WarehouseIndiaSafeTranslation.tsx`.
- **`FLYWAY_OUT_OF_ORDER`** — a warehouse-first install (V5xxxxx applied) that later adds dealer
  (V2xxxx) is **refused** unless the flag is flipped; the effective Docker setting is
  `out-of-order: false`. State it in the install guide (`IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §3.7 rule 4).
