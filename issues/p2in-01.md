## Why a fifth module, said once

**`D-1`.** The brief's four modules carry a *vertical* axis and **no jurisdiction axis**. Every India
rule — e-way bill, delivery challan, job work and ITC-04, the Rule 56 stock account, MRP
declarations, bonded warehousing — would otherwise live either in `warehouse`, **making the core
product unsellable outside India**, or in `warehouse-adapter-dealer`, **making it invisible to a
pharma customer**. Accounting took the same decision and shipped `accounting-india`.

**`A-4` splits it into two waves.** **v1 / P2-IN** — the documents required to move goods legally.
**v2 / P4** — the statutory registers and filings. The line between them is **"can the truck
leave?"**

## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Q-006`.** `WH-SC-304` is the portal-unavailability rule, and it is a
> **product decision, not an error handler**: **master-data entry is never blocked by a government
> portal being down.** A GSTIN profile saves with verification `UNVERIFIED` when the taxpayer
> endpoint returns `502` or exceeds the environment's `timeout_ms`; every attempt writes a
> `whin_compliance_api_logs` row with `http_status`, `duration_ms` and `correlation_id`; the
> screen reads *"GSTIN not verified — the portal did not respond; saved and queued"*, **never
> *verified*, and never a bare `500` with the form's contents lost**. Retries are idempotent on
> the correlation id, and **the row never flips to verified without a portal response** — an
> expired `token_expires_at` produces the same shape, not a false pass.

**`V540010` — WIN-01 — `whin_gstin_profiles`, `whin_gstin_profile_branches`,
`whin_compliance_registrations`, `whin_compliance_registration_document_kinds`.**

`whin_gstin_profiles`: `gstin` **unique**, `company_id`, `state_code`,
`registration_type` (**a catalogue FK, not a `CHECK`** — `REGULAR` · `COMPOSITION` · `SEZ_UNIT` ·
`SEZ_DEVELOPER` · `CASUAL` · `NON_RESIDENT` · `UNREGISTERED`), `legal_name`, `trade_name`,
`effective_from`, `effective_to` (**both `DATE`**), `is_active`, `certificate_document_id`.
**The profile no longer carries `branch_id`** (`RG-002`). A company holds one registration per state,
and every other branch in that state is an additional place of business under it. With a scalar
branch, only one of two Delhi branches could resolve the Delhi GSTIN, so a site registered to the
other could issue no challan and no e-way bill.

**`whin_gstin_profile_branches` — the places of business under a registration** (`RG-002`). It uses
R22 §1.3's junction shape, under `D-14` item 1:
- **Columns:** `gstin_profile_id`, `branch_id` ↓ platform `branches(id)`, `company_id` (copied from the
  profile by trigger), `place_role` (`PRINCIPAL` / `ADDITIONAL`), and `effective_from` / `effective_to`.
  The two dates are `TIMESTAMPTZ` and half-open `[from, to)`, because a resolver asks for the link at an
  instant.
- **Constraints:** a partial unique index allows one current `PRINCIPAL` per profile.
  `EXCLUDE USING gist (company_id WITH =, branch_id WITH =, tstzrange(effective_from, effective_to,
  '[)') WITH &&)` keeps a branch under one registration per company at every instant. The migration
  declares `btree_gist` itself and fails loudly if it is absent.
- **`place_role` is a statute-closed set.** It is therefore a `CHECK`, under `OD-5`'s ruling for
  closed system vocabularies — not a `D-10` catalogue and not a code list (`GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.6).
- **The history is `IRR-64`.** The Rule 56 account needs to know which branches a registration covered
  in Q4, and `branches.gst_number` has no dates.

**The GSTIN is stored on the profile and checked against the branch** — the choice `RH-005` required
this task to make. *"Read from the branch"* and a unique `gstin` column cannot both stand, so `gstin`
**stays**, with two guards:
- **On save**, the service refuses a place-of-business link whose branch's `branches.gst_number`
  differs from the profile's `gstin`.
- **Every night**, an assertion compares every current link and writes a drift row to `P3-16`'s health
  grid. Platform can edit the number, and warehouse cannot block that edit.

The GSTIN is still never duplicated onto the warehouse. **A site's GSTIN resolves site → its
`REGISTERED` link at the document date → branch → `whin_gstin_profile_branches` → profile** (R22
§1.2.4 row 1). Under `D-14` item 2, `whb_warehouses` carries no branch, no tax registration and no
legal-entity column.

**WS-173 gains a *Places of business* row action** (`D-14` item 8b) — a modal over
`whin_gstin_profile_branches` (branch, place role, from, to) with *Add place* and *End link*; the link is
dated, so *End link* sets `effective_to` and nothing is deleted. It takes no new screen id (`GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §4.0). The cast's second Delhi branch
is the proof: an additional place of business under the Delhi GSTIN resolves the profile and issues a
challan (`WH-SC-313`).

**Three `WarehouseBranchLinkValidator` beans** (R22 §1.2.2, `RG-001`). `warehouse-base` exposes the
`List<WarehouseBranchLinkValidator>` bean collection (`P1-05`) and never learns a jurisdiction rule
(`D-8`). This module contributes three validators:
- **(a) The `REGISTERED` branch's GSTIN state code equals `whb_warehouses.state_code`.** An additional
  place of business is always in its registration's own state, so a cross-state `REGISTERED` link is
  refused (`WH-SC-311`). `SERVING`, `FULFILMENT` and `RETURNS` links may cross a state line. Across one
  they are an inter-state supply, and recording that is the point of the link.
- **(b) The branch's GSTIN profile belongs to the warehouse's company.**
- **(c) The change rule.** A `REGISTERED` change to a branch under a different GSTIN is refused while
  the site holds any stock (R22 §1.2.6). This is `OD-19`'s safe default, and it stays until an adviser
  answers that decision.

`whin_compliance_registrations`: the registration of a GSTIN profile with a compliance provider —
`gstin_profile_id`, `provider_id`, `environment`, `registered_at`, `status`, `token_expires_at`.
**Its `document_kinds` list is a child table**, `whin_compliance_registration_document_kinds`
(`registration_id`, `document_kind_code`). Storing a list in one column is `RG-018`'s defect, and §1.6
forbids the `JSONB` that would otherwise carry it.

**The India `tax_treatment_code` values** (`RL-008`). `P0-04` renames `whb_reason_codes.itc_treatment`
to `tax_treatment_code` in the country-neutral core. This module seeds the India values — the
input-tax-credit treatments — in `V540010`, using `RL-003`'s guarded seed idiom: it inserts only codes
it owns, and raises an error if another module owns one.

**And it seeds them into `whb_reason_code_tax_treatments`, because nothing else can.** `P0-04`'s
`V500072` creates that dated per-scheme side table (`RG-020`, `FR-468`) and ships it **EMPTY**. It
cannot do otherwise: a backfill from `whb_reason_codes.tax_treatment_code` is permanently a no-op —
base always seeds that scalar `NULL` (`RL-008`), and **this module's band, `V540000+`, sorts AFTER
`V500072`** on every install, for ever. A backfill in base is therefore unreachable code with a
verification that passes vacuously, and it has been removed from `V500072` for exactly that reason.

So **`V540010` writes BOTH**: the scalar, for v1 readers that have not moved, **and** one
open-ended `whb_reason_code_tax_treatments` row per reason code it treats — `country_code = 'IN'`,
`scheme_code = 'GST'`, `effective_from` the date the treatment applies from (**not** "today", which
would leave every movement already posted against the code outside every treatment range), and
`effective_to NULL`. Guarded with `WHERE NOT EXISTS` on `(reason_code_id, scheme_code)` so a re-run
stays idempotent and the table's `EXCLUDE` never fires on our own second run. A second jurisdiction's
pack seeds its own `scheme_code` the same way, with zero commits to `warehouse-base`.

The registration's **`_credentials` and `_auth_sessions` children are `P2-IN-02`'s
`V540011`**, so **WS-175's *Rotate credentials* and *Test connection* actions cannot ship until
`P2-IN-02` lands** — build the grid and the Register action here and state the sequencing on the
screen.

**`V541000`–`V541049`** — wave-1 permissions, `permission_dependencies` (**inserted, never
created**), the menu tree with `menu_translations` **en / fr / hi** and `WHERE NOT EXISTS` guards,
grid configuration with **both** `default_columns` and `default_filters`, and `admin_settings`.

**Routes: `/warehouse/india/*` is this module's exclusive root** (§0.8). Files are `whin`-prefixed.

**Mobile.** `none` for WS-173 and WS-175 — *this module is a compliance desk*. The one mobile screen
in `warehouse-india` is `screens/whinEwayBill` (`P2-IN-04`), and it does **Fill Part-B and Update
vehicle only**. Stated as a decision, per `D-13`.

- **`OD-9` gates this task and it decides the module's size.** *Does `warehouse` carry a tax engine,
  or never compute tax?* `FR-325` has it carrying one; `FR-294` and R3 say it never does. **The
  answer is: warehouse never computes tax.** It captures the facts — HSN, place of supply,
  `is_taxable_supply` frozen at creation, taxable value — and hands them over.
  **This drops 6 conditional tables**: the India pack is **50 or 56** depending on the answer.
  Confirm it is recorded in `DECISIONS.md` §3 before writing `V540000`.
- **Two live divergences between the two authorities, carried and not silently resolved.**
  `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §1.4 states both and rules that **`DATA-MODEL.md` is the migration
  authority**:
  (a) `INDIA-LOCALISATION-PACK.md` §11.1 counts **14** wave-1 tables including three seed masters
  (`whin_gst_state_codes`, `whin_hsn_codes`, `whin_uqc_codes`); `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.6 puts the
  equivalents at **`WIN-12`, `V540110`, wave 2**, leaving **12** in wave 1.
  (b) the two documents name the registration table differently —

- **The India pack's own migration blocks collide with `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.6 and must not be used.**
  §11.1 puts `whin_delivery_challans` at `V540100`–`V540119` and `whin_eway_bills` at
  `V540200`–`V540229`; `DATA-MODEL.md` puts them at **`V540020`** and **`V540030`** and reserves
  `V540100`+ for **wave 2** (`whin_eway_bill_vehicle_updates` is `V540100`). §11.2 additionally puts

- **`warehouse-base` must not gain a foreign key into `warehouse-india`, in either direction**
  (`D-11`, `MODULE-INTEGRATION.md:889`). **Since `D-14` item 2, base holds no tax identity at all.**
  `whb_warehouses.tax_registration_id`, `branch_id` and `legal_entity_id` are dropped. The only link
  base keeps is `whb_warehouse_branches` → platform `branches`, and this module resolves the rest,
  branch → `whin_gstin_profile_branches` → profile. In a non-India install there is simply no profile.
  The `ArchitectureInvariantsTest` proving `warehouse-base` names no `whin_` table keeps it that way.
- **`place_role` is a `CHECK`, and that is correct** (`RG-002`, `OD-5`). `PRINCIPAL` / `ADDITIONAL`
  is a statute-closed set that no install may extend. Do not "fix" it into a `D-10` catalogue or a
  code list. `OD-5` permits a closed vocabulary only where the product itself defines it and no
  install may extend it, and this is that case.
- **The `EXCLUDE` needs `btree_gist`, and a standalone install has nothing else that creates it.**
  Declare the extension in `V540010` and fail loudly if it is absent. Do not assume `accounting-base`
  or `assets` created it, because `D-7`'s reference install has neither.
- **GSTIN is in three of our own tables today and they disagree by design** (census run 2026-09-01):
  `branches.gst_number VARCHAR(15)` (`platform/…/V182__Add_financial_columns_to_branches.sql:7`) is
  the per-state registration `CLAUDE.md:148` names; `companies.gstin`
  (`automotive/…/V10002__Create_companies_table.sql:19`) **contradicts that rule and was never
  dropped**; `dealers.gstin` (`dealer/…/V20000:11`) is a third candidate.
  **Read the branch.** Duplicating onto the warehouse is how the three-way disagreement happened.
- **There is no GST state-code master and no state-code column anywhere.**
  `grep -rn "gst_state_code\|state_code" --include="*.sql" .` excluding client/test/seed → **0**.
  `branches.state` is a free-text `VARCHAR(100)` (`platform/…/V149__create_branches_table.sql:33`),
  **not** the two-digit code Part A of an e-way bill requires. `whb_warehouses.state_code`
  (`P1-05`) is a **denormalised snapshot with a derivation rule** — the first two digits of a valid
  GSTIN — not a second source of truth. The state-code **master** is `WIN-12`, wave 2.
- **`D-3` — never name anything `wms_*` or `scc_*`.** `platform/…/V528:37,57,59` and `V663:17-19`
  still carry hardcoded `wms_*`/`scc_*`/`warehouse-*` **permission exclusions** from a deleted
  earlier module; those migrations have already run, so a new `wms_`-prefixed permission would
  inherit a decision nobody took.
- **`T-15` — do not copy `accounting-india`'s banding.** It is `V602000`–`V602999`, **inside**
  `accounting-base`'s `V600000`–`V609999` — a sub-band carve-out putting a dependent module's DDL
  numerically **below** its base's later migrations. `warehouse-india`'s band is **disjoint**.
- **`T-12`** — all module migrations are flattened into one directory at build time
  (`Dockerfile.backend:140-181`); a number outside the band is a **startup failure**.
- **`T-9`** — `WHERE NOT EXISTS` on every menu insert. **`T-10`** — `permission_dependencies` rows
  inserted, never created; columns `permission_id` / **`dependent_permission_id`**.
- **`T-8`** — `grid_preferences` needs **both** arrays as `'[…]'::jsonb`.
- **`P-045` / `FR-327`** — `effective_from`/`effective_to` are **`DATE`/`LocalDate`**, filtered
  **`dateOnly`**. The prior art shipped statutory dates as `TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE` mapped to
  `OffsetDateTime`, so *"expired today"* shifted across a day boundary.
- **`AF-2`** — en / fr / hi, in `WarehouseIndiaSafeTranslation.tsx`.
- **`FLYWAY_OUT_OF_ORDER`** — a warehouse-first install (V5xxxxx applied) that later adds dealer
