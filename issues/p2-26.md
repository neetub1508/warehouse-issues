## Scope

**Two adapters ship in v1, not one, because one adapter proves nothing about genericity**
(`FR-352`). *The port is accepted only if neither adapter required a base-schema change the other did
not want.* `warehouse-adapter-services` is also **the largest consumer of the port**, and it is a
clean sheet: `C-034` — **`services`, `field-service`, `assets`, `insurance-360` and `submittals`
contain zero stock/parts/consumable tables**, and `services.parts_used` is free `TEXT`
(`services/…/V40095__Change_parts_used_from_jsonb_to_text.sql`).

**Migrations.**
- **`V521000`** — bootstrap + source-system, movement-type and document-type seed, **idempotently**.
- **`V521010`** — `whas_material_requests`, `whas_material_request_lines`.
- **`V521011`** — `whas_job_part_issues`.
- **`V521012`** — `whas_fitted_serials`.
- **`V521100`–`V521149`** — permissions, `permission_dependencies` (inserted), menus
  (`WHERE NOT EXISTS`), grid config with **both** default arrays, i18n en / fr / hi. The permissions
  include the three verbs the material request lacked: `whas_material_requests:reserve`,
  `:return_unused` and `:cancel` (`RJ-005`). `:issue` already exists in `BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §10.2.
- `whas_warranty_holds` (`V521013`) is **v2**. Do not write it here.

**The full v1 loop** (`FR-360`): a **material request against a job card** → **a reservation before
it is an order** → **issue in parts as the job progresses** → **return to store of unused parts,
crediting the job** → the warranty split. *Without the return path the job is over-costed, the stock
is short, and the variance appears three months later as shrinkage.*
Every step posts through the **public port** with `holder_system = ADAPTER_SERVICES` and the job
card as the holder document.

**Which site a job may draw from — `FR-461` applied** (`D-14` item 3, `RK-002`). `P2-25` owns
`FR-461` and writes it in full.
- **Whose branch.** The job card's branch lives in `services`, and the adapter reads it (`D-11`).
- **When it may post directly.** A material request posts directly from a site **only when** that
  branch holds a current `REGISTERED` or `SERVING` link to the site **and** its `branches.gst_number`
  equals that of the site's `REGISTERED` branch at the request date.
- **The tax registration.** The issue's registration is the site's `REGISTERED` branch.
- **A different-GSTIN `SERVING` site** is refused with the same `422 CROSS_GSTIN_COUNTER_SALE`, and
  offered the same *Raise request* (`P2-02`) — one rule, one refusal code (`GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.1).
- **WS-199's warehouse picker** offers the linked sites only.

**The material-request ladder** (`RJ-004`). It is derived from WS-199's actions and uses §0.11's
columns. Partial issue and partial return are **quantities on the line**, not states — as
part-approval is on a transfer. *Close* joins WS-199's actions: the last unused part returning to the
store is what closes a request.

| Table | From | To | Verb | Actor (permission) | Guard | Terminal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `whas_material_requests` | — | `REQUESTED` | Request | `whas_material_requests:create` | job card open; the `FR-461` pair rule passes | no |
| `whas_material_requests` | `REQUESTED` | `RESERVED` | Reserve | `whas_material_requests:reserve` | reservations taken through the public API, holder = the job card | no |
| `whas_material_requests` | `RESERVED` · `ISSUING` | `ISSUING` | Issue | `whas_material_requests:issue` | consumes the reservation progressively, never release-and-retake | no |
| `whas_material_requests` | `ISSUING` | `ISSUING` | Return unused | `whas_material_requests:return_unused` | returned ≤ issued − already returned; credits the job at layer cost | no |
| `whas_material_requests` | `ISSUING` | `CLOSED` | Close | `whas_material_requests:return_unused` | every unissued reservation released | **yes** |
| `whas_material_requests` | `REQUESTED` · `RESERVED` | `CANCELLED` | Cancel | `whas_material_requests:cancel` | nothing issued; reservations released by holder quad | **yes** |

`whas_material_request_lines.line_status` is `OPEN` until its header reaches a terminal state, and
then takes that state. A line never has a state its header does not.

**Parts issued to open jobs are neither stock nor cost of sale** (`FR-361`) and are reported as
**work-in-progress at every month end**. That report is **WS-200 with `openJobsOnly` on**, and the
WIP value is `Σ (issued − returned) × layer cost` from `P2-16`'s consumptions.

**A serialised part fitted to a customer's vehicle records the vehicle it was fitted to at issue
time** (`FR-362`) — `whas_fitted_serials.vehicle_ref_type` + `vehicle_ref_id`, captured **at issue**.
*Retro-linking a year of parts issues to vehicle identifiers is impossible, and a vehicle-linked
recall depends on it.*
The reference is a **generic pair with no FK** out of the adapter (§3.3), because the vehicle may be
an automotive record or a services record depending on the install.

## Traps

- **`C-034` — this is a clean sheet, and that is the risk.** There is no parts table in `services`
  to migrate from and no prior art to copy; every design choice is new. Budget accordingly.
- **The genericity proof is falsifiable and it is the point.** If this adapter needs a
  `warehouse-base` change that `P2-25` did not, **the port has failed its acceptance** (`FR-352`).
  Record any such need as a finding against the port, not as a quiet base commit.
- **The package is a sibling: `ai.warehouseadapterservices`**, never `ai.warehouse.adapter.services`
  (`D-1`) — `@ComponentScan("ai.warehouse")` would load it in every install.
- **`WH-SC-226`'s fallback is the acceptance for `FR-357`.** With the services resolver present the
  job-card reference renders as a job card; **without it, on a Mode-A install, it renders through the
  base fallback and does not break.** Test both.
- **The vehicle reference is a generic `(type, id)` pair with no FK.** An FK from `whas_` into
  `automotive` couples the adapter to a specific install shape; `OD-4`'s rule — *never an FK from
  base into another module* — generalises here, and R7 §3.3 records that the previous attempt at
  this seam died of **84 FK references into `scc_*`, several to tables that never existed**.
- **WIP is computed from consumptions, not from a stored balance.** `Σ (issued − returned) × layer
  cost` reading `whb_cost_layer_consumptions`. A stored WIP column is a second truth.
- **`T-16`, `T-9`, `T-10`, `T-11`, `T-12`, `FR-356`** apply exactly as in `P2-25`: controllers under
  `ai.warehouseadapterservices.controller…`; `WHERE NOT EXISTS` on menus; `permission_dependencies`
  inserted never created; `whas`-prefixed files under `/warehouse/services/*`; numbers strictly
  inside `V521000`–`V521999`; idempotent reference-data seed.
- **`AF-2`** — ship **en / fr / hi**, in this module's own SafeTranslation file.
- **`T-6`** — three filter scopes into `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`; every date pair is two keys.
  `requestedFrom`/`To`, `issuedFrom`/`To` and `fittedFrom`/`To` are all `date` pairs.
