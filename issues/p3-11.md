## Scope

- **`wh_work_orders`, `wh_work_order_lines`, `wh_vas_service_types`** (`V510102`, `DATA-MODEL.md`
  §7.3 WH-92).
- **The work order is both the VAS record and the light-manufacturing record** (`FR-262`): type, kit,
  output item, planned / produced / scrapped quantities, a scrap reason, planned and actual start and
  end, assignee, a **client-instruction document reference** and a **billing charge code**. The
  instruction document exists because VAS instructions arrive as a PDF and the operator needs exactly
  the version that was priced.
- **Kit assembly and disassembly are balanced ledger transactions, never an update to a parent
  quantity** (`FR-261`). Components out, kit in, **one movement**.
- **Assembly completion balances by value, not by quantity** (`FR-263`), and carries a **variance
  reason** where it does not. A quantity-balanced assembly is arithmetically impossible: three inputs
  do not equal one output in units, and only value conserves.
- **Repack, break-case and UoM conversion are two-sided ledger events, never an `UPDATE` to a
  quantity in a different unit** (`FR-264`). A break-case posts `−1 CASE / +12 EA` — the **same 12
  base `EA` on both sides**, so `L-1` holds exactly.
- **A kit's cost split breaks its ties on a declared ordinal** (`RF-009`). A kit's cost apportioned
  across its outputs is a largest-remainder split (`R-4`). `R-4`'s tie-break is the sink's declared
  ordinal: `line_no` on `wh_work_order_lines`, and `sequence` on each kit output row. So Σ parts ==
  whole, and next year's re-run reproduces the same split.
- **Packaging and consumables are ordinary stock items** (`FR-265`), consumed by a **line on the same
  movement as the pack**, owned by the house owner unless the client supplies them — which is why the
  packaging master carries a **nullable owner**.
- **The four work-order verbs are real permissions** (`RJ-005`): `wh_work_orders:release`,
  `:issue_components`, `:complete` and `:cancel` are seeded in `V511208`, and their
  `permission_dependencies` rows (each verb requires `:view`) in `V511238`, this task's `WH-206`
  pair (`RJ-005`). A transition with no verb permission is a transition anybody with `:edit` can
  perform (`RJ-005`).
- **The kit half of `WH-SC-135`'s lot trace lands here** (`RJ-013`). The scenario's *"consumed into
  two assembly work orders"* clause needs work orders, which are v1.1; `P1-07` keeps the trace across
  shipments and locations, and the trace across a kit is walkable once this task writes
  `whb_transformations` at completion, with `P5-19` owning genealogy (`RJ-013`).

## Traps

- **Never `UPDATE` a parent quantity.** `FR-261` and `L-2`: the ledger is append-only and
  double-sided, so assembly is components out and kit in on one movement. An implementation that
  decrements components and increments the kit through two separate movements loses the link and
  makes `WH-SC-288`'s variance uncomputable.
- **Value conservation, not quantity conservation, and it is an `OD-11` question.** `L-1`…`L-14`
  conserve **quantity only**; `OD-11` is the open decision that adds `L-15` for value. The assembly
  completion is the second place in the product (after landed cost) where quantity does not balance
  and value must. **Do not invent a local rule** — read `OD-11`'s resolution before writing the
  trigger, and if it is still open when this task starts, that is a blocker on `P2-16`, not a
  judgement call here.
- **Break-case balances because both sides are expressed in the base unit.** `−1 CASE` is `−12 EA` of
  base quantity; `+12 EA` is `+12 EA`. If the ledger stores the transacted UoM without a base
  quantity, `L-1` fails on every conversion and the fix is a re-key.
- **Packaging is consumed on the same movement as the pack, not on a second one.** `FR-265`,
  `WH-SC-289`. A separate consumption movement means the carton and the tape are unattributable to
  the shipment that used them, which is exactly the figure a 3PL bills.
- **The packaging master's owner is nullable** — house-owned unless the client supplies it. A
  `NOT NULL` owner here forces house ownership of client-supplied cartons and puts a client's asset
  on our balance sheet (`L-14`).
- **Genealogy is written at completion, and only at completion** (`FR-105`/`FR-266`, `IRR-54`,
  `PNR-3`). Which input lots went into which output unit is knowable only at the moment of
  transformation — the evidence is then physically inside the kit. **`FR-266` (genealogy surviving
  assembly) is `P5-19`'s**, but the `whb_transformations` tables shipped in v1 and this task is the
  first thing that populates them; not populating them here means a permanent hole for every
  assembly done before P5.
- **`scrap_factor_percent` and every other `*_percent` is `DECIMAL(9,6)`**; per-unit figures such as
  `unit_cost` are `DECIMAL(19,6)`; extended totals are `DECIMAL(19,4)` (`DATA-MODEL.md` §5.1). The
  `unit_*` name pattern beats the `value`/`_amount` pattern.
- **The client-instruction document reference is a version, not a link to "the current PDF".** The
  operator needs exactly the version that was priced; a reference that follows the latest upload
  changes what was agreed after the fact.
- **`wh_work_order_lines` must not be mapped `cascade = ALL, orphanRemoval = true`** (R1 `T-1`, 105
  occurrences repo-wide, live example `accessories/.../entity/SalesOrder.java:161`).
