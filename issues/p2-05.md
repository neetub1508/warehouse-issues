Part of __P2__ · Module **`warehouse-base` + `warehouse`** · Migrations **none** · Screens **WS-221 Expiry & Shelf-life Register** — lots and serials are `P1-07`'s, the shelf-life columns `P1-03`'s

## Scope

No DDL. This task turns columns that already exist into behaviour, and it ships **the job, the
recipients and the report or it does not ship at all** (`FR-160`, and `FR-165`'s rule that a dated
obligation with no executor is a defect at merge).

**Expiry is a state, not an alert** (`FR-160`).
- A **scheduled job** moves lots past expiry into the `EXPIRED` stock status **by a balanced
  status-change movement with a reason code** — two lines at the same location, `actor_type =
  SCHEDULED_JOB`, no `UPDATE` to a position row.
- The allocator **refuses** `EXPIRED`: it does not consider the lot at all, and a forced despatch is
  refused with a field-level error on `lines[0].lot_id`.
- A **notification at `expiry − near_expiry_days`** to named recipients.
- A **near-expiry report with configurable buckets** ships with it.

**The four shelf-life enforcement points are named separately, not collapsed into the word FEFO**
(`FR-161`). *A design that says FEFO and implements only the second fails a food or pharma demo on
the first and third, and fails an audit on the fourth.*

| # | Enforcement point | Where it fires |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Refuse receipt below X % remaining shelf life** | receipt validation, `min_shelf_life_receipt_pct` on the item |
| 2 | **Allocate earliest-expiry-first** | the `FEFO` allocation strategy row (`P2-07`), never an `if` |
| 3 | **Refuse to ship below Y days remaining**, per customer or channel, resolved counterparty → channel → item (`RJ-009`) | despatch confirm |
| 4 | **Auto-expire on a schedule** | the job above |

v1 ships all four; **grading and the refurbishment half of `FR-161` is `P5-13`**.

**Point 3 resolves most-specific-first, and the refusal names where its minimum came from**
(`RJ-009`). The service reads three nullable columns in order:
1. `whb_counterparties.min_shelf_life_ship_pct` (`P1-08`, `V500011`);
2. `whb_channels.min_shelf_life_ship_pct` (`P1-11`, `V500051`);
3. `whb_items.min_shelf_life_ship_pct`.

The first non-null value wins. Before round 4 only the item column existed, so `WH-SC-134`'s
customer-specific 40 % had nowhere to live. `GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.7 f takes these two columns rather
than a rule table with a category axis. `FR-161` point 3 and the scenario need only customer and
channel, and the columns need no new screen.

**A lot has its own lifecycle status, independent of item and location** (`FR-096`), set by jobs —
`EXPIRED` is this task's. **A lot *hold* is not that status** (`RJ-008`). It is one `wh_holds` row
with `hold_scope = LOT` (`P2-03`), which holds the lot everywhere at once without moving anything. The
allocator reads open holds, never the lot's status column, for a hold.

**The retest date has a job** (`RJ-012`, `FR-165`). `whb_lots.retest_date` (`P1-07`) is a dated
column, and until round 4 nothing read it.
- A daily job registered in `P0-13`'s dated-obligation register places a `wh_holds` row of the system
  hold type `RETEST_DUE` (`P2-03`'s seed) on every lot whose retest date has passed. It posts no
  movement.
- It notifies the site's QA role by the same path as the near-expiry notice, and writes a
  `whb_job_runs` record.
- WS-221 lists lots due for retest in its own bucket.
- A QA holder releases the hold after the retest. The lot cannot be allocated while it is held.

**A serial answers the three post-sale questions** (`FR-099`): *where is it now, who did we sell it
to, is it in warranty* — sold-to party, warranty start and end, current custody, and **the shipment
that carried it**. The columns are `P1-07`'s; this task makes the three answers queryable and puts
them on the serial detail view.

**Aged stock and no-movement buckets are measured from the last outward movement, not from
receipt** (`FR-162`), with value per bucket and a branch and warehouse split. **The branch split is by
the site's `REGISTERED` branch**, as at the report date (`RG-001`, R22 §1.2.4 row 11). It is never by a
linked branch, which would count a shared site once per branch. *A lot received two
years ago that shipped last week is not aged stock.* This is the raw material for the obsolescence
percentage (`FR-393`, `P2-21`) and for `P2-20`'s ageing report; this task owns the **measurement
rule**, `P2-20` owns the report `WS-212`.

**WS-221 · Expiry & Shelf-life Register** · `/warehouse/reports/expiry` · Customer reference ·
scope `WAREHOUSE_EXPIRY_REGISTER`. Lot, item, warehouse, owner, quantity, value, best-before,
use-by, days remaining, percentage remaining, bucket, current stock status. Bucket filter is a
**dropdown of named buckets**, so it works on the handheld (`C-044`).

**Mobile.** `mobile/src/screens/whLot` gains the lot status and the days-remaining column; the
expiry register itself is a controller report — `none`, stated.

## Traps

- **`FR-165` inverted — the job is the deliverable.** A `near_expiry_days` column with no scheduled
  job that reads it, or a job with no notification recipients, is a defect at the moment it is
  merged. The expiry job is registered in `P1-20` **together with** its cache names and filter
  scopes; verify it is there before closing this task.
- **`P-045` — best-before and use-by are `DATE`/`LocalDate`, filtered `dateOnly`.** The prior art
  shipped them as `TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE` mapped to `OffsetDateTime`, so *"expired today"* shifted
  across a day boundary; the fix was `DATE` + `LocalDate` + `CURRENT_DATE`.
  **Lot expiry is the one that bites: FEFO allocation and the expiry sweep both branch on it.**
- **Best-before and use-by are two columns with different despatch rules** (`P1-07`), not one
  `expiry_date`. Enforcement point 3 reads the one the item's policy names.
- **The expiry job posts movements, so it obeys `L-8`.** A movement into a locked stock period is
  refused **including this one**. The job must handle `PERIOD_CLOSED` as an expected outcome and
  surface it on the job-run record, not swallow it.
- **`actor_type = SCHEDULED_JOB`** on the movement, with no `actor_user_id` (`FR-024`,
  `WH-SC-037`). A job posting as a service account that looks like a person makes the adjustment
  register unreadable.
- **The allocator must not learn about expiry.** Point 2 is a **row** in
  `whb_allocation_strategies` (`FR-172`, `P2-07`), not a branch. `IRR-47`: *by the third hardcoded
  strategy the cost exceeds the original build.*
- **`T-6` — `WAREHOUSE_EXPIRY_REGISTER` goes into `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`**, and its date filters
  are **`dateOnly`** because the columns are `DATE`.
- **Ageing measured from last outward movement needs an index.** The query is *"max `occurred_at`
  over outward movement lines per position"*; it is not free at ledger scale, and `PP-7` applies —
  enforce a bounded scope on the register in v1.
- **Precision.** `min_shelf_life_receipt_pct`, `min_shelf_life_ship_pct` **`DECIMAL(9,6)`**.

## Acceptance
- [ ] The four enforcement points are **four separately named service methods with four separate tests**, and the word FEFO appears in none of their names
- [ ] The expiry job posts a balanced two-line status-change movement with `actor_type = SCHEDULED_JOB` and a reason code, and writes no position row directly
- [ ] The job is registered alongside the count and reservation-expiry jobs, and a run into a closed period is recorded as a handled outcome rather than an exception
- [ ] A notification fires at `expiry − near_expiry_days` to a named recipient set, and the recipients are configuration, not code
- [ ] An expired lot is invisible to the allocator and cannot be force-despatched
- [ ] A lot hold — one `wh_holds` row — blocks the lot at **every** location in one action; the lot's lifecycle status is never read as a hold (`RJ-008`)
- [ ] Enforcement point 3 takes the first non-null minimum of counterparty, then channel, then item, and the refusal names which of the three supplied it (`RJ-009`)
- [ ] The retest job places a `RETEST_DUE` hold on every lot past `retest_date`, notifies the QA role, writes a `whb_job_runs` record, and the lot cannot be allocated until the hold is released (`RJ-012`)
- [ ] The aged-stock branch split groups a shared site under its `REGISTERED` branch only, once (`RG-001`)
- [ ] A serial answers *where is it now*, *who did we sell it to* and *is it in warranty* in one query, and names the shipment that carried it
- [ ] Ageing buckets are computed from the **last outward movement**; a long-held, recently-shipped lot lands in the newest bucket
- [ ] Every expiry, best-before, use-by and manufacture filter is `dateOnly`, and every such column is `DATE`/`LocalDate`
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-130` · `WH-SC-131` · `WH-SC-132` · `WH-SC-133` · `WH-SC-134` · `WH-SC-139` · `WH-SC-107` · `WH-SC-286` · `WH-SC-090`
