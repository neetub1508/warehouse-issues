## Scope

- **`wh_asns`, `wh_asn_lines`, `wh_asn_line_serials`** (`V510012`, `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.3 WH-03). The
  ASN is a **document**, not a transport object, and warehouse owns it (`FR-136`): lines carry
  shipped quantity, lot, expiry, serial and **SSCC**. It drives dock appointments, pre-allocation and
  cross-dock.
  - `wh_asn_line_serials` is the **normalised replacement for the prior art's `serial_numbers JSONB`**
    (`FR-383`: no JSONB on any new warehouse business table).
- **Live dock appointments** on WS-086. The **schema shipped in v1** at `V510010` (`P1-06`) with
  `arrived_at`, `docked_at`, `departed_at`, `no_show` and `detention_minutes` already being captured
  (`WH-SC-274`); **this task ships the screen** and the check-in / dock / depart / no-show
  transitions.
- **Seal numbers captured at load and at unload** (`FR-211`), and **the receiving session owns the
  seal, the gate pass, the driver and the arrival photographs** — not the GRN and not the PO. This is
  `P-020`'s document-ownership rule: *a document should own only the information that legally belongs
  to it*. Receiving Session owns physical arrival; GRN owns the legal receipt; Invoice owns finance;
  PO owns the lifecycle.

## Traps

- **`arrived_at` starts the dock-to-stock clock and cannot be backfilled** (`WH-SC-274`,
  `IRREVERSIBLE.md` PNR-3 — observational, not schema). A check-in keyed an hour later is an hour of
  detention nobody can bill and a KPI nobody can defend. The Check-in action writes `now()`, not a
  user-entered time.
- **`detention_minutes` is metered from the same row.** Do not compute it in the frontend — CLAUDE.md
  FRONTEND RULE 8 and `PLATFORM-DEPENDENCIES.md` §2.11: **there is no decimal library on the
  frontend** (`ls */frontend/package.json` → 1 file, 38 dependencies, no `decimal.js`/`big.js`), so
  every quantity, duration and money figure is computed on the backend in `BigDecimal` and sent
  formatted.
- **Serials are rows, never a JSONB array.** `wh_asn_line_serials` exists precisely because the prior
  design used `serial_numbers JSONB` (`FR-383`, `T-013`, `P-007`, `C-036`). CLAUDE.md's blanket
  "NO JSONB" is overstated — `ArchitectureInvariantsTest.java:225-239` freezes a baseline — but the
  rule that holds is **no jsonb on a new `wh_` business table**, and grid-preference columns are the
  only permitted use.
- **The seal is the receiving session's, not the GRN's.** `P-020`: four owners, four documents. A
  seal on the GRN cannot answer *"was the seal intact when the truck arrived"* for a truck carrying
  three POs, because there are three GRNs and one truck.
- **A seal is captured twice — at load and at unload** (`FR-211`). One column cannot hold both, and
  a mismatch between them is the finding the whole feature exists to surface.
- **Arrival photographs go through `documents` with warehouse's own link table.** The platform
  `documents` table (`V81:39`) has **no polymorphic owner**; every module builds its own link table.
  Use `NO ACTION` on the FK, **not** `ON DELETE CASCADE` — `acc_document_links` uses cascade
  (`V600110:104-105`) and for a damage certificate that silently deletes the evidence when the file
  row goes.
- **`wh_dock_appointments` schema is already allocated to `V510010` and is `P1-06`'s.** This task
  writes `V510012` only. Adding a column to the appointment table here is a `P1` amendment, not a new
  migration in this block.

## Acceptance
- [ ] An ASN is created with lines carrying shipped quantity, lot, expiry, serial and SSCC, and its serials are **rows**
- [ ] Receiving against the ASN pre-populates the receipt and the variance against shipped quantity is visible
- [ ] A truck booked for 10:00 checks in at 09:41, docks at 10:12 and departs at 13:05, and `detention_minutes` is computed on the backend from those three columns
- [ ] Check-in writes `arrived_at` at the moment of the action and offers no editable time field
- [ ] A no-show is recordable and appears on the grid
- [ ] The seal number captured at load and the seal number captured at unload are **two values**, and a mismatch is surfaced
- [ ] The gate pass, driver and arrival photographs hang off the receiving session, not the GRN
- [ ] The mobile dock screen offers check-in and nothing else; the mobile ASN screen is read-only
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-274`'s screen half
