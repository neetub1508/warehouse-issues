## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Y-006`.** **Split and merge execute here.** `P1-07` states the rule and
> seeds the two `INTERNAL` movement types; this task performs them the same way it already
> performs an LPN move — **one movement with stored expanded lines** — conserving under `L-1`,
> refusing on a non-`OPEN` source LPN, and recomputing `is_mixed_item` / `is_mixed_lot` /
> `is_mixed_owner` on the surviving LPN. A lost LPN is the reason-coded write-off, not a delete.

- **An LPN move is one movement whose lines the service expands from the LPN's current contents and
  *stores*** (`FR-101`) — never left implicit. Moving a pallet holding 6 items across 9 lots writes
  **18 lines**, not a reference to *"whatever was on the pallet"*. Reading that movement a year later
  shows exactly what moved, without re-deriving it from a pallet whose contents have since changed.
- **Nested LPNs.** `parent_lpn_id` is a **v1 column** (`V500018`, `P1-07`); nested-LPN *operations*
  are v2 (`FR-101` is `P3·P5`, the follow-on is `P5-11`). This task handles the single-level move and
  leaves the nesting behaviour to P5, stated rather than silent.
- **GS1 element-string parsing** (`FR-063`): AI `01` (GTIN), `10` (lot), `17` (expiry), `21`
  (serial), `00` (SSCC) and `310n` (net weight with an implied decimal), honouring **fixed against
  variable length** and the **FNC1 separator**, returning a composite
  `{gtin, lot, expiry, serial, sscc, quantity}` from **one scan**.
  - **The resolver's return shape already admits a composite from v1** (`P1-02`), so this is a new
    parser behind an existing contract and **not a rewrite of every scan surface** (`WH-SC-266`).

## Traps

- **`whb_lpns.received_at` is untouched by a move** (`WH-SC-145`). It is the **storage-anniversary
  anchor** and the only thing anniversary storage billing can key off (`IRR-15`); resetting it on a
  bin-to-bin move makes per-pallet billing for every past period undefined, and `wh3_` billing is v2
  so the damage is invisible until then.
- **Expand and store, never expand at read time.** A movement that references the LPN and resolves
  its contents when queried gives a different answer every time the pallet changes. `L-12`
  (traceability reconstructible in both directions) fails silently.
- **Quantity is never stored on the barcode** (`WH-SC-262`). A `CASE` scan yields 12 `EA` because the
  **packaging level row** says the case contains 12 — a quantity on the barcode, or a UoM on the
  barcode, is the same dual truth in a different column.
- **`310n` carries an implied decimal position in the AI itself.** `3102` means two decimal places:
  `3102000750` is 7.50 kg, not 750. Getting this wrong silently mis-weighs every catch-weight receipt
  — and `secondary_quantity`/`secondary_uom_code` are already reserved on the line and position for
  exactly this (`IRR-35`).
- **FNC1 is only required after a variable-length AI**, and different scanners emit it as different
  characters (GS `\x1D` most often, but keyboard-wedge configurations vary). Parse defensively and
  **surface an unparseable string rather than guessing** — `WH-SC-265` asserts that an unrecognised
  string is handled at every one of the six scan surfaces.
- **The parser lives in `warehouse-base` and is called by every surface**, including the RF family
  (`P3-01`) and the mobile scan path. One parser, not one per screen.
- **Expiry from AI `17` is `YYMMDD` and a day component of `00` means "end of month"** — a real
  encoding, not a data error. It resolves to a `DATE`, and `FR-327` makes every statutory, expiry,
  manufacture and count date a SQL `DATE` with a `dateOnly` filter, never a `TIMESTAMPTZ`.
- **This task writes no migration.** `whb_lpns`, `parent_lpn_id` and the identifier registry all
  shipped in v1 (`V500016`, `V500018`). If a column is missing, it is a `P1` amendment — and
  after `V500030` the ledger is sealed against `UPDATE`, so a movement-line column added now is
  `NULL` forever on every existing row (`PNR-2`).
