## **Both migrations must land before `PNR-1` (`V500030`, `P0-02`)**

`whb_stock_movements.company_id` and `whb_stock_movements.period_id` are both **`NOT NULL`**
(`DATA-MODEL.md` §2.1; `IRR-17`, `IRR-22`). `DATA-MODEL.md` `WHB-19` states it explicitly:
*"Must precede `V500030` — `period_id` is `NOT NULL` on the movement."*

`FR-025`: a GST return computed from guessed entities is a **filing error**, and the company axis
cannot be added after the ledger has rows. `FR-021`: rows posted before periods existed belong to no
period, **so the first close has an un-closeable opening set**. Neither is recoverable.

**The data model is the authority on the column set of `whb_companies`, `whb_company_external_refs`,
`whb_stock_periods` and `whb_stock_period_overrides`, and must be re-read immediately before the
migration is written.**

## Scope

- **`V500001`** — `whb_companies` (`code`, `name`, `legal_name`, `base_currency_code` ↓platform
  `currencies`, `country_code`, `is_default` with a **partial unique index**) and
  `whb_company_external_refs` (`source_module` **opaque**, `external_id`, `external_label`).
- **`V500019`** — `whb_stock_periods` (`period_code`, `company_id`, **`warehouse_id` nullable = all
  sites**, `start_date`/`end_date` as `DATE`, `status` OPEN → SOFT_CLOSED → CLOSED, `closed_by`/
  `closed_at`, `reopened_by`/`reopened_at`) and `whb_stock_period_overrides`.
- **The soft/hard close ladder and the override with its recorded reason** (`FR-020`, `L-8`). A
  movement whose `posting_date` falls in a **`CLOSED`** period is refused **including a reversal**;
  `SOFT_CLOSED` admits it only with the override permission and **records the override** with its
  reason code and justification. The trigger itself is `I-10`, `V500032`, owned by `P0-02` because it
  needs both this migration and the ledger.
- **Synchronisation with accounting periods** (`FR-251`): **stock periods are separate from
  accounting periods and close earlier**, and the two locks are synchronised so the two ledgers
  cannot be closed at different dates.

## Traps

- **`whb_companies` is warehouse's own, and it is not `automotive.companies`.** The real company
  master for the dealer vertical lives in `automotive` and warehouse **must not depend on it**
  (`P-010`, `C-035`). The prior art made this exact mistake: `SccCompany` was the designated owner of
  a shared company master and **was never built**, leaving an unconstrained `scc_company_id UUID`
  column pointing at no table in any module (`SCC_MODULE_ISSUES_ANALYSIS.md` §4.4). External links go
  through `whb_company_external_refs` with an **opaque `source_module`**, never an FK out of the
  module.
- **`period_id` is `NOT NULL` on the movement, so periods must be seeded before the first post.**
  An install with no period row cannot post at all — that is intentional (`FR-021`), and the install
  guide must say so, or the first demo fails with an FK violation nobody can read.
- **A `CLOSED` period refuses a reversal too.** This is the clause reviewers soften under pressure
  ("but they need to fix it") and it is exactly what `L-8` forbids. A soft close plus an approved,
  **recorded** override is the sanctioned path; a hard close admits nothing.
- **The override is gated by a session GUC in the trigger** (`I-10`, `V500032`).
  `SET LOCAL` + `current_setting('warehouse.soft_close_override', true)` is transaction-scoped and
  *should* be safe through the connection pool, but the idiom is **untested in this codebase**.
  Verify it in a real Docker run before relying on it.
- **`pendingHandoverCount` couples this screen to `P0-12`.** The close action refuses while the
  accounting-handover queue is non-empty; if `P0-12` has not landed the count is zero and the guard
  is vacuous. Sequence the two, or land the guard disabled behind an explicit flag and enable it in
  `P0-12` — do not ship a guard that reads as enforced and is not.
- **`branches.branch_name`, not `branches.name`.** Any join from a warehouse screen onto the platform
  branch table uses `branch_name` (R1 §8 trap `T-18`, `V149:18`).
- **Both date pairs are `dateOnly`, not `date`.** `start_date`/`end_date` are SQL `DATE`
  (`FR-327`), and `filterUtils.ts:49-56` documents that `date` shifts the lower bound to the previous
  calendar day for users east of UTC.
- **Three grids need `default_columns` AND `default_filters`** as `'[…]'::jsonb`, three scopes in
  `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`, and the two `dropdown.*` names in `CacheConfiguration.java`.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A movement whose `posting_date` falls in a `CLOSED` period is refused — **including a
      reversal** (`WH-SC-021`)
- [ ] A `SOFT_CLOSED` period admits a movement **only** with `whb_stock_periods:override`, and the
      override is written to `whb_stock_period_overrides` with its reason code and justification, and
      appears on WS-046 (`WH-SC-022`)
- [ ] Closing a stock period is refused while `pendingHandoverCount > 0`, and the modal names the
      count (`WH-SC-163`)
- [ ] Stock periods close **earlier** than the accounting period and the two locks cannot be set to
      different dates (`FR-251`)
- [ ] Exactly one default company per install, enforced by a partial unique index, with a field-level
      error on the second
- [ ] `V500001` and `V500019` both run before `V500030`
