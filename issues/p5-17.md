## Scope

- **`V510213` — `wh_weighing_instruments` + `wh_weighing_records`** (`WH-113`). A weighing or measuring
  instrument is a **legal instrument**: it carries a **verification certificate number and validity**, and
  **a weighing performed on an out-of-verification instrument is flagged on the receipt**. The flag is on
  the record, not only in a report — the receipt must carry it.
- **`V510214` — `wh_labour_tasks`** (`WH-114`). **Labour tasks are timed**: who, from when to when,
  **paused seconds**, units processed, reference and device. *The same record is the only honest input to
  VAS-by-the-minute billing and to cost-to-serve.*
- **This task deliberately builds no standard.** `FR-228` (`P6-03`) says it plainly: *engineered labour
  standards are not built before a year of our own actual data.* We measure and report; we do not claim to
  engineer a standard we cannot evidence, and **incentive pay is never computed from a warehouse metric**
  (refusal §7 rows 3 and 4).

## Traps

- **⚠ `PNR-3` again: a duration cannot be backfilled.** `DATA-MODEL.md` records it for the receipt
  lifecycle timestamps and it is the same argument here — *"the first client's month-one dock-to-stock
  report cannot be produced."* Every task type that will ever be measured must be timed **from the day
  this ships**, not from the day someone wants a report.
- **`paused_seconds` is a column, not a derivation.** Elapsed minus paused is the only honest duration; a
  design that stores only start and end bills a client for a lunch break, and no rule recovers the pause
  afterwards.
- **The verification flag lives on the receipt, not in a report.** *"A weighing performed on an
  out-of-verification instrument is flagged **on the receipt**"* — a report-only flag is invisible at the
  moment the decision is made.
- **The certificate validity is a dated obligation and therefore needs its job** (`FR-165`): warn before
  expiry, and flag every weighing taken after it. Ship both with the column.
- **Device attribution needs `actor_type = DEVICE` and `device_id`**, which are v1 ledger columns
  (R7 §6 item 11). A scale posting a weight has no attributable human actor, and *"diagnosing a
  mis-scanning device retroactively is impossible"* without them.
- **Do not compute a standard, a target or a rate here.** The moment a `target_seconds` column appears,
  someone will compute pay from it. `P6-03` measures; `P6-07` prices. This task records.
- **These two grids are log-style** — no audit columns on the grid, and therefore **none in the export**
  either. The rule is grid↔export parity, not a fixed column list.
- **`wh_labour_tasks` is the cost side of `P6-07`'s client profitability**, so it must carry the client's
  owner: a task with no `owner_id` is unattributable and profitability is unbuildable for the past.
- Weight is `DECIMAL(18,4)`; duration is an integer count of seconds, never a float. There is **no float
  or double anywhere in the stack including frontend DTOs** (`OD-7`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] A weighing on an expired-certificate instrument is **flagged on the receipt**, visible to the receiver at the moment of receipt, and cannot be silently accepted
- [ ] The certificate-expiry warning job runs on a schedule and the warning is visible before expiry, not after
- [ ] A labour task records who, start, end, `paused_seconds`, units, reference and `device_id`; a task paused and resumed yields elapsed-minus-paused
- [ ] A device-posted weighing carries `actor_type = DEVICE` and the `device_id`
- [ ] `wh_labour_tasks` carries `owner_id`, so a task is attributable to a 3PL client
- [ ] No `target_seconds`, no standard, no rate and no pay computation exists anywhere in this task
- [ ] **Mobile:** timing is captured on the RF task screens (`screens/whRfPick`, `whRfPutaway`, `whRfMove`) — a task timed only from a desk is not timed; the instrument master is `none` with the reason stated (`D-13`)
