## Scope
- Table (`V552010`) **`wht_document_shares`**: `document_type` (`PO`, `PO_RELEASE`, `RFQ`, `CUSTOMER_QUOTATION`, `REGRET_LETTER`,
  `SO_ACK`, `PO_FOLLOWUP`), `document_id`, `document_version`, `channel` (`EMAIL` / `WHATSAPP`), recipient name / address,
  `print_job_id` (core print job of the rendered PDF), `status` (`QUEUED` → `SENT` → `DELIVERED` / `FAILED`),
  `provider_message_id`, `failure_reason`, `attempt_count`, `sent_by`, `sent_at`; idx(document_type, document_id).
- Delivery: platform e-mail service with templates registered in `EmailTemplateDefaults` (`WHT_DOCUMENT_SHARE_<TYPE>`, i18n en / fr / hi)
  and the platform WhatsApp provider (Twilio; templates `trade-document-share-<type>`); WhatsApp offered only when the provider is
  configured, else `503 TRADE_SHARE_CHANNEL_UNAVAILABLE`. Sending is after commit (`NotificationDeliveryListener` pattern);
  provider callbacks update the row; *Retry* on `FAILED` (`wht_document_shares:send`).
- Recipients default from the counterparty's contacts (base counterparty contacts with e-mail / mobile), editable per send;
  `422 TRADE_SHARE_NO_RECIPIENT` when none. Only approved / issued versions (`422 TRADE_SHARE_DOCUMENT_NOT_APPROVED`); a PO share
  sets `ISSUED`, an SO-ack share sets `ack_status = SENT`, a quotation share `SENT` — exactly as printing does. PO attachments with
  `share_with_supplier` (p9-07, FR-748) go with it.
- Supplier follow-up (FR-764): WS-295 *Send reminder* (template `WHT_PO_FOLLOWUP_REMINDER`) logs a share + a follow-up row (mode
  `EMAIL` / `WHATSAPP`); `TradeSupplierReminderJob` when `warehouse.trade.expedite.auto_remind_supplier` = true (default false —
  client Q-P8), once per schedule line per trigger (`TPO-T3-07`), as the service principal.
- Settings (`V552011`): `warehouse.trade.share.channels` (`EMAIL,WHATSAPP`), `warehouse.trade.expedite.auto_remind_supplier` (false).
  Permissions `wht_document_shares:{view,send,export}` (→ `:view`); buyer and sales bundles get `:send`, auditor `:view`.

## Traps
- A share is a print job: render once, attach the same PDF to every recipient, and flag it a reprint after the first.
- Never send before commit; a rolled-back approval must not have e-mailed the supplier.
- Approval notifications by e-mail / WhatsApp are core's engine (`p8-30`), not this task.
- Phone numbers and e-mails are personal data: the share log is visible to `:view` holders only and is never exported to the auditor beyond `:view`.
