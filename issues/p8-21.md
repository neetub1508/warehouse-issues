### Behaviour — five tabs of WS-696, each backend-filtered, paginated and exportable (`FR-400`)
1. **Vendor rejection** (`QRG-T5-01`): per supplier × item × period (month by default): received quantity (posted GRN lines), inspected quantity (stock-bearing `GOODS_RECEIPT` inspections), accepted, accepted under deviation, rejected (`REJECT`/`RTV`/`SCRAP`), **IQC rejection %** = rejected ÷ inspected; **line rejections** = store-return quantities attributed to the supplier (`P8-07` R32 trace, `FR-716`) **plus** the quantities of NCRs with source type `IN_PROCESS_INSPECTION` and responsibility `SUPPLIER` naming that supplier and item (MFG PQC rejects of a purchased component, `QRW-T4-20`; R32-fix F-R32MS-023), never counting one rejected unit twice, and **line-rejection ppm** = line-rejected ÷ received × 1,000,000. Filters `warehouseId`, `supplierCounterpartyId`, `itemCategoryId` → `itemId` (cascade), `dateFrom`/`dateTo` (`DateRangePicker`, posting date of the GRN — the clock is named on screen, `RC-001`). Drill to WS-080 and WS-690.
2. **Defect Pareto** (`QRG-T5-02`): count of NCRs and Σ non-conforming quantity per defect code, sorted descending, with share % and cumulative %; codes up to `pareto_cutoff_percent` cumulative are flagged *vital few*. Filters stage, source type, supplier, item category, date range (NCR `raised_at`). A bar + cumulative-line chart beside the grid.
3. **Open NCR / CAPA** (`QRG-T5-03`): NCRs not `CLOSED`/`CANCELLED` with age buckets 0–7, 8–30, 31–60, > 60 days (site-local), owner, severity, open / overdue CAPA count, target close date; CAPA actions overdue listed with owner and days overdue.
4. **Deviations** (`QRG-T5-04`): every deviation with type, item, supplier, limit, consumed, remaining, validity, status; tiles Active · Expiring ≤ `deviation_expiry_alert_days` · Exhausted · Expired.
5. **Pass rate by stage** (`QRG-T5-05`): per inspection stage (`INCOMING`, `IN_PROCESS`, `FINAL`, `RETURN`, `PERIODIC`, `FIRST_ARTICLE`) × item × period: inspections completed, accepted first time (no `REWORK` and no re-inspection of the same source line), **first-pass %**. Manufacturing's stage-level yield by routing operation stays `P10-13` (#604); this tab reads only core inspections, so it works on a core-only install.
- Statistics tiles are filter-aware and uncached (`FR-395`); exports reproduce filter and sort and include `createdByName`-free columns exactly as the grid (a report grid has no audit columns).

## Traps
- **No table behind any of the five report grids above** (`DATA-MODEL.md` §8.3 item 4) — a `CREATE TABLE` for one is a second truth.
- **Rejection % divides by inspected, ppm by received** — mixing denominators makes a skipped-inspection supplier look perfect.
- **Line rejections count once**: a store-return quantity that also became a supplier return is not counted again from the return.
