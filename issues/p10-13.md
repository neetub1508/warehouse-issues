### WR3-13 · Report the Rule 56(12) monthly production account per product (minor, MISSING-TASK)

> **Statutory content.** Sources are cited under Evidence; anything marked UNVERIFIED there, and every figure, must be confirmed against the law in force at build time.

#### Why this matters

A GST officer or auditor asks for the monthly production account: RM consumed per product, goods manufactured, waste and by-products. They use it to test input-output ratios and ITC on losses. The stock account gives totals by head but not per product.

#### Scope

**R46 S1 DECISION (W, built in `warehouse-india`, not in `warehouse-manufacturing`):** the india stock-account period's *Generate* (`WhinStockAccountPeriodService`, `whin_stock_account_periods` GENERATED → FILED, `V540130`) also writes production-account lines per GSTIN: for each FG output item, input consumed per component (issues + backflush), output, co- / by-products, scrap and wastage under their statutory heads — read from posted base-ledger movements of source system `WAREHOUSE_MANUFACTURING`, grouped by source document (the order), and frozen when the period is FILED, so the tie-out to stock-account consumption holds by construction. **No standard-vs-actual ratio** (the MFG consumption-variance tab shows it). No Global Setting. India off ⇒ not shown. Migrations: next free india band (`V540185`–`V540189` free at `abb84e0`; the V541 permission / leaf row above `V541250`) — re-check at S2; `V561110` / `V563290` do not cover it. MFG never depends on india; india tables take no FK to `whm_`.

Original scope: a monthly per-GSTIN report: for each FG item, quantities of each input consumed, output, co- and by-products, scrap and wastage. Exportable, and frozen with the stock-account period.

#### Acceptance

- For a month with 2 orders of PUMP-100, the report shows each component's consumed quantity equal to the backflush plus issues, and the output equal to the receipts.
- By-product and waste rows appear separately.
- Totals tie to the Rule 56 stock account consumption.

CGST Rule 56(12), https://www.taxheal.com/rule-56-cgst-rules-2017.html (secondary). Plan: grep `production account|56(12)` → 0. V540130 heads are RM / FG / SCRAP / WASTAGE only.
