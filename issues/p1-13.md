- **A session is one truck against N POs × N ASNs × N GRNs, with a nullable supplier** for a
  consolidator's load (`FR-124`, `P-021`).
- **Receiving behaviour is per-warehouse configuration, not two parallel workflows** (`FR-126`,
  `P-057`): receiving mode, GRN timing relative to the physical count, QC policy.
- **Verification always; inspection optionally** (`FR-127`, `P-029`). They are **different acts and
  different screens** — the 72-table prior design did not distinguish them.
- **Blind receipt as a first-class v1 flow** (`FR-128`): item, quantity, UoM, owner, status and
  location — nothing else required. **Three of our own scenarios have no PO at the dock.**
  **Ratified 2026-09-14** (`docs/contracts/receipt-qc-putaway.contract.md` `RQP-OPEN-15`, user decision):
  the GRN still names a counterparty (`FR-120`) without the operator keying one — it is **derived** from
  the owner's `whb_owners.counterparty_id`; only a **house-owned** blind receipt, whose owner has no
  counterparty, shows a counterparty picker, and the value is required (`COUNTERPARTY_REQUIRED`). The
  receipt balances against that counterparty's virtual location (`VIRT-SUPPLIER-<SITE>` /
  `VIRT-CUSTOMER-<SITE>`). `session_id` is nullable: WS-075 *Create GRN* sets it, a blind receipt leaves it
  null; the GRN number is issued at create; a line with `po_line_id` needs its PO `APPROVED`,
  `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED` or `RECEIVED` and the line not `CLOSED`/`CANCELLED`, at create and at Post (`H2`,
  `H5`).
- **Receipt into a non-available status on the first transaction**, defaulted **item → supplier →
  `AVAILABLE`** (`FR-129`). **Hard-coding `AVAILABLE` is the defect.**
- **Over/short tolerance** on the item with a warehouse default, setting **`match_status`**
  (`MATCHED`/`QTY_OVER`/`QTY_UNDER`/`ITEM_MISMATCH` — the last, a received item on no attached PO
  line, joins here from `p2-12`, `RJ-014`) and an **explicit close-short action** (`FR-130`).
- **The item identified by `id` or `sku` or `barcode` or `(source_module, external_id)`** (`FR-038`).
- **The UoM convertibility guard on the GRN modal** (`FR-143`) — *the prior system had it on the PO
  and offered the unconvertible unit on the receipt.*
- **Tracking decided by the item's lot and serial control policy, never a mode string read in
  isolation** (`FR-144`).
- **Free / scheme quantity with its `scheme_reference`** (`FR-141`), and the landed value spread
  across billed **plus** free.
- **The nullable `cross_dock_reference` on the receipt line** (`FR-137`) — v1 column, v2 feature, so
  *"which receipts were cross-docked"* is answerable for the period before the feature shipped.
- **Every inbound receipt names a counterparty** (`FR-120`, `P1-08`).

## Traps

- **`whb_entity_attribute_values` has DDL and NOTHING ELSE.** `P0-05` created the table in `V500010`
  — `entity_kind`, `entity_id`, `attribute_key_id`, the four typed value columns and
  `uk(entity_kind, entity_id, attribute_key_id)` — and shipped **no entity, no repository and no
  service**. Nothing in the product can write a row. This task is the first writer (`RL-007`, scope
  item 4 above), so **the persistence layer for it is this task's to build**, not something to
  assume exists. Two things it must get right: the value goes in the column matching the key's
  `value_type` (a number in `value_string` is unfilterable and unexportable, which is the whole
  reason `FR-026` made the key a registry row); and a **second value for one `(entity_kind,
  entity_id, attribute_key_id)` must come back as a FIELD-LEVEL error naming the key**, never as the
  raw unique-constraint violation, which reaches the browser as an opaque `500` on a GRN post.
- **Hard-coding `AVAILABLE` on receipt is the defect `FR-129` names**, and it is the one that looks
  harmless. Every QC-controlled item is then available to promise the moment it lands. The default
  chain is **item → supplier → `AVAILABLE`**, and the received status is a column on the line.
- **Verification and inspection are different acts.** `FR-127`: verification **always** happens;
  inspection is optional and is `P1-14`'s screen. Collapsing them means an item that needs no QC
  cannot be received without one, or an item that needs QC is received without it.
- **Blind receipt is not a degraded path.** `FR-128` makes it first-class in v1 — item, quantity,
  UoM, owner, status, location. A flow that requires a PO cannot receive the truck that is at the
  dock now.
- **The UoM convertibility guard must be on the GRN modal**, not only on the PO (`FR-143`). This is a
  named prior-system failure, not a hypothetical.
- **Tracking comes from the item's control policy** (`FR-144`). The prior system read a
  `tracking_mode` string in isolation and received serial-controlled stock without serials.
- **`match_status` needs an explicit close-short action** (`FR-130`). A short receipt left `QTY_UNDER`
  forever is an open PO line nobody closes and a reorder that never fires.
- **Post is the only path that writes the ledger**, through `P0-03`'s single writer service, asserted
  by the architecture test (`FR-436`). A GRN service that touches `whb_stock_positions` fails the
  build.
- **Physical counts in `accessories` never post**: `generateAdjustments()` sets `status='POSTED'` and
  **creates nothing**, and `InventoryCountService` has **zero** references to `StockLevel`
  (`InventoryCountService.java:322-345`, `C-023`). The same shape — a document that says POSTED and
  writes no stock — is the failure mode to test for here.
- **Accessories receiving has no supplier field at all** — ten fields, none a vendor
  (`StockReceiptRequest.java:23-51`, `C-031`). `FR-120` makes the counterparty mandatory.
- **`cross_dock_reference` is nullable in v1 and must not be omitted** because the feature is v2
  (`FR-137`).
- **`conversion_factor_used` is frozen on the ledger line at post** (`L-7`, `IRR-34`). The GRN
  supplies it; the ledger stores the one it used.
- **Two grids × two platform files**, both with ~10 filter keys, plus a **`receivedWithin` dropdown**
  for mobile because `ListHeader` has no date filter (`mobile/…/ListHeader.tsx:210-218`, `C-044`).
  `RB-001` (`C-044` is wrong — `ListHeader` supports date ranges): Mobile out of scope for the classic build — user decision 2026-09-11.
- **`wh_goods_receipt_line_serials` is the normalised replacement for `serial_numbers JSONB`**
  (`P-007`, `FR-383`).

## Hand-offs from `P1-12` (C1 fix cycle, 2026-09-15)

`P1-12` shipped the PO side of each item below. Finishing each one is this task's job, because each
needs the GRN tables.

- **Cancel the draft GRNs through the GRN service, not the raw `UPDATE`.**
  - Today `WhPurchaseOrderRepositoryCustomImpl.cancelDraftGrns` runs `UPDATE wh_goods_receipts SET
    status = 'CANCELLED'`, called by `WhPurchaseOrderService.cancel`. It records no reason and writes
    no audit entry.
  - `RQP-T1-15` requires the §0.11 cancel pattern. Replace the `UPDATE` with the GRN service's Cancel
    (reason recorded, audited), inside the PO cancel transaction (`RQP-T4-13`, H1).
- **Add a `RQP-GRD-13` integration test against real tables.**
  - A posted GRN line refuses the PO Cancel and names the GRN line.
  - An `ARRIVED` session refuses it and names the session.
  - `P1-12`'s tests mock the repository, so they prove none of the SQL in `findReceivedGrnLines` or
    `findArrivedSessions`.
- **The GRN post goes through the PO header lock.**
  - `WhPurchaseOrderLineStatusService.applyReceipt` is the one writer of the PO line counters. It
    locks the `wh_purchase_orders` row (`WhPurchaseOrderRepository.findByIdForUpdate`) before it
    reads the line.
  - Call it inside the post transaction, and take no PO lock of your own in a different order.
    Otherwise a Cancel racing the post can deadlock, or erase `received_quantity` (`RQP-GRD-13`).
  - Before its `RQP-GRD-28` read, the GRN post locks the PO headers of every GRN line in **ascending
    `poId` order** (`findByIdForUpdate`). A GRN spanning several POs then takes its locks in the same order
    as any other multi-PO GRN, so two posts cannot deadlock. Once locked, it reads the headers and lines.
  - `applyReceipt` re-checks `RQP-GRD-28` for a receipt under the same lock (409 `PO_NOT_RECEIVABLE`,
    P1-12 fix pass 2). It refreshes the header and lines first, so a stale copy loaded before the lock
    is not trusted. The post's own GRD-28 check stays: it is the one that names `lines[n].po_line_id`.
- **WS-073's GRNs and Movements tabs become child grids, and Movements uses the `FR-036` lineage query.**
  - `WhPurchaseOrderQueryService.MOVEMENT_COUNT` counts `wh_goods_receipt_lines.receipt_movement_id`,
    which misses the `REVERSAL`, putaway and disposition movements.
  - Read the lineage through `GET /warehouse/ledger/movements/management` filtered by
    `sourceDocumentType` / `sourceDocumentId` (`WhbStockMovementController`), with no
    `gridIdentifier` per tab (BUILD-SPEC WS-073, H2).
- **Check-in refuses an unusable dock door (from `P1-06`, C1 fix pass 2).**
  - A door whose location is not of type `DOCK`. A door created before D3 keeps its old location
    (dock-door contract `OPEN-01`).
  - A `BLOCKED` door or a retired door (dock-door contract `DD-T4-03`).
