## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Z-002`.** **The counter resets on a period boundary, and the reset is part
> of the allocation.** `whb_number_series_issued` carries **`period_key VARCHAR(10) NOT NULL`**
> (`'ALL'` for `NEVER`, `'FY2026-27'` for `YEARLY`, `'2026-04'` for `MONTHLY`) and `I-20`'s index
> becomes `uk(series_id, period_key, issued_value)`. `whb_next_document_number(series_id)`
> computes the current `period_key` under the **same `FOR UPDATE` lock**, resets `current_value`
> to 0 and stamps `last_reset_at` when it differs from the last issue's — so the reset can never
> be a separate job that misses a midnight. The nightly assertion becomes `MAX(issued_value) =
> COUNT(*)` **per `(series_id, period_key)`, and only where `is_gapless = true`**. `WS-061` shows
> `currentPeriodKey`, and **Preview next number** renders the post-reset number once the boundary
> has passed.

> **Round-4 addition — `RG-001` · `RL-004`.** (1) **A branch-scoped series resolves to the `REGISTERED`
> branch at the document date.** For a branch-scoped series (challan, transfer invoice),
> `whb_number_series.branch_id` is matched to the issuing site's `REGISTERED` link in
> `whb_warehouse_branches` (`P1-05`, `V500012`) at the document date; warehouse-scoped series (GRN,
> pick, ship) are unaffected. A re-registration switches series from that instant and renumbers
> nothing (`RG-001` row 2, `FR-307`). (2) **`formatted_number` is unique per series, not per install**:
> `uk(series_id, formatted_number)` replaces the global `uk(formatted_number)`, so company B's
> `GRN-000001` does not collide with company A's and no prefix needs a company token (`RL-004`).

- **`V500020`** — `whb_number_series`, `whb_number_series_issued`, the `whb_next_document_number()`
  function, and **`I-20`**.
- **Gapless numbering from a locked counter row**, **module-scoped** so a future consumer need not
  build a second generator (`FR-426`). GRN, pick, ship, adjustment, transfer, count, return and
  challan numbers all come from here.
- **The issued-numbers audit table** — append-only, with **no `updated_*` columns at all**
  (`DATA-MODEL.md` §1.4).

## The mechanism, and the one that must not be used

**Use:** a counter row taken with `@Lock(LockModeType.PESSIMISTIC_WRITE)` in its own
`REQUIRES_NEW` transaction — the assets precedent, six sequence repositories, e.g.
`AssetTagSequenceRepository.java:19-27`, which **documents the exact race** it exists to prevent:
*"two concurrent asset creations in one category read the same value and one of them fails on
`uk_assets_tag` after the counter has already moved."* The alternative shape is
`pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext(:key))` + `UPDATE … RETURNING`, used at
`DocumentFolderService.java:266`, `GridPreferenceService.java:144`,
`services/BoomBarrierWebhookService.java:146`.

**Do not use:** `SequentialCodeGenerator` (`platform/…/util/SequentialCodeGenerator.java:9-19`,
`:41-53`). It is **scan-based and explicitly not gapless**; it races behind a unique constraint and
is used by accessories transaction numbers (`InventoryStockAdjustmentService.java:152-159`).
**Correct for masters, wrong for a statutory series** (`C-019`).

## Traps

- **`SequentialCodeGenerator` is scan-based and says so in its own header** (`:9-19`). A reviewer
  will suggest it because it is the platform utility. It is the wrong tool, and the failure is
  invisible until two documents share a number under load (`C-019`).
- **Gapless and unique are different properties.** A unique constraint plus a retry gives *unique*
  and **not gapless**: every failed attempt burns a number. A statutory series must have no holes,
  which is why the counter is **locked**, not optimistic.
- **The lock serialises.** `accounting-base/V600111:364-386`, `:673-676` states the cost plainly:
  *"from its first write to COMMIT, a mutating transaction holds this row's lock, so mutating
  transactions for ONE COMPANY serialise."* Scope the counter row as narrowly as the numbering
  policy allows — per `(module, series, company, warehouse, branch, reset period)` — so the
  serialisation is per series, not per install.
- **`REQUIRES_NEW` is what makes the number survive a rollback of the *document*** — and it is also
  what makes it **not** gapless if the document then fails. Decide explicitly: either the number is
  issued **inside** the document's transaction (gapless, but the lock is held longer), or in its own
  (shorter lock, holes on rollback). **`FR-426` says gapless, so it is the former** — and the issued
  table records it either way, which is what makes a hole auditable rather than mysterious.
- **`{FY}`-style tokens and the reset policy must be designed now.** A series that cannot express
  "reset annually, prefix with the financial year" is re-keyed when the first Indian customer arrives
  — and every issued number already carries the old shape. The accounting set records this as a
  one-line-now decision.
- **`whb_number_series_issued` has no `updated_*` columns and no `version`** (`DATA-MODEL.md` §1.4).
  It is append-only by design; an `UPDATE` path is a defect.
- **`branches.branch_name`, not `branches.name`** (`V149:18`, R1 §8 trap `T-18`).
- **`uk(series_id, formatted_number)` makes a resetting series collide with its own previous
  period** unless the period is in the string. The series modal refuses a `YEARLY`/`MONTHLY` series
  whose mask carries no period token (`RL-004`, `Z-002`).
- **The branch on a branch-scoped series is resolved, never copied from a warehouse column** —
  `whb_warehouses.branch_id` no longer exists; the `REGISTERED` link at the document date is the only
  input (`RG-001`).
- **Two grids × two platform files**; `dropdown.whbNumberSeries` in `CacheConfiguration.java`.
- **Preview must not issue.** It reads the counter and formats the *next* value; if it increments,
  every operator who checks the next GRN number burns one.

  `number_assigned_on VARCHAR(12) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'CREATE'` (CHECK `CREATE` / `SUBMIT` / `APPROVAL` / `POSTING`) and

  from the series only when `event` equals the type's `number_assigned_on`; before that the document carries a

  Because the number is drawn only at that event, a draft rejected or withdrawn burns no number and the series stays
  gapless (PRD §3 cancelled / rejected drafts). **Every existing type keeps `CREATE`** — no behaviour change on upgrade;
  an install sets `APPROVAL` or `POSTING` per type on WS-002 (*default — client Q-G3*).
