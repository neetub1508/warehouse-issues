**`V510080` — WH-70 — `wh_landed_cost_documents`, `wh_landed_cost_allocations`,
`wh_revaluations`, `wh_revaluation_lines`.**

**Under `D-6` warehouse computes the cost and hands over valued movements** (`P2-16`). Both objects
here are **movements**, not `UPDATE`s, and both carry `handover_id` + **`posting_status`**; a
`REJECTED` handover lands in the named, owned queue (`P2-18`, `P2-22`) rather than in a log.

**Landed cost has an apportionment basis per charge** (`FR-238`) — `VALUE` / `QUANTITY` / `WEIGHT` /
`VOLUME` / `MANUAL` — and it **retrospectively revalues the receipt layer**. Where the layer is
already **partly consumed**, the application splits into a **layer adjustment** and a **COGS
adjustment for what already shipped**, with a link from the charge document back to the receipt
movements it loads. *Duty, freight, insurance, clearing and port handling arrive weeks after the
goods, on a different document.*
**The column lands in v1 always zero; the full charge *document* with its allocation ladder is
v2/`P5-16`** — v1 ships the document object, the five bases, the apply action and the allocation
rows, which is what makes the v2 work an extension rather than a rebuild.
**The trap `FR-238` names explicitly: a port whose `unit_cost` is write-once cannot carry this.**
`PORT-AND-ADAPTER-CONTRACT.md` `PC-11` settles it — zero-quantity, non-zero-value movements are
legal and there is no `CHECK (quantity <> 0)` on the line.

**The apportionment basis lives on the receipt, not on the freight charge** (`FR-239`, `G-023`).
*The charge is a transport fact; the effect on stock value is a warehouse fact.* Put it on the
receipt/GRN, and let the landed-cost document reference it — not the other way round.

**`wh_landed_cost_allocations` stores the allocated amount rather than recomputing it.** Rounding
rule **R-4**: the apportionment must be **reproducible next year**, and a recomputation against a
changed line set would not be. Largest remainder: `floor` each provisional share, then distribute the
remaining minor units one at a time, **largest fractional remainder first, ties broken by
`line_no`**, so `Σ parts == whole`, always.

> **Round-3 fold — `RF-003` (`GAP-REGISTER-R3.md` §2.6, §4.2).** **A charge on a partly-consumed
> layer splits in the allocation row, not in arithmetic nobody stored.** `V510080` gives
> `wh_landed_cost_allocations` three more columns — `quantity_remaining_at_apply` (`DECIMAL(18,4)`),
> `amount_to_layer` and `amount_to_cogs` (`DECIMAL(19,4)`) — with
> **`CHECK (amount_to_layer + amount_to_cogs = allocated_amount)`**. The apply movement is then
> **three lines**: `+amount_to_layer` at the receipt location, revaluing the open remainder;
> `+amount_to_cogs` at `VIRT-COST_OF_SALES-<SITE CODE>`, the cost-of-sales virtual location `P1-05`'s
> `V500013` seeds per site (code `COST_OF_SALES`); and `−allocated_amount` at the value-offset location.
> Quantity and value both conserve. `WH-SC-153`'s ₹4,500 on 120 `EA` with 48 shipped is `+2,700` /
> `+1,800` / `−4,500`; without the split the 72 `EA` still in stock would be overstated by ₹25 each.
> **Falsifier:** a charge applied to a **fully consumed** layer moves no stock value at all —
> `amount_to_layer = 0`, and the whole charge lands at cost of sales.

**Revaluation is a document, not an `UPDATE`** (`FR-240`) — a movement type with **zero quantity and
a non-zero value** — *so the stock ledger and the ledger of record stay in step through a price
escalation or a year-end write-down.*
**Warehouse posts the movement; accounting approves the revaluation**, because approval is a
downstream act on the ledger of record. `wh_revaluations` carries a local `approved_by`/`approved_at`
for the warehouse-side authorisation to *post*; it does not model the accounting approval.

**Both post through `VALUE_OFFSET`**, the value-offset virtual location of `OD-11` — see the Traps:
**the location seed and the value-conservation invariant are not this task's to create.**

**`wh_landed_cost_documents.grn_id` is nullable and derived** (`RG-019`). It holds the only GRN,
when there is only one. A freight bill that loads three GRNs reaches them through
`wh_landed_cost_allocations`, whose rows already carry the receipt movements they load.
*"Landed costs against GRN X"* is a query over the allocation rows, never over the header column.
Otherwise a multi-GRN freight bill contradicts its own header.

**The two ladders** (`RJ-004`). They are derived from WS-147's and WS-148's actions and use §0.11's
columns.

## Traps

- **`OD-11` gates this task and it is not this task's to answer.** `L-1`…`L-14` conserve **quantity
  only**, and a landed-cost movement posts `quantity = 0` with a value, so it **balances perfectly
  while unbalancing the value column**. The resolution — a value-offset virtual location plus a
  value-conservation invariant applying only where `quantity = 0 AND unit_cost IS NOT NULL` — must
  land in **`P0-02`'s `V500030`** (the trigger) and **`P1-05`'s `V500013`** (the seeded location).
  `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §7 says the tighter deadline wins: **before `P0-02`**, because *a
  conservation invariant is a constraint on the table, not on the report.*
- **The value-offset location has two names in the source set and neither is seeded yet.**
  `DECISIONS.md` `OD-11` and `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` `P2-28` call it **`VALUE_OFFSET`**;
  `PORT-AND-ADAPTER-CONTRACT.md` `PC-12` calls it **`LANDED_COST_OFFSET`** and says *"one seed row
  … or the reuse of an `ADJUSTMENT_OFFSET`-typed location"*. **`FR-084`'s seeded virtual-location
  list contains neither.** Pick one code before `V500013` is written; **do not seed a second row
  under the other name.** Filed in `DEFECTS-FOUND.md`.
- **`PC-11`'s four v1 value-only movement types** are `LANDED_COST_APPLY`, `COST_ADJUSTMENT`,
  `REVALUATION` and `WRITE_DOWN` (`FR-046`), registered by `P0-04`'s `V500002`. If they are not in
  that seed, this task has no movement type to post.
- **`R-4` is not optional and it is the reason `wh_landed_cost_allocations` exists.** A recomputed
  apportionment is not reproducible; a stored one is. Ties break by `line_no`, deterministically.
- **A partly-consumed layer splits two ways.** The layer adjustment and the **COGS adjustment for
  what already shipped** are different postings with different classification quads (`P2-18`), and
  getting the split wrong is invisible until a margin report is run.
- **Approval belongs downstream.** Do not build an accounting-approval workflow in `warehouse`;
  `FR-240` says warehouse posts and accounting approves. The local approval is a warehouse
  authorisation to post.
- **`T-2`** — a JPA field initialiser beats the column `DEFAULT`
  (`accessories/…/entity/StockLevel.java:58-64`). `amount` and `total_value_change` are decided in
  one place.
- **`T-6`** — two scopes; `appliedFrom`/`appliedTo` is `date`, `effectiveFrom`/`effectiveTo` is
  **`dateOnly`**, and all four keys are listed.
- **Precision.** `amount`, `total_value_change`, `allocated_amount` **`DECIMAL(19,4)`**;
  `old_unit_cost`, `new_unit_cost` **`DECIMAL(19,6)`**; the weight and volume bases read
  `DECIMAL(12,3)` kg and `DECIMAL(15,2)` cc from the item.
