## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Q-006`.** `WH-SC-302` adds the two refusals this task's happy-path list
> was missing. **A supersession that would close a cycle is refused at save** — `422`
> `SUPERSESSION_CYCLE`, printing the path it would close — which matters here more than elsewhere
> because **interchange rows are bidirectional by design** (`FR-073`), so the cheapest data-entry
> mistake in the feature is exactly the one that creates a loop. `MERGE_STOCK` on a cycle is
> refused for a second, independent reason: the movement it would emit has **no defined
> direction**. And because imported chains predate the guard, the **allocator is bounded — maximum
> chain depth 10** — allocating from the parts it did reach and raising the cycle as a
> data-quality row on the item; it never loops, never times out, and never degrades into a bare
> *"not in stock"*.

**A supersession records its stock and demand treatment** (`FR-072`), so the decision is
reproducible:

| Treatment | What it means |
|---|---|
| **`KEEP_SEPARATE`** | the two parts remain distinct stock and distinct demand |
| **`MERGE_DEMAND`** | the predecessor's demand history is folded into the successor, **weighted by the quantity ratio** |
| **`MERGE_STOCK`** | a **movement** converts the old part's stock into the new, at the old part's **cost layers** |

*Without it, the successor's computed stocking level is zero the day it supersedes.*
`MERGE_STOCK` is a movement, not an `UPDATE` — the old part's layers are consumed and the new part's
layers are created at the consumed cost (`P2-16`).

> `E-056` places this at v1.1; `DECISIONS.md` §5 makes v1.1 = P3 execution, and the parts
> differentiators are in the v1 exit criterion, **so it lands v1/P2**. That reasoning is `FR-072`'s
> own — do not re-defer it.

**Interchange and alternate parts are bidirectional supersession rows** (`FR-073`), and the counter
enquiry shows **"not in stock — 2 available as `<interchange>`"** with availability **across every
branch**.

**"Across every branch" groups sites by their `REGISTERED` branch** (`RG-001`, R22 §1.2.4 row 12).
- A shared site appears **once**, under its `REGISTERED` branch, tagged with the branches that serve
  from it. It is never repeated under each linked branch, which would show the same eight units three
  times.
- A **sister** is a site with a **different** `REGISTERED` branch, so a site is never its own sister.
- Where the counter cannot sell from the site that holds the stock, the enquiry offers *Raise
  request*, never a sale. That happens when the site belongs to a different GSTIN, or the counter's
  branch has no link to it (`FR-461`, `P2-25`). The request lands on `P2-02`'s transfer. The chain table is `P1-03`'s `whb_item_supersessions` (`V500050`); the **treatment
columns**, the **bidirectional rows** and the **enquiry behaviour** are this task's. The allocator's
supersession-aware path under an explicit rule flag is `P2-07`'s `FR-176`.

**`V520010` — `whad_vehicle_fitments`** (`FR-074`). **Vehicle fitment lives in

## Traps

- **`D-11`'s hard rule: `warehouse-adapter-dealer` ships with zero commits to `warehouse-base`.**
  The ratchet is literally `git log --oneline -- warehouse-base/ | wc -l` unchanged at the merge
  commit, recorded in the PR description. If the treatment columns need to go on
  `whb_item_supersessions`, that is a **`warehouse-base` change in `P1-03`'s migration**, reviewed
  on its merits — **not an `ALTER` from `V520010`.** An adapter never adds a column to a base table
  and never widens a base vocabulary by `ALTER`; it inserts a row.
- **This task's dependency runs `P2-24 → P2-25`, which reads backwards and is correct.**
  `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §2.3 lists `P2-24`'s deps as `P1-03 P2-25`, because the counter-sale
  surface `FR-073`'s enquiry lives on is `P2-25`'s. Build the treatment columns and the chain
  behaviour first, the enquiry with the counter.
- **`MERGE_DEMAND` is weighted by the quantity ratio, and the ratio is stored.** A 1:2 supersession
  folding 40 hits of the predecessor is not 40 hits of the successor. `quantity_ratio` is
  **`DECIMAL(9,6)`** — *`_ratio` takes the percentage type*, not the money type.
- **`MERGE_STOCK` at the old part's cost layers.** Creating the successor's layer at today's average
  is the `C-027` drift in a new place.
- **Bidirectional means two rows, not a `reverse` flag.** A single row with a direction column
  cannot express *"A interchanges with B and B interchanges with A, but A supersedes C and C does
  not supersede A"*.
- **"Availability across every branch" crosses the warehouse-scoped access predicate** (`FR-405`,
  `P1-18`). The enquiry shows **quantities at branches the user may not transact at** — decide and
  state whether that is permitted, and if it is, it is an explicit exception with its own permission,
  not a hole in the predicate.
- **`T-18`** — the fitment grid joins automotive's model master; verify its actual column names
  before writing native SQL. Java field names do not always match DB column names.
- **`T-11`** — adapter files are `whad`-prefixed and `/warehouse/dealer/*` is
  `warehouse-adapter-dealer`'s exclusive route root (§0.8).
- **`T-6`** — `WAREHOUSE_DEALER_VEHICLE_FITMENT` into `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`. Note `D-10`'s
  corollary: the ratchet is *"zero commits to `warehouse-base`"* and **never** *"zero commits to
  `platform`"* — every new grid edits `filterUtils.ts` and `CacheConfiguration.java`, and a contract
  claiming otherwise is false on day one.
