## Scope
- Tables per contract §0 (`V550120`; + `return_by_date`, `received_short`); `core_rma_id` ↓ `wh_rmas`.
- SRA ladder incl. withdraw (`TSR-T1-02a`), `TSR-GRD-01` against core shipped quantities, `TradeApprovable` `SRA`.
- On approval `WhRmaPort.createRma` creates the core RMA **`APPROVED`** with `approvedByUserId` = the last
  approver, `expiryDate` = `return_by_date` (default `requested_date` +
  `warehouse.trade.sales.return_validity_days`, 30), `originalDemandOrderLineId` per line; key
  `WHT_SRA:<id>:RMA_CREATE:1` (`TSR-T4-03`). Cancel after approval → `cancelRma`, key `WHT_SRA:<id>:RMA_CANCEL:1`
  (`TSR-T1-04`). `getRmaPosition` reconciles received quantities (`p8-15`, `OD-33`).
- Register trade's `RmaChangeGuard` bean refusing core-screen `EDIT`, `APPROVE`, `CANCEL` of a
  trade-created RMA with `409 RMA_OWNED_BY_SOURCE_MODULE`; `EXPIRE` is allowed (`TSR-GRD-05`, `OD-39`; `WH-SC-478`).
- **Core RMA status mapping** (core statuses are exactly `OPEN`, `APPROVED`, `MATCHED`, `EXPIRED`,
  `CANCELLED`): `WH_RMA_MATCHED` → SRA `RECEIVED`, or `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED` with `received_short` when matched <
  authorised (final — `WH-SC-581`); `WH_RMA_EXPIRED` → `CLOSED`, outcome *expired*; `WH_RMA_CANCELLED` →
  `CANCELLED` (`TSR-T1-05`, `-07`, `-08`, `TSR-T4-01`). Both received states are closable.
- `REPLACE` → replacement dispatch release `DRAFT` for the matched quantity (`TSO-T1-16`, `is_replacement`,
  `source_sra_id`), approved through `SO_RELEASE`; the SO stays open until the SRA closes (`TSO-T3-03`,
  `WH-SC-587`). On a `SHORT_CLOSED` / `CANCELLED` SO the replacement must be declined with a reason (`TSR-T1-05`).
- Core supplier return `REPLACE` dispatched (`WH_SUPPLIER_RETURN_DISPATCHED`) → a **system amendment of origin
  `SUPPLIER_RETURN`** is drafted on the PO (p9-11, `TPO-T4-07`); trade never calls `amendOrder` from the
  handler. On a `SHORT_CLOSED` / `CANCELLED` PO nothing is reinstated and the buyer is told (`WH-SC-578`).
- `CREDIT` intent stamps `accounting_handover_marked_at` only.
- `TradeSalesJob` return-overdue alert (`TSR-T3-01`, `TRADE_RETURN_OVERDUE`).
- Print `SALES_RETURN_AUTHORISATION`: this task seeds its minimal default template (FR-615); p9-14 adds the final layout.
- Port authority: the SRA approver and sales-executive bundles hold `warehouse:orders:port` (`V551130`).
