## Scope

- **`V510212` — `wh_nrv_assessments`** (`WH-112`). A **register, not a one-way provision column**: item or
  lot, assessed net realisable value, basis, assessor, date, amount, **and the reversal linked to the
  original** — because **Ind AS 2 requires reversal when the cause ceases**.
- **The split is `D-6`'s.** **Warehouse supplies the evidence and the candidates** — ageing, slow-moving,
  obsolete, damaged, expired — and posts the resulting movement. **Accounting owns NRV and provisioning**
  and the judgement someone signs. Warehouse writes no `acc_*` row and posts no journal.
- **The COGS recognition point is a configured policy** — `DISPATCH` · `DELIVERY` · `INVOICE` — with a
  **stock state per stage** and an **in-transit account**. Warehouse computes the cost of goods sold at
  the configured point **from its own layers** (`P2-16`) and hands it over **valued**; accounting posts
  it. *Hardcoding "COGS on dispatch" is wrong every month end for every delivery-terms customer.*
- The policy is an `admin_settings` key in the `WAREHOUSE` category, resolvable per company × site, and
  the resolved value is **snapshotted onto the movement** so a later policy change does not restate last
  year.

## Traps

- **⚠ `OD-1` is the gate behind this task.** `D-6` requires the **accounting** design set to stand down
  `acc_stock_balances`, `acc_physical_stock_counts`, `acc_stock_journals` and `acc_cost_layers`' quantity
  grain when a warehouse product is installed — *a third install state*. That edit is **a different
  repository's to make** and it must land before this task hands over a valued COGS, or two systems will
  both believe they own the number.
- **NRV is warehouse's evidence and accounting's judgement.** If you find yourself computing a provision
  amount and posting it, the boundary has been crossed. Warehouse posts the **movement** (a write-down is
  a value-only or status movement); accounting posts the **entry**.
- **A one-way provision column is the anti-pattern this requirement names.** The reversal is not an
  afterthought: `reversal_of_assessment_id` and the reversal's own assessor and date are in the first
  migration, or Ind AS 2 compliance is unreachable for every assessment already written.
- **`OD-11` / `L-15` governs the value-only movement.** `L-1`…`L-14` conserve **quantity only**; a
  write-down posts `quantity = 0` with a value against the `VALUE_OFFSET` virtual location that `P2-28`
  shipped. If `OD-11` was answered differently, this task changes shape — check before writing.
- **Non-own stock is never written down** (`L-14`). `owner_type != OWN` carries a custody liability and an
  insured value on a different report (`P5-08`/WS-227); an NRV assessment against a client's bailed stock
  is a category error and must be refused with a field-level message, not silently skipped.
- **The recognition point must be snapshotted, like the moving average.** `P2-16` already establishes the
  rule — *"a backdated receipt recomputes the average and without the snapshot there is no way to say what
  the ledger believed at the time."* The same applies to the policy in force when COGS was recognised.
- **Precision:** assessed NRV is a **per-unit** figure ⇒ `DECIMAL(19,6)`; the write-down amount is an
  extended total ⇒ `DECIMAL(19,4)` (`OD-7`; `unit_*` beats `value`).
- **Never let the frontend compute a valuation number.** There is **no decimal library in any frontend**
  (R1 `C-013`) — all arithmetic in `BigDecimal` on the backend; the frontend renders strings.
- The NRV strip is filter-aware ⇒ **no `statistics.*` cache name** (R1 `C-043`,
  `CacheConfiguration.java:190-196`).

## Decisions recorded at build
Build round (lane C, wave 2 round 2), where the spec was silent — each for the driver to ratify or reverse:
- **NRV ladder is `DRAFT → POSTED | REJECTED`, no Edit / Cancel / Delete.** Approve posts in the same transaction (the one `WRITE_DOWN` value-only movement through the P2-28 poster, its ledger approval by the approver, the layer restatements); Reject needs a note. A wrong DRAFT is rejected and raised again — an assessment is never amended, which is the register property Ind AS 2 needs. FR-408: the approver may not be the assessor (403, `WhbMakerChecker`).
- **A write-down's lines are the server's**: every OPEN cost layer of (site, owner, item[, lot]) whose current value per unit is above the assessed NRV, restated to `quantity_remaining × NRV`; the quantity and the unit value before are frozen on the line and re-verified at Approve (409 `NRV_LAYER_CHANGED` if the layer moved). The client sends no line.
- **A reversal is a second row** (`reversal_of_assessment_id`), at most one live (DRAFT or POSTED) per original (partial unique index + service guard). Each reversal line references one original line and gives back `min(the original line's write-down pro rata to the quantity still held, the amount restoring the layer to min(revised NRV, the unit value before the write-down))` — never more than was written down, only on stock still held. The revised NRV must exceed the one written down to.
- **The reversal posts a forward `WRITE_DOWN` movement with a POSITIVE value** and the reason `NRV_REVERSAL` (the write-down carries `NRV_WRITE_DOWN`, a new `NRV` reason context). It does not use `WRITE_DOWN_REVERSAL`: that type mirrors a movement (and would reverse all of it), whereas an NRV reversal is a new, capped, separately-assessed judgement on possibly partly-sold stock.
- **`assessed_at` is stamped by the server; the movement's posting date is the approval date.** No user-entered assessment date.
- **Basis** is a service-held closed list (`AGEING`, `SLOW_MOVING`, `OBSOLETE`, `DAMAGED`, `EXPIRED`, `MARKET_PRICE`, `OTHER`; `OTHER` needs a note) with no DB `CHECK` (D-10), fetched by the screen.
- **L-14** is refused first by the service (422 on `ownerId`, `NRV_OWNER_NOT_OWN`), backed by the `trg_wh_nrv_assessments_own_stock_only` trigger (owner type must be `is_house`); the Add modal's owner list offers house owners only; the candidates report reads house-owner positions only.
- **The candidates report is an on-demand read with request thresholds** (age 180 days, idle 90 days by default) — not stored, so no FR-165 job. A candidate row IS the stock position (`positionId`); its drill is **Assess**, which opens Add with the grain and the first piece of evidence filled in.
- **Money precision:** `write_down_amount`, `wh_nrv_assessment_lines.value_change` and `wh_cogs_recognitions.cogs_value` are `DECIMAL(19,4)` — each is the same figure as the ledger's `extended_cost` / `layer_value` (`DECIMAL(19,4)`), so a narrower column could not hold what the movement moved. `assessed_nrv` / unit values `DECIMAL(19,6)`, quantity `DECIMAL(18,4)` (OD-7).
- **COGS recognition point** — install default `admin_settings.warehouse.cogs.recognition_point` (`DISPATCH` | `DELIVERY` | `INVOICE`, default `DISPATCH`); per-company and per-site override rows in `wh_cogs_recognition_policies` (site beats company beats install).
- **The snapshot lives on a register row, not on the movement.** The movement is append-only and gains no column after PNR-2, so every SALES despatch writes one `wh_cogs_recognitions` row carrying the point in force, its source level, the stock state (`SOLD` / `IN_TRANSIT`) and the account role (`COST_OF_SALES` / `GOODS_IN_TRANSIT`) — written once, never restated by a later setting change. The row is recognised only by the event it was snapshotted with: at dispatch, at `confirmDelivery`, or on an invoice event.
- **The COGS value is read once from the despatch movement's own lines** — `ABS(Σ extended_cost)` over its stock-side lines whose owner type posts to our GL and whose cost basis is not `ZERO_BAILMENT` (L-14: non-own lines are absent, not zero). No row when that is zero, and none for a non-SALES demand order (transfers, vendor returns, job issues).
- **No screen for the COGS register in this task** (the design set allocates WS-149 alone). Its grid is seeded (`wh_cogs_recognitions`) so its export is the export of a configured grid (ledger-style: no audit columns), with `/management`, `/export` and `PATCH /invoiced` under `wh_cogs_recognitions:*`.
- **Mobile: none** (D-13 declaration).

**OPEN INTERFACES (the accounting hand-over, OD-1 open):**
- **NRV write-down / reversal.** Warehouse posts the `WRITE_DOWN` value-only movement (negative value for a write-down, positive for a reversal) with the NRV reason, `source_document_type = NRV_ASSESSMENT` and the assessment id; it goes out on the existing movement hand-over. The ledger owner must post the **provision entry** (and its reversal) from that movement, classifying it by the NRV reason, and must not treat it as a plain revaluation. Warehouse computes no provision amount and posts no entry.
- **COGS.** The ledger owner must take cost of goods sold **from `wh_cogs_recognitions`** at each row's `recognised_at` (cost of sales), and for a `DELIVERY`/`INVOICE` row post the value to goods-in-transit at `dispatched_at` first — **not** assume the despatch `ISSUE` is COGS. The port that consumes the rows and acknowledges them (`handover_status`, today always `QUEUED`) is to be defined with OD-1. The ISSUE envelope still goes out unchanged.
- **The invoice event** has no source inside warehouse: `PATCH /warehouse/inventory/cogs-recognitions/invoiced` (`wh_cogs_recognitions:recognise`) is the seam the invoicing system calls.

**Recorded, not fixed:**
- No management UI for `wh_cogs_recognition_policies` (company / site overrides are seeded or inserted by an administrator until a screen is allocated).
- No screen for the COGS register (export and API only).
