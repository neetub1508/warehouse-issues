Part of __P2__ · Module **`warehouse`** · Migrations **V510070, V511207, V511237, V500039** · Screens **WS-140 Replenishment Runs**, **WS-141 Replenishment Suggestions**, **WS-143 Demand History** — plus two deliverables that are not screens: the **scheduled run** and the **first warehouse notification** (`RK-004`, `RH-007`)

## Scope

**`V510070` — WH-60 — `wh_replenishment_runs`, `wh_replenishment_suggestions`,
`wh_demand_history`.**

**The run produces a document, not a grid** (`FR-253`). A `wh_replenishment_runs` header with
suggestions under it, *so "why did we order that" is answerable months later*. Each suggestion
carries item, site, **on-hand, allocated, on-order, reorder point, suggested quantity**, a
**source** (`PURCHASE` / `TRANSFER` — one spelling everywhere, `RJ-014` b), a source warehouse, a
supplier and **the reason the number is what it is** — which the buyer edits and converts in one
action.
**The warehouse never becomes a purchase-order engine**: Accept creates a PO (`P1-12`), or a transfer
in `REQUESTED` at the destination, which the source approves (`P2-02`, `FR-462`), and stops there.
**A source warehouse for a `TRANSFER` suggestion is a sister site**: a site with a **different**
`REGISTERED` branch, so a site is never its own sister. Availability is grouped by `REGISTERED`, and a
shared site appears once (`RG-001`, R22 §1.2.4 row 12).

**The run is scheduled, not only pressed** (`RK-004`, `FR-253`, `FR-165`). Before round 4 a reorder
point was a threshold column with no job. `wh_replenishment_runs` had `run_by` and no schedule, so the
only reader of reorder point, minimum and safety stock was a report somebody had to remember to open.
- **`run_type` gains `SCHEDULED`** beside the manual run.
- **Each site runs on the install schedule** `warehouse.replenishment.default_schedule`, nightly by
  default, seeded by `P1-20`'s `V511200`. The schedule is applied in the site's own timezone, through
  the ordinary job framework, and writes a `whb_job_runs` record.
- `FR-165`'s enumerated obligations now name the **quantity thresholds** — reorder point, minimum and
  safety stock — and `P0-13`'s register row points here.
- **A run that produces suggestions notifies the site's buyer role**, in-app and by email, with the
  count, the value and a link to WS-141.
- **There is no auto-PO.** The human still accepts, and `FR-253`'s *"never a purchase-order engine"*
  stands. `WH-SC-315`.

**This is the first warehouse notification in v1, and it needs three things outside `warehouse`**
(`RH-007`):
1. **`NotificationCategory.WAREHOUSE_REPLENISHMENT`** — the category is a platform Java enum, so this
   is a platform commit.
2. **One `EmailTemplateDefaults` registration** — templates are registered in platform code before
   `notification_templates` can override them, so this is a platform commit too.
3. **A menu row whose route `NotificationCategoryMenuMapping` resolves** — a warehouse migration,
   carried in `V511207`. An unknown category resolves to nothing, and the unread badge never appears.

Both platform commits are listed in `PP-9` and `PLATFORM-DEPENDENCIES.md` §1.9. The ratchet is
*"zero commits to `warehouse-base`"*, never *"zero commits to `platform`"* (`D-10`). **SMS is not
offered**: platform's SMS delivery is a logging stub, and `P3-16` hides `notify_sms` until it ships.
In-app and email are real. `P3-16` carries every later warehouse category.

**The two ladders** (`RJ-004`). The suggestion ladder is R24 Appendix A, and the run's ladder is
derived from the job; both use §0.11's columns. *Accept* creates its document in the same transaction,
so `ACCEPTED` is momentary. A failed creation rolls both back.

**The verbs** (`RJ-005`).
- `V511207` seeds `wh_replenishment_suggestions:accept` and `:reject`, together with the notification's
  menu row.
- `V511237` inserts their `permission_dependencies` rows.

**Demand history is maintained by movement posting** (`FR-256`), not by a nightly recompute:
`wh_demand_history` keyed `uk(item_id, warehouse_id, period_year_month)` with `hit_count`,
`quantity`, `lost_sale_count`, `lost_sale_quantity`, `is_migrated`.
**Adjustments, warranty issues and internal consumption are excluded via the reason code's
`affects_demand_history`** (`P2-01`, `FR-146`). *Hits matter more than quantity — two hits of one
unit is a stocking case, one hit of two units is not.*

**Lost sales are captured** (`FR-257`) — **automatically** by the insufficient-stock guard at the
counter (`P2-25`) and at the job-issue screen (`P2-26`), and **manually** for *"not catalogued"*,
with a type and a resolution. They land in `P2-01`'s `wh_insufficient_stock_log`
(`is_lost_sale`, `lost_sale_reason_code_id`, `captured_manually`) — **one table, because they are
the same event seen twice** — and are rolled into `wh_demand_history`'s lost-sale columns.
*The demand you refused is still demand; without it the stocking level converges on the stock you
happen to have, and fill rate has no honest denominator* (`FR-393`, `P2-21`).

**Order source on the purchase document** (`FR-260`) — `STOCK` / `DAILY` / `VOR` / `EMERGENCY` /
`SPECIAL_ORDER` / `BACK_ORDER` / `INITIAL_STOCK` — with a **customer-waiting flag**, a linked job
reference and a promised date. The column is on `wh_purchase_orders` (`P1-12`, `V510011`); this task
owns the **rule**: **VOR and emergency purchases are excluded from lead-time statistics**, *or the
reorder mathematics is poisoned*, and the order mix is a monthly management question.

**The computed best stocking level is `P6-02`, v3, and it is not this task.**
`IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §5.3 calls that deferral *"the most commercially dangerous in the plan"* —
CDK, Reynolds, Tekion, Karmak and Autologue all ship it and *"it is the single row a parts manager
tests us on"*. v1 ships the reorder-point arithmetic against the stored ROP, not a computed level.
**Say so on the screen.**

**Screens.** WS-140 · `WAREHOUSE_REPLENISHMENT_RUN` · Service Vehicle, with a `runType` column and
filter (`MANUAL` / `SCHEDULED`). WS-141 · `WAREHOUSE_REPLENISHMENT_SUGGESTION` · Customer — Accept →
creates a PO or a `REQUESTED` transfer · Reject (reason) · **Bulk accept**. The `suggested_source`
column ships here with the value `TRANSFER`. **WS-141 cites no v2 requirement** (`RJ-014` b). The
*preference* for a sister-branch transfer over a purchase is later work, and this screen does not
claim it. WS-143 · `WAREHOUSE_DEMAND_HISTORY` · Customer — **log-style,
no audit columns**, and therefore **no audit columns in its export** either (grid↔export parity is
the rule, not a fixed column list).

**Mobile.** `none` for all three — replenishment is a buyer's desk workflow and demand history is a
report. Stated as a decision per `D-13`/`FR-218`.

## Traps

- **`wh_demand_history` is written by the movement-posting path, which means it is written inside the
  ledger transaction.** Keep it a single `INSERT … ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE` on the
  `(item, warehouse, period)` key; a read-modify-write is `C-024`'s race in a new place.
- **`affects_demand_history` is read from the reason code on the movement, not inferred from the
  movement type.** A `COUNT_ADJ` and a `WRITE_OFF` share a type family and differ by reason.
- **`is_migrated` exists so an incumbent's history can be loaded at go-live** (`FR-415`) **without
  being double-counted** by the posting path. Set it on import; never on a posted movement.
- **The insufficient-stock guard already exists** (`P2-01`, `V510034`). Do not create a second lost-sale
  table. `C-045`: `accessories` ships `accessory_insufficient_stock_logs` plus the global
  block-versus-pend switch (`AccessoryStockEnforcementSettingService.java:17,31`) — **copy the idea,
  not the code**, which is FK-bound to `accessory_products`.
- **`FR-254`'s sister-branch preference is not built here.** Ship `suggested_source` and
  `source_warehouse_id`; the *preference logic* is `P6`. A half-built preference reads as a bug.
- **`period_year_month` is a `VARCHAR(6)` `YYYYMM`, filtered as a text pair** (`periodFrom`/
  `periodTo`) — not a date pair. That is what `BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §3.5 specifies, and it is what
  makes the filter work on a handheld where dates are unsupported (`C-044`).
- **Log-style grids carry no audit columns and their exports carry none either.** Adding export-only
  created-by/updated-by to `wh_demand_history` emits a column no reader can select
  (CLAUDE.md, Table Rules) and `ExportServiceContractTest.WITHOUT_AUDIT_COLUMNS` is the ratchet.
- **`T-5`** — one `getWh…FieldMappings()` per grid, always with a default sort.
- **`T-6`** — three scopes; `runFrom`/`runTo` is a `date` pair, both keys listed.
- **Precision.** `on_hand`, `allocated`, `on_order`, `reorder_point`, `suggested_quantity`,
  `quantity`, `lost_sale_quantity` **`DECIMAL(18,4)`**; `total_value` `DECIMAL(19,4)`;
  `hit_count`, `lost_sale_count` integers.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A replenishment run persists a **run row** with suggestions under it, and the reason text is stored, not recomputed
- [ ] Accepting a suggestion creates a PO **or** a transfer and nothing else; there is no order-management code in `warehouse`
- [ ] A movement whose reason code has `affects_demand_history = false` does **not** increment `hit_count` or `quantity`
- [ ] A guard-triggered refusal at the counter and a manual "not catalogued" entry both produce lost-sale rows in the **same** table, and both roll into `wh_demand_history`
- [ ] Demand history is written inside the posting transaction as an upsert, with no read-modify-write
- [ ] Supplier lead-time statistics exclude `VOR` and `EMERGENCY`; the exclusion is asserted by a test that changes the answer
- [ ] `wh_demand_history` has no audit columns and its export has none
- [ ] The suggestions screen states that the **computed** best stocking level is v3
- [ ] A suggestion's source is `PURCHASE` or `TRANSFER` only; accepting a `TRANSFER` suggestion creates a `REQUESTED` transfer (`RJ-014`, `P2-02`)
- [ ] A sister-branch source is a site whose `REGISTERED` branch differs from the requester's (`RG-001`)
- [ ] The scheduled run fires on `warehouse.replenishment.default_schedule`, writes a `whb_job_runs` row, notifies the buyer role once, and raises no PO by itself (`RK-004`)
- [ ] The notification is sent under `NotificationCategory.WAREHOUSE_REPLENISHMENT`, its template is registered in `EmailTemplateDefaults`, and the menu row exists (`RH-007`)
- [ ] Both ladders have no state without an inbound transition and no non-terminal state without an outbound one; *Accept* and *Reject* are each refused to a user holding only `:edit` (`RJ-004`, `RJ-005`)
- [ ] The export of a filtered, sorted grid reproduces **that** filter and **that** sort - `getEntitiesForExport` consumes its filters map and a sort key, ratcheted by the module's `ExportServiceContractTest` (`P2-29` Rule 1, `FR-400`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-281` · `WH-SC-282` · `WH-SC-283` · `WH-SC-284` · `WH-SC-315`

## Functional-gate rulings (2026-09-18, driver) — overlay

Amends the text above by addition; a row here wins over the sentence it names. The functional contract is
`classic/warehouse/docs/contracts/replenishment.contract.md`.

- **F2 — a reversal is judged by the original.** A reversal's line takes back demand by the line's reason, else the
  ORIGINAL movement's header reason — never the reversal's own header reason, which is the `REVERSAL`-context reason
  every reversal carries (I-3).
- **F3 — a scheduled run names no person** (`FR-024`). `run_by`, `created_by`, `updated_by` are null; the audit line
  names `SCHEDULED_JOB`. The RPL number is issued with a null issuer and `issued_by_actor_type = SCHEDULED_JOB`:
  **`V500039`** (claimed from the declared base gap; sorts after `V500020`, which creates `whb_number_series_issued`)
  drops `issued_by`'s NOT NULL, adds `issued_by_actor_type VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'USER'` and a CHECK
  `(issued_by IS NOT NULL OR issued_by_actor_type = 'SCHEDULED_JOB')`, mirroring `P2-05`'s `wh_holds.placed_by`.
  `WhbDocumentNumberIssuer.issueForScheduledJob` is the only null-issuer path. `SystemUsers.SCHEDULER_USER_ID` is not used.
- **F4 — Reject has reasons on day one.** `V511207` seeds `REPLENISHMENT_REJECT` `NOT_NEEDED`, `SUPPLIER_ISSUE`,
  `TRANSFER_INSTEAD`, `OTHER` (note required), idempotently (the `V500030` §8 idiom).
- **F5 — no double proposal.** On order also counts the open lines of DRAFT / SUBMITTED purchase orders that a
  CONVERTED suggestion of the same site created; an item × site with an open `PROPOSED` suggestion is not proposed
  again.
- **F6 — the notification is site-scoped.** Only holders of `wh_replenishment_suggestions:accept` whose own warehouse
  scope on `wh_replenishment_suggestions` includes the run's site are told (`WhbAlertNotifier`'s optional site scope;
  every other alert stays install-wide).
- **`TRANSFER_DEPART` is not demand.** Stock leaving for a sister site adds nothing to `wh_demand_history`, and its
  reversal takes nothing back.
- **`WH-SC-283` — a not-catalogued lost sale.** `V510070` makes `wh_insufficient_stock_log.item_id` nullable, adds
  `item_description VARCHAR(500)` and a CHECK `(item_id IS NOT NULL OR item_description IS NOT NULL)`. The manual capture
  may record one; it never rolls into `wh_demand_history`.

**Carried, not built** (each is a row in the contract's §4):
- the **TRANSFER accept** — refused after its sister-site check until transfer orders ship (`P1-17` / `P2-02`);
- the **guard half of lost sales** — the counter and job-issue screens that hit the guard (`P2-25` / `P2-26`);
- the **warranty reason seed** (`P2-26`);
- **F7 — the lead-time / order-mix report is API-only in v1** (`GET /warehouse/inventory/replenishment-suggestions/supplier-lead-times`);
- **ledger-less sites are not ticked** by `WHB_REPLENISHMENT_RUN` (also noted in `warehouse-base/docs/DATED-OBLIGATION-REGISTER.md`).
