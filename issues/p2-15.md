Part of __P2__ · Module **`warehouse`** · Migrations **V510070** · Screens **WS-140 Replenishment Runs**, **WS-141 Replenishment Suggestions**, **WS-143 Demand History**

## Scope

**`V510070` — WH-60 — `wh_replenishment_runs`, `wh_replenishment_suggestions`,
`wh_demand_history`.**

**The run produces a document, not a grid** (`FR-253`). A `wh_replenishment_runs` header with
suggestions under it, *so "why did we order that" is answerable months later*. Each suggestion
carries item, site, **on-hand, allocated, on-order, reorder point, suggested quantity**, a
**source** (`PURCHASE` / `SISTER_BRANCH_TRANSFER`), a source warehouse, a supplier and **the reason
the number is what it is** — which the buyer edits and converts in one action.
**The warehouse never becomes a purchase-order engine**: Accept creates a PO (`P1-12`) or a transfer
(`P1-17`/`P2-02`) and stops there.

**Demand history is maintained by movement posting** (`FR-256`), not by a nightly recompute:
`wh_demand_history` keyed `uk(item_id, warehouse_id, period_year_month)` with `hit_count`,
`quantity`, `lost_sale_count`, `lost_sale_quantity`, `is_migrated`.
**Adjustments, warranty issues and internal consumption are excluded via the reason code's
`affects_demand_history`** (`P2-01`, `FR-146`). *Hits matter more than quantity — two hits of one
unit is a stocking case, one hit of two units is not.*

**Lost sales are captured** (`FR-257`) — **automatically** by the insufficient-stock guard at the
counter (`P2-25`) and at the job-issue screen (`P2-26`), and **manually** for *"not catalogued"*,
with a type and a resolution. They land in `P2-01`'s `wh_insufficient_stock_log`
(`is_lost_sale`, `lost_sale_reason_code_id`, `captured_manually`) — **one table, because they are
the same event seen twice** — and are rolled into `wh_demand_history`'s lost-sale columns.
*The demand you refused is still demand; without it the stocking level converges on the stock you
happen to have, and fill rate has no honest denominator* (`FR-393`, `P2-21`).

**Order source on the purchase document** (`FR-260`) — `STOCK` / `DAILY` / `VOR` / `EMERGENCY` /
`SPECIAL_ORDER` / `BACK_ORDER` / `INITIAL_STOCK` — with a **customer-waiting flag**, a linked job
reference and a promised date. The column is on `wh_purchase_orders` (`P1-12`, `V510011`); this task
owns the **rule**: **VOR and emergency purchases are excluded from lead-time statistics**, *or the
reorder mathematics is poisoned*, and the order mix is a monthly management question.

**The computed best stocking level is `P6-02`, v3, and it is not this task.**
`IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §5.3 calls that deferral *"the most commercially dangerous in the plan"* —
CDK, Reynolds, Tekion, Karmak and Autologue all ship it and *"it is the single row a parts manager
tests us on"*. v1 ships the reorder-point arithmetic against the stored ROP, not a computed level.
**Say so on the screen.**

**Screens.** WS-140 · `WAREHOUSE_REPLENISHMENT_RUN` · Service Vehicle. WS-141 ·
`WAREHOUSE_REPLENISHMENT_SUGGESTION` · Customer — Accept → creates a PO or a transfer · Reject
(reason) · **Bulk accept**; `FR-254`'s rule that the run **proposes a sister-branch transfer before
a purchase** where another branch holds stock above its own minimum is `P6`-owned behaviour whose
`suggested_source` column ships here. WS-143 · `WAREHOUSE_DEMAND_HISTORY` · Customer — **log-style,
no audit columns**, and therefore **no audit columns in its export** either (grid↔export parity is
the rule, not a fixed column list).

**Mobile.** `none` for all three — replenishment is a buyer's desk workflow and demand history is a
report. Stated as a decision per `D-13`/`FR-218`.

## Traps

- **`wh_demand_history` is written by the movement-posting path, which means it is written inside the
  ledger transaction.** Keep it a single `INSERT … ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE` on the
  `(item, warehouse, period)` key; a read-modify-write is `C-024`'s race in a new place.
- **`affects_demand_history` is read from the reason code on the movement, not inferred from the
  movement type.** A `COUNT_ADJ` and a `WRITE_OFF` share a type family and differ by reason.
- **`is_migrated` exists so an incumbent's history can be loaded at go-live** (`FR-415`) **without
  being double-counted** by the posting path. Set it on import; never on a posted movement.
- **The insufficient-stock guard already exists** (`P2-01`, `V510034`). Do not create a second lost-sale
  table. `C-045`: `accessories` ships `accessory_insufficient_stock_logs` plus the global
  block-versus-pend switch (`AccessoryStockEnforcementSettingService.java:17,31`) — **copy the idea,
  not the code**, which is FK-bound to `accessory_products`.
- **`FR-254`'s sister-branch preference is not built here.** Ship `suggested_source` and
  `source_warehouse_id`; the *preference logic* is `P6`. A half-built preference reads as a bug.
- **`period_year_month` is a `VARCHAR(6)` `YYYYMM`, filtered as a text pair** (`periodFrom`/
  `periodTo`) — not a date pair. That is what `BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §3.5 specifies, and it is what
  makes the filter work on a handheld where dates are unsupported (`C-044`).
- **Log-style grids carry no audit columns and their exports carry none either.** Adding export-only
  created-by/updated-by to `wh_demand_history` emits a column no reader can select
  (CLAUDE.md, Table Rules) and `ExportServiceContractTest.WITHOUT_AUDIT_COLUMNS` is the ratchet.
- **`T-5`** — one `getWh…FieldMappings()` per grid, always with a default sort.
- **`T-6`** — three scopes; `runFrom`/`runTo` is a `date` pair, both keys listed.
- **Precision.** `on_hand`, `allocated`, `on_order`, `reorder_point`, `suggested_quantity`,
  `quantity`, `lost_sale_quantity` **`DECIMAL(18,4)`**; `total_value` `DECIMAL(19,4)`;
  `hit_count`, `lost_sale_count` integers.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A replenishment run persists a **run row** with suggestions under it, and the reason text is stored, not recomputed
- [ ] Accepting a suggestion creates a PO **or** a transfer and nothing else; there is no order-management code in `warehouse`
- [ ] A movement whose reason code has `affects_demand_history = false` does **not** increment `hit_count` or `quantity`
- [ ] A guard-triggered refusal at the counter and a manual "not catalogued" entry both produce lost-sale rows in the **same** table, and both roll into `wh_demand_history`
- [ ] Demand history is written inside the posting transaction as an upsert, with no read-modify-write
- [ ] Supplier lead-time statistics exclude `VOR` and `EMERGENCY`; the exclusion is asserted by a test that changes the answer
- [ ] `wh_demand_history` has no audit columns and its export has none
- [ ] The suggestions screen states that the **computed** best stocking level is v3
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-281` · `WH-SC-282` · `WH-SC-283` · `WH-SC-284`
