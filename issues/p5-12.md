## Scope

- **`V510206` — `wh_shipment_ndrs` + `wh_ndr_actions`** (`WH-106`). **NDR is a workflow with a response
  clock, not an exception code**: a reason, a **`response_due_at` computed from a per-carrier SLA**, an
  action (reattempt · reattempt on a date · change address · change phone · authorise RTO · hold at hub),
  the carrier acknowledgement, the outcome, an escalation and an attempt number.
  A queue **sorted by due time**, bulk actions and a breach counter.
  **No response and the shipment auto-RTOs at the client's cost** — which is why the clock is a column and
  not a report.
- **`V510207` — `wh_cod_remittances` + `wh_cod_remittance_lines`** (`WH-107`). **COD reconciliation happens
  where the AWB lives.** A remittance carries a **UTR**, gross, deductions and net; its lines match to
  shipments with a **match status and a variance**; **unmatched is a first-class state**;
  delivered-but-not-remitted **ages on a report**; and the matched net emits a **receipt envelope** to
  accounting while the deduction emits an **expense line**. **Warehouse posts no journal itself** (`D-6`).
- **RTO is an inbound stock stream**, not a status: the authorised RTO becomes a return receipt with
  `return_type = RTO` — a **v1 column** (`P2-12`, `WH-SC-200`) precisely so this task has somewhere to
  land.

## Traps

- **⚠ Every threshold column ships with the scheduled job that reads it** (`FR-165`,
  `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §10 addition 4). `response_due_at` with no job is *a dated obligation with no
  actor* — **a defect at the moment it is merged**, not when it is noticed. The auto-RTO job, the
  escalation job and the aged-unremitted report are all part of this task.
- **`response_due_at` is computed against the carrier SLA *and* the working calendar** (`P5-10`). An NDR
  raised at 18:40 on a Saturday does not have a Sunday-morning deadline.
- **`wh_shipment_ndrs` and `wh_cod_remittances` are two of R7 §2.6's five movable boundaries.** Reference
  carriers and shipments by stable code where the reference will cross into `logistics`; **RTO's receipt
  never moves** — *"never fully — the receipt stays"* (`G-013`).
- **Money never balances by construction here.** `gross − deductions = net` is the carrier's claim, not a
  fact: assert it, and where it fails hold the remittance **unmatched** rather than posting a plug.
  `unmatched` is a first-class state precisely so nobody invents a balancing line.
- **Warehouse posts no journal.** The matched net is a **receipt envelope** and the deduction an
  **expense line**, both through accounting's source-document port with a stable idempotency key. An
  architecture test fails the build if any `ai.warehouse*` class references an `acc_*` table (`D-6`).
- **UTR is the reconciliation key and it is not unique across carriers.** Match on
  `(carrier, utr)`; a global unique on `utr` will fail in production and the failure looks like a
  duplicate remittance.
- **Bulk actions on the NDR queue must be transactional per row with a per-row result**, the same shape as
  the port's batch endpoint (`PC-`/`F-092`): forty NDRs actioned must not lose thirty-nine because of one
  bad row.
- `overdueOnly` is a boolean filter; `raisedFrom`/`To` and `remittedFrom`/`To` are `date` pairs. Register
  both scopes in `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS` (`filterUtils.ts:346`) or they are dropped silently. Both strips
  are filter-aware ⇒ **no `statistics.*` cache name** (R1 `C-043`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] `response_due_at` is computed from the per-carrier SLA **and** the working calendar, and the queue sorts by it
- [ ] An unanswered NDR auto-RTOs on a schedule, records the cost attribution, and the RTO arrives as a return receipt with `return_type = RTO` whose disposition offers **no refund path**
- [ ] The escalation job and the aged delivered-but-not-remitted report both exist and both run
- [ ] A remittance whose `gross − deductions ≠ net` is held **unmatched**; no balancing line is invented
- [ ] Matching emits one receipt envelope and one expense line to accounting with a stable idempotency key; a retry posts neither twice
- [ ] With accounting absent, the envelopes queue and are exportable — standalone stays a complete product (`D-7`)
- [ ] A bulk action over 40 NDRs returns a per-row result array and commits the 39 good rows
- [ ] **Mobile:** `none` for both, **with the reason** — the NDR console is a call-centre desk surface and COD reconciliation is finance (`D-13`, `FR-218`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** the ids this task authors, plus `WH-SC-200` re-walked at the RTO boundary
