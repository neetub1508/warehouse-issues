## Scope

- **`V510206` — `wh_shipment_ndrs` + `wh_ndr_actions`** (`WH-106`). **NDR is a workflow with a response
  clock, not an exception code**: a reason, a **`response_due_at` computed from a per-carrier SLA**, an
  action (reattempt · reattempt on a date · change address · change phone · authorise RTO · hold at hub),
  the carrier acknowledgement, the outcome, an escalation and an attempt number.
  A queue **sorted by due time**, bulk actions and a breach counter.
  **No response and the shipment auto-RTOs at the client's cost** — which is why the clock is a column and
  not a report.
- **`V510207` — `wh_cod_remittances` + `wh_cod_remittance_lines`** (`WH-107`). **COD reconciliation happens
  where the AWB lives.** A remittance carries a **UTR**, gross, deductions and net; its lines match to
  shipments with a **match status and a variance**; **unmatched is a first-class state**;
  delivered-but-not-remitted **ages on a report**; and the matched net emits a **receipt envelope** to
  accounting while the deduction emits an **expense line**. **Warehouse posts no journal itself** (`D-6`).
- **RTO is an inbound stock stream**, not a status: the authorised RTO becomes a return receipt with
  `return_type = RTO` — a **v1 column** (`P2-12`, `WH-SC-200`) precisely so this task has somewhere to
  land.

## Traps

- **⚠ Every threshold column ships with the scheduled job that reads it** (`FR-165`,
  `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §10 addition 4). `response_due_at` with no job is *a dated obligation with no
  actor* — **a defect at the moment it is merged**, not when it is noticed. The auto-RTO job, the
  escalation job and the aged-unremitted report are all part of this task.
- **`response_due_at` is computed against the carrier SLA *and* the working calendar** (`P5-10`). An NDR
  raised at 18:40 on a Saturday does not have a Sunday-morning deadline.
- **`wh_shipment_ndrs` and `wh_cod_remittances` are two of R7 §2.6's five movable boundaries.** Reference
  carriers and shipments by stable code where the reference will cross into `logistics`; **RTO's receipt
  never moves** — *"never fully — the receipt stays"* (`G-013`).
- **Money never balances by construction here.** `gross − deductions = net` is the carrier's claim, not a
  fact: assert it, and where it fails hold the remittance **unmatched** rather than posting a plug.
  `unmatched` is a first-class state precisely so nobody invents a balancing line.
- **Warehouse posts no journal.** The matched net is a **receipt envelope** and the deduction an
  **expense line**, both through accounting's source-document port with a stable idempotency key. An
  architecture test fails the build if any `ai.warehouse*` class references an `acc_*` table (`D-6`).
- **UTR is the reconciliation key and it is not unique across carriers.** Match on
  `(carrier, utr)`; a global unique on `utr` will fail in production and the failure looks like a
  duplicate remittance.
- **Bulk actions on the NDR queue must be transactional per row with a per-row result**, the same shape as
  the port's batch endpoint (`PC-`/`F-092`): forty NDRs actioned must not lose thirty-nine because of one
  bad row.
- `overdueOnly` is a boolean filter; `raisedFrom`/`To` and `remittedFrom`/`To` are `date` pairs. Register
  both scopes in `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS` (`filterUtils.ts:346`) or they are dropped silently. Both strips
  are filter-aware ⇒ **no `statistics.*` cache name** (R1 `C-043`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] `response_due_at` is computed from the per-carrier SLA **and** the working calendar, and the queue sorts by it
- [ ] An unanswered NDR auto-RTOs on a schedule, records the cost attribution, and the RTO arrives as a return receipt with `return_type = RTO` whose disposition offers **no refund path**
- [ ] The escalation job and the aged delivered-but-not-remitted report both exist and both run
- [ ] A remittance whose `gross − deductions ≠ net` is held **unmatched**; no balancing line is invented
- [ ] Matching emits one receipt envelope and one expense line to accounting with a stable idempotency key; a retry posts neither twice
- [ ] With accounting absent, the envelopes queue and are exportable — standalone stays a complete product (`D-7`)
- [ ] A bulk action over 40 NDRs returns a per-row result array and commits the 39 good rows
- [ ] **Mobile:** `none` for both, **with the reason** — the NDR console is a call-centre desk surface and COD reconciliation is finance (`D-13`, `FR-218`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** the ids this task authors, plus `WH-SC-200` re-walked at the RTO boundary

## Decisions recorded at build
Build lane P5-12 (wave 4), as built in `classic` (`V510206`, `V510207`, menu + notification keying `V511274`):
- **The response clock is per carrier.** `wh_carriers` gains `ndr_response_hours` (default 24, 1..720), `ndr_auto_rto_grace_hours` (default 24, 0..720) and `cod_remittance_days` (default 7, 1..365), edited on the Carrier modal. At raise, `WhNdrClock` resolves the site + client working calendar on the SITE day through P5-10's `WhWorkingCalendarService` and freezes `response_due_at` (SLA working hours) and `auto_rto_at` (+ grace working hours) on the row. No calendar resolves -> wall clock, logged, `calendar_code` NULL. The queue sorts by `response_due_at`.
- **An NDR is a workflow, not a record.** One live NDR per shipment (partial unique); a desk raise while one is live is 409. No edit, no delete. Status OPEN -> ACTIONED -> CLOSED with an outcome (DELIVERED / RTO / AUTO_RTO / SUPERSEDED / CANCELLED); every response is an append-only `wh_ndr_actions` row. Responses: REATTEMPT, REATTEMPT_ON_DATE (site day, today..+30), CHANGE_ADDRESS, CHANGE_PHONE, AUTHORISE_RTO, HOLD_AT_HUB; the carrier acknowledgement is a separate `:action`.
- **NDR producer (P5-09 F6 closed).** `WhTrackingEventIngestService` hands every NEW event to `WhNdrLifecycleService.onCarrierEvent` in the same transaction: NDR raises the next attempt (a repeat of the live attempt is ignored unless an OUT_FOR_DELIVERY came after it - the second failed attempt on another day is a NEW NDR); DELIVERED closes the live NDR DELIVERED; RTO_INITIATED closes it RTO (no cost bearer, no consignment - the carrier initiated it).
- **Per-attempt notification keying (P5-09 F6 closed).** `V511274` adds `wh_tracking_notifications.attempt_no` and re-keys the fire-once unique to (shipment, event, attempt, channel). NDR = the shipment's NDR count; OUT_FOR_DELIVERY = NDR count + 1; every other event = 1. A second OFD / NDR on another day notifies and is stored; a repeat of the same attempt is not.
- **Jobs.** `WH_NDR_ESCALATION` (15 min): OPEN past due -> `breached_at`, site holders of `wh_shipment_ndrs:action` told once, escalated (level + 1, ESCALATED action) only when the alert reached someone; reached no one FAILS the run and re-fires. `WH_NDR_AUTO_RTO` (15 min, offset 5): OPEN past `auto_rto_at` -> RTO consignment under `NDR_UNANSWERED` (or the existing one reused), `rto_cost_bearer = CLIENT`, closed AUTO_RTO. Both close an NDR whose shipment settled (DELIVERED / CANCELLED) instead - there is no hook in `WhShipmentService`. Billing the cost is the 3PL meter's (OPEN INTERFACE).
- **The RTO comes back as a return receipt `return_type = RTO`** (`WhRtoReceiptLinker`, called from `WhReturnReceiptService.post` / `reverse`): posting receives the shipment's INITIATED RTO consignment (`received_at`, `return_receipt_id`, status RECEIVED); reversing un-receives it. The warehouse has no refund path at all, so none is offered; WH-SC-200's disposition rules apply unchanged.
- **COD is site-scoped and owner-unscoped to write.** A remittance is the carrier's statement for a site and spans owners: reading is site-tiered (lines of owners outside the caller's owner scope are hidden); create / edit / reconcile need an owner-unscoped caller (403 `WH_COD_REMITTANCE_OWNER_SCOPED_CALLER`). UTR unique per carrier (409). Gapless `COD-` number per site; no delete, no toggle.
- **Reconciliation, where the AWB lives.** An AWB matches a shipment at the site through `wh_shipments.tracking_number` OR a CLAIMED / USED `wh_awb_numbers` row (P5-11 never writes the claimed AWB to the shipment). A line MATCHES only on a DELIVERED shipment with COD orders not already MATCHED elsewhere, at exactly `SUM(cod_amount)` rounded once HALF_UP; otherwise SHORT / OVER by its variance or UNMATCHED with the reason. The remittance is MATCHED only when gross - deductions = net, the lines sum to gross and every line MATCHED; anything else is held UNMATCHED with both variances - no balancing line. Editing an UNMATCHED statement re-opens it and clears its matches.
- **Envelopes (D-7).** MATCHED stamps `WH-COD:{id}:RECEIPT` and, only when deductions > 0, `WH-COD:{id}:EXPENSE`, and moves hand-over to QUEUED - once; a retried reconcile of a MATCHED remittance changes nothing. The accounting side is an OPEN INTERFACE (`WhCodAccountingSink`, idempotent on the key); warehouse implements none, so envelopes stay QUEUED and ride the grid export (`handoverStatus` + both keys).
- **Ageing.** "Unremitted ageing" on the COD page: DELIVERED COD shipments with no MATCHED line, per carrier, bucketed 0-7 / 8-15 / 16-30 / 31+ SITE days since delivery, site- and owner-scoped. `WH_COD_UNREMITTED_AGEING` (daily 06:15) alerts site holders of `wh_cod_remittances:reconcile` for COD past the carrier's `cod_remittance_days`, and retries QUEUED hand-overs when a sink exists.
- **Bulk NDR response** is transactional per row (`REQUIRES_NEW` per NDR) and answers one result per requested id; the grid's selection checkbox sits inside the Shipment No. cell (no `select` grid column - export = grid).
- **Not taken (P5-11 leftovers, recorded):** a DELIVERED carrier event still does not move `wh_shipments.status`, and the claimed AWB is still not written to `wh_shipments.tracking_number` - COD matching reads both sources instead.
- **Mobile: none** - the NDR console is a call-centre desk surface and COD reconciliation is finance (`D-13`, `FR-218`).
- **No cache** on either screen (both strips filter-aware, FR-395).

### Fix round (wave 4 gate, `p512-func` F1-F12) - `classic` a3b0c7de8b, `V511276`
Contract amendments, superseding the bullets above where they differ:
- **F1 - ACTIONED is not a dead end.** `WH_NDR_AUTO_RTO` also closes every ACTIONED NDR whose shipment settled (DELIVERED -> outcome DELIVERED, CANCELLED -> CANCELLED) in the same slot. A desk raise is refused (409 `WH_NDR_ALREADY_LIVE`) only while an OPEN NDR exists; an ACTIONED NDR is SUPERSEDED by the desk raise, as by the carrier's. The Raise picker lists shipments with no OPEN NDR.
- **F2 - the settled close commits.** A response on a settled shipment closes the NDR in the row's own transaction, which COMMITS; the 409 `WH_NDR_CLOSED` is thrown after the commit (single) or answered as the row's refusal (bulk, `success=false`, status CLOSED).
- **F3 - no NDR after an RTO.** A shipment with a `wh_rto_consignments` row is returning to origin: a carrier NDR is ignored (logged), a desk raise is refused 422 `WH_NDR_SHIPMENT_RETURNING`, and the Raise picker excludes it. Initiating an RTO on WS-122 closes the live NDR (OPEN or ACTIONED) with outcome RTO, linked to the consignment, through `WhNdrLifecycleService.closeOnRtoInitiated`; `rto_cost_bearer` stays NULL (the consignment carries none) - a desk decision is never put on the client by default.
- **F4 - a carrier RTO leaves a consignment.** A carrier `RTO_INITIATED` on a DISPATCHED shipment initiates its RTO consignment (or reuses the existing one) under the new RTO reason `CARRIER_INITIATED` (`V511276`), `initiated_at` = the event time, and closes the live NDR RTO linked to it with no cost bearer. This happens with or without a live NDR (a carrier may RTO without one).
- **F5 - carrier DELIVERED proves delivery for COD.** The unremitted-ageing report, `WH_COD_UNREMITTED_AGEING` and the reconcile matcher treat a still-DISPATCHED shipment with a normalised DELIVERED tracking event as delivered (aged from the first such event). Moving `wh_shipments.status` itself stays OPEN - record owner: a later dispatch/POD task.
- **F6 - a new attempt.** A carrier NDR is attempt N + 1 when an OUT_FOR_DELIVERY came after the live NDR, OR it falls on a later SITE day than the live NDR, OR the live NDR was ACTIONED before it. Otherwise it repeats the live attempt.
- **F7 - AWB candidates per carrier.** Reconcile only considers shipments of the remittance's carrier (`wh_carriers.counterparty_id = wh_shipments.carrier_counterparty_id`).
- **F8 - the response window starts at max(raisedAt, keyed/ingested time).** `raised_at` keeps the backdated / carrier time; `response_due_at` and `auto_rto_at` are computed from the later of it and the moment the desk keyed it or the feed ingested it. Test: `WhNdrLifecycleServiceTest`.
- **F9 - RECORDED, not fixed:** a carrier acknowledgement of an NDR closed RTO (AUTHORISE_RTO) is not supported - acknowledge requires ACTIONED and the V510206 guard freezes `carrier_ack_ref` once CLOSED. The RTO's own return AWB on WS-122 is the carrier's confirmation of the return.
- **F10** - `rtoConsignment.statuses.RECEIVED` labelled en/fr/hi.
- **F11** - Record / Edit / Reconcile are hidden for owner-scoped callers, read from `GET /warehouse/outbound/cod-remittances/caller-scope` (`{ownerScoped}`, `wh_cod_remittances:view`); hidden until the scope is known.
- **F12 - only MATCHED remittances remit.** A line MATCHED on a remittance held UNMATCHED / OPEN does not count as remitted (ageing + AWB candidates). `V511276` replaces the V510207 partial unique `uk_wh_cod_remittance_lines_matched_shipment` (which would refuse the corrected statement's line) with a plain partial index and `trg_wh_cod_remittances_matched_once`: a remittance turning MATCHED is refused (23505) when one of its MATCHED shipments is already on another MATCHED remittance, serialised per shipment by a transaction-scoped advisory lock.
- In code, stated in the contract: RTO consignment status RECEIVED (T1 row on the RTO contract); escalation stays capped at level 1.

### Fix round 2 (re-verify `N5`) - `classic` 776625b915, `V511277`
- **N5 (T4: remitted-once invariant incl. within one remittance)**: `V511277` adds the partial unique index `uk_wh_cod_remittance_lines_matched_per_remittance (remittance_id, shipment_id) WHERE match_status = 'MATCHED'` — two MATCHED lines for one shipment on the same remittance are refused by the database again, while a corrected statement on another remittance stays allowed (F12's intent). A pre-check raises a named exception if an install already holds such duplicates. `trg_wh_cod_remittances_matched_once` is now `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE` (a header inserted as MATCHED is judged; `OLD` read only under UPDATE).
- **Recorded assumption**: the trigger is race-free only under READ COMMITTED (the default) — after the per-shipment advisory lock each statement takes a fresh snapshot and sees the other transaction's committed header. COD reconciliation must not run at REPEATABLE READ / SERIALIZABLE.
- **Carry-forward from P5-05 (W5 fix round):** CLIENT-borne RTO cost, once metered, must be metered **RATED** - a non-outbox row left METERED is unrated and **blocks** the client's billing run.
- **RECORDED from P5-05 F6 (not changed here):** the same SENT gap applies to `WhCodAccountingSink`'s hand-over queue. P5-05 found that a timed-out (SENT) AR envelope had no user action and was hidden by the queue's default filter, and fixed it for `wh3_ar_handovers` (Retry accepted from SENT, default filter `PENDING,REJECTED,SENT`). The COD envelope queue in the `warehouse` module has the same port shape and was not touched by the P5-05 fix round; its owner should check whether a timed-out COD envelope can be re-sent from the UI.
