**Cartons are mandatory and the reasons are enumerated** (`FR-191`) — six of them, each
independently true:
1. you load and scan **cartons**, not a thousand items;
2. the consignment note (LR/bilty) has a **mandatory package-count field**;
3. the **e-way bill requires package count and description** (`P2-IN-04`);
4. **a carton rides exactly one truck** in a split dispatch;
5. **damage and loss claims are per handling unit**;
6. **3PL handover manifests are carton-based**.

`wh_carton_contents` resolves **to the serial**, so claim 5 and `P2-21`'s forward traceability
(*lot → every shipment and consignee*) are one query.

**Pack evidence is captured at pack time in v1** (`FR-206`) — `wh_carton_evidence` holds the
**pack photograph** and the **scale weight**, *because the evidence for a carrier weight-discrepancy
dispute cannot be created after the dispute*. The **dispute object, its due date and the
carrier-invoice reconciliation are v2 (`P5-13`)**; the evidence is v1 or it does not exist.
`scale_weight_kg` sits beside `expected_weight_kg` so the variance is computable at pack time.

**Cartonisation and dimensional weight are v2**; item dimensions, weights and stackability are **v1
columns on the item** (`P1-01`/`P1-03`). `volumetric_weight_kg` exists on the carton from v1 and is
null until v2 fills it.

## Traps

- **`P-037`'s ten locked decisions are prior art.** Take the six reasons cartons are mandatory
  verbatim into the migration comment — they are the argument that stops a later author "simplifying"
  cartons away when v1 ships one carton per shipment.
- **`wh_carton_evidence` stores a reference, not a blob.** The photo is a platform `documents` row;
  the link table follows `P1-04`'s `ON DELETE NO ACTION` rule — *a GRN photo that vanishes with the
  file is not evidence*, and neither is a pack photo. Deleting the underlying document must be
  **refused**, naming the warehouse references (`WH-SC-269`).
- **`pack_session_id` is nullable in v1** and `wh_pack_sessions` does not exist until `V510101`.
  Do **not** create the table here to make the FK non-null; `P3-07` owns it. Declare the column and
  add the FK in `V510101`.
- **`scale_weight_kg` is `DECIMAL(12,3)`; the dimension columns are `DECIMAL(12,2)` cm and
  `volume_cc` is `DECIMAL(15,2)`.** These are the physical-measure types, not the money types.
- **`T-13`** — the weight variance is computed on the **backend**. There is no decimal library on
  the frontend and `expected − scale` in IEEE-754 is how a 0.001 kg variance becomes a dispute.
- **`T-6`** — `WAREHOUSE_CARTON` into `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`, including `weightVarianceOver`
  (a `number` filter — one of the eight types; there is no range type).
- **Grid↔export parity.** The carton grid shows `createdByName`/`updatedByName`, so the export
  carries them; `ExportServiceContractTest.WITHOUT_AUDIT_COLUMNS` is a ratchet that *may shrink,
  never grow*.
- **`T-1`** — `wh_carton_contents` is not `cascade = ALL, orphanRemoval = true` off the carton.
