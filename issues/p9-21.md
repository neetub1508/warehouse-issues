  BLANKET the `FIRM` schedule quantity, OPEN the released quantity (an OPEN line has no ordered quantity; R32 F-R32TR-025); filters date, dispatch site, item, customer, executive; tiles (open lines, in production, pending dispatch value in company
  currency, overdue lines, invoiced this month); export superset.
- SO display status (`TSO-T4-18`): `APPROVED` → `IN_PRODUCTION` → `PARTLY_DISPATCHED` → `FULLY_DISPATCHED` → `INVOICED` → `CLOSED`
  derived on read, shown on WS-303 / WS-304; stored status untouched (`TRD-03`).
- Report *SO pending to produce* (ordered − produced − free stock reserved, ordered on the basis above), under *Trade Reports — Sales*
  (p9-14's split).
- Report **Sample status** (PRD §13.1; R32 F-R32TR-035, ledger `crm-so-dispatch-reports:13.1-04`): per enquiry line with a
  `sample_request_ref` — customer, item, requested, MFG status / customer feedback read live through `SampleRequestPort.status`
  (`CN-19`), age; filters customer, executive, status, period; export superset; a leaf under *Trade Reports — Sales* (`V552060`
  menu, `V552061` grid; permissions `wht_rpt_sample_status:{view,export}`, one resource per report as `trade-reports` §0). Without MFG the report is empty with
  *No manufacturing module*.
- Customer 360 (FR-777, `TRP-T2-04`): WS-316 (reference Customer): header (profile, grade, credit position via `CN-17` when present),
  tabs Enquiries & quotations · Orders (order-book rows) · Dispatches & returns · Samples (`SampleRequestPort.status` read) · Billing (p9-20) — ≤ 2 sections
  per tab; permission `wht_customer_profiles:view_360` (→ `wht_customer_profiles:view`); every tab exports, audited.
- Without MFG: produced / in-production columns read 0 with *No manufacturing module* and no `IN_PRODUCTION` status.
