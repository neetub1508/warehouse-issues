## Traps

- **Conditions and recipients are rows, not JSONB and not arrays.** `FR-383` and `WS-069`'s block are
  explicit that this replaces the prior art's `trigger_conditions JSONB`,
  `recipient_roles` and `recipient_user_ids`. CLAUDE.md's blanket "NO JSONB" is overstated —
  `ArchitectureInvariantsTest.java:225-239` freezes a baseline — but **no jsonb on a new `whb_`
  business table** holds absolutely, and grid-preference columns remain the only permitted use.
- **`subject_column` and `operator` are whitelisted.** An alert condition that becomes a dynamic
  `WHERE` fragment is a SQL-injection surface, and `SqlSortBuilder`'s whitelist discipline
  (CLAUDE.md CRITICAL 3) exists for exactly this reason.
- **A watcher with no baseline detects nothing.** `WH-SC-251`: *movements posted today **against a
  baseline***. A signal that reports "42 movements today" and has nothing to compare to is a number,
  not a control.
- **A job that cannot fail is not a control** (`WH-SC-015`). The corrupted-cache case is itself a
  test that must be run — the exception row names the position key, the cached value, the rebuilt
  value and the delta, and appears on the reconciliation-exception grid with an action.
- **`FR-165`: every one of these signals is a scheduled job and needs a `whb_job_runs` row** (WS-064).
  The health grid reads job outcomes; it must not *be* the job's only record.
- **Notifications go through the platform's `NotificationService`**, delivered by
  `NotificationDeliveryListener` with `@TransactionalEventListener(AFTER_COMMIT, fallbackExecution = true)`
  — do not write a second delivery path. And note the live trap: an `@Async` notification builder that
  touches a lazy association after the transaction closes throws `LazyInitializationException`;
  resolve the recipient's display fields **inside** the transaction.
- **`whb_alert_events` is where escalation lives, and `escalationMinutes` is a dated obligation** —
  it needs its job (`FR-165`) or an unacknowledged critical alert escalates to nobody.
- **`unacknowledgedOnly` defaults to true**, which means the default filter strip must be seeded in
  `grid_preferences.default_filters` as `'[…]'::jsonb`. `filter_definitions.default_visible = true`
  alone does nothing (`V18:12`, `V229:50-51`).
- **`alert_type`, `scope_type` and `urgency` carry no `CHECK`** (`D-10`).
