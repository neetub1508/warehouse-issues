## The rule, in one sentence

**Whichever system is authoritative for quantity is authoritative for cost; accounting is always
authoritative for the ledger** (`D-6`, `FR-230`). Warehouse installed → warehouse runs the costing
engine, because only warehouse holds the grain the costing methods need (owner, duty status, lot,
serial, location — FEFO and specific identification are not expressible at accounting's
item × godown × batch × serial grain), and because a **standalone** install must be able to produce an
inventory valuation with **no accounting module present** (`D-7`, `FR-446`). Accounting then does
**not** re-cost what it is handed.

**Warehouse never writes an `acc_*` table and never imports an `ai.accounting*` type** (`FR-231`).

## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `O-002` · `O-003`.** **The receiving side cannot express what this seam
> promises.** (1) `acc_posting_rule_lines.account_strategy` is closed at ten values in
> `accounting/docs/DATA-MODEL.md`, and none is keyed on reason code, item group or owner type.
> `WH-SC-156`'s promise that *"accounting resolves it to an account"* is therefore
> **unimplementable as accounting is specified today**. Either accounting adds
> `REASON_CODE_MAPPED`, `OWNER_TYPE_MAPPED` and `ITEM_GROUP_MAPPED` strategies — the reciprocal
> edit, `OD-1` — or `WS-051` must carry the reason-code → account map on the warehouse side, which
> contradicts `WH-SC-156`. **Decide before `V500042`**, and add it to the `OD-1` reciprocal-edit
> list. (2) `posting_status`'s four values do not cover the receiving side's seven: add
> **`DISCARDED`** (terminal, carrying the counterpart's reason) and **`BLOCKED`** (accounting's
> `PENDING_PREDECESSOR`) to the `CHECK` **in the `PNR-1` DDL**, map accounting `FAILED` →
> `REJECTED`, and give `SUPERSEDED` a `handover_superseded_by_id`. `IRR-41` means this vocabulary
> cannot be widened cheaply later. The matching queue amendment is in `P2-18`: the
> rejected-handover queue must separate actionable (`REJECTED`) from terminal (`DISCARDED`) from
> waiting (`BLOCKED`), because re-sending a discarded envelope is idempotent and will simply be
> discarded again.

> **Round-4 addition — `RJ-011` · `RL-002` · `RG-005` · `RG-001`.** (1) **A rejected handover can
> be voided** (`RJ-011`): `REJECTED → VOIDED` via **`whb_accounting_handovers:void`** (seeded by
> `P0-15` in `V501000`). The approver is never the requester (`FR-408`), and the void is allowed only
> once the movement is reversed or reclassified `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Reversing a never-posted movement
> voids **both** envelopes. `VOIDED` is terminal and outside `P0-07`'s close guard, and a billing run
> may cancel once its AR handover is `VOIDED` (`P2-18`, `P2-22`, `P5-05`). `VOIDED` joins the status
> `CHECK` in `V500042`. (2) `whb_accounting_handovers` gains **`envelope_version SMALLINT NOT NULL
> DEFAULT 1`**, so a stored handover replays at the version it was written in (`RL-002`). (3)
> `whb_gl_posting_rules.item_category_id` matches the item's **`STOCKING`-scheme category from
> `whb_item_category_assignments` at the movement's `occurred_at`** — never today's category
> (`whb_items.category_id` is dropped, `RG-005`). (4) The envelope's `branch` is the site's
> `REGISTERED` branch in `whb_warehouse_branches` **at `occurred_at`**, frozen in the hashed payload
> (`FR-233`; `RG-001`, R22 §1.2.4 row 10).

- **`V500042`** — `whb_accounting_handovers` and `whb_gl_posting_rules`.
- **`handover_id` + `posting_status` on the movement** (`FR-232`), `posting_status` ∈
  `NOT_APPLICABLE` / `PENDING` / `POSTED` / `REJECTED`. **Both columns are on `whb_stock_movements`
  and are therefore written by `P0-02`'s `V500030`** — they are `IRR-41` and they share `PNR-2`.
  This task owns the tables they point at and the behaviour; **it does not, and cannot, add them
  later.** State it in the `V500030` review.
- **The rejected-handover queue with an owner and an action** — that queue is the answer to *"does
  the stock ledger tie to the GL?"*, and without it the question is unanswerable.
- **One envelope per posting-relevant event**, handed to accounting's existing source-document port.
  Base emits; accounting consumes; neither imports the other.
- **Movements whose `owner_type != OWN` are handed over for quantity and custody reporting only and
  are never valued** (`L-14`, `FR-112`). A 3PL that posts its clients' stock to its own balance sheet
  has a catastrophe in both directions.
- **The architecture test that fails the build** if any class under `ai.warehouse*` names an `acc_*`
  table or an `ai.accounting*` type (`FR-231`).

## Traps

- **`handover_id` and `posting_status` cannot be added by this task.** They live on
  `whb_stock_movements`, which `V500030` seals against `UPDATE` in the same file that creates it. If
  they are missed there, **every movement written before the fix has `posting_status = NULL` forever
  and no backfill exists** (`IRREVERSIBLE.md` §3.1). Review `V500030` for `IRR-41` **before** it
  merges, not when this task starts.
- **`posting_status` has a column `DEFAULT 'NOT_APPLICABLE'` and a JPA field initialiser will beat
  it** — `DATA-MODEL.md:204` calls this out specifically, and the live instance is
  `accessories/…/entity/StockLevel.java:58-64` (R1 §8 trap `T-2`). Decide in one place.
- **`R5 S-066` is overruled by `D-6`.** A reviewer reading R5 alone will conclude accounting computes
  value. It does not, where warehouse is installed. `D-6` states the authority rule and `OD-1` is the
  amendment that carries it into the accounting design set — **an edit to a different repository,
  and that set's to make.**
- **The payload column is `TEXT`, never JSONB.** `D-10`/`FR-383` forbid JSONB on new warehouse
  business tables; grid-preference columns are the only permitted use. The prior art put JSONB on
  eleven business tables and made it a convention (`P-007`, `WMS_DATABASE_DESIGN.md:99`).
- **`whb_gl_posting_rules` resolution is most-specific-first over wildcard dimensions**, and the
  **Test resolution** modal is what makes that auditable. A rule engine nobody can interrogate is how
  a mis-posting survives a year.
- **Retry must be idempotent by construction**, not by a flag. The handover carries its own
  `idempotency_key`; accounting's port de-duplicates on it exactly as warehouse's port does
  (`P0-08`).
- **`whb_accounting_handovers` gates period close.** `P0-07`'s Soft close / Close / Reopen modals
  refuse while `pendingHandoverCount > 0`. If this task lands after `P0-07`, that guard reads as
  enforced and is vacuous — sequence them, or ship the guard behind an explicit flag flipped here.
- **Two scopes in `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`; the handover status filter is a `multiselect`** defaulting
  to `PENDING + REJECTED`, because a queue defaulted to "all" is a queue nobody works.
- **`VOIDED` is not a way to make a rejection disappear** (`RJ-011`). It is refused while the
  movement still stands as financial; the ledger must be reversed or reclassified first. Otherwise
  the close guard is satisfied by deleting the evidence that the stock ledger and the GL disagree.
- **Resolve the posting rule's category at `occurred_at`, never from the item's current row**
  (`RG-005`). A re-categorisation between post and retry would otherwise route a retried envelope to
  a different account than the original.
- **`ArchitectureInvariantsTest` is per-module and is an edit, not a copy** — accounting-base's is
  660 lines including self-tests that assert the scanners are not passing vacuously
  (`C-041`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] An architecture test **fails the build** when a class under `ai.warehouse*` references an
      `acc_*` table name or imports an `ai.accounting*` type (`WH-SC-162`)
- [ ] Every posting-relevant movement produces exactly one envelope with a `handover_id`, and the
      movement's `posting_status` moves `PENDING` → `POSTED` on acknowledgement
- [ ] A rejected handover lands on WS-052 with its rejection code and message, has an **owner and a
      retry action**, and **blocks the period close** (`WH-SC-158`)
- [ ] A movement whose owner type has `posts_to_our_gl = false` is handed over for **quantity and
      custody only** and carries no value (`L-14`)
- [ ] `Test resolution` shows which `whb_gl_posting_rules` row wins for a given five-tuple, most
      specific first
- [ ] Retrying a handover twice posts once, proved by row count in the consumer
- [ ] A standalone install with **no accounting module** sets `posting_status = NOT_APPLICABLE` and
      never queues anything (`D-7`)
- [ ] A `REJECTED` handover is voided only by a `:void` holder who is not its requester, and only
      after its movement is reversed or reclassified `NOT_APPLICABLE`. A `VOIDED` handover does not
      block period close, and reversing a never-posted movement voids both envelopes (`RJ-011`)
- [ ] Every handover carries `envelope_version`, and a replay parses the stored payload at that
      version (`RL-002`)
- [ ] After an item is re-categorised, **Test resolution** and the posting path both pick the rule
      for the `STOCKING` category that held at the movement's `occurred_at` (`RG-005`)
- [ ] The envelope's `branch` is the site's `REGISTERED` branch at `occurred_at`, including for a
      site re-registered after the movement (`RG-001`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-158` · `WH-SC-162`

## Blocked on
**`OD-1`** — the reciprocal accounting edits. `D-6` requires the `accounting` set to add a **third
install state** ("a warehouse product is present and owns quantity") and to stand down
`acc_stock_balances`, `acc_physical_stock_counts`, `acc_stock_journals`, `acc_cost_layers`' quantity
grain and the port's `unit_cost` semantics. It is cheap now — accounting's P3 is unbuilt — and
expensive once `acc_valuation_entries` has rows. **It is that repository's edit to make**, and this
task cannot define the envelope's contract until it is taken.
