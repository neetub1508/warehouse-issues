## Traps
- Trade never numbers, posts or prints an invoice or credit note — it proposes; accounting's gapless GST series (Rule 46) is accounting's.
- A proposal is built from core quantities read at the event, never from a stored shipped quantity (`TRD-03`).
- No JSON payloads: proposal lines are rows (CLAUDE.md, no JSONB).
- Without accounting the proposals stay `PROPOSED` and the close rule is `SHIPPED` — nothing waits forever.
