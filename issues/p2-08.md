## Scope

**`V510040` — WH-30 — `wh_demand_orders`, `wh_demand_order_lines`.**

**One demand model for every demand type** (`FR-177`) — sales, transfer, work order, replenishment,
VAS, sample, scrap, job issue — with document-specific extras in **adapter tables keyed to the
demand header**, never in a second order table. *Allocation, waving, picking, packing and shipping
are identical regardless of why the stock is leaving; three parallel implementations mean three
places to fix every allocation bug and three sets of KPIs that do not add up.*
**There is no second order table anywhere in the product.**

**Six quantity columns on the line** (`FR-178`): `ordered_quantity`, `allocated_quantity`,
`picked_quantity`, `shipped_quantity`, `cancelled_quantity`, `backordered_quantity`. **None is
derived from another.** *Deriving backorder from two of them fails the moment a partial cancel
lands*, and makes a short ship indistinguishable from a cancellation.
Per rounding rule **R-3**, complementary pairs are computed **by subtraction, never by a second
rounding** — `backordered = ordered − allocated − shipped − cancelled` is how the value is
*computed*, and it is then **stored**, so the six columns always sum back to the parent exactly.

**`priority`, `promised_ship_at`, `promised_deliver_at` and an SLA reference are v1 columns**
(`FR-180`). *Every operational KPI in this category is a difference between two timestamps and none
can be recovered later.* `P2-21`'s on-time-ship KPI reads them; they cannot be backfilled.

**Order holds are records and the release gate is a query** (`FR-182`): an order releases to picking
only when it has **zero open holds**, evaluated against `P2-03`'s `wh_holds` with the hold type's
`blocks_allocation` / `blocks_pick` flags. Not a status column.

**Screens.** WS-099 · `wh_demand_orders` · `WAREHOUSE_DEMAND_ORDER` · **Service Vehicle** reference ·
cache `dropdown.whDemandOrder`. WS-100 is a **detail route, no grid**, with tabs
Lines · Allocations (the `whb_reservations` holding this order) · Tasks · Shipments · Cartons ·
Holds · Movements · Documents · Audit.
Transition modals — **each its own modal, never a field on the edit form**: Allocate · **Release** ·
Place hold / Release hold · Short pick · Cancel.
Filters exactly per `BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §3.3, including the `customerCounterpartyId` async
typeahead that **scopes before it caps** and the `lateOnly` boolean **computed on the backend**
against `promised_ship_at`.
**Statistics strip:** open · allocated · picking · packed · shipped today · late against promise ·
on hold — **filter-aware, no cache name** (`FR-395`). `dropdown.whDemandOrder` **is** registered in
`CacheConfiguration.java`.

**Mobile.** `mobile/src/screens/whDemandOrder` — list and detail; picking is **WS-232 RF Pick**
(`P3`). Mobile filters are the dropdowns plus `orderNumber` text; **the two date ranges become a
`promisedWithin` dropdown**, because `mobile/…/ListHeader.tsx:210-218` supports
`'dropdown' | 'text'` and **not dates** (`C-044`, `PP-3`).

## Traps

- **The adapter extension rule is `D-11`, and it is what keeps `FR-177` true.**
  `whad_counter_sales` and `whas_material_requests` key **to** `wh_demand_orders`; they do not
  duplicate it and they **must not** add a column to it. If an adapter needs a field the demand
  header does not have, that is a `warehouse` change reviewed on its merits — not an `ALTER` from
  the adapter's migration.
- **`FR-180`'s four columns are `PNR`-shaped.** Ship them empty rather than not at all. *The first
  time the KPIs are asked for — month two of the first client — the answer has to cover month one*
  (`FR-392`).
- **`lateOnly` is backend-computed.** CLAUDE.md rule #8: the frontend is display-only, with no
  `.filter()` on data arrays in the page. A `lateOnly` computed in the page is both a standards
  violation and wrong under pagination.
- **`orderDateFrom`/`orderDateTo` are `dateOnly`** (`order_date` is a `DATE`);
  `promisedShipFrom`/`promisedShipTo` are **`date`** (`promised_ship_at` is `TIMESTAMPTZ`).
  `filterUtils.ts:49-56` explains why the distinction matters east of UTC. **All four keys** go in
  `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS.WAREHOUSE_DEMAND_ORDER` — an unlisted key is silently dropped
  (`filterUtils.ts:346` onward).
- **`dropdown.whDemandOrder` must be registered in `CacheConfiguration.java`.** An unregistered name
  throws `IllegalArgumentException` on the **first call**, not at startup — the warning is in the
  file at `:375-377`. The statistics strip gets **no** name (`:190-196`, issues `#790`/`#791`).
- **The async customer typeahead scopes before it caps.** Capping first and scoping after returns a
  page of other owners' customers and then filters it to nothing.
- **`T-13` — there is no decimal library on the frontend.** Only `platform/frontend/package.json`
  exists (38 dependencies, no `decimal.js`/`big.js`). The six quantities are computed on the backend
  in `BigDecimal` and sent as formatted strings (`FR-031`).
- **`T-1`** — the line collection is **not** `cascade = ALL, orphanRemoval = true`. `getLines().clear()`
  on a released order is the shape that works on drafts and fails in production.
- **Precision.** The six quantities `DECIMAL(18,4)`; `unit_price` **`DECIMAL(19,6)`** (per-unit
  beats `*_amount`); `line_total` `DECIMAL(19,4)`; `priority` an integer.
