## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `FR-449`, `Y-003`.** This task makes a pick move stock to **a real, countable
> staging location** — which creates a state the set had no exit from: an order **cancelled after the
> pick and before the ship confirm** leaves units in staging with their reservation gone, belonging to
> nothing, counted by nobody, and reported nowhere. **A cancellation after pick-to-staging must offer
> a de-stage path** — a put-back movement from staging to the source or to a putaway task — and where
> it cannot, it is **refused with `409 STAGED_STOCK`** naming the staging location and the quantity.
> Silently cancelling is the one option that is not available.

**`V510041` — WH-31 — `wh_pick_tasks`.** The **1:1 pick extension of `whb_tasks`** (`P0-10`,
`V500034`) — `D-3`'s worked example of a `wh_` table sitting over a `whb_` one, and the fix for
`DATA-MODEL.md` §3.4's defect **X-4**. It does **not** duplicate `whb_tasks`' lifecycle timestamps;
it adds the pick-specific columns and points at the task.

**The one sentence the whole team holds: dispatch is the inventory-relief event and it is the only
one** (`FR-189`). *Every alternative reading produces a different set of bugs.* Write it into the
service javadoc, the screen help text and the API docs.

**Pick moves stock to a real, countable staging location** (`FR-188`): `−qty` at the pick face /
`+qty` at `STAGE-OUT-01`, a genuine `whb_locations` row with `counts_as_on_hand = true` — **not** a
virtual location and **not** off the books. *If stock is relieved at pick, picked-not-shipped goods
vanish from on-hand while physically present, and a count of the staging lane disagrees with the
position table.* The reservation stays **open** and is now attached to the staged stock.

**Ship confirm relieves from staging to the virtual customer location and closes the reservation in
one transaction** — `−qty` at `STAGE-OUT-01` / `+qty` at `VIRT-CUSTOMER`, the reservation closes,
the demand line's `shipped_quantity` advances. **One transaction.**

**A short pick is a first-class outcome, not a silently reduced quantity** (`FR-185`). It:
1. records the shortfall against an **exception code**;
2. **releases the unmet reservation**;
3. offers **re-allocate / emergency replenish / short the line**;
4. under a per-site switch, **auto-creates a cycle-count task for the location** — *a short pick is
   the highest-quality signal of an inventory error a warehouse ever gets*.

**v1 is discrete picking only, and the deferral is stated rather than discovered** (`FR-187`,
`FR-186`): v1 ships **Release on the demand header** (`P2-08`); **batch pick is v1.1 (`P3-06`)**;
cluster, zone, pick-and-pass, pick-to-carton and put-wall are v2/v3. The `pick_method` column
carries `DISCRETE`/`BATCH`/`CLUSTER`/`ZONE` from v1 so the later methods are a new value, not a
re-key. `wh_pick_tasks.wave_id` is nullable in v1.

**WS-102** · `wh_pick_tasks` · `WAREHOUSE_PICK_TASK` · Service Vehicle reference.
Columns `taskNumber`, `orderNumber`, `waveNumber`, `pickMethod`, `itemCode`, `lotCode`,
`serialNumber`, `sourceLocationCode`, `requestedQuantity`, `pickedQuantity`, `status`,
`assignedToName`, `startedAt`, `completedAt`, `exceptionCode`.
Filters `warehouseId` → `zoneLocationId` → `status` **multiselect** · `assignedTo` typeahead ·
`pickMethod` select · `waveId` select · `orderNumber` text · **`shortPickOnly`** boolean.
Actions Assign · Complete · **Short pick** · Cancel.
**The pick task carries `owner_id` from v1** (`P0-10`), so cross-client waving is possible later
without a re-key.

**Mobile.** The v1.1 RF pick screen (**WS-232**, `P3`) is a **separate screen family**, not an
`EntityListScreen` instance (`FR-217`, `FR-220`). In v1 `mobile/src/screens/whPickTask` offers the
list, assign and complete; short pick is available because the operator who was short is the one who
knows. State that explicitly.

## Traps

- **`P-038` is the workflow spine and it is prior art worth adopting.** Pick Confirm
  (`Allocated → Picked`) → pack → shipment `READY_FOR_TRANSPORT` → carrier → e-invoice → e-way bill
  → gate pass → **Dispatch (`Packed → Shipped` — only here)**. R6's single most valuable line is
  *"Dispatch is the inventory-relief event and it is the only one"*, which is `T-046` expressed as a
  rule a team can hold.
- **The staging location is `counts_as_on_hand = true`.** If it is modelled as one of `FR-084`'s
  virtual locations, `WH-SC-054`'s count of the lane fails and the whole point is lost.
- **Ship confirm is one transaction, and the reservation close is inside it.** `T-046` is exactly
  the failure of doing the relief and the reservation close in two.
- **`wh_pick_tasks` is a 1:1 extension, not a copy.** §3.4 defect X-4 is the shape where the
  application table re-declares `assigned_at`/`started_at`/`completed_at` and the two drift.
  `whb_tasks` owns the lifecycle timestamps; `P2-21`'s KPIs read them from there.
- **A short pick that reduces the requested quantity is the defect.** `requested_quantity` is
  immutable on the task; `picked_quantity` and `exception_code` carry the truth.
- **The auto-created cycle-count task is behind a per-site switch and it creates a `wh_counts`
  document** (`P2-04`), not an ad-hoc adjustment.
- **`T-6`** — `WAREHOUSE_PICK_TASK` into `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`, including `shortPickOnly`.
- **`PP-7`** — the pick-task grid is ledger-adjacent; enforce a bounded default scope in v1.
- **`T-16` — controllers must live in `ai.warehouse.controller…`** or
  `UserActivityTrackingAspect`'s pointcut (`:59`, `within(ai..controller..*)`) cannot see them and
  the pick never appears in the activity log.
