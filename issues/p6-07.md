## Scope

- **`V530110`** — the escalation rule: client or standard card, index or fixed percentage, frequency,
  effective anchor, rounding, and the review state of each generated version.
  **Rate escalation generates a new rate-card version for review; it never mutates an active card.**
- **`V530111`** — the SLA-penalty posting record: confirmed breach → proposed penalty → approval →
  the **negative billable event** on the `SLA_CREDIT` charge code, and the approver.
- **`V530100` — `wh3_client_profitability_snapshots`** (`W3-14`, the one v3 table `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.5
  allocates): client, period, revenue, labour minutes, labour cost, storage units, storage cost, margin,
  margin percent.
  **Revenue** is the metered, rated total from `P5-03`/`P5-05`. **Cost** is `P6-03`'s measured labour
  minutes at a loaded rate plus `P2-28`'s storage snapshots at a space cost. *It is the honest answer to
  "which clients are we losing money on".*
- `V530110`–`V530111` are carved from `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.5's *"reserved for corrections"* range;
  `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §2.9 divergence 5 records it. Add both rows to §7.5 in the same PR.

## Traps

- **⚠ Escalation must never mutate an `ACTIVE` card.** `P5-02` designed the immutability and the version
  ladder for exactly this moment. An escalation that edits a card in place silently restates every
  already-rated event on it, and `WH-SC-236`'s *"a dispute three months later re-rates to the same
  number"* becomes false — which is the whole reason the meter exists.
- **The generated version lands in `DRAFT` for review, not `ACTIVE`.** An escalation that auto-activates
  is a price change nobody approved, sent to a client who did not agree it.
- **The SLA credit is a charge code, not a branch in the biller** (`F-013`, `F-078`). `SLA_CREDIT` was
  seeded in `P5-02`; the penalty is a **negative-quantity billable event** on it, subject to approval, and
  it appears as a visible credit line on `wh3_billing_run_lines` with `is_sla_credit = true`.
  `wh3_sla_breaches.billable_event_id` already exists from `P5-07`, so **this is a service, not a
  migration** — if you find yourself adding that column, `P5-07` was built wrong.
- **Approval is separated from confirmation.** The breach confirmer and the credit approver are different
  people above a threshold, exactly as `P5-05`'s accessorials require — and the credit posts into the
  **current open run**, never into a frozen approved one.
- **Profitability must compare like with like.** Revenue is rated at the card version live on each
  **event's** date; cost is measured minutes at a loaded rate **for the same period**. Mixing a current
  rate card with historic minutes produces a number that looks precise and is not.
- **The loaded rate is an input, not a computed truth**, and it must be recorded on the snapshot. A margin
  recomputed later against a different loaded rate is a different number, and someone will ask which was
  shown at the board meeting.
- **A profitability snapshot is a cache.** It must be rebuildable from the meter, `wh_labour_tasks` and
  the storage snapshots exactly — the same discipline as `L-4`.
- **Non-own stock is never valued** (`L-14`). Profitability is a **revenue-and-cost** statement about
  serving a client, and it must never acquire an inventory-value column for that client's goods.
- `periodFromFrom`/`To` are two **`dateOnly`** filters; `marginPct` is `DECIMAL(9,6)` and every amount
  `DECIMAL(19,4)` (`OD-7`). The strip is filter-aware ⇒ **no `statistics.*` cache name** (R1 `C-043`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] An escalation produces a **new `DRAFT` version** for review; the active card is byte-identical afterwards
- [ ] Re-rating a period that predates the escalation returns the pre-escalation total, unchanged
- [ ] A confirmed breach posts **nothing** until approved; on approval it posts a **negative** event on `SLA_CREDIT` into the current open run and appears as a visible credit line
- [ ] The breach confirmer may not be the credit approver above the threshold
- [ ] `wh3_sla_breaches.billable_event_id` is populated by this task and was **created** by `P5-07` — no new column
- [ ] A profitability snapshot reconciles: revenue equals the meter's rated total for the period; labour cost equals `P6-03`'s measured minutes × the recorded loaded rate; storage cost equals the snapshot units × the recorded space cost
- [ ] The snapshot is fully rebuildable from its three sources, and a job proves it
- [ ] The snapshot carries **no** inventory-value column for the client's goods
- [ ] Both new migration numbers are recorded in `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.5 in the same PR
- [ ] **Mobile:** `none` — profitability, escalation and credit approval are desk surfaces (`D-13` declaration)
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-236` · plus the ids this task authors into `SCENARIO-CATALOGUE.md`
