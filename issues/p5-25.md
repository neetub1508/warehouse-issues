## Scope — a second persona on `FR-284`'s surface, not a second portal (`RK-006`)

A dealer's parts counter is increasingly a wholesale business. Garages, fleet operators and
sub-dealers buy from it by phone and WhatsApp, and they want to see availability, place an order and
download the challan (`RK-006`). `FR-284`'s portal is already the right shape: *"a permission surface
over the existing screens, not a second application"*. This task adds the trade buyer as a **second
persona** on that surface. It is a separate task so the 3PL portal (`P5-08`) can ship alone (`FR-464`).

1. **`wh_trade_portal_users`** (`V510221`) — `user_id` (a platform user), `customer_counterparty_id`,
   `effective_from`, `effective_to`; one current row per `(user_id, customer_counterparty_id)` by
   `EXCLUDE` (`D-14`, R22 §1.3). A fleet manager may buy for two garages; each is a row. The counterparty
   must hold a current `CUSTOMER` role link (`P1-08`).
2. **The scope key is the customer counterparty**, resolved by the **single server-side resolver**
   (`FR-406`) that already resolves `P5-08`'s owner scope. It is one resolver with a second persona, never
   a second resolver. Every read endpoint the persona reaches is filtered by it.
3. **`WS-240` Trade Portal** — tabs over existing screens, as `WS-172` is:
   - **availability** — per item, a **flag**: *in stock*, *on order* or *not stocked*, across the sites of
     the dealer's company. Never a quantity, and never a site breakdown;
   - **orders** — the customer's own demand orders (`WS-099` scoped), and a *New order* action that
     creates a `demand_type = SALES` order in **`DRAFT`** for the counter to confirm (`P2-08`);
   - **shipments** — status of the customer's own shipments (`WS-105` scoped);
   - **documents** — download of the challan and packing slip printed for the customer's shipments.
4. **Permissions** (`V511209`, `V511239`): the portal resource's `:view` and the order-draft verb, their
   dependency rows, and the menu row guarded `WHERE NOT EXISTS`. The persona gets **no** other warehouse
   permission.

## Traps

- **A flag, never a number** (`RK-006`). A quantity tells a buyer, who may also buy from the next dealer,
  the dealer's whole stock position. The availability endpoint returns an enum; a test asserts the
  response carries no numeric stock field.
- **`DRAFT` reserves nothing.** A portal order enters the one demand model (`P2-08`) and waits for the
  counter. Allocation happens at the counter's confirm, under the counter user's permissions and the
  `FR-461` serving-branch rule. A portal draft that reserved stock would let an outside buyer hold the
  dealer's stock.
- **`403`, never an empty result**, on a request naming another customer (`FR-300`, `WH-SC-140`). The
  negative test per endpoint that `P5-08` wrote for owners is extended to this persona. It is not a
  separate test suite that can be skipped.
- **No pricing engine, no payment.** Prices come from the price-level home that `RA-002` resolves, and
  refusal #18 stands. The portal shows the price the counter will charge, or none.
- **One `grid_identifier` per grid, still** (refusal §7 row 21). A portal tab that needs different columns
  gets a grid preference on the existing identifier, exactly as in `P5-08`.
- **The persona is not a 3PL client.** A trade customer owns no stock in the warehouse. Its scope is the
  documents it is party to, not an owner. Reusing `whb_owner_grants` for it would make the garage an
  owner of goods it has not bought.
