## Why this task has no migration

`whb_transformations`, `whb_transformation_inputs` and `whb_transformation_outputs` shipped at
**`V500035`** in v1 (`WHB-35`), and `wh_labour_tasks` ships at `V510214` in `P5-17`. This task is the
**recording at the moment of transformation** and the **pricing that reads the timed task** — both
behaviour, both on tables that already exist. **If the transformation tables are absent, that is a
`P0`/`P3-11` defect**, and there is no migration number allocated here to compensate.

## Scope

- **Lot and serial genealogy survives assembly**: which input lots and serials went into which output
  unit, **recorded at the moment of completion** — not reconstructed afterwards, because after the fact
  there is nothing to walk. *A recall that must cross a kit boundary has nothing to walk otherwise.*
- **Answerable in both directions** (`L-12`): forward — *where did this lot go, including into which
  kits, and where did those kits go* — and backward — *what went into this unit*.
- **VAS priced by the labour minute** reads `P5-17`'s timed work-order task: elapsed minus paused,
  multiplied by the rate-card line for the VAS charge code. **The same record is the cost side of
  `P6-07`'s client profitability**, which is why one record serves both and neither gets its own.
- The billable event is `work_order.completed` (or `task.completed` for the minute detail) on the outbox
  at the granularity `PC-42` fixed in v1.

## Traps

- **Recorded at the moment of transformation, or not at all.** This is the whole requirement. A kit
  completed without its input lots captured can never acquire them, and `P5-14`'s recall then stops at the
  kit boundary and reports a **partial list as complete** — the worst possible output of a recall.
- **A kit assembly is a balanced ledger transaction, never a status change** (`P3-11`). N input lines out,
  one output line in, conserving quantity per `(owner, item, lot, serial, duty status)` — `L-1`. A
  transformation that does not balance is refused by the service **first, with a field-level error**, and
  by the deferred constraint trigger as the backstop. *A trigger firing in production is an incident, not
  a validation.*
- **Disassembly is the same table in the other direction**, and its genealogy matters as much: one input
  unit, N output lots.
- **The cost side and the revenue side read one record.** If VAS pricing invents its own duration table,
  `P6-07`'s profitability compares two different numbers and the answer to *"which clients are we losing
  money on"* is not defensible.
- **Minutes are integers of seconds, never floats** (`OD-7` — no float or double anywhere in the stack,
  including frontend DTOs). The rate is `DECIMAL(19,6)`; the amount `DECIMAL(19,4)`.
- **`whb_transformations` is base; the pricing is 3PL.** The pricing reads the transformation through the
  outbox or a public read API — **it never joins across the module boundary in SQL**, and
  `Wh3plCouplingTest` asserts no `wh3_` → `whb_` FK beyond `wh3_clients.owner_id`.
- **Non-own stock is never valued** (`L-14`). A VAS performed on a client's bailed goods produces a
  **billable event** and **no inventory value change** — the two are independent and confusing them posts
  a client's stock to our balance sheet.
- The traceability report (WS-218) crossing kit boundaries will exceed export limits on a real recall;
  use the streaming path, not `BaseExportService`'s 10,000-row default (`PP-6`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] Completing a work order records every input lot and serial against every output unit, in the same transaction as the movement
- [ ] The forward query returns shipments, consignees, consuming work orders, **output lots** and on-hand remaining — in one query, crossing the kit boundary
- [ ] The backward query returns everything that went into a named output serial
- [ ] A transformation whose lines do not balance is refused by the service with a field-level error naming the line, before the trigger fires
- [ ] Disassembly records genealogy in the reverse direction
- [ ] A VAS work order prices from elapsed-minus-paused on `wh_labour_tasks`, and the **same** minutes are the cost input to `P6-07`
- [ ] A VAS on `owner_type != OWN` produces a billable event and **no** valuation change
- [ ] **Mobile:** input-lot scanning at work-order completion on the RF screens — genealogy captured at a desk after the fact is not captured at the moment of transformation (`D-13`)
