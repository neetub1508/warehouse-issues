## This is the highest-risk task in P2 — it is the number the customer signs

**State it plainly, because a builder who half-remembers `D-6` will build the wrong thing:**

> **Under `D-6`, whichever system is authoritative for quantity is authoritative for cost.**
> Warehouse is the system of record for every movement, position, count, adjustment and physical
> truth at full grain — **and therefore for cost**. **Warehouse runs the costing engine and owns
> `whb_cost_layers`.** Accounting is always the system of record for **the ledger**: it receives
> **already-valued** movements through its source-document port and posts them.
> **Accounting does not re-cost what warehouse hands it. A re-costing step on the receiving side is
> a defect, not a safeguard** (`FR-446` falsifier 2).
> **Warehouse never writes an `acc_*` table and never writes a journal.** It hands over one envelope
> per posting-relevant event and carries `handover_id` + **`posting_status`**
> (`NOT_APPLICABLE` · `PENDING` · `POSTED` · `REJECTED`) on its own movement.
> **A `REJECTED` handover is not a log line — it lands in a queue that has a name, an owner and an
> ageing clock** (`P2-18`, `P2-22`, `WH-SC-158`). Cost that nobody reconciles is cost nobody trusts.

`DECISIONS.md` **rewrote `D-6`** to say this, and it **overrules R5 `S-066`** (*"accounting computes
value"*). Two reasons: only warehouse holds the grain the costing methods need — owner, duty status,
lot, serial, location, and **FEFO and specific identification are not expressible at accounting's
item × godown × batch × serial grain** — and a **standalone** install (`D-7`) must produce an
inventory valuation with **no accounting module present**.

> **`IRREVERSIBLE.md` §7.3 item 1 is stale and must not be used to re-open this.** It records the
> cost-layer table placement as *"could not resolve"*, written before `D-6` was rewritten.
> `DATA-MODEL.md` §2.1 and §7.2 place `whb_cost_layers` in **`warehouse-base`** at `V500021`.
> That is settled. Filed in `DEFECTS-FOUND.md`.

**Cost is held as layers with a remaining quantity, plus a consumption table** (`FR-234`).
`whb_cost_layers`: `company_id`, **`owner_id`**, `item_id`, `warehouse_id`, `lot_id`, `serial_id`,
`duty_status`, `receipt_movement_line_id` (**a bare UUID**, §1.9), `receipt_occurred_at`,
`layer_date`, `quantity_in`, **`quantity_remaining`**, `unit_cost`, `layer_value`,
**`currency_code`**, **`exchange_rate`**, `cost_basis`, `is_open`.
`whb_cost_layer_consumptions`: `cost_layer_id`, `issue_movement_line_id` (bare), `issue_occurred_at`,
`quantity_consumed`, `unit_cost`, `value_consumed`, **`reversal_of_consumption_id`**.

**`duty_status` on the layer is a foreign key into registry 15** (`RL-001`). It is
`VARCHAR(40) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'DOMESTIC' REFERENCES whb_duty_statuses(code)`, in `P0-17`'s
`V500021`, matching the ledger line.
- Base seeds `DOMESTIC` only. `BONDED`, `MOOWR`, `SEZ`, `FTWZ` and `EXPORT_UNDER_BOND` are
  `warehouse-india`'s seed (`P4-07`).
- The engine never compares a duty status to a literal. It reads the registry's flags, such as
  `is_duty_paid`, so a jurisdiction's regimes never become code in the country-neutral core.

*A cost column on a balance cannot produce FIFO, cannot produce specific identification, cannot be
revalued without destroying history and cannot answer what the stock we shipped last March cost.*
**A sales return or credit note consumes in reverse and restores the original layer** — that is what
`reversal_of_consumption_id` is for, and it is `P2-12`'s restock path.

**Returns with no consumption to reverse, and returns to a vendor, have a rule** (`RJ-010`):
- **Matched return** — the original consumption is reversed, as above.
- **Unmatched return** — a blind return, or a return of stock issued before go-live, has no
  consumption to reverse. It creates a layer at **the site's current method cost at `occurred_at`**, as
  the valuation policy resolves it, with **`cost_basis = RETURN_UNMATCHED`**. WS-049 lists such layers
  by that basis, so they are reported, not buried (`WH-SC-318`, `P2-12`).
- **Return to vendor with an `origin_grn_id`** — relieves **that receipt's layer**, by specific
  identification. Without an origin, the site's method relieves it as for any issue (`P2-13`).

**Method resolution reads the item's `STOCKING` category at the movement's `occurred_at`**
(`RG-005`). An item's category is a dated assignment, `whb_item_category_assignments` (`P1-01`), and
`whb_valuation_policies` resolves against the `STOCKING` scheme only. Re-categorising an item
therefore changes the method for movements after the change, and **re-costs nothing already posted**.
Before round 4 a scalar `category_id` edit silently re-costed history.

**Methods: weighted average and FIFO in v1** (`FR-235`, `OD-6`), the layer table present, the method
**configurable per item category × site** through `whb_valuation_policies`
(`uk(company_id, category_id, warehouse_id, effective_from) NULLS NOT DISTINCT`, nullable =
"all").
**Specific identification for serial- and lot-tracked items is required here even though the
accounting set defers it** — vehicles, high-value electronics and any serialised spare are
non-interchangeable by definition.
**Standard cost with purchase-price and usage variances is v1.1 (`P3`).**
**LIFO is never built, and the product says why**: prohibited under Ind AS 2 / IAS 2 and ICDS II.
**The method dropdown must not offer it.**

**The valuation grain is declared, not emergent** (`FR-236`): **`(company, owner, item, site)`**.
Site-level makes an inter-site transfer a valuation event; company-level hides branch performance.
`IRR-40` is `PNR-3`: *changing the grain restates every historical balance, which is an accounting
event with a disclosure and audit consequence, not a migration.* The inter-site transfer rule is
written at the same time: **transfer at the sending site's cost**, so there is no profit in stock to
eliminate.

**The moving average after the movement is snapshotted on the movement row** (`FR-237`,
`IRR-39`) — `whb_stock_movement_lines.moving_average_after`. *A backdated receipt recomputes the
average; without the snapshot there is no evidence of what the cost was on 31 March and the year-end
valuation cannot be reproduced.*

**`currency_code` and `exchange_rate` on the layer from v1** (`FR-245`), defaulted to the install's
base currency. *Imported spares are bought in USD or EUR, and a layer without them can never record
what was actually paid.* `exchange_rate` is **`DECIMAL(19,8)` and it is the only column in the
warehouse set with that type.**

**`FR-446`'s two falsifiers are acceptance tests, not aspirations.**
1. **A standalone install with no accounting module produces an inventory valuation report that
   reconciles to the ledger and to a full rebuild** — `WH-SC-062`. *If the costing engine lived in a
   module that may not be installed, this cannot pass and `D-6` would be wrong.*
2. **On an install with accounting present, a handed-over movement's value is the value warehouse
   computed** — `WH-SC-157`, to the paisa, with no re-costing step on the receiving side.

**Screens.** WS-049 `whb_cost_layers` · `WAREHOUSE_COST_LAYER` · Customer reference — **read-only:
a layer is created and consumed by the ledger, never edited.** View drills into a `DataTable` of
`whb_cost_layer_consumptions` — *which layer fed which issue*, which is what a credit note needs.
Filter `hasRemaining` boolean defaults **true**. WS-050 `whb_valuation_policies` ·
`WAREHOUSE_VALUATION_POLICY` · Department reference — Add/Edit/End-date.

**Mobile.** `none` for both — valuation is a finance-desk surface. Stated as a decision.

## Traps

- **`C-027` is the concrete failure to avoid.** In `accessories`, valuation is **one moving-average
  number on the balance row** — no layers, no FIFO, no as-at date — and the reversal *"recomputes the
  average by subtracting the reversed receipt's own cost"* (`StockReceiptService.java:386-408`),
  **which is not the inverse of a weighted average**. Worse, the valuation report reads
  **`last_cost`, not `average_cost`** (`StockReportQueryService.java:543`), so the report and the
  balance disagree by construction.
- **`IRR-38` — two tables, not one.** *A layer without a consumption link cannot answer what an
  issue cost.* And *an AVCO-only v1 keeps no layer history, so switching to FIFO in v2 has nothing to
  build layers from* — the change silently begins at the switch date and the opening layer set is a
  guess no auditor will accept. Ship both tables even where the policy is AVCO.
- **`OD-6` must be closed before this task merges** (`IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §7). So must **`OD-1`** —
  the reciprocal edit to the `accounting` design set adding a **third install state**
  (*"a warehouse product is present and owns quantity"*). It is **that repository's edit to make**,
  it is cheap now because accounting P3 is unbuilt, and it is expensive once `acc_valuation_entries`
  has rows. **`OD-11`** must also be closed — it constrains `P2-17` and `P2-28`, and its trigger
  belongs to `P0-02`'s `V500030`, not here.
- **`WH-SC-162` is a build-time ratchet, not a code review.** An architecture test **fails the build**
  if any class under `ai.warehouse*` references an `acc_*` table name or imports an `ai.accounting*`
  type. Verify it exists and covers `warehouse-base` before this task closes.
- **`cost_basis` on the layer and on the envelope is what makes `L-14` enforceable.**
  **Non-own stock is never valued**: `owner_type != OWN` carries `cost_basis = ZERO_BAILMENT`,
  produces no valued envelope, and the valuation report **values only `HOUSE` stock**
  (`WH-SC-032`). *A 3PL that posts its clients' stock to its own balance sheet has a catastrophe in
  both directions.*
- **The layer's movement-line references are bare UUIDs, not FKs** (§1.9), because
  `whb_stock_movement_lines` is **partitioned by range** and a foreign key into a partitioned table
  is not what you want here. Index them.
- **Backdating recomputes forward and must do it inside the posting transaction**, not in a nightly
  job — the same discipline the accounting set records for its balance cache. Two reads of the same
  valuation an hour apart must not differ.
- **Rounding rule `R-2`: round once**, at the boundary where a computed value becomes a stored
  column; intermediate arithmetic runs at full `BigDecimal` precision. **Rule `R-4`: splits use
  largest remainder** — `floor` each provisional share, then distribute remaining minor units
  largest-fractional-remainder-first, ties broken by `line_no`, so `Σ parts == whole` always.
- **Precision, and the two normative tie-breaks.** `unit_cost`, `moving_average_after`,
  `old_unit_cost`, `new_unit_cost` **`DECIMAL(19,6)`** — **`unit_*` beats `value` and `*_amount`**;
  `layer_value`, `value_consumed` **`DECIMAL(19,4)`** (extended totals only);
  `quantity_in`, `quantity_remaining`, `quantity_consumed` `DECIMAL(18,4)`;
  `exchange_rate` **`DECIMAL(19,8)` — the currency-conversion type and nothing else**.
- **`T-13`** — there is no decimal library on the frontend. Every cost figure is computed in
  `BigDecimal` on the backend and sent as a formatted string (`FR-031`).
- **`T-3`** — do not expose a generated column on the entity.
  `accessories`' `quantity_available` is `@Column(insertable=false, updatable=false)` and is **stale
  in memory** after an in-session write (`StockLevel.java:66-68`). `quantity_remaining` is a real,
  service-maintained column.
- **`T-6`** — `WAREHOUSE_COST_LAYER` and `WAREHOUSE_VALUATION_POLICY` into `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`;
  `layerDateFrom`/`layerDateTo` and `effectiveFromFrom`/`effectiveFromTo` are **`dateOnly`** pairs
  (both are `DATE` columns), and **all four keys** must be listed.
