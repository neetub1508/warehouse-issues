## Scope

- **`V520014` — `whad_core_exchanges`** (dealer adapter sub-band). **Cores are inventory**: a `CORE`
  **item type** linked to the serviceable part, a core charge, a **core-bank location with its own
  valuation**, and a core transaction ledger with a due date and a status ladder from `CHARGED` through
  `RETURNED` to `CREDITED`, plus an **ageing job on uncredited cores**.
  *A parts business that cannot track cores loses real money, and no tier-1 WMS models it* (`F`/`T-082`).
- **`V521013` — `whas_warranty_holds`** (services adapter sub-band). **Warranty scrap-and-hold**: a part
  replaced under warranty moves to a **warranty-hold location tagged with the claim**, held until a
  retention date, with a **scheduled job flagging holds past the date for scrap approval** and a
  **permission required to release early**. *Scrapping it early forfeits the claim.*
- Both are **adapters**: they post through `POST /api/warehouse/movements`, own their own `whad_`/`whas_`
  tables, and **add no column to any base table**.

## Traps

- **The `CORE` item type is a catalogue row, not an enum.** `whb_item_types` carries `is_stocked`,
  `is_serial_default`, `is_lot_default`, `is_returnable_equipment`, `is_asset_shaped`, `is_value_only` and
  **no `CHECK`** (`D-10`). The adapter's own seed migration inserts the row; it never `ALTER`s a base
  vocabulary — that is on the adapter MUST-NOT list (`D-11`, `PC-72` assertion 2).
- **The core bank is a `whb_locations` row with its own valuation, not a flag on a bin.** A core is not
  worth the serviceable part; commingling their valuation is what makes core accounting wrong everywhere
  it is wrong.
- **An adapter may not write `whb_`/`wh_` tables directly, add a column to a base table, or be depended
  upon by base or app** (`D-11`). `WarehouseBaseCouplingTest` assertion 2 scans base-band migrations for
  the tokens `whad_` and `whas_` inside a `REFERENCES` clause; assertion 1 scans for the imports.
- **Two adapters, one shape.** `D-11`'s point is that *one adapter proves nothing about genericity*: if
  this task needs a base change that the other adapter does not want, **that is a base gap found now**
  and it goes back to `P0-14`/`warehouse-adapter-example`, not into `warehouse-base`.
- **Two dated obligations, two jobs.** The uncredited-core ageing job and the past-retention scrap-approval
  job both ship in this task (`FR-165`, §10 addition 4). A retention date with no job is a defect at merge.
- **The early-release permission is a real permission with a `permission_dependencies` row**, not a role
  check in a service. `permission_dependencies` is platform's (`V248:17-30`) — **`INSERT` only, never
  `CREATE TABLE`**; `dealer/…/V20501:10` is the vestigial second attempt (R1 `C-017`).
- **`whas_fitted_serials` and friends deliberately use `(vehicle_ref_type, vehicle_ref_id)` rather than a
  real FK** where the vertical's row may be archived independently (`DATA-MODEL.md` `G15`). Follow that
  shape for the claim reference rather than FK-ing into a `services` table that a retention policy may
  purge.
- Money `DECIMAL(19,4)`; the core charge is a per-unit figure ⇒ `DECIMAL(19,6)` (`OD-7`).
  `dueBefore` and `retentionUntil` are `dateOnly` filters.

## Acceptance
