## Scope

- **`wh_consignments`, `wh_manifests`, `wh_manifest_shipments`, `wh_handovers`, `wh_pickup_requests`**
  (`V510104`, `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.3 WH-94).
- **Three objects, not one** (`FR-194`), and the distinction is what lets a logistics module take
  over the transport leg later without touching the carrier manifest:
  - **Manifest** — the **carrier's** signed AWB list.
  - **Handover** — **our** record that N shipments physically left, with whom, at what time, against
    which signature, carrying the vehicle, driver, **seal number** and gate-pass reference.
  - **Pickup request** — the scheduled request that **brought the vehicle**, with its window.
- **Consignments** (`FR-193`, owned by `P2-10`; the grid ships here) — 1:1 with a shipment and
  optional, carrying `lrNumber`, `lrDate`, freight terms, declared value and the e-way bill
  reference.
- **`FR-195`'s gate-pass gate lives on WS-111 and is contributed by `warehouse-india`**, not owned
  here: the gate pass issues freely where no e-way bill is required and **blocks only where one is
  legally required and absent** (`WH-SC-210`). This task builds the handover and the hook; the India
  module registers the button.

## Traps

- **Collapsing the three into one is the failure this task exists to prevent.** A single "dispatch"
  object cannot answer *"which shipments did carrier X actually take on Tuesday"* separately from
  *"what did we tell carrier X we were giving them"* — and the difference between those two lists is
  every short-delivery claim.
- **The handover carries a signature and a receiver name.** *Our* record is only worth having if it
  says **who took it**; a handover with a carrier id and no `receivedByName` proves nothing.
- **`FR-195` — the gate pass blocks only where an e-way bill is legally required and absent.** Full
  decoupling would let e-way-mandatory goods leave the yard without one, which is a seizure at the
  first checkpoint (`WH-SC-210`, `P-038`). Full coupling would block every domestic sub-threshold
  movement. The hook must accept a **conditional** verdict from `warehouse-india`, not a boolean.
- **`warehouse` must not import `warehouse-india`.** The India module registers the gate-pass gate
  through the seam; `wh_handovers` gains no FK to a `whin_` table in either direction
  (`D-11`, `MODULE-INTEGRATION.md`).
- **`manifestDate` and `lrDate` are `DATE` columns and therefore `dateOnly` filters**, while
  `handedOverAt` and `requestedForAt` are `TIMESTAMPTZ` and therefore `date`. `filterUtils.ts:49-56`
  documents why: `date` shifts to UTC day boundaries and **moves the "from" bound back a calendar day
  for users east of UTC** — an LR dated 1 April disappearing from an April report is this bug.
- **Native-query timestamps come back as any of four Java types.** Copy
  `platform/.../service/UserActivityLogQueryService.java:288-307` verbatim into every
  `RepositoryCustomImpl` mapping `Object[]`; returning `null` for an unrecognised type is silent data
  loss that renders as a blank cell (`WH-SC-255`, R1 `T-4`).
- **A consignment is optional and 1:1 with a shipment.** Making it mandatory breaks every parcel
  shipment that has an AWB and no LR.
