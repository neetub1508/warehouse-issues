**Counter sale is keyboard-first** (`FR-359`): **scan or part number → quantity → price level →
print → next.** *A sub-ten-second bill*, with **trade price levels** and a cash ticket.
**One focused input, no mouse.** *The bar is set by the Indian retail incumbents, not by an ERP* —
and `COMPETITOR-BENCHMARK.md` lists Marg, GoFrugal and Busy as the incumbents whose muscle memory
this must not fight.

**Three trade-counter behaviours ride on it**, and each is a different task's substance surfacing
here:
- **supersession substitution** — `is_superseded_substitute`, `original_item_id` on the line
  (`P2-24`);
- **the interchange offer** — *"not in stock — 2 available as `<interchange>`"* with availability
  **across every branch** (`FR-073`, `P2-24`);
- **lost-sale capture straight into `wh_insufficient_stock_log`** when the guard refuses
  (`FR-257`, `P2-01`/`P2-15`).

**The adapter reserves and issues through the *public* API**, with `holder_system = ADAPTER_DEALER`
and its own document type — **not through an internal service call and not with a new identifier for
the holder** (`WH-SC-217`, `L-10`).

**Which site a counter may sell from — the serving-branch rule** (`FR-461`, `D-14` item 3,
`GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.2). `RK-002` is merged into `RG-001`. This task owns `FR-461`, so the whole
rule is written here once. `P2-02` carries its transfer path, and `P2-26` applies it to the job issue.
1. **Every stock-consuming document's tax registration is the fulfilling site's `REGISTERED` branch**
   at the document date, never the selling branch's.
2. **A document handed over at the requesting branch** — a counter sale, a job issue — posts directly
   from a site **only when both** of these hold:
   - the selling branch holds a current `REGISTERED` or `SERVING` link to that site; **and**
   - its `branches.gst_number` equals the `gst_number` of the site's `REGISTERED` branch at the sale
     date.

   The adapter compares `branches.gst_number` because it cannot read `whin_` (`D-1`). It reads the
   links through `warehouse-base`'s public API and never writes one (`D-11`).
3. **A `SERVING` branch under a different GSTIN draws by transfer, never by a direct sale.**
   - The counter refuses the sale with **`422 CROSS_GSTIN_COUNTER_SALE`** and offers *Raise
     request*: a `REQUESTED` transfer (`RK-001`, `P2-02`) from the shared site to a site `REGISTERED`
     to the selling branch.
   - That draw is a cross-GSTIN supply, with `is_taxable_supply = true`.
   - R22 row 6's *"Raise transfer"* became *Raise request* in `GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.1, so the clerk
     uses the one object.
4. **A demand order despatched to a third party** may be taken by any branch with a visibility link.
   It is billed from the site's `REGISTERED` GSTIN at despatch, and the ordering branch is a label
   (`P2-08`).
5. **Same GSTIN, different branch** — a `SERVING` issue under the same registration — is a
   non-supply challan, not a transfer invoice (`P2-IN-03`).

**On WS-194, the `branchId → warehouseId` cascade means *"linked to"*** (R22 §1.2.4 row 15).
- It offers the sites the selling branch holds a current `REGISTERED` or `SERVING` link to, with
  `REGISTERED` sites first.
- A different-GSTIN `SERVING` site is listed with its badge and refused at save, so the clerk sees why.
- A site with no link to the branch is not offered, and is refused with a field-level error on
  `warehouseId` if it is posted directly.

`whad_counter_sales.(branch_id, warehouse_id)` is the pair the rule validates. Both are recorded, so a
filed sale can always say which registration it used (`WH-SC-309`).

*Before round 4 the design had two failure modes and chose neither.* **Permissive:** a counter bills
goods that left another state from its own GSTIN. **Restrictive:** a satellite with no warehouse can
select none, and keeps a paper warehouse per counter.

**OEM part numbers are identifiers scoped by their counterparty** (`RL-005`).
- A scanned or typed OEM part number resolves through `whb_item_identifiers` rows whose
  `identifier_type` is the `IDENTIFIER_TYPE` code-list value for an OEM part number.
- Each row is keyed with the OEM's `counterparty_id`, never install-wide, because two OEMs
  legitimately reuse a number.
- The resolver order is session owner → counterparty context → `AMBIGUOUS_IDENTIFIER`. The counter
  renders that last case as a pick list of the candidates, not as a refusal.

**The counter-sale ladder** (`RJ-004`). It is derived from the flow above and uses §0.11's columns.

| Table | From | To | Verb | Actor (permission) | Guard | Terminal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `whad_counter_sales` | — | `OPEN` | Start a bill | `whad_counter_sales:create` | the `FR-461` pair rule passes for `(branch_id, warehouse_id)` | no |
| `whad_counter_sales` | `OPEN` | `OPEN` | Add a line | `whad_counter_sales:edit` | the item prices at the bill's level and date, then a HARD hold is taken under the holder quad | no |
| `whad_counter_sales` | `OPEN` | `OPEN` | Remove a line | `whad_counter_sales:edit` | the line's hold is released by holder quad and the remaining lines renumber 1..n | no |
| `whad_counter_sales` | `OPEN` | `COMPLETED` | Print and next | `whad_counter_sales:complete` | the tax evidence the mode demands, then the bill's holds released, then ONE `SALE_ISSUE` through the port | **yes** |
| `whad_counter_sales` | `OPEN` | `CANCELLED` | Cancel | `whad_counter_sales:cancel` | the bill's reservations released by holder quad; posts nothing | **yes** |

The two `OPEN → OPEN` rows are the verbs that change a bill without changing its state, and they belong in
the table because `whad_counter_sales:edit` is a separate grant from `:create` and from `:complete` — a
reader who cannot see them reads the ladder as three permissions when the screen needs five. **`:complete`
is its own permission, not `:create`**: completing issues stock through the port, which is not something
"may open a bill" should imply.

**Completion releases the bill's holds BEFORE it posts**, inside the one transaction. A counter line holds
its goods `HARD`, and a `HARD` hold hides that quantity from every document — including this one — so a
bill that posted first would be refused by its own hold at a site whose on-hand exactly covers the sale. A
port refusal rolls the release back with it, so nothing is ever exposed. **Stated deviation**: the hold
ends `RELEASED` under the default `HOLDER_CANCELLED` reason rather than `CONSUMED`, because warehouse-base
has no consume path yet (`RSV-T1-06`) and an adapter may not add one (§8.3 `B1`).

A return after `COMPLETED` is a return receipt (`P2-12`), never a state of the sale. The one verb the
flow lacked, `whad_counter_sales:cancel`, is seeded in the adapter's own band, `V520100`+ (`RJ-005`).

**Printing the ticket, v1** (`FR-359`, `FR-225`). `V520000` seeds `CASH_TICKET` into base's
`PRINT_TEMPLATE_KIND` list, but **warehouse-base ships that vocabulary with no template table and no
renderer behind it**, and `D-11`/§8.3 `B1` forbid an adapter building one. A print stack — template
rows, a render service, a format per `PRINT_FORMAT` — is **warehouse-base's own future task**, and it
is not in this task's scope, its migration band or its module.

So box 6's *printed ticket* is met in v1 by the **browser's print of the COMPLETED bill**: a
print-friendly cash-ticket window carrying the site and its `REGISTERED` GSTIN, the bill number, the
date and time, the lines with quantity / unit price / line total, the subtotal, the tax **as the
resolved `warehouse.tax.mode` reports it and never computed** (a NULL amount prints as "no tax
document recorded", never `0.00` — `OD-9`), the total and the payment mode. It goes up automatically
the instant *Complete* succeeds, so *print and next* costs no keystroke of its own, and a
keyboard-reachable **Print ticket** action on each `COMPLETED` row repeats it. **No print permission,
no template row and no PDF library are added** — the ticket prints what `whad_counter_sales:view`
already shows.

When the base print stack lands, `CASH_TICKET` is the kind its renderer resolves, and this screen
swaps the browser window for that call. The seed exists so the code's name is already claimed by this
module rather than colliding with base's later one.

**Core return** is part of `FR-358`'s v1 sentence; the **core-exchange object with the core bank,
deposit, deadline and grading is `WS-198`, v2**. v1 ships the movement path for a core coming back
as an ordinary return (`P2-12`), and says so.

## Traps

- **The package is a *sibling*, never `ai.warehouse.adapter.*`.** `@ComponentScan("ai.warehouse")`
  would load `ai.warehouse.adapter.dealer` **unconditionally, in every install, including ones where
  dealer is not built** (`D-1`). This is the exact trap the accounting round-4 review found and
  corrected. The package is **`ai.warehouseadapterdealer`**.
- **`T-16` — controllers under `ai.warehouseadapterdealer.controller…`** or
  `UserActivityTrackingAspect`'s pointcut (`:59`, `within(ai..controller..*)`) cannot see them.
  Module resolution takes **package segment 2, uppercased** (`:167-174`), so the activity log will
  render this module's name accordingly — check what it renders before shipping.
- **`ModuleImportSelector.java` is the gate that fails *silently*.** A module JAR on the classpath
  with no entry there loads **nothing** — the build succeeds and the module is inert
  (`R1 §2` row 8, `:52-55` constants, `:154-186` blocks). Verify with
  `docker logs platform-backend | grep "Module configuration found"`.
- **`FR-356` — idempotent seed or a redeploy takes the backend down.** `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`
  needs a real unique key to conflict on; a seed row with a `NULL` in the key **never conflicts**
  and duplicates on every re-run (`P-043`'s `T-M11` is exactly this defect, shipped). And a
  `DO NOTHING` that **does** collide is silent: a row that already exists with a different meaning
  is kept, and nothing says so. Use the guarded seed idiom (`RL-003`), which fails loudly.
- **`FR-461` moves with the counter sale** (`OD-9`). If the counter sale is re-planned out of this
  task, the serving-branch rule goes with it; it is not a base rule and must not land in `P1`.
- **`T-9` — menus.** `menus` has **no** unique constraint on `(name, parent_id, menu_level)`, so
  `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` does not stop a duplicate on a Flyway retry. `WHERE NOT EXISTS`, per
  `assets/…/V60171__…:154-155`.
- **`T-10` — `permission_dependencies` is a PLATFORM table.** Insert rows; **never** `CREATE TABLE`.
  Columns are `permission_id` and **`dependent_permission_id`**.
- **`D-3` — do not name anything `wms_*` or `scc_*`.** `platform/…/V528:37,57,59` and `V663:17-19`
  still carry hardcoded `wms_*`/`scc_*`/`warehouse-*` **permission exclusions** from a deleted
  earlier module. Those migrations have already run, so they grant a new module nothing — but a new
  `wms_`-prefixed permission would inherit a decision nobody took.
- **`T-12` — every module's migrations are flattened into one directory at build time**
  (`Dockerfile.backend:140-181`). A number outside `V520000`–`V520999` collides with another
  module and is a **startup failure**, not a merge conflict.
- **`T-11` — the frontend merge is last-write-wins across the whole `src/` tree.** `whad`-prefix
  every file; `/warehouse/dealer/*` is this module's exclusive route root.
- **`AF-2` — ship en / fr / hi.** *The newest module ships all three*
  (`accounting-base/frontend/src/i18n/locales/{en,fr,hi}/`); CLAUDE.md's *"every module ships `en`
  only"* is stale. Register in the adapter's own SafeTranslation file, and **never** import `t()`
  from `@platform/hooks/useSafeTranslation`.
- **The counter's sub-ten-second target is a performance requirement with a test.** The dominant
  cost is the availability call and the reservation; measure them, do not assume.
- **`R1 §2` — a new module is 16 files and ~40 edits across 2 build systems.** `P0-14` did the
  scaffold and the reference adapter; this task must not re-do it, and must not skip
  `startX2.sh` **silently** — accounting skipped it and `AF-4` records that as
  *optional-but-name-it*.
