## Scope

- **`V500033`** — `whb_reservations` + **`I-12`**, plus `whb_allocation_strategies` and
  `whb_allocation_strategy_rules`.
- **The holder quad is the whole point** (`FR-167`, `L-10`):
  `(holder_system, holder_document_type, holder_document_id, holder_line_no)` **plus `expires_at`**.
  With a counter you cannot say **who** holds a reservation, cannot release one order's holds, and
  cannot expire a stale one. `DELETE /reservations?holder=…` answers *"release everything trip X
  held"*, and that question is unanswerable against a `quantity_reserved` column.
- **Availability is computed, never stored** (`FR-168`):
  `available = Σ on_hand where status.is_allocatable − Σ open reservations`. The computation lives in
  `P0-03`'s writer service; this task supplies the open-item side of the subtraction.
- **`owner_id` is `NOT NULL` on the reservation** (`FR-107`) and participates in its uniqueness.
- **Allocation strategy as a configured row, not an `if`** — `whb_allocation_strategies`
  (`scope_type` ∈ GLOBAL/WAREHOUSE/ITEM_CATEGORY/OWNER/CHANNEL, `scope_ref`, `priority`) over
  `whb_allocation_strategy_rules` with a **whitelisted** `ordering_key`
  (FIFO/FEFO/LIFO/LOT_SPECIFIED/NEAREST_LOCATION/FEWEST_PICKS/ZONE_PRIORITY/HIGHEST_QUANTITY),
  `direction`, and whitelisted `filter_column`/`filter_operator`/`filter_value`. **Never a free
  expression language** — the fifth rule engine always introduces one.

## `whb_reservations` carries a `PNR-1`-class deadline of its own

`IRREVERSIBLE.md` §3.4: *"`whb_reservations` — **PNR-1** — the holder quad must exist before the
first reservation."* A reservation written before the quad exists cannot be attributed to a holder
afterwards, and the release-by-holder path has nothing to match on. **`DATA-MODEL.md` §2.1 is the
authority on the column set and must be re-read immediately before `V500033` is written**;
`IRR-45` is the row that binds it.

## Traps

- **There is no prior art. Reservations in `accessories` are schema-only.** `quantity_reserved` is
  read in **7** places and written in **0**; `StockLevel.reserveQuantity()` and
  `releaseReservedQuantity()` (`StockLevel.java:189-206`) have **zero call sites** (`C-022`). Budget
  this as net-new and do not let a reviewer point at the accessories column as a pattern.
- **A stored `quantity_reserved` column is the failure mode.** It drifts, and its drift is invisible
  until a picker finds nothing on the shelf. `FR-168` is explicit: availability is computed. The
  reconciliation job of `P0-03` writes `RESERVATION_DRIFT` and `ORPHANED_RESERVATION` findings for
  exactly this.
- **`expires_at` is a threshold column, so it needs its job in the same merge** (`FR-165`): a dated
  obligation with no actor is a defect **at the moment it is merged**, not when it is noticed. The
  reservation-expiry job is registered in `P1-20` and appears on **WS-064 Job Runs** (`P0-13`); the
  ageing report and the holder notification are `P2-07`. State the ownership here so it is not lost.
- **A partial unique index cannot be declared `DEFERRABLE` in PostgreSQL** — four files in this repo
  record it (`accounting-base/V600001:150`, `V600004:111`, `V600120:90`,
  `insurance-360/V120175:35`, `V120180:41`) and each names the consequence: **the write path owes an
  explicit `flush()`**. Any "one active reservation per holder line" index inherits that obligation.
- **The rule columns are whitelisted, not free text.** `ordering_key`, `filter_column` and
  `filter_operator` are bounded vocabularies; `filter_value` is a scalar. `FR-041` and
  `PORT-AND-ADAPTER-CONTRACT.md` §2.9 both forbid an expression, a script or a rule DSL anywhere in
  this product.
- **`WAREHOUSE_RESERVATION` must be added to `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`**, and `expiresFrom`/`expiresTo`
  are **two `date` keys** — there is no `daterange` type (`filterUtils.ts:30`).
- **`holderDocumentId` is a `VARCHAR`, not a UUID and not an FK.** The holder may be a document in a
  module that is not installed; the display resolver falls back (`FR-357`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] A reservation is a **row** with the full holder quad and an `expires_at`; there is no
      `quantity_reserved` counter column anywhere (`WH-SC-025`)
- [ ] `DELETE /reservations?holder_system=…&holder_document_id=…` releases exactly that document's
      holds and nothing else (`WH-SC-182`, `WH-SC-183`)
- [ ] `available` falls by the reserved quantity and **on-hand does not move** — a reservation is not
      a movement (`WH-SC-188`)
- [ ] A reservation against a non-allocatable stock status is refused (`WH-SC-188`)
- [ ] A reservation on a blocked location behaves per `FR-093`'s rule and is visible as such
      (`WH-SC-185`)
- [ ] The **Release** action ships in v1 with its reason code, and the reservation records the
      `strategyCode` and `ruleSequence` that chose the stock
- [ ] The reservation-expiry job is registered with `expires_at` in the same merge, and a
      `whb_job_runs` row proves it ran
