## Why this task exists

A dispute upheld at 00:30 IST on the 1st is dated the previous day and lands in last month's bill. For Indian clients the credit note date (GST tax-point) is wrong and the monthly statement does not match the client's records.

## Scope

Date uphold/reverse credits by the site's timezone day (client site; company timezone when the event has no site), consistent with snapshot days. Migrate nothing retroactively; apply to new credits. Close OPEN-2 in the contract.

## Acceptance

- Dispute upheld 2026-12-01 00:30 Asia/Kolkata -> credit dated 2026-12-01, included in December run.
- Same instant for a UTC site -> dated 2026-11-30.
- Run reconciliation (R-1..R-6) still balances across the month boundary.
