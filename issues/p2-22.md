Part of __P2__ · Module **`warehouse-base`** · Migrations **none** · Screens **none of its own** — `whb_inbound_messages` is `P0-08`'s and `whb_accounting_handovers` is `P0-12`'s; the queue is worked on **`WS-053` Port Monitor — Inbound Messages** and **`WS-054` Port Rejected Queue** (`P0-08`) and **`WS-052` Accounting Handover Queue** (`P0-12`)

## Scope

**One grid over two failure sources** (`FR-045`): **failed inbound messages** — `whb_inbound_messages`
with `status IN ('FAILED','RECEIVED')`, indexed for exactly that predicate — and **failed posting
events** — `whb_accounting_handovers` with `status IN ('PENDING','REJECTED')`, likewise indexed.
Plus a **reprocess action that is idempotent by construction** and **an alert when the queue is
non-empty beyond a threshold**.

**Idempotent by construction, not by care.** `L-9`: `(source_system, idempotency_key)` is unique and
**the key is never server-generated**. A reprocess re-submits the **stored original payload** under
**the same key**; a movement that already posted returns `200` with the **original** `movement_id`
and **original** `sequence_no` and posts nothing (`WH-SC-023`). *That is the whole design: a
reprocess button that constructs a new key is a duplicate-generation button.*

**The rejected-handover half is the queue `D-6` requires.** Under `D-6` warehouse computes cost and
hands over **valued** movements; a rejected handover is not a swallowed exception — the movement's
**`posting_status`** flips to **`REJECTED`** and the row lands here, **with an owner, a rejection
code, a rejection message and an ageing clock**, and it is worked (`P2-18`, `WH-SC-158`).
Cost that nobody reconciles is cost nobody trusts.

**An escalation path.** A threshold, a notification target and a stated owner. *An alert with no
recipient is a log line.*

**The dead-letter half is somebody else's and must not be merged into this one.** `PC` §4.7's
poison-message grid over **outbox deliveries** that exhausted retries is `P0-11`'s **`WS-058`**, v1.
This task's queue is the **inbound-message** and **accounting-handover** side. Link the two from each
other; do not build a third grid that unions all three, because the reprocess semantics differ —
an inbound message is re-posted under its original key, an outbox delivery is **replayed from a
cursor**.

**Mobile.** `none` — an integration-desk surface. Stated as a decision.

## Traps

- **`C-025` is the anti-pattern this task exists to prevent, and it is live.**
  `AccessoryIssuanceService.java:277-279` **swallows non-`BusinessException` failures and continues**
  (line reference refined from R1's `:275-278` by `COEXISTENCE.md` §8.1); the `BusinessException`
  branch immediately above at `:270-276` correctly re-throws, with a comment explaining why. The
  general `catch (Exception e)` is the one that produces a silent queue of nothing.
- **`FR-044` — the request is persisted *before* it is processed.** `whb_inbound_messages` holds the
  payload, the hash and the status **before** any validation runs; otherwise a failed post is a log
  line and there is nothing to reprocess (`WH-SC-180`).
- **The payload hash is over the canonical caller-supplied envelope, on the documented field set,
  *before* any server enrichment** (`PC` §3.3, `WH-SC-166`). A hash taken after enrichment differs
  between two client versions posting the same logical movement.
- **Reuse of a key with a *different* payload is `409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`, and the body names
  the original movement id** so the caller can diff the two (`WH-SC-165`). It is not silently
  accepted and it is not silently ignored.
- **The threshold alert must name its recipients as configuration.** `FR-165`'s rule: a dated or
  threshold obligation with no executor and no recipient is a defect at the moment it is merged.
- **`T-7` — no `statistics.*` cache name.** The queue's counts are filter-aware.
  `CacheConfiguration.java:190-196`, issues `#790`/`#791`.
- **`T-6` — the filter scopes for both grids** go into `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`; `receivedFrom`/
  `receivedTo` and `lastAttemptFrom`/`lastAttemptTo` are `date` pairs, both halves listed.
- **`payload` is `TEXT`, not `JSONB`** (§1.6). The grid renders it in a viewer, not as a parsed tree.
- **`T-16`** — the controller lives under `ai.warehousebase.controller…` or
  `UserActivityTrackingAspect`'s pointcut (`:59`) cannot see the reprocess action, and the one
  action in the product that most needs an audit trail has none.
