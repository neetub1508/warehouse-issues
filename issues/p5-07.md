## Scope

- **`V530050` — `wh3_sla_definitions`, `wh3_sla_measurements`, `wh3_sla_breaches`** (`W3-12`).
- **A definition** names a metric, a target, a comparison (`GTE`/`LTE`), a measurement window, a
  **calendar** (`P5-10`'s `wh_working_calendars`, by code), exclusion rules and a **penalty charge code**
  from `P5-02`.
- **A measurement stores the numerator and the denominator**, not just the percentage — *"every SLA
  conversation begins with 'which orders were in the denominator'"* (`F-075`). It also stores
  `sample_count` and `computed_at`.
- **A breach** is confirmed by a human, is waivable with a reason, and carries a nullable
  `billable_event_id` — **the column and the FK exist from v2 so `P6-07`'s automatic SLA credit is a
  service, not a migration.** No penalty is posted in this task.
- **The portal's performance tab** (WS-167 rendered inside WS-172) shows trend plus current period,
  **permission-filtered to the client's own owner**, with **drill-through to the failing orders** and a
  scheduled monthly export. *A measurement nobody can drill into is a number nobody believes.*

## Traps

- **The calendar is `P5-10`'s and is used by *the same code* that computes a promise.** Two calendars —
  one for promising, one for measuring — is how a customer is shown a breach the contract does not
  consider one (`FR-181`). Reference it by **code**, not by FK, so the object stays movable.
- **Storing only the percentage is the failure this requirement exists to prevent.** A recomputed
  denominator three months later will not match, and there is no way to prove which one was right.
- **No penalty is posted here.** `FR-299` is deferred to `P6-07` *"because until the measurements are
  trusted an automatic credit is a liability"* — but **the charge code and the foreign key exist from
  v2**, so ship `billable_event_id` and the `penalty_charge_code_id` FK now. Adding the column in v3 means
  every v2 breach has a null nobody can backfill.
- **Waiver is a permissioned act with a mandatory reason**, and the waiver author is never the breach
  confirmer where the penalty exceeds a threshold — the same separation-of-duties rule as `P5-05`'s
  accessorials.
- **Drill-through is an owner-scoped query, not a UI filter.** `P5-08`'s per-endpoint negative test covers
  these three grids; a drill-through that returns another client's failing orders is a contract breach.
- **Measurement windows are computed on a schedule and every threshold column ships with the job that
  reads it** (`FR-165`, `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §10 addition 4). A dated obligation with no actor is a
  defect **at merge**, not when it is noticed.
- `windowFromFrom`/`To` are two **`dateOnly`** filters; `isBreach` is a boolean filter. Register the three
  scopes in `COMMON_FILTER_CONFIGS`. The measurement and breach strips are filter-aware ⇒ **no
  `statistics.*` cache name** (R1 `C-043`).

## Acceptance
- [ ] A measurement row carries numerator, denominator and sample count, and the percentage is derived, never stored alone
- [ ] A 24-hour SLA measured across a holiday on the client's calendar does **not** report a breach, and the same calendar code drives the promise on the demand order
- [ ] `wh3_sla_breaches.billable_event_id` and `penalty_charge_code_id` exist and are nullable; **nothing posts a penalty in this task**
- [ ] A waiver requires a reason and records its author; above the threshold, the waiver author may not be the confirmer
- [ ] Drill-through from a measurement returns only the caller's own owner's orders; a request naming another client is `403`, **never an empty list**
- [ ] The measurement job runs on a schedule and its failure is visible — the job-run row is written whether it succeeds or not
- [ ] The portal performance tab renders trend plus current period and exports monthly on a schedule
- [ ] **Mobile:** `none` — declared (`D-13`)
- [ ] **Scenarios** the ids this task authors into `SCENARIO-CATALOGUE.md` §3.14
