## Scope

### Data model (`V562120`)
`whm_tools`, `whm_routing_operation_tools`, `whm_tool_life_entries`, `whm_maintenance_plans`, `whm_maintenance_orders` (DATA-MODEL §2.7.10); `whm_operation_confirmations` + `tool_id`.

### Rules
- **Tools** (FR-832, `SFL-T1-40`, `-41`, `SFL-GRD-10`, `-12`, `SFL-T4-05`): life unit `SHOTS` / `UNITS` / `HOURS`; routing operations list required tools with `life_per_unit`; a booking names the tool used and appends a life entry; booking refused when the tool is not available or would exceed `life_total` (`409 TOOL_UNAVAILABLE`); alert at `warn_at_percent`; `AT_JOB_WORKER` while on a job-work challan as capital goods.
- **Maintenance** (FR-833, `SFL-T1-42`…`-44`, `SFL-T3-01`, `SFL-T4-04`): plans by calendar days, run hours (from bookings on the machine) or shots (tool life); nightly job raises alerts for due plans; orders `PREVENTIVE` / `BREAKDOWN` with `MNT-` numbers; `IN_PROGRESS` takes the machine / tool out of service (bookings refused `409 MACHINE_UNAVAILABLE` / `TOOL_UNAVAILABLE`); completion records downtime minutes with a `DOWNTIME` reason, re-dates the plan, resets tool life when the plan says so; `maintenance.overdue_mode = BLOCK` refuses bookings on an overdue machine `409 MAINTENANCE_OVERDUE` (default `WARN`); spares go out by an ordinary core store issue with cost object `MACHINE`. Planned PM minutes are deducted in the capacity load (`p10-13`).
- No IoT, no OEE, no measuring-instrument calibration (core `p8-20`).
