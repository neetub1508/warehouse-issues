`P1-17` created `wh_transfer_orders` / `wh_transfer_order_lines` at `V510031` **in P1**, because the
two branch references, `is_taxable_supply`, `transfer_price`, `valuation_method` and
`ownership_transfer_point` must exist before the challan (`P2-IN-03`) does. **This task writes no
DDL.** Its two migrations are permission rows. `V511202` seeds the transfer verbs, and `V511232`
inserts their `permission_dependencies` rows (`WH-206`, `RJ-005`). It builds the workflow over
`P1-17`'s schema, plus the one report that makes the residue visible.

**Three legs, not two.**

1. **Depart** — posted by the shipment's dispatch, `wh_shipments:dispatch` (`RJ-003`): `−qty` at
   `SITE-A/STAGE-OUT-01`, where the pick staged it, / `+qty` at `SITE-A/IN_TRANSIT-<transfer number>`.
   The transit location is **per transfer** (`FR-085`), never one global `IN_TRANSIT` bucket, so two
   consignments on the road are separately countable and separately ageable. It is a **child of the
   source warehouse**, of location type `IN_TRANSIT`, created at dispatch (`FR-147`, `RJ-002`). It is
   never a location at a separate transit site.
2. **Arrive** — `−qty` from that transit location / `+qty` at `SITE-B/RECV-01`, posted by
   `wh_transfer_orders:receive`. A storekeeper scoped to SITE-B therefore posts against a location at
   SITE-A. `P1-18` authorises the receive verb and the transit-loss adjustment against **that
   transfer's** transit location, regardless of site scope, and against nothing else at the source
   site (`RJ-002`).
3. **The residue** — where shipped and received differ, an explicit **transit-loss adjustment**
   against the transit location with a mandatory reason code (`P2-01`'s document, `FR-164`'s
   value-destroying movement). **There is no field in which to record "received 15 of which 3 are
   missing"** — the arrival document accepts what physically arrived, and the difference stays in
   transit until somebody reason-codes it.

**The in-transit ownership policy is stated, not implied** (`FR-148`): **the sender bears the risk
and holds the in-transit stock until receipt.** That decision determines which site the in-transit
lines carry, and it changes valuation and branch reporting, which is why it is taken in v1 and
written down here rather than discovered by the first branch manager who asks whose stock it is.

**Transfer is at the sending site's cost** (`FR-236`, owned by `P2-16`) — so no profit sits in stock
and nothing needs eliminating. The transfer's **two numbers** — the transfer price (the tax
document) and the cost (what follows the goods) — are `FR-244`, owned by `P2-18`; this task
populates neither, it consumes the columns `P1-17` created.

**Bin-to-bin is an ordinary two-line movement** (`FR-150`) — same status, same owner at both ends,
no special document, no adjustment, no status change.

**"Delivery is not a movement" is true for a customer and false for our own branch** (`FR-342`).
Delivery to a customer posts **nothing**, because the stock left at despatch and despatch is the
only relief event (`FR-189`). Delivery to our own branch posts an **arrival**, and a partial
arrival is fewer lines. *An implementer who reads only the first rule builds inter-branch transfers
that never arrive.* Write that sentence into the service's javadoc.

**The transit location resolves its own registration** (`RJ-002`, R22 §1.2.4 row 13). A line at the
transit location sits at the source site. It therefore classifies under the source site's
`REGISTERED` link at its `occurred_at`, which is the transfer's frozen `source_branch_id`. In-transit
stock is held by that branch, never by a transit pool's registration. R22 §1.2.3's special rule for a
transit pool's administrative registration is no longer needed.

**Request and approve — the pull half** (`RK-001`, `FR-462`, `GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.3). Before round
4 only the sender could push a transfer. The branch that needed the stock phoned, and the request that
caused the transfer was recorded nowhere.
- **Request.** A user scoped to the **destination** site creates a transfer in `REQUESTED` with
  `wh_transfer_orders:request`. Both sites must hold a current `whb_warehouse_companies` link to one
  company, and the destination picker offers only sites sharing a current company link with the
  source — `P1-17`'s guard,
  `422 CROSS_COMPANY_TRANSFER` (`RK-007`).
- **Approve.** A holder of `wh_transfer_orders:approve` scoped to the **source** site approves,
  part-approves or rejects the request. `FR-408` applies: the approver is never the requester.
- **Part-approval is a line quantity, not a state.** The approver sets `approved_quantity` (`P1-17`'s
  line column) below the requested quantity, and the header goes to `APPROVED`. R25's
  `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` is not adopted.
- **Refused demand is still demand.** A rejection, or the unapproved remainder of a part-approval,
  carries a reason code and writes `wh_insufficient_stock_log` with `source_type = TRANSFER_REQUEST`
  (`P2-01`'s table).
- **`requires_approval` stays the switch for a transfer the sender raises.** Where it is set,
  `DRAFT → APPROVED` needs `:approve`.
- **One object, more producers.** A `TRANSFER` replenishment suggestion accepted at the destination
  creates a `REQUESTED` transfer (`P2-15`), and `P5-18`'s v2 sister-branch suggestion will create the
  same object.

**Reserved, picked and staged by the one demand model** (`RJ-003`, `FR-177`). Approval creates **one**
`wh_demand_orders` row of demand type `TRANSFER`. Where `requires_approval` is false, submitting a
`DRAFT` creates it instead.
- The order is linked by `wh_transfer_orders.demand_order_id`, which `P2-08`'s `V510040` adds by
  `ALTER`. `TRANSFER` is a `DEMAND_TYPE` code-list row (`RL-006`).
- **The stock is reserved from that moment.** Before round 4 nothing reserved a transfer between
  creation and dispatch, so a customer order could allocate the same units.
- Reservation, pick and staging are `P2-07` and `P2-09`. Dispatch is `wh_shipments:dispatch`
  (`P2-10`), posting `TRANSFER_DEPART` into the transfer's transit location instead of `VIRT-CUSTOMER`.
- **There is exactly one reservation path and one dispatch.** `FR-189`, restated: *dispatch is the
  only relief event; for a transfer the relief is into transit.*
- **WS-090 keeps Receive, Report variance, Cancel, the challan action and Print. It loses Pick and
  Dispatch**, which belong to the demand order and the shipment. §10.2's `wh_transfer_orders:dispatch`
  row therefore gates no action. Do not wire it to a second dispatch path.

**Which branch each end is, and whether the transfer is a supply** (`RG-001`, R22 §1.2.4 rows 4, 5
and 13).
- **Frozen at creation.** `source_branch_id` and `destination_branch_id` are snapshots taken when the
  transfer is created (`FR-305`), each its site's `REGISTERED` branch at that instant. `P1-17`'s
  `source_warehouse_branch_id` and `destination_warehouse_branch_id` record the two
  `whb_warehouse_branches` rows they came from, so the derivation is auditable.
  - They are frozen at creation, not at `dispatched_at` (`GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.7 a). `P1-05`
    refuses a registration change while a site holds stock under a different GSTIN, so a created
    transfer cannot go stale.
- **The supply test compares `branches.gst_number` at the two ends.**
  - Different: `is_taxable_supply = true`. The transfer is a cross-GSTIN supply, carrying a challan or
    tax invoice and, above the threshold, an e-way bill (`P2-IN-03`, `P2-IN-04`).
  - Equal: a non-supply challan.
- **A `SERVING` branch under another GSTIN draws through this transfer, never by a direct sale**
  (`FR-461`, `D-14` item 3).
  - The source is the shared site, whose `REGISTERED` branch becomes the source branch.
  - The destination is a site `REGISTERED` to the serving branch.
  - The counter refuses the direct sale and offers *Raise request*, which lands here (`P2-25`,
    `WH-SC-308`).

**A transfer cancelled in transit comes home** (`RJ-006`). Before round 4 Cancel was offered after
dispatch, but nothing brought the stock back.
- **`IN_TRANSIT → CANCELLED` posts `TRANSFER_RETURN`**, a base movement type `P0-04` seeds in
  `V500003`. It moves the stock from the transfer's transit location back to the source's receiving
  location, with a mandatory reason code.
- It is legal only while nothing has been received. Once any quantity has arrived, the residue is
  cleared by *Report variance*.
- Cancelling before dispatch cancels the demand order, which releases the reservation. Staged stock
  is de-staged, or the cancel is refused with `409 STAGED_STOCK` (`FR-449`, `P2-09`). `WH-SC-316`.

**`expected_arrival_date` has a job** (`RJ-012`, `FR-165`). Before round 4 the column fed a report
(WS-220) but had no job and no recipient.
- **The job.** It runs daily and is registered in `P0-13`'s dated-obligation register. It lists every
  `IN_TRANSIT` or `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED` transfer past its expected arrival date, writes a
  `whb_job_runs` record, and puts each one in WS-220's overdue bucket.
- **The recipients** are the destination site's receiving role and the source site's dispatcher.
- **The notification waits for v1.1.** Its category is `P3-16`'s, because `RH-007` makes `P2-15`'s the
  only v1 category. Until then the recipients read the overdue bucket. The job, the record and the
  bucket all ship in v1.

**The ladder** (`RJ-004`). It is R24 Appendix A with `RK-001`'s `REQUESTED`, adopted by
`GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.3, in §0.11's columns. `P1-17` carries the status vocabulary.

**The verbs** (`RJ-005`).
- `V511202` seeds `wh_transfer_orders:request`, `:approve`, `:reject`, `:report_variance` and
  `:cancel`, beside §10.2's existing `:dispatch` and `:receive`.
- `V511232` inserts their `permission_dependencies` rows. Each verb requires `wh_transfer_orders:view`,
  and approval never requires execution or the reverse (§10.2).

*A transition with no verb is a transition anybody with `:edit` can perform.* That is how
`requires_approval` and `approved_by` sat dead in the schema until round 4.

## Traps

- **`R1 §5.1 item 15`, corrected by `COEXISTENCE.md` §8.1.** The claim *"the goods are never in
  transit, so a two-step transfer is not modelled"* is **not quite right**: accessories **does**
  model the two-step transfer as a *document* (`StockTransferService.java:283-316`, `:322`). What
  does not exist is in-transit **stock** — between ship and receive the goods are on no balance row
  anywhere. That is the defect this task exists to avoid, and it is `E-032`'s *"transit as a
  status"*. Read the corrected finding, not R1's headline.
- **One movement that teleports is the failure mode.** The prior WMS art carried
  `from_warehouse_id/from_bin_id` **and** `to_warehouse_id/to_bin_id` on one row (R2 `T-001`).
  `D-4` forbids it. Two movements, four lines, a real location in between.
- **The transit location must be generated per transfer and must be a real `whb_locations` row**
  with `counts_as_on_hand = true`, **under the source warehouse** — it is countable (`FR-335`). It
  is **not** one of `FR-084`'s virtual locations; `IN_TRANSIT` in that seed list is the *location
  type*, and the per-reference instance is created by this workflow.
- **`TRANSIT-WH` is gone** (`RJ-002`). A pseudo-warehouse with no REGISTERED link has no GSTIN and
  no storekeeper; do not reintroduce one to "hold" in-transit stock. The destination's arrival is
  authorised on that transfer's transit location alone (`P1-18`).
- **Part-approval is a quantity, not a state** (`RK-001`). `approved_quantity` on the line carries
  it; a `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` status is the defect.
- **`demand_order_id` arrives by `ALTER` in `P2-08`'s `V510040`** (`RJ-003`). This task writes no
  DDL for it, and a second reservation path beside the demand order is the defect.
- **The supply classification is frozen at creation** (`GAP-REGISTER-R4.md` §3.7 a). A later edit
  to a site's links does not re-classify a transfer already raised.
- **`FR-085` per-reference transit locations are `P1-05`'s schema.** If `V500013`'s location-type
  registry did not ship `is_virtual_counterparty`/`counts_as_on_hand` behaviour flags, this task
  cannot proceed — raise it against `P1-05`, do not `ALTER` the base table (`D-11`).
- **`WS-090`'s "Generate delivery challan" button is registered by `warehouse-india`, not by
  `warehouse`** (`BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §3.2). Do not add a `whin_` dependency to `wh_` code; the
  action is contributed, and on a Mode-A install it is simply absent.
- **`T-18` — verify DB column names.** The transfer grid renders `sourceBranchName` /
  `destinationBranchName` from **`branches.branch_name`**, not `branches.name`.
- **`T-4` — the ageing report maps `Object[]` rows.** A `timestamp with time zone` can come back as
  `OffsetDateTime`, `Instant`, `java.sql.Timestamp` **or** `LocalDateTime`. Copy
  `platform/…/service/UserActivityLogQueryService.java:288-307` verbatim, including its warning on
  an unknown type; returning `null` is silent data loss and the cell renders blank.
- **`PP-7` — enforce a date-range filter on the ageing report.** `OFFSET` depth is not survivable at
  ledger scale; keyset paging is v1.1.
- **Replay hand-off from `P0-08` (`MPR-GRD-33`, `MPR-OPEN-20`, settled by default 2026-09-16).** The
  port's batch replay site check (`WhbPortReferenceResolver`) does not apply the `RJ-002` transit
  exception, so a submitter whose batch member posted through it would get `403` on retry. It is inert
  while `wh_transfer_orders:receive` is seeded nowhere; the replay site check must honour the exception
  **before** that permission is seeded.
