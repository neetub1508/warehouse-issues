## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Z-008` · `Z-009`.** **Two corrections to cutover.** (1) **Reverse on a
> `POSTED` opening batch is an `L-3` ledger reversal, not the import framework's row-delete.** It
> posts a mirrored `OPENING_BALANCE` movement per original through the single writer service with
> a mandatory reason code, unwinds the cost layers the batch created, moves the batch to
> `REVERSED`, and **reopens the `wh_cutover_checklists` row**, invalidating any certificate
> already issued against the reversed number. `P1-10`'s framework reversal applies to a batch in
> `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED` only, where nothing has posted — and `P1-10`'s acceptance criterion is
> amended to say so, so the two tasks do not test each other's behaviour. (2) **The checklist is
> seeded per master in dependency order**, not as a single *"masters loaded"* row: UoMs and
> registries → item categories → owners → items → identifiers and packaging → UoM conversions →
> counterparties and roles → warehouses → locations → item × site → item × location → number
> series. `WS-151` renders them in `sequence` and refuses to complete an item whose predecessor is
> still open.

**`V510090` — WH-80 — `wh_opening_stock_batches`, `wh_opening_stock_lines`,
`wh_cutover_checklists`, `wh_cutover_checklist_items`.**

**Opening stock is a first-class feature, not an import script** (`FR-411`). It carries quantity
**and cost and layers and lot and expiry and MRP and serial and bin and owner and duty status**;
it **stages into a batch with a status ladder** (`DRAFT` → `VALIDATED` → `POSTED` → `REVERSED`),
validates, **runs dry**, reports errors per row, applies, and is **re-runnable after a failed
attempt**. Design for **200,000 to 1,000,000 position rows**.

**It posts `OPENING_BALANCE` movements from the `VIRT-OPENING` virtual location** — never a direct
write to the position table (`FR-249`, `P2-18`). Each creates the item's **first cost layer** with
its unit cost, **so the first issue has a cost**. *Writing opening stock straight to the balance
table means the ledger does not explain it, the rebuild invariant fails on day one, and the first
issue has no cost.*
The whole batch is **reversible** through `P1-10`'s import-framework reversal path.

**Go-live produces a closing-value tie-out and a reconciliation certificate** (`FR-412`). The batch
carries `source_system_name`, `source_closing_value` (typed in from the incumbent) and
`posted_value`, and the tie-out computes `tie_out_variance`. **The certificate names both numbers
and the variance** — *that certificate is what makes the customer sign off the number.*

**A cut-over checklist screen** (`FR-413`) — masters loaded ✔ · mappings resolved ✔ · opening posted
✔ · **value matched** ✔ · period opened ✔ — with `is_blocking` items and a documented
**freeze-count-load-verify runbook**, including a **stock freeze window as an object**.
*The count that establishes opening stock happens on a Sunday and go-live is Monday; anything that
moves between is manual.* **The period cannot be opened while the value differs.**

**Screens.** WS-150 · `WAREHOUSE_OPENING_STOCK_BATCH` · **Service Vehicle** reference (state ladder +
`ImportButton`) — Upload · **Validate** (dry run, **persists nothing**) · **Post** · **Reverse** ·
Export errors; the line child grid carries `validationStatus` and `errorDetail` per row.
WS-151 · `WAREHOUSE_CUTOVER_CHECKLIST` · Service Vehicle — Complete item · **Certify** (blocked while
any `is_blocking` item is open; produces the certificate) · Reopen.

**Mobile.** `none` for both — go-live is an implementation-desk activity. Stated as a decision.

## Traps

- **`WH-SC-048`'s defect is the one that bites here.** *This codebase has met the opposite defect,
  where a validate endpoint persisted and the subsequent real import produced duplicate rows.*
  **Assert on the backend handler, not on a frontend dry-run flag**: call `validate` twice, then
  `apply` once, and assert row counts on the target table **and** on the import-batch row tables
  before and after. 38,000 rows, not 76,000 and not 114,000.
- **Ship it on `P1-10`'s handler-registry import framework**, not as a bespoke loader. The batch, the
  rows, the typed handler per import kind and the **reversal path** already exist at `V500046`.
- **`P2-16` must land first.** *Ship the costing engine late and the opening-stock task has no
  layers to write into, which is the one thing a go-live tie-out cannot fake*
  (`IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §4 `R6`). This task's dependency on `P2-16` is real, not nominal.
- **`WH-SC-193` names two implementation properties.** Do **not** call `findById` in a loop
  (CLAUDE.md CRITICAL #5) — batch-load with `findAllByIdsWithRelations()` — and do **not** paginate
  in Java memory. At 1,000,000 rows both are the difference between minutes and hours.
- **`IRR-51` is `PNR-3` and it lands next to this task.** The daily stock-position snapshot job
  (`whb_stock_position_snapshots`, `V500045`) **begins on the day it is switched on**; occupancy on
  each past day cannot be cheaply reconstructed. Switch it on **at go-live**, in this task's runbook,
  or storage billing, ageing, days-on-hand and obsolescence all start late.
- **The period-open gate is a real gate.** `wh_cutover_checklists.blockingOpenCount > 0` blocks
  certification, and certification blocks opening the first stock period. A soft warning here is how
  a customer goes live on an unreconciled number.
- **`as_at_date` is a `DATE`**, filtered **`dateOnly`**; so is `target_go_live_at`'s date part.
  `filterUtils.ts:49-56` is the reason.
- **`T-6`** — two scopes; `asAtFrom`/`asAtTo` and `targetGoLiveFrom`/`targetGoLiveTo` are **four**
  keys and all four are listed.
- **`certificate_document_id` points at a platform `documents` row** with `ON DELETE NO ACTION` —
  a certificate that vanishes with the file is not a certificate.
- **Precision.** `source_closing_value`, `posted_value`, `tie_out_variance` **`DECIMAL(19,4)`**;
  line `unit_cost` **`DECIMAL(19,6)`**; line quantity `DECIMAL(18,4)`.
