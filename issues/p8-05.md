## Scope

(`WhQualityInspectionSampleSize.java:35-38`); `FIXED_QUANTITY` and `PERCENTAGE` fall back to the whole lot
(`:22-24`, `:55`) because `wh_inspection_plans` has no column for the count or the percent
(`V510015:80-115`); the `aql` column is never used; the plan is chosen only from the item category
(`WhQualityInspectionRepositoryCustomImpl.java:342-354`). The client wants its own bands

### Behaviour
- `WhQualityInspectionSampleSize.propose` gains `FIXED_QUANTITY`, `PERCENTAGE`, `BAND_TABLE` (`IIS-T1-06`); the `AQL_TABLE` branch and its constants are **unchanged**.
- `WhQualityInspectionRepositoryCustomImpl.findDefaultInspectionPlanId` is replaced by `WhInspectionPlanResolver.resolve(itemId, supplierId, receiptDate)` (precedence `IIS-T1-05`, category fallback last). The GRN post uses it to decide `NO_INSPECTION`.
- Copy-on-write (`IIS-T1-03`) re-points every plan on the old version to the new row in the same transaction; **Deactivate** is refused while an active `BAND_TABLE` plan uses the scheme (`409 SAMPLING_SCHEME_IN_USE`, `IIS-T1-03a`, R29 F30).
- Complete proposes the verdict (`IIS-T1-08`; with `Re = Ac + 1` it is `ACCEPT_LOT` for ≤ Ac, else `REJECT_LOT`); Disposition pre-fills from it (`IIS-T1-09`); destructive quantity posts an `ISSUE` to `VIRT-CONSUMED` under **`source_document_type = QCINSP`** (the registered QC type, `WhQualityInspectionRepository.java:36`) with the QC department cost object stored on the inspection line (`IIS-T4-02`, R29 F11) — the cost-object columns on the inspection line come from `P8-09`'s seam; until `P8-09` lands the destructive field is hidden (feature-gated on the resolver bean).

- **Do not change the Z1.4 constants** — the client's bands are a new option, not a replacement (`STO-D6`).

- **Round up and cap at the lot** for every formula.
- **`NO_INSPECTION` is recorded, not absent** — the GRN line audit names the assignment.
- Switching rules / skip-lot dynamics are refused (FRD §12 row 4); `SKIP_LOT` keeps today's meaning.
- Date-only `effective_from/to` → `formatDateSync`.
