### Behaviour (`MRA-T4-01`, `-02`)
- GRN posting service: when the site requires MRN, an allocatable resolved received status becomes `MRN_PENDING` and `putawayTaskService.createForReceipt` is skipped.
- Disposition service: `RESTOCK_SELLABLE` on a `GOODS_RECEIPT` source targets `MRN_PENDING`, and `createForRelease` is skipped.
- Authorise: lock GRN; per line, find `MRN_PENDING` on-hand traceable to the GRN line (movement lineage `source_document_type` ∈ {`GRN`, `QUALITY_INSPECTION`} with the GRN line's lot/LPN/serials) and post `WhbStockLedgerWriter.changeStatus` (`:606`) `MRN_PENDING → AVAILABLE` with reason `MRN_AUTHORISED`, `source_document_type = MATERIAL_RECEIPT_NOTE`; then `createForRelease` per line; outbox `WH_MRN_AUTHORISED`.
- Receipt reversal refused while an MRN is `SUBMITTED` (`MRA-GRD-07`).
- Print: `SYS-MRN` (kind `MATERIAL_RECEIPT_NOTE` in `PRINT_TEMPLATE_KIND`) through `WarehousePrintDataProvider` — the MRN data contract in `WhPrintBuiltInDataProvider`; WS-077 gains **Print** on `SYS-GRN`.

## Traps
- **Setting off = today exactly** (`MRA-T4-04`); the `P1-13`/`P1-14`/`P1-15` suites must pass with the setting off without change.
- **Availability is by status, not a new flag** — `MRN_PENDING.is_allocatable = false` is the whole stock-availability rule; do not add a second check in the allocator.
- **Authoriser ≠ maker ≠ (default) inspector** — service checks against stored actors (`MRA-GRD-03`, `-04`).
- Quantities on the MRN line are not editable; a dispute is Reject → maker cancels → reversal / re-inspection.
- The status change happens wherever the stock is (receiving staging / QC area); no putaway before authorisation (`MRA-OPEN-02`).
- Store returns and production receipts never need an MRN (`MRA-UNR-04`).
