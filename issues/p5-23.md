## Scope — one register, not three

A short shipment, a damage-in-transit, a quality reject at QC, an obsolescence return and a price
difference are **the same object**: a claim against a supplier, for a value, arising from a document,
which is acknowledged, approved in part, settled by a credit note or a replacement, and which ages
until it is. Building one per source produces three registers that no one can total, and the number
the finance team asks for is exactly the total (`E-081`).

1. **`wh_supplier_claims`** — the header: number, supplier, claim type, the source document, the
   claimed value, the status ladder `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → ACKNOWLEDGED → APPROVED / PART_APPROVED /
   REJECTED → SETTLED`, plus `WITHDRAWN`, and the settlement — mode, reference, date.
2. **`wh_supplier_claim_lines`** — the item, quantity and value claimed, each traced to the **receipt
   line that evidences it**, so a claim can be defended without a spreadsheet.
3. **The ageing report** — claims by supplier by bucket. This is the whole commercial value of the
   register: an unsettled claim is money the business has already spent, and nobody currently has a
   number for it.
4. **`wh_obsolescence_returns` stays**, as the *authorisation* — the window, the allowance, the
   eligibility rules — and points at a claim here rather than carrying its own settlement columns.
5. **The wrong-item claim path (`Y-005`).** `P2-12` adds the `ITEM_MISMATCH` case and its three
   resolutions; **return-to-vendor is one of them**, and the claim raised from that return lands here
   as `QUALITY_REJECT` or `SHORT_SHIPMENT` depending on what the supplier agrees. This task provides
   the destination; it does not re-litigate the receipt rule.

## Scenarios closed

- A GRN short by four units raises a claim from the receipt screen, pre-filled from the receipt line.
- The supplier acknowledges, approves two of the four, and the claim settles as `PART_APPROVED` with a
  credit-note reference; the ageing report moves it out of the open bucket on the settlement date.
- A damaged carton found at QC raises a claim traced to the same receipt line as a quality reject, and
  both appear under one supplier total.
- An obsolescence authorisation is exercised and the resulting claim carries the authorisation
  reference, with no settlement columns on the authorisation.
- A claim is withdrawn; it leaves the ageing report and stays in the register.
- The ids for these are authored into `SCENARIO-CATALOGUE.md` in the same PR.

## Traps

- **The warehouse raises and ages the claim; accounting posts the credit.** `FR-274`'s rule holds —
  *there is no refund screen, no refund amount and no payment path in any warehouse module* — and a
  settlement here is a **reference to** the accounting document, never a posting. `settled_value` is
  what was agreed, not what was paid.
<!-- check-design-set: table-names begin wh_damage_claims — the counter-example this trap forbids: a second claim register nobody can total. Named so it is not built, never cited as a design object -->
- **One register, five claim types.** Adding `wh_damage_claims` later is the failure this task
  prevents; the type is a column and the type-specific fields belong on the line, not in a new table.
<!-- check-design-set: table-names end -->
- **Every line traces to a receipt line.** A claim with no evidencing document is a note, and the
  supplier will treat it as one.
- **Ageing is computed from `claim_date` and the settlement date**, not from `created_at`. A claim
  entered a fortnight late ages from when the event happened.
- **`claim_number` comes from `whb_number_series`** — gapless, module-scoped. A missing claim number is
  a commercial question, not just an audit one.
- **Do not fold `wh_supplier_returns` into this.** A supplier return is a *physical movement of goods*
  and already exists at `WS-084`; a claim is a *commercial position*. One can exist without the other
  in both directions — a short shipment has no return, and a return under warranty may carry no claim.
- **`PART_APPROVED` is a terminal-ish state that still ages.** The unapproved balance is either
  written off explicitly or stays open; leaving it implicit is how a claim register stops being
  totalled.
