## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Q-006`.** This task's acceptance was **entirely happy-path** until round 2
> measured it. `WH-SC-301` is the guard: **an inspection cannot be completed while a mandatory
> criterion on any line has no result** — `422` `INSPECTION_INCOMPLETE`, naming each unrecorded
> criterion **by line number and criterion code** — and **a completed inspection cannot be
> re-opened**; a corrected verdict is a **re-inspection with its own number** against the same
> GRN, so the first verdict stays on the record. Without the first guard the mixed-result rollup
> can silently mean *"we did not look"*; without the second, a supervisor can overwrite a `FAIL`
> and leave no trace that it happened.

> **Round-4 addition — `RJ-005` · `RJ-017` · `RJ-014` · `RG-018`.** **(1) A scrap disposition needs an
> approver** (`RJ-005`, `FR-164`): the scrap arm of **Disposition** stays pending until a holder of
> `wh_quality_inspections:approve` — seeded by `P1-20`'s `V511000`, `→ :view` in `V511001` — other than
> the disposer approves it (`FR-408`); the `SCRAP` movement is written at once with
> `approval_status = PENDING` and moves no stock until then (ratified below). Release, reject and
> return to supplier need no approval. **(2) Putaway follows release** (`RJ-017`): for stock received
> into a QC-held status, `P1-15`'s putaway task is created when **Disposition → release** posts, for
> the released quantity — never at GRN post; rejected and scrapped quantity gets no putaway task.
> **(3) One inspection series per GRN; a re-inspection takes its own number** (`RJ-014`, `FR-133`) —
> the reading that reconciles `FR-133` with `WH-SC-301`. **(4) The plan per item × supplier × site is
> v2** (`RG-018`): v1 keeps the category default; see Traps.

> **Ratified 2026-09-14 — `docs/contracts/receipt-qc-putaway.contract.md`** (`RQP-OPEN-01`, `-12`, `-13`,
> `-16`, `H3`, `H7`). **(1) The disposition codes are `whb_dispositions` rows**: release
> `RESTOCK_SELLABLE` (→ `AVAILABLE`), reject `REJECT` (→ `REJECTED`), return to supplier `RTV` (a status
> change to `BLOCKED`; the despatch `ISSUE` is `P2-13`'s), scrap `SCRAP`. **`REJECT` and the `REJECTED`
> stock status are not in `P0-05`'s applied `V500005`/`V500010` seed**, and `P0-05`'s merge-blocker rule
> refuses a code the seed lacks (`WH-SC-071`, `WH-SC-072`), so this task ships **`V500058`**: `REJECTED`
> in `whb_stock_statuses` with `QUARANTINE`'s behaviour flags, and `REJECT` in `whb_dispositions`
> (`STATUS_CHANGE` → `REJECTED`, `requires_inspection = true`) — idempotent and forward-only, never an
> edit of an applied migration (user decision 2026-09-14). **(2) Scrap** posts the `SCRAP` movement at
> once through the single writer; the seeded `SCRAP` type has `requires_approval = true`, so it lands
> `approval_status = PENDING` with no stock effect, found by `source_document_type`/`source_document_id`.
> WS-080 **Approve scrap** / **Reject scrap** (`wh_quality_inspections:approve`, approver ≠ disposer)
> approve or reject that movement; no approval column is added to `wh_quality_inspection_lines`
> (`MPR-OPEN-05`, settled 2026-09-14). **(3) Disposition requires a completed inspection** and at most the

> `PARTIAL`** — `X-055` is fixed and `FR-133`/`WH-SC-070` now agree. **(5) Web only** (`RB-001`).

## Traps
- **The mixed-result rollup value is stated twice in this set, differently, and this task carries one
  of the two.** `FR-133` and `WH-SC-070` say **`CONDITIONAL`**; `DATA-MODEL.md:979`,
  `BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md:1505` (WS-080's `result` column) and **this file, in three places** say
  **`PARTIAL`**. It is the value stored in `wh_quality_inspections.result`, so a half-applied choice
  is a data migration on a populated column. **Filed as `X-055`, fixed 2026-09-14: the value
  is `PARTIAL`** (`docs/contracts/receipt-qc-putaway.contract.md` `RQP-OPEN-01` — `DATA-MODEL.md` owns the
  stored value); `FR-133` and `WH-SC-070` were amended in that change, so all five sites now agree.

- **One inspection series per GRN, not one per GRN line; a re-inspection takes its own number**
  (`RJ-014`). `P-029` is explicit and the prior art's 72-table design got it wrong. N documents per receipt means N dispositions, N approvals and a
  rollup nobody can compute.
- **The rollup must be stated to the value.** `all pass → PASS`, `all fail → FAIL`, **anything mixed
  → `PARTIAL`**. Leaving it to the implementer produces "PASS if >90%" in one place and "PARTIAL if
  any fail" in another, and both look reasonable in review.
- **`inspection_criteria JSONB` and `sampling_plan JSONB` are the prior art's shape and are
  forbidden** (`FR-383`, `P-007`, `WMS_DATABASE_DESIGN.md:99` made JSONB a convention on eleven
  business tables). Criteria are **rows** in `wh_inspection_plan_criteria`; results are **typed rows**
  in `wh_quality_inspection_results`.
- **`sample_size_formula` is whitelisted, never a free expression.** `FR-041` and
  `PORT-AND-ADAPTER-CONTRACT.md` §2.9 forbid an expression, a script or a rule DSL anywhere in this
  product — *"the fifth rule engine always introduces one."*
- **Disposition is QA-role-gated with its own verb permission.** A transition with no verb permission
  is a transition anybody with `:edit` can perform, which is the failure `FR-408` names. Register
  `wh_quality_inspections:disposition` in `P1-20`'s `V511000` with its `→ :view` dependency row.
- **The disposition posts a movement; it does not update a position.** Rejected stock moves to the
  `REJECTED` status (the `REJECT` disposition, seeded by this task's `V500058`) by a **balanced
  two-line `STATUS_CHANGE` movement at the same location** (`FR-103`), through the single writer
  service (`FR-436`).
- **The inspector may not be the approver where the disposition needs approval** (`FR-408`); the
  reverse dependency row (`:disposition → :view` only, never `:view → :disposition`) must not be
  created.
- **`whb_item_categories.default_inspection_plan_id` is a *generic reference*, not an FK** — the plan
  lives in `warehouse` and **base may not FK into app** (`DATA-MODEL.md` §3.3, `P1-01`'s WS-022
  note). Resolve it in the service, not in the schema.
- **Do not grow the category default into a scope table here** (`RG-018`). The plan per item ×
  supplier × site is `wh_inspection_plan_assignments` (most-specific-first, the `whb_allocation_rules`
  shape) at **v2** in this task's own increment (`V510220`, below, folded from former `P5-24`); v1 ships the single
  `whb_item_categories.default_inspection_plan_id`, and that increment supersedes it.
- **Two grids × two platform files**, and `result` and `dispositionCode` are registry-backed
  selects that must **fetch** their values, never a TypeScript string union (`OD-5`, `FR-380`).
