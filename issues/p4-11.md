- **A second, tax-basis value carried alongside the book value** where the jurisdiction requires
  inventory to be valued **inclusive of duties and taxes actually paid** (`FR-250`).
- **Warehouse carries both bases because warehouse owns the cost** (`D-6`: *whichever system is
  authoritative for quantity is authoritative for cost*). With **one** unit cost per movement the
  adjustment is a spreadsheet forever.
- The valuation reports built in `P2-20`/`P2-21` gain a **basis** dimension — book and tax — that is
  selected, never summed together.

## The column question, and why this task writes no migration

`FR-250` is `base·india`, `v2`, `P4`, and `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §2.6 allocates it **no migration
number**. That is only correct if the tax basis is derivable from columns the ledger line already
carries. `IRREVERSIBLE.md` §4.2 lists what the line has: `unit_cost` `DECIMAL(19,6)`,
`extended_cost` `DECIMAL(19,4)`, **`cost_basis VARCHAR(30)` `NOT NULL`** with values
`ACTUAL` · `STANDARD` · `AVERAGE` · `INFORMATIONAL` · `ZERO_BAILMENT`, `moving_average_after` and
`cost_layer_id` — all **v1 columns**, all landing **before `V500030`**.

**So the first thing this task does is answer, in writing, one question:** *is the tax basis
derivable from `whb_cost_layers` plus the duty and tax facts captured on the receipt, or does it need
a second amount on the line?*

- **If derivable** — it is a computation and a report dimension, and `—` is the right migration.
- **If it needs a column on `whb_stock_movement_lines`** — **it cannot be added.** `V500030` sealed
  the ledger against `UPDATE` for every actor (**PNR-2**), and *a column added later is `NULL`
  forever on every pre-existing row*, with no backfill, support script or migration able to set it.
  In that case this task's real deliverable is a **finding against `P0-02`**, raised the moment it is
  known, and it must be raised **before** `P0-02` merges, not in P4.

**This task must not discover that in P4.** The question is answerable during P0 and the answer
belongs in `P0-02`'s review.

## Traps

- **`PNR-2` is the whole risk in this task.** `V500030` is the migration that sets the position
  unique key **and, in the same file, seals the ledger against `UPDATE` forever**
  (`DATA-MODEL.md` §7.7, `IRREVERSIBLE.md` §3.2). Anything this task needs on the ledger line is
  either already there or is permanently unavailable.
- **Never sum the two bases and never show a grand total across them.** They are two valuations of
  the same stock under two rules; adding them is meaningless and subtracting them is the adjustment,
  which is the number this feature exists to remove from a spreadsheet.
- **`L-14`: non-own stock is never valued.** A movement whose `owner_type != OWN` is handed over for
  quantity and custody reporting only; it has neither a book value nor a tax basis on our books. A
  3PL that posts its clients' stock to its own balance sheet has a catastrophe in both directions.
- **Warehouse never writes an `acc_*` table.** It hands over one envelope per posting-relevant event
  through accounting's existing source-document port and carries `handover_id` and `posting_status`
  on its own movement (`D-6`). **An architecture test fails the build if any class under
  `ai.warehouse*` references an `acc_*` table or an `ai.accounting*` type.** A second valuation basis
  does not change that.
- **`OD-1` is the reciprocal edit and it belongs to a different repository's design set.** `D-6`
  requires `accounting` to stand down `acc_stock_balances`, `acc_physical_stock_counts`,
  `acc_stock_journals`, `acc_cost_layers`' quantity grain and the port's `unit_cost` semantics when
  warehouse is installed. If that amendment has not been made, a second basis here produces a number
  accounting will independently recompute and disagree with.
- **`OD-6` decides what the book basis even is** — weighted average + FIFO in v1, standard cost with
  variances in v1.1, **LIFO never** (prohibited under Ind AS 2 / IAS 2). The tax basis is a second
  basis over whichever of those is configured, not a third method.
- **Precision.** Per-unit figures are `DECIMAL(19,6)`; extended values are `DECIMAL(19,4)`; any
  `*_percent` is `DECIMAL(9,6)`. `DECIMAL(19,8)` is the currency-conversion type and nothing else.
- **`OD-11`, if still open, applies here too**: a duty or landed-cost movement posts `quantity = 0`
  with a value, and `L-1`…`L-14` conserve **quantity only**.

## Acceptance
- [ ] The question *"is the tax basis derivable from v1 columns"* is answered in writing, with the columns named, **before any code is written**
- [ ] If it is not derivable, a finding is raised against `P0-02` and this task stops rather than adding an unbackfillable column
- [ ] A stock valuation can be produced on the **book** basis and on the **tax** basis for the same date and the same stock
- [ ] The two bases are never summed and no report shows a total across them
- [ ] Stock whose `owner_type != OWN` carries neither basis
- [ ] No class under `ai.warehouse*` references an `acc_*` table or an `ai.accounting*` type, asserted by the architecture test
- [ ] The tax basis is a second basis over the configured book method, not a third valuation method
- [ ] Every amount column follows the precision ladder
- [ ] **Scenarios** — none claimed; see the coverage note above

## Blocked on
**`OD-1`** — the reciprocal accounting edits. `D-6` requires the accounting set to add a **third
install state** (*"a warehouse product is present and owns quantity"*). Without it, a second
valuation basis produced here is a number accounting will recompute for itself. It is an edit to a
different repository's design set and is **that set's to make**.
