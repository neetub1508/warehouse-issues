## The requirement is a refusal with a measurement attached

`FR-228`: *"**Engineered labour standards are not built before a year of our own actual data.** We
measure actual task duration and report it; we do not claim to compute an engineered standard, and
**incentive pay is never computed from a warehouse metric.**"*

Two deliberate refusals sit behind it (`COMPETITOR-BENCHMARK.md` §7 rows 3 and 4): the labour-standards
engineering engine — *"engineered standards need industrial-engineering time studies per site"* — and
incentive pay — *"payroll consequences from a warehouse metric is a labour-relations product; a wrong
number is a legal problem, not a bug."* We lose to Manhattan LM here and it is **the right loss**:
Manhattan's labour management alone is larger than our whole v1.

## Scope

- **`V510301`** — the measurement rollup: per task type × warehouse × zone × period, the count, the
  total and mean elapsed-minus-paused duration, the distribution (p50 / p90), the units processed and the
  derived units-per-hour — computed by a scheduled job from `P5-17`'s `wh_labour_tasks`.
  **This table is new**: `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §2.9 divergence 4 carves `V510300`–`V510302` out of
  `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.3's correction reserve for the three v3 app tables. Add the row to §7.3 in the same
  PR.
- **Actual-versus-target reporting is explicitly *not* refused** (§7 row 3's re-entry path) — but there is
  **no target in this task**, because we have none to defend. What ships is the measurement, its
  distribution and its trend.
- **Cost-to-serve** reads the same rollup: minutes × a loaded rate, which is `P6-07`'s revenue-versus-cost
  input. One record, two consumers — the same discipline as `P5-19`.

## Traps

- **⚠ A year of data is a precondition, not a slogan.** `PNR-3`: durations cannot be backfilled. If
  `P5-17` shipped nine months ago, this task reports nine months and **says so on the report**; it does
  not annualise, extrapolate or fill.
- **The moment a `target_seconds` column exists, someone computes pay from it.** That is the whole reason
  the refusal is written down. If a customer asks for a target, the answer is *"here is your own p50"* —
  a percentile of their own measured data, labelled as such, not a standard.
- **Exclude paused seconds, and exclude nothing else.** A rollup that also quietly drops outliers is a
  standard by another name and cannot be defended in a labour-relations conversation.
- **The rollup must carry `owner_id`**, or `P6-07`'s client profitability cannot attribute cost and the
  answer to *"which clients are we losing money on"* is unavailable for the past.
- **A rollup table is a cache** — same rule as `L-4`. It must be rebuildable from `wh_labour_tasks`
  exactly, and a job must prove it. A rollup nobody can reproduce is a number nobody can defend.
- **Filter-aware statistics get no cache name** (R1 `C-043`, `CacheConfiguration.java:190-196`; issues
  classic#790, #791). Most warehouse strips are filter-aware and this one certainly is.
- Durations are integer seconds; units-per-hour is `DECIMAL(18,4)`; **no float or double anywhere in the
  stack, including frontend DTOs** (`OD-7`), and there is **no decimal library in any frontend**
  (R1 `C-013`) — compute on the backend, render strings.
- The rollup job is a scheduled job with a `whb_job_runs` row whether it succeeds or fails
  (`V500043`, `WHB-43`) — a job whose failure is invisible is a report that silently goes stale.
