### Rules
- **Trigger** (FR-813, `PUR-T1-10`): last-stage good of a `pdi_required` unit sets its `whm_unit_wip` row `AWAITING_PDI` (`pdi_attempt_no` + 1); MFG registers the `PDI_UNIT` source resolver (non-stock-bearing; `producedByUserIds` = the last stage's crew and poster).
- **Checklist**: the model's PDI plan (plan assignment stage `PDI` on the variant item, else the model item, else `pdi_checklist_plan_id`) plus the order's `whm_pdi_order_points`; 100 %; criterion **serials match** computed from the unit's bindings vs the BOM's serial-controlled lines.
- **Outcomes** (`PUR-T1-12`, `-13`, `-16`, `QRW-T1-23`): acted on only at the inspection's **result-final** point (core's once-only `onDisposition`, at Complete with result approval `NOT_REQUIRED` or at *Approve result*, `p8-19` `QIC-T4-02`; a `RETURNED` result keeps the record `OPEN`); `PASS`; `REWORK` → the unit `REWORK`, rework job cards at the named stage **and every later operation** (`QRW-T1-22`), the unit back to `AWAITING_PDI` with attempt n + 1 when the last completes; at the cycle limit (`QRW-GRD-20`) the exit is *Scrap unit* (`PUR-T1-07`); a `REWORK` records core outcome `REJECT` on the failed lines so core raises one NCR with source type `PDI_INSPECTION` (`NCR-T1-01`, `p8-17`); `HOLD` with a `PDI_HOLD` reason, released by the QC Manager. Inspector among the producers refused by core `403 INSPECTOR_IS_PRODUCER`.
- **Certificate** (FR-814, `PUR-T1-14`): *Submit for signature* opens one `p8-30` engine request of document type **`WHM_PDI`** (`WhApprovable` bean registered here; approve verb `whm_pdi_records:sign`, default policy `SINGLE_LEVEL`, `excludedActorIds` frozen = inspector, result recorders, the unit's producers; `ADMIN` refused too; kept code `403 PDI_SELF_SIGN`); the final approval is the Sign: it issues `PDC-` (gapless per site), renders `PDI_CERTIFICATE` to PDF stored as a platform document; re-PDI supersedes the previous certificate. Engine reject / withdraw leaves the record `PASSED`, unsigned, re-submittable.
- **Stock re-PDI** (FR-816, `PUR-T1-11`, `-15`): starting a stock PDI sets the unit's `VALID` certificate `SUSPENDED` — dispatch refused until a new one is signed; a stock `REWORK` posts `STATUS_CHANGE AVAILABLE → REJECTED` and creates a rework **order** (`QRW-T4-06`, `-07`), never a rework job card; the rework order's receipt needs a signed `PASS` of PDI attempt n + 1 against that order.
- **Receipt after PDI** (FR-815, `PUR-GRD-08`): `p10-07` accepts only signed-`PASS` units of a `pdi_required` item (`422 PDI_NOT_PASSED`), received in `AVAILABLE` — no second final QC, FR-644's `QC_HOLD` rule does not apply (`PRC-T4-20`).
- **Before dispatch from stock** (FR-816, `PUR-GRD-05`): `WhmMovementPostGuard` refuses an outbound shipment post of such a serial without a `VALID` (never `SUSPENDED`) certificate signed within `pdi_revalidate_days` (default 30 — **(default — client Q-Q2)**) and after the registry's `last_returned_at` — `409 PDI_REQUIRED_BEFORE_DISPATCH`; a stock PDI starts from WS-390 on an in-stock unit.

## Traps
- **PDI as a stock-bearing inspection** — the unit is WIP until the receipt; the resolver is non-stock-bearing.
- **The inspector signing** — signer ≠ inspector.
- **Editing a certificate** — never; re-PDI supersedes.
- **Dispatch check only in the UI** — it is the movement post guard (MD-35).
- **Signing on an unapproved result** — only the result-final callback counts.
- **Dispatching on last month's certificate after a failed re-PDI** — it is `SUSPENDED` from the moment the stock PDI starts.
