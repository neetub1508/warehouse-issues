## Scope

### Data model (`V562080`)
`whm_pdi_order_points`, `whm_pdi_records`, `whm_pdi_certificates` (DATA-MODEL §2.7.10, FR-813, FR-814).

### Rules
- **Trigger** (FR-813, `PUR-T1-10`): last-stage completion of a `pdi_required` unit sets it `AWAITING_PDI`; MFG registers the `PDI_UNIT` source resolver (non-stock-bearing; `producedByUserIds` = the last stage's crew and poster).
- **Checklist**: the model's PDI plan (plan assignment stage `PDI` on the variant item, else the model item, else `pdi_checklist_plan_id`) plus the order's `whm_pdi_order_points`; 100 %; criterion **serials match** computed from the unit's bindings vs the BOM's serial-controlled lines.
- **Outcomes** (`PUR-T1-12`, `-13`): `PASS`; `REWORK` → rework job card at the named stage (`QRW-T1-22`, cycle limit `QRW-GRD-20`); `HOLD` with a `PDI_HOLD` reason, released by the QC Manager. Inspector among the producers refused by core `403 INSPECTOR_IS_PRODUCER`.
- **Certificate** (FR-814, `PUR-T1-14`): *Sign* by `whm_pdi_records:sign` (QC Manager, ≠ inspector, `403 PDI_SELF_SIGN`) issues `PDC-` (gapless per site), renders `PDI_CERTIFICATE` to PDF stored as a platform document; re-PDI supersedes the previous certificate.
- **Receipt after PDI** (FR-815, `PUR-GRD-08`): `p10-07` accepts only signed-`PASS` units of a `pdi_required` item (`422 PDI_NOT_PASSED`), received allocatable — no second final QC.
- **Before dispatch from stock** (FR-816, `PUR-GRD-05`): `WhmMovementPostGuard` refuses an outbound shipment post of such a serial without a `VALID` certificate signed within `pdi_revalidate_days` (default 30 — **(default — client Q-Q2)**) and after the unit's last return — `409 PDI_REQUIRED_BEFORE_DISPATCH`; a stock PDI starts from WS-390 on an in-stock unit.
