### Rules

- **Two WIP models by supply method** (`FR-638`, `MD-05`): `ISSUE` → consumed at issue into `VIRT-PRODUCTION-<site>`;
  `STAGE_BACKFLUSH` → physical `LINE_SIDE`, consumed at receipt; `BACKFLUSH` → consumed at receipt from supply.
- **Pick-to-line** (`FR-639`): a staging list from open `STAGE_BACKFLUSH` (optionally `ISSUE`) requirements; each
  stage action posts **one `PRODUCTION_STAGE` movement** (idempotency `STAGING_LIST:<id>:<seq>`). Core has no
  reservation transfer, so in the same transaction the movement consumes the order's source hold, its remainder is
  re-created at the source and a **HARD** hold (whatever `component_reservation` says) is created at the line side —
  MD-36, R29 F16/F17; reservation ids are never stored, always queried by holder. *Unstage* posts
  `PRODUCTION_UNSTAGE` the same way. Line-side stock up to that HARD hold is never allocatable to another holder.
  **Open to stage** = required − (staged − unstaged) − (issued − returned) − backflushed outside the line side — one
  formula for generation and for `MSI-GRD-03` (R29 F11). Staging pick list prints lots and bins. Stale lists alert after
  `staging_stale_days` (scheduler, own actor).
- **Manual issue is the core store issue** (`FR-640`, `MD-04`): *Request materials* calls
  `WhMaterialRequisitionPort.create` with cost object `PRODUCTION_ORDER`, purpose `PRODUCTION`,
  envelope `PortEnvelope(warehouse-manufacturing, PRODUCTION_ORDER:<orderId>:MRQ_CREATE:<seq>, requester)` (FRD-STORES
  registry R.1/R.2; the requester holds `warehouse:orders:port`, granted to the supervisor bundle by `V561040`); stores posts a core store issue
  landing in `VIRT-PRODUCTION` (**SEAM-M1**). The MRQ **reserves nothing** because the resolver's `ownsReservations()` is true (R29 MFG-F12); its `reservationsFor`
  hands the issue the order's release reservations (MD-16; STORES `FR-531`/`FR-533` amended). Late issues and returns
  are accepted while the order is `COMPLETED` (MD-14). An MRQ shortfall reaching trade is core's
  `PurchaseRequisitionHandoff` (`p8-09`, `FR-528`) — MFG adds nothing to it.
- **Returns** (`FR-641`): issued stock by core store return (`p8-11`); staged by *Unstage*. Rollups
  `issued/returned/staged/unstaged_quantity` update **in the posting transaction** through the resolver hooks
  `assertIssuable`, `onIssuePosted`, `onReturnPosted` (**SEAM-M2**; no `onIssueReversed` — STORES `STO-D13`, MD-17).
- **Substitution** (`FR-642`): from the alternate group (priority order) or ad hoc with `PRODUCTION_SUBSTITUTION`
  reason and `whm_production_orders:substitute`; original open qty reduced, substitute line with
  `substitute_of_line_id`, reservation released and re-made, UoM re-resolved; under `STRICT` ad hoc needs a second user.
- **Consumption policy** (`FR-643`): deviation beyond `consumption_tolerance_percent` or an unplanned item —
  `STRICT` refuses (`422 ITEM_NOT_ON_ORDER`), `WARN` needs `PRODUCTION_DEVIATION`, `FLEXIBLE` free. Over-issue
  shows as a variance line on the order. Enforced in `assertIssuable` (SEAM-M2) and at backflush (`p10-07`).

## Traps
- **An MFG issue slip** duplicates `p8-10` (`RM-022`). Manual issue is core's document only.
- **Rollups updated after commit** drift from the ledger — they must be in the posting transaction (SEAM-M2).
- **Issue landing in `VIRT-CONSUMED`** expenses WIP — refused until SEAM-M1 lands; do not work around by a second movement.
- **Staging without reservation transfer** lets an outbound pick take line-side stock (`WH-SC-619`).
- One `PRODUCTION_STAGE` movement **per stage action**, never per line.
