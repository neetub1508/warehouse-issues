### WR3-01 · Keep production and QC scrap as saleable on-hand stock, not VIRT-SCRAP (major, PLANNED-WRONG)

> **Statutory content.** Sources are cited under Evidence; anything marked UNVERIFIED there, and every figure, must be confirmed against the law in force at build time.

#### Why this matters

A factory sells its turnings, offcuts and rejected parts by weight, on a tax invoice with e-way bill and TCS. CAS-6 credits the realisable value of normal scrap to material cost. If production scrap leaves stock to a virtual location with no value, the scrap yard holds goods the books say do not exist. The scrap sale then has no stock to issue from, and the statutory stock account shows scrap with no closing balance.

#### Scope

Add a scrap disposition per scrap reason: `SALEABLE` lands as on-hand stock with no allocation, either in a scrap item (with UoM conversion, e.g. 1 pump → 23.4 kg "MS scrap") or in the `SCRAP` status of the same item. `NON_SALEABLE` (wastage) keeps VIRT-SCRAP. Saleable scrap is valued at realisable value (scrap price list, or last sale rate), and that value is credited to the order pool before the co-product split. Scrap returned from a job worker uses the same path.

#### Acceptance

- A receipt with 5 scrapped pumps (reason MATERIAL_DEFECT, saleable, 23.4 kg each) leaves 117 kg of the "MS scrap" item on hand in the scrap store, valued at the scrap rate.
- The order pool is reduced by that value; the abnormal share is still reported separately.
- A scrap sale outbound (P4-04) can pick that stock; the Rule 56 account shows the scrap opening, receipt, supply and closing.
- PROCESS_LOSS (wastage) still goes to VIRT-SCRAP with no value.

#### Evidence (classic HEAD 4843b1648d, 2026-11-03)

CAS-6 https://icmai.in/upload/CASB/CAS-MATERIAL-COST.pdf ; CAS-4 https://www.icmai.in/upload/CASB/CAS_4_Revised_28022019.pdf. P4-04 (#32) says "Scrap is inventory, and its sale is a supply", with the `SCRAP` status `is_on_hand=true, is_valued=true`. PRD lines 206 and 355 say "Scrap → Scrap store" and "scrap sales (from Scrap store, with GST/TCS)". P10-02 line 24, by contrast: "production/QC scrap leaves stock to `VIRT-SCRAP` … the india-owned `SCRAP` status is never used". P10-07 line 55: `SCRAPPED` → `VIRT-SCRAP`.

#### Global setting

`warehouse.manufacturing.scrap.default_disposition` default=SALEABLE_SCRAP_ITEM alternatives=WASTAGE (R46 S1 F2: key renamed to the module convention; `SCRAP_STATUS_SAME_ITEM` dropped)

The default is the recommendation; the alternatives are selectable per install through the settings page.
