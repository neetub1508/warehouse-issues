## Scope

- **`V540120`** — `whin_job_work_registrations`, `whin_job_work_dispatch_lines`,
  `whin_itc04_returns`, `whin_itc04_lines` (`DATA-MODEL.md` §7.6 WIN-14).
- **Goods leave under a job-work challan to a location at the job worker's premises with the owner
  unchanged** (`FR-312`). The location is an ordinary `whb_locations` row; the owner does **not**
  change, because `L-11` makes ownership change an explicit movement type with its own reason code
  and this is not one.
- **The expected return date and the deemed-supply clock** ride on the **challan line**, and those
  columns shipped in **wave 1** with `P1-17`/`P2-IN-03`: `expected_return_date`,
  `deemed_supply_due_date`, `quantity_returned`, `closed_at`, `job_work_type` on
  `whin_delivery_challan_lines`. **The clock is measured from the challan date**, so a movement
  recorded before the challan object existed can **never** acquire one — that is why the challan is
  v1 and the return is v2.
- **A scheduled job ages open challan lines against the statutory clock and raises the obligation
  *before* it becomes a deemed supply** (`FR-165`: a threshold column with no scheduled job that
  reads it is a defect at the moment it is merged).
- **The ITC-04 return** — period, registration, quantities sent and received, the filed snapshot.
  Generate · Review · Mark filed · Export.

## Traps

<!-- check-design-set: table-names begin whin_itc04_runs whin_job_work_returns — INDIA-LOCALISATION-PACK.md §11.2 group A's names for objects DATA-MODEL.md §7.6 WIN-14 names differently. The trap records the divergence and states that the data model wins -->

- **There is one challan table.** `FR-313` and `WH-SC-207`: warehouse's delivery challan **is** the
  challan, with its own per-branch series from `whb_number_series`; the accounting set's parallel
  job-work challan is reduced to a view over it or deleted. **This task must not create a second
  challan object** — `whin_job_work_dispatch_lines` references the wave-1 challan line, it does not
  replace it.
- **The owner does not change.** `L-11`: ownership never changes silently, and a title transfer is an
  explicit movement type with its own reason code. Goods at a job worker are **ours** at **their**
  location, which is exactly the same mechanism as consignment and goods on approval (`WH-SC-276`).
- **The clock runs from the challan date, not the dispatch date and not the movement date.** They
  differ, and the difference is the days that decide whether a deemed supply has arisen.
- **The obligation is raised *before* it becomes a deemed supply, not after.** An ageing job that
  reports lines already past the window has reported a tax liability, not an obligation. The alert
  lead time is configuration, and the job needs its `whb_job_runs` row (WS-064).
- **`quantity_returned` is a running total on the challan line and a partial return is normal.** A
  job worker returning 60 of 100 leaves 40 on the clock; modelling return as a boolean loses the 40.
- **A filed return is a snapshot.** `whin_itc04_lines` stores what was filed, not a live query — a
  return that changes after filing is worse than no return. This is the same discipline the exit
  criterion demands of the Rule 56 account: **the same closed period must reproduce the same figures
  a quarter later**.
- **Every statutory date is a SQL `DATE` mapped to `LocalDate` with a `dateOnly` filter**
  (`FR-327`, `P-045`). The prior art typed licence and expiry dates as `TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE`
  mapped to `OffsetDateTime`, so *"expired today"* shifted across a day boundary. And
  `filterUtils.ts:49-56`: a `date` filter on a `DATE` column moves the "from" bound back a calendar
  day for users east of UTC — which for an Indian statutory register is every user.
- **No `whb_`/`wh_` table gains a foreign key to a `whin_` table** (`D-11`). The dispatch line
  references the movement by the source-document quad, not by FK.
- **`whin_itc04_returns` naming.** `INDIA-LOCALISATION-PACK.md` §11.2 group A calls these
  `whin_job_work_returns` / `whin_itc04_runs` / `whin_itc04_lines`; `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.6 WIN-14 and
  `BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §5.2 call them `whin_job_work_registrations` / `_dispatch_lines` /
  `whin_itc04_returns` / `_lines`. **`DATA-MODEL.md` wins on any table or column name**
  (`BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` authority note); the divergence is filed in DEFECTS-FOUND.
