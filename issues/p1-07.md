## Scope

> **Round-2 addition — `Y-006`.** **LPN split, merge and loss are three sentences and one seed,
> and the set has none of them.** A **split** is a movement whose lines move quantity from one LPN
> to a newly created LPN at the same location, conserving under `L-1`, permitted only while the
> source LPN is `OPEN`. A **merge** is the same in reverse, and sets `is_mixed_item`,
> `is_mixed_lot` and `is_mixed_owner` from the resulting contents. A **lost** LPN is a
> reason-coded write-off against the shrinkage virtual location that closes the LPN. Two
> `INTERNAL` movement types join `V500003`'s seed. The **execution** half — performing the split
> or merge as one movement with stored expanded lines — belongs to `P3-15`, which already owns
> that shape.

> **Round-4 addition — `RG-006` · `RG-007` · `RG-022` · `RJ-008` · `RJ-013`.** (1) **A lot names its
> parties by role.** `V500018` creates **`whb_lot_counterparties`** (`lot_id`, `counterparty_id`,
> `role_code → whb_counterparty_roles(code)` — registry 11 — `effective_from` defaulting to the receipt
> date, `effective_to`; one current row per `(lot, role)`) and seeds `MANUFACTURER`, `PACKER` and
> `IMPORTER` into registry 11 beside `SUPPLIER`; `whb_lots.counterparty_id` is dropped. The party list
> is a **RE-VERIFY** row in `INDIA-LOCALISATION-PACK.md` (`RG-006`). (2) **A serial carries any number
> of secondary identifiers.** `V500018` creates **`whb_serial_identifiers`** (`serial_id`,
> `identifier_type` an open string, `identifier_value`, `owner_id` denormalised;
> `uk(owner_id, identifier_type, identifier_value)`, **never global**); `whb_serials.secondary_serial`
> is dropped, and `P1-02`'s scan resolver reads the table (`RG-007`). (3) **`whb_lpns.owner_id` stays
> `NOT NULL` and means the custodian/label owner** — the house owner on an `is_mixed_owner` pallet;
> owner reporting reads positions, never this column (`RG-022`). (4) **A lot hold is only a `wh_holds`
> row** (`hold_scope = LOT`, `P2-03`). `whb_lots.status_code` carries lifecycle states set by jobs
> (e.g. `EXPIRED`) and is never a hold; `hold_reason_code_id` is dropped; a status-change movement is
> for physical segregation only (`RJ-008`). (5) **`WH-SC-135` is split**: this task keeps the trace
> across shipments and locations, and the trace through a kit moves to `P3-11`/`P5-19` (`RJ-013`).

- **`V500018`** — `whb_lots`, `whb_serials`, `whb_lpns`.
- **`V500035`** — `whb_transformations`, `whb_transformation_inputs`, `whb_transformation_outputs`.
- **A lot is an entity, keyed `uk(owner_id, item_id, lot_code)`, with codes normalised on write**
  (`FR-094`). *On a `VARCHAR` you cannot hold a lot across every location at once.*
- **Best-before and use-by are separate columns with different despatch rules** (`FR-095`) — never one
  "expiry". Plus `manufacture_date`, `expiry_date`, `retest_date`, `receipt_date`.
- **A lot has its own lifecycle status, independent of item and location** (`FR-096`), and **a whole
  lot is held everywhere at once without moving anything** — by a `wh_holds` row with
  `hold_scope = LOT`, never by `whb_lots.status_code` (`RJ-008`).
- **A serial is an entity** with current location, status, owner, LPN and lot, keyed
  `uk(owner_id, item_id, serial_number)` — **never globally unique** (`FR-097`), because two owners
  legitimately hold the same manufacturer serial.
- **Serial control and lot control are *modes* on the item, not a mode string read in isolation**
  (`FR-098`): serial ∈ `NONE`/`RECEIPT`/`SHIP`/`FULL`, lot ∈ `NONE`/`OPTIONAL`/`REQUIRED`, expiry a
  policy.
- **The LPN is a ledger object** (`FR-100`): code, **`sscc`**, type, **`parent_lpn_id`**, current
  location, status, mixed-item/lot/owner flags, gross weight, dimensions, and **`received_at`**.
- **Duplicate serial / LPN / lot detection at receipt** (`FR-106`), with a **site-level duplicate
  warning where the scope permits the duplicate**.
- **Genealogy recorded at the moment of transformation** (`FR-105`) — a kit, repack, decant, split or
  merge writes its inputs and outputs — **answerable in both directions** and **never reconstructed**.
- **MRP, net content, country of origin and pack month/year live on the lot**, with item defaults
  (`FR-320`, `P1-03`). **This is where `OD-10`'s recommended answer lands.**

## `whb_lpns.received_at` is `PNR-3`, and it is the anchor for money

★ **3PL anniversary storage billing is defined over `received_at` and cannot be computed for a past
the ledger never recorded** (`IRREVERSIBLE.md` §4.6, `IRR-15`). The feature is v2; the column is v1.

## Traps

- **A globally unique serial is the defect, and it is unrecoverable in a direction people miss.** Two
  owners legitimately hold the same manufacturer serial; a global unique **rejects** the second
  receipt, so **the rows are never written and there is nothing to migrate from later**
  (`FR-097`, `IRREVERSIBLE.md` §3.4). The modal's duplicate check must be **scoped**, and a
  site-level duplicate **warning** is shown where the scope permits the duplicate (`FR-106`).
- **Lots and serials in `accessories` are `VARCHAR` columns on the balance row**, part of a composite
  index — no lot entity, no serial entity, no genealogy, no uniqueness (`V30130:17-19`, `:39-46`,
  `C-028`). There is no prior art to port.
- **Three expiry columns, not one.** `expiry_date`, `best_before_date` and `use_by_date` have
  **different despatch rules** (`FR-095`); collapsing them means a best-before item is despatched
  under a use-by rule or blocked under one it does not have.
- **Genealogy is recorded at the moment of transformation, never reconstructed** (`FR-105`). A split
  that writes only the resulting lots leaves "what went into this unit" unanswerable, and no
  after-the-fact query recovers it. `whb_transformation_inputs`/`_outputs` are written in the same
  transaction as the movement.
- **`received_at` on the LPN is money.** Anniversary storage billing has **no other anchor**
  (`IRR-15`). It is a v1 column with a v2 consumer.
- **`expiryFrom`/`expiryTo` are `dateOnly`** — `expiry_date` is a SQL `DATE` (`FR-327`) and
  `filterUtils.ts:49-56` documents that `date` moves the lower bound back a calendar day east of UTC.
  An expiry filter that silently includes the previous day is a recall that misses a batch.
- **`expiringWithinDays` is a *dropdown* on purpose**, so the mobile screen can carry the same filter
  within `ListHeader`'s dropdown-only constraint (`mobile/…/ListHeader.tsx:210-218`, `C-044`).
- **`whb_lot_counterparties.counterparty_id` FKs to `whb_counterparties` (`V500011`, `P1-08`)** —
  `whb_lots.counterparty_id` is dropped (`RG-006`). That ordering is fine (`V500018` > `V500011`),
  unlike `whb_owners`' (see `DEFECTS-FOUND.md`).
- **`owner_id` is `NOT NULL` on all three tables** (`FR-107`) and participates in the lot and serial
  unique keys.
- **Lot and serial ownership are identity — KEEP-SCALAR** (`RG-023`). `uk(owner_id, item_id,
  lot_code)` and `uk(owner_id, item_id, serial_number)` make the owner a member of the key (`I-19`); a
  unit two owners claim is two identities, never one row with two owners.
- **`whb_lpns.owner_id` is not the owner of a mixed pallet's contents** (`RG-022`). It is the
  custodian/label owner; a junction would be a second truth beside the positions (the
  `whb_lpn_contents` argument), so owner stock is always read from positions.
- **A hold written to `whb_lots` is the mechanism `RJ-008` removed.** The allocator reads `wh_holds`
  only; a job-set lifecycle state such as `EXPIRED` is not a hold and is not released by *Release lot*.
- **`Move LPN` expands to one movement whose lines the service derives from the LPN's current
  contents and *stores*** — never leaves them implicit (`FR-101`, expansion in `P3-15`). An implicit
  move is a movement whose lines cannot be reconstructed.
- **Four grids × two platform files**; `dropdown.whbLot` in `CacheConfiguration.java`.
