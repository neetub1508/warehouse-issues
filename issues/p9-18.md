## Traps
- Recoverable IGST never enters stock cost (Ind AS 2); only BCD, SWS, anti-dumping, safeguard and clearing charges do.
- The customs rate is a document fact, not an FX table — trade still converts nothing else (`TRD-06`).
- Never post a movement: the landed cost is core's posting through the port; a consumed layer is corrected by revaluation.
