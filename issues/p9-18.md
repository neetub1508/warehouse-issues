## Scope
- PO header (`V552030` alters `wht_purchase_orders`): `supply_origin` (`DOMESTIC` default / `IMPORT`, immutable after submit),
  `incoterm_code` (code list `trade_incoterm`, p9-02 seed), `port_of_loading`, `port_of_discharge`; foreign currency allowed (already a
  header field); `TPO-GRD-24`.
- Tables (`V552030`): **`wht_bills_of_entry`** (company, `boe_no`, `boe_date`, `port_code`, `boe_type` `HOME_CONSUMPTION` /
  `WAREHOUSING` / `EX_BOND`, `customs_exchange_rate DECIMAL(19,8)`, currency, `customs_broker_counterparty_id`, clearing charges,
  `status`, `filed_at`, `costed_at`; uk(company, port_code, boe_no, boe_date)), **`wht_bill_of_entry_lines`** (BoE, `po_line_id`,
  quantity, foreign value, freight, insurance, `assessable_value` in company currency — the duty base),
  **`wht_bill_of_entry_line_receipts`** (BoE line, `grn_line_id` (core GRN line, bare UUID — no FK up into core documents beyond
  the port's reference), `quantity DECIMAL(18,4)`, `assessable_value DECIMAL(19,4)` share, `landed_cost_ref`, `costed_at`;
  uk(BoE line, GRN line); Σ quantity ≤ BoE line quantity — R32 F-R32TR-015),
  **`wht_bill_of_entry_duties`** (BoE line, `duty_head_code` from `trade_customs_duty_head` — `BCD`, `SWS`, `IGST_IMPORT`,
  `COMP_CESS`, `ANTI_DUMPING`, `SAFEGUARD` — `rate_percent`, `amount`, `is_recoverable`).
- Lifecycle `DRAFT` → `FILED` → `PARTLY_COSTED` → `COSTED` → `REVERSED` (`TPO-T1-24`; `409 TRADE_BOE_DUPLICATE`,
  `422 TRADE_BOE_EXCHANGE_RATE_REQUIRED`); `COSTED` only once every BoE line is fully allocated and every allocation costed.
- Valuation at receipt: trade implements core's optional SPI `WhReceiptExchangeRateProvider` (`CN-15` (a), declared by core
  **`P8-26`**): `openBoeLines(poLineId)` lists filed BoE lines with unallocated quantity for the GRN line's BoE picker;
  `valuationFor(poLineId, boeLineRef, quantity)` returns the BoE line, quantity, customs rate and base-currency unit value and
  records the allocation row; core refuses a GRN line without one (`422 RECEIPT_EXCHANGE_RATE_REQUIRED`) or above the BoE line's
  unallocated quantity (`422 TRADE_BOE_LINE_OVER_ALLOCATED {boeLineId, open}`).
- **Each cost component enters once** (FR-768, R32 F-R32TR-016): unit value = PO price × customs rate; assessable value is the duty
  base only; actual freight / insurance enter by landed cost under `EXW` / `FCA` / `FAS` / `FOB` (forwarder / insurer document or
  BoE clearing charges), and nothing is added under `CFR` / `CIF` / `CPT` / `CIP` / `DAP` / `DPU` / `DDP`. The payable is
  accounting's at its Ind AS 21 transaction rate; the difference to the customs rate is accounting's exchange difference.
- Landed cost (FR-770, `TPO-T4-17`): *Apply duties* (auto on `goods_receipt.posted` when `warehouse.trade.import.auto_cost_on_grn` = true)
  posts, per receipt allocation, its quantity share of the non-recoverable duties + clearing charges through
  `WhLandedCostPort.applyReceiptCharge` (`CN-15` (b)) onto that GRN line (partial costing; BoE `PARTLY_COSTED` until complete),
  key `WHT_BILL_OF_ENTRY:<id>:BOE_LANDED_COST_APPLY:<seq>`; reversal `reverseReceiptCharge`; recoverable IGST / cess →
  `BILL_OF_ENTRY` proposal through p9-20's table.
- WS-317 (Service Vehicle reference: list + filters + transition modals *File*, *Apply duties*, *Reverse*), grid + filters + export
  (`V552031`); permissions `wht_bills_of_entry:{view,create,edit,file,cost,reverse,export}` with `wh_landed_cost_documents:apply` +
  `:view:branch` on the bundle that costs (port principal rule, FR-617); setting `warehouse.trade.import.auto_cost_on_grn` (true).

## Traps
- Recoverable IGST never enters stock cost (Ind AS 2); only BCD, SWS, anti-dumping, safeguard and clearing charges do.
- The customs rate is a document fact, not an FX table — trade still converts nothing else (`TRD-06`).
- Never post a movement: the landed cost is core's posting through the port; a consumed layer is corrected by revaluation.

## Acceptance
- [ ] `WH-SC-760` end to end: filed BoE, GRN valued at PO price × customs rate (CIF PO: nothing added), landed cost of BCD + SWS on the GRN line, IGST to accounting, BoE `COSTED`
- [ ] One BoE line received across two GRNs: two allocation rows, BoE `PARTLY_COSTED` after the first, `COSTED` after the second; one PO line under two BoEs: each GRN line names its BoE line and takes that BoE's rate
- [ ] A `FOB` PO with a forwarder freight bill: freight enters stock cost once (landed cost), never through the receipt value
- [ ] A GRN line of an import PO without a filed BoE is refused by core
- [ ] Reversal after part of the lot was issued is refused `409 LANDED_COST_LAYER_CONSUMED` and nothing changes
