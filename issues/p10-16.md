## Scope

### Data model (`V562010`, DATA-MODEL §2.7.10)
- **`whm_operations`** — FR-789 columns; uk `(company_id, code)`.
- **`whm_routings`** — FR-790 header; unique index `(item_id, version_no, COALESCE(warehouse_id, nil-uuid))`; gist EXCLUDE no two `APPROVED` rows of `(item_id, COALESCE(warehouse_id))` overlap on `daterange(effective_from, effective_to, '[)')` (the BOM rule, MD-27).
- **`whm_routing_operations`** — FR-790 columns; uk `(routing_id, operation_seq)`; children cascade only while `DRAFT` (no `orphanRemoval`).

### Rules
- **Lifecycle** as the BOM (`SFL-T1-04`…`-07`): `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED → OBSOLETE`, approval through `p8-30` with `bom.approval_levels` levels (Design Head, Plant Head by default — FR-782), `APPROVED` immutable, *New version* copies to `DRAFT` (through an ECN when `ecn_required_for_released_change`, FR-794). *Copy from BOM operations* seeds a draft from an existing BOM's operation list.
- **Guards** `SFL-GRD-03` (structure; machine of its work centre; job-work operations complete) and `SFL-GRD-04` (effectivity overlap, service pre-check with `flush()` before the EXCLUDE).
- **Selection service** `WhmRoutingSelectionService.select(itemId, warehouseId, date, unitSerial?)`: site-specific before all-sites, effective on the date (or by `effective_from_unit_serial`, FR-792) → `409 ROUTING_NOT_EFFECTIVE` when the item × site `uses_job_cards` (FR-791) and none is found. Used by `p10-05` at release and by `p10-25`.
- **Compare** two routing versions (operations added / removed / changed by seq), backend diff, export.
- Each operation copies the operation master's defaults (work centre, skill, operators required, QC checkpoint, inspection plan) and may override them; `pqc_blocks_next_stage` defaults to `warehouse.manufacturing.pqc_blocks_next_stage_default` (`true`).
- Caches `statistics.whmRouting`, `dropdown.whmOperation` registered, evicted on writes.

- **Partial unique indexes are not deferrable** — explicit `flush()` before the overlap query.
- **Effective dates are `DATE`** — `formatDateSync`, `dateOnly` filters.
