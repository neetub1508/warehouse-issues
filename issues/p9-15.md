## Scope
- **No tax engine in warehouse** (`OD-9`, `D-21` item 1). Calls core SPI `WhTaxPreviewProvider.preview(TaxPreviewRequest)` (FRD-TRADE
  §12c `CN-16`, declared by core **`P8-26`**) through core's port package; the accounting adapter (`p8-23`) implements it over
  accounting's tax determination. `warehouse-india` keeps its HSN / state-code masters as reference data and implements nothing.
  Trade never imports `ai.warehouseindia` or an accounting class (`TRD-09`); ArchitectureInvariantsTest keeps proving both.
- Request (FR-761): company, our GSTIN / site state, counterparty GSTIN / registration type / state, **bill-to and ship-to states**,
  **supply kind** `DOMESTIC` / `IMPORT` / `EXPORT` / `SEZ`, goods / services, the **effective document date** (document date;
  an amendment's effective date), lines (item, HSN / SAC, taxable value). The provider decides the place of supply (IGST Act
  s.10(1)(b) for bill-to-ship-to), the heads (CGST + SGST, UTGST in a UT, IGST, cess), zero-rating of `EXPORT` / `SEZ`, and the
  **reverse-charge flag from the supplier's registration + HSN / SAC**. Trade sets the supply kind: an `IMPORT` PO
  (`supply_origin`, p9-18) asks **no** preview — its lines read *IGST on bill of entry* and `TRADE_PLACE_OF_SUPPLY_UNRESOLVED`
  never applies; a ship-to outside the company's country → `EXPORT`; the provider may upgrade `DOMESTIC` to `SEZ` from the
  counterparty registration, never the reverse. Place-of-supply inputs: purchase = receiving site; sales = bill-to + ship-to; a
  prospect quotation with no address = `prospect_state_code` (p9-08). An OPEN line (null quantity) is previewed per unit and
  recomputed per release (R32 F-R32TR-025).
- Tables (`V552000`): **`wht_po_line_taxes`**, **`wht_vendor_quotation_line_taxes`**, **`wht_customer_quotation_line_taxes`**,
  **`wht_so_line_taxes`** — each: parent line FK, `tax_head_code` (`CGST`/`SGST`/`UTGST`/`IGST`/`CESS`), `rate_percent`
  `DECIMAL(9,6)`, `taxable_value` / `tax_amount` `DECIMAL(19,4)`, `is_reverse_charge` (the provider's flag), `place_of_supply_state_code`,
  `supply_kind`, `computed_at`; uk(line, head). No rate or HSN column is stored as a master (the provider is the only source). Header totals `taxable_total`, `tax_total`, `gross_total` on `wht_purchase_orders`,
  `wht_vendor_quotations`, `wht_customer_quotations`, `wht_sales_orders` (added here).
- Recompute on every save while the document is editable; **freeze at approval**; an amendment recomputes changed lines.
  Guards `422 TRADE_TAX_CLASSIFICATION_MISSING {lineNo}`, `422 TRADE_PLACE_OF_SUPPLY_UNRESOLVED` (the provider's
  `PLACE_OF_SUPPLY_UNRESOLVED` answer; never on an `IMPORT` PO) at submit.
- CS (FR-762): *landed net of ITC* and *gross* columns; setting `warehouse.trade.cs.rank_basis` (`LANDED_NET_OF_ITC`); L1 per basis
  within one currency (`OD-34`). Approval value basis `warehouse.trade.approval.value_basis` (`NET`) is seeded by p9-03 and read here.
- Prints `PURCHASE_ORDER`, `SCHEDULE_RELEASE`, `CUSTOMER_QUOTATION`, `SALES_ORDER_ACK`, `COMPARATIVE_STATEMENT`: HSN, rate,
  heads and amounts, totals; without the provider they keep *Taxes extra as applicable*.
- Detail tabs: a *Tax* section on the line drawer (≤ 2 sections per tab); grid columns `taxTotal`, `grossTotal` on WS-292 / WS-303
  (grid definitions + export superset, `V552001`).

## Traps
- The tax shown is a **preview for commitment and approval**; the tax invoice is accounting's (FR-773 hands the recomputed rows over).
- A tax table or rate column anywhere in warehouse (trade or india) is the engine `OD-9` forbids — read the provider only.
- RCM is the provider's call from supplier registration + SAC / HSN; trade only stores and prints the flag (ledger
  `purchase-inward:BEYOND-7`: posting RCM / TDS stays accounting's).
- Money `DECIMAL(19,4)`, rates `DECIMAL(9,6)`; never sum across currencies (FR-616).
- Frozen rows are never recomputed on read; a changed HSN master affects only drafts.
