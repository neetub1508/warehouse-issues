## Scope
- **No tax engine in trade.** Calls core SPI `WhTaxPreviewProvider.preview(TaxPreviewRequest)` (FRD-TRADE §12c `CN-16`) through
  core's port package; `warehouse-india` implements it over its built `whin_hsn_tax_master`, `whin_gst_state_codes` and GSTIN
  profiles. Trade never imports `ai.warehouseindia` (`TRD-09`); ArchitectureInvariantsTest keeps proving it.
- Place of supply (FR-761): purchase = receiving site's state (our GSTIN), sales = ship-to address state, service = recipient's
  state; same state → CGST + SGST (UTGST in a UT), different → IGST; cess and reverse charge as the HSN master says.
- Tables (`V552000`): **`wht_po_line_taxes`**, **`wht_vendor_quotation_line_taxes`**, **`wht_customer_quotation_line_taxes`**,
  **`wht_so_line_taxes`** — each: parent line FK, `tax_head_code` (`CGST`/`SGST`/`UTGST`/`IGST`/`CESS`), `rate_percent`
  `DECIMAL(9,6)`, `taxable_value` / `tax_amount` `DECIMAL(19,4)`, `is_reverse_charge`, `place_of_supply_state_code`,
  `computed_at`; uk(line, head). Header totals `taxable_total`, `tax_total`, `gross_total` on `wht_purchase_orders`,
  `wht_vendor_quotations`, `wht_customer_quotations`, `wht_sales_orders` (added here).
- Recompute on every save while the document is editable; **freeze at approval**; an amendment recomputes changed lines.
  Guards `422 TRADE_TAX_CLASSIFICATION_MISSING {lineNo}`, `422 TRADE_PLACE_OF_SUPPLY_UNRESOLVED` at submit.
- CS (FR-762): *landed net of ITC* and *gross* columns; setting `warehouse.trade.cs.rank_basis` (`LANDED_NET_OF_ITC`); L1 per basis
  within one currency (`OD-34`). Approval value basis `warehouse.trade.approval.value_basis` (`NET`) is seeded by p9-03 and read here.
- Prints `PURCHASE_ORDER`, `SCHEDULE_RELEASE`, `CUSTOMER_QUOTATION`, `SALES_ORDER_ACK`, `COMPARATIVE_STATEMENT`: HSN, rate,
  heads and amounts, totals; without the provider they keep *Taxes extra as applicable*.
- Detail tabs: a *Tax* section on the line drawer (≤ 2 sections per tab); grid columns `taxTotal`, `grossTotal` on WS-292 / WS-303
  (grid definitions + export superset, `V552001`).
