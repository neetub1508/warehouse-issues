#### Why this matters

p9-15 zero-rates any `EXPORT` SO, while FRD-TRADE M38 says export documentation "stays out". Zero-rating without payment of IGST is lawful only under a valid LUT for that FY. The goods must leave India within 3 months of the invoice, or IGST + 18% interest is due. The dispatch desk is where the facts arise:
- the shipping bill no./date and port
- the container no. and the self-seal / e-seal no.
- the EWB to the port (sub-type Export)
- the date the goods left
Accounting #60/#294 expect these on the invoice, for GSTR-1 Table 6A and EDPMS, but no screen captures them. The result: export invoices with no shipping bill, refund claims that fail, and no alert before the 3-month default.

#### Scope


- An EXPORT SO cannot be approved zero-rated unless the company GSTIN has an LUT valid on the invoice date (read through the accounting port). Otherwise it is IGST-paid export.
- An export shipment captures port code, shipping bill no./date, container no., e-seal no., LEO date and vessel/flight, editable until the invoice is locked, and passes them on the invoice request.
- EWB sub-type `EXPORT`, with ship-to = port, for the domestic leg.
- An alert at 75 days after the invoice date with no LEO/shipping bill (the 3-month clock).

#### Acceptance

- A zero-rated export SO is refused when no LUT covers the FY; the IGST-paid option remains
- The export invoice request carries shipping bill no./date and port code
- A shipment without a shipping bill 75 days after the invoice appears on the export clock report
- The EWB for the port leg is filed with sub-type Export
