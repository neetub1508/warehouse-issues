## Traps

- **`asset_po_receipts` receives *assets*, not stock** (R1 §6). It is a model to **extend, not
  reuse** — reusing it means the spare is capitalised at receipt and never exists as stock, which is
  the opposite of the boundary `FR-364` states.
- **State the boundary in the product, not only in this file.** The hand-off is an **event carrying
  the serial**, and `whaa_asset_item_links.capitalised_at` is the moment custody changes. Without a
  recorded moment, *"is this serial stock or an asset"* has two answers.
- **`assets` and `services` have no stock capability at all** (`C-034`). There is no existing spare
  ledger to reconcile against; this adapter is the first.
- **The adapter must not write `whb_`/`wh_` tables directly, must not be depended upon by base or
  app, and must not widen a base vocabulary by `ALTER`** (`D-11`). Every issue posts through the
  port; the ratchet is `git log --oneline -- warehouse-base/ | wc -l` **unchanged** at merge.
- **`ai.warehouseadapterassets` is a sibling package** (`D-1`), and controllers live in
  `ai.<module>.controller…` (`UserActivityTrackingAspect:59`, `:167-174`).
- **Vendors are mapped, never copied.** `whb_counterparty_external_refs` links to `asset_vendors`;
  **never an FK from base into another module** (`OD-4`) — the prior seam died of 84 such FKs into
  `scc_*`, several to tables that never existed.
- **A serialised spare records the asset it went onto at issue time.** Retro-linking a year of
  issues to assets is not possible — the same rule `FR-362` states for a part fitted to a customer's
  vehicle (`S-048`).
- **`movementSequenceNo` on the grid is the ledger link and is why this grid is log-style** — no
  `createdByName`/`updatedByName` column, and therefore none in the export either
  (`BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §0.5, `ExportServiceContractTest.WITHOUT_AUDIT_COLUMNS` may shrink, never
  grow).
- **Menu inserts need `WHERE NOT EXISTS`** — `menus` has no unique constraint on
  `(name, parent_id, menu_level)` and `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` does not stop a duplicate on a Flyway
  retry (`assets/.../V60171:154-155`).
- **`permission_dependencies` is a PLATFORM table** created at `V248:17-30`. **Insert rows; never
  `CREATE TABLE`.** Columns are `permission_id` and **`dependent_permission_id`**.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A spare is issued against a complaint resolution and the issue is a movement posted through the port
- [ ] The consumption row carries the complaint reference, the asset reference, the serial and the ledger `movementSequenceNo`
- [ ] Capitalising the spare writes a `whaa_asset_item_links` row with `capitalised_at`, and the serial is thereafter the assets module's
- [ ] Before capitalisation the serial is warehouse stock and appears in the position report; after it, it does not
- [ ] Vendors resolve through `whb_counterparty_external_refs` to `asset_vendors`, with no FK from base into assets
- [ ] The technician issues a spare from `screens/whaaSpareConsumption` in the field
- [ ] `git log --oneline -- warehouse-base/ | wc -l` is unchanged; `ArchitectureInvariantsTest` passes
- [ ] `permission_dependencies` rows are inserted and the table is not created
- [ ] **Scenarios** — none claimed; see the coverage note above
