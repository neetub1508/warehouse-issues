## Why the schema is in P1 and the workflow is not

**Because the columns must exist before the challan does.** `A-4` moved the India movement documents
into **v1/P2-IN**, and `whin_delivery_challans` reads the transfer's two branch references and its
frozen taxable-supply flag. **The three-leg workflow is `P2-02`; the challan is `P2-IN-03`.** This
task creates the table and the columns, and nothing else.

- **`V510031`** — `wh_transfer_orders`, `wh_transfer_order_lines`.
- **The two branch references and `is_taxable_supply`, derived at creation and frozen** (`FR-305`):
  a transfer between two branches under **different GSTINs is a deemed supply**. Derived later, from
  today's branch data, it is wrong for every historical transfer whose branch registration has since
  changed.
- **The transfer's valuation method and price** — cost, transfer price or open-market value
  (`FR-306`) — **so the basis is defensible three years later.**
- **`ownership_transfer_point` on the transfer document as a v1 column** (`FR-344`): the in-transit
  location says *where*, the owner says *whose*, the transfer point says *when it changed*. `P1-12`
  carries the purchase side.

**`DATA-MODEL.md` §2.2 (`WH-21`) is the authority on both column sets and must be re-read immediately
before the migration is written.**

## The frozen flag is the irreversible part

> **`is_taxable_supply` is derived at creation and frozen on the row.** A branch's GST registration
> changes; a transfer created in April under one registration and re-derived in October under another
> produces a different answer for a document that has already been filed. There is no re-derivation
> that is correct for both, and the return is already lodged.

The same argument governs the two branch references: they are **snapshots of the transfer's own
endpoints**, not live joins to `branches`.

## Traps

- **Deriving `is_taxable_supply` at read time is the defect**, and it will look like good normalising
  practice in review. The branch registration is mutable; the filed return is not.
- **The two branch references are snapshots, not joins.** Same argument. A live join to
  `branches.branch_name` for display is fine; a live join to decide taxability is not.
- **`branches.branch_name`, not `branches.name`** (`V149:18`, R1 §8 trap `T-18`) — for the display
  join and for the snapshot columns.
- **The transfer carries two numbers** (`FR-244`, `P2-18`): a **transfer price** (the tax document)
  and a **cost** (what follows the goods). They are different columns and the schema must not
  collapse them, even though the valuation logic is P2.
- **`valuation_method` is a bounded vocabulary — cost / transfer price / open-market value** — and it
  is on the document, not derived from a policy at read time, for the same defensibility reason.
- **Do not build the workflow here.** The three legs — depart into a **per-transfer** in-transit
  location at the sending site, arrive from it at the receiving site, with the residue visible — are
  `P2-02`, and the per-transfer transit location is `P1-05`'s `FR-085`. A two-leg implementation
  shipped early is what makes in-transit stock uncountable.
- **`whb_transport_details` attaches polymorphically to this document** (`P1-11`, `V500052`) by
  `(document_type, document_id)` and **not by an FK** — `warehouse-base` may not FK into `wh_`
  (`FR-350`).
- **India must not leak into the column names.** `is_taxable_supply`, `source_branch_id`,
  `destination_branch_id` and `valuation_method` are country-neutral (`D-8`); `whin_` tables read
  them. A `gst_` prefix here breaks `D-8` and `warehouse` stops being sellable outside India.
- **`ownership_transfer_point` is a v1 column with a v2 consumer** — the `IRREVERSIBLE.md` pattern
  again.
- **Do not add `demand_order_id` to `V510031`** (`RJ-003`). The FK targets `wh_demand_orders`, which
  `P2-08`'s `V510040` creates *after* this migration runs; `V510040` adds the nullable column to
  `wh_transfer_orders` by `ALTER`. A column here would be an FK to a table that does not yet exist.
- **No grid config in P1.** The `WAREHOUSE_TRANSFER_ORDER` scope, the grid-config migration and the
  cache name are `P2-29`'s to allocate; adding them here consumes numbers from another task's block.
