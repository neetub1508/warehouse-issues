**Because the columns must exist before the challan does.** `A-4` moved the India movement documents
into **v1/P2-IN**, and `whin_delivery_challans` reads the transfer's two branch references and its

task creates the table and the columns, and nothing else.

## The frozen flag is the irreversible part

> **`is_taxable_supply` is derived at creation and frozen on the row.** A branch's GST registration
> changes; a transfer created in April under one registration and re-derived in October under another
> produces a different answer for a document that has already been filed. There is no re-derivation
> that is correct for both, and the return is already lodged.

The same argument governs the two branch references: they are **snapshots of the transfer's own
endpoints**, not live joins to `branches`.

## Traps

- **Deriving `is_taxable_supply` at read time is the defect**, and it will look like good normalising
  practice in review. The branch registration is mutable; the filed return is not.
- **The two branch references are snapshots, not joins.** Same argument. A live join to
  `branches.branch_name` for display is fine; a live join to decide taxability is not.
- **`branches.branch_name`, not `branches.name`** (`V149:18`, R1 §8 trap `T-18`) — for the display
  join and for the snapshot columns.
- **The transfer carries two numbers** (`FR-244`, `P2-18`): a **transfer price** (the tax document)
  and a **cost** (what follows the goods). They are different columns and the schema must not
  collapse them, even though the valuation logic is P2.
- **`valuation_method` is a bounded vocabulary — cost / transfer price / open-market value** — and it
  is on the document, not derived from a policy at read time, for the same defensibility reason.
- **Do not build the workflow here.** The three legs — depart into a **per-transfer** in-transit
  location at the sending site, arrive from it at the receiving site, with the residue visible — are
  `P2-02`, and the per-transfer transit location is `P1-05`'s `FR-085`. A two-leg implementation
  shipped early is what makes in-transit stock uncountable.
- **`whb_transport_details` attaches polymorphically to this document** (`P1-11`, `V500052`) by
  `(document_type, document_id)` and **not by an FK** — `warehouse-base` may not FK into `wh_`
  (`FR-350`).
- **India must not leak into the column names.** `is_taxable_supply`, `source_branch_id`,
  `destination_branch_id` and `valuation_method` are country-neutral (`D-8`); `whin_` tables read
  them. A `gst_` prefix here breaks `D-8` and `warehouse` stops being sellable outside India.
- **`ownership_transfer_point` is a v1 column with a v2 consumer** — the `IRREVERSIBLE.md` pattern
  again.
- **Do not add `demand_order_id` to `V510031`** (`RJ-003`). The FK targets `wh_demand_orders`, which
  `P2-08`'s `V510040` creates *after* this migration runs; `V510040` adds the nullable column to
  `wh_transfer_orders` by `ALTER`. A column here would be an FK to a table that does not yet exist.
- **No grid config in P1.** The `WAREHOUSE_TRANSFER_ORDER` scope, the grid-config migration and the
  cache name are `P2-29`'s to allocate; adding them here consumes numbers from another task's block.

## Round-4 additions (folded from former P5-26, #159) — inter-company movement

**v2 increment of this task — design the schema now, build in the v2 wave.** Round 4 filed the
inter-company movement as its own task, `P5-26` (`#159`). On 2026-09-10 it was closed as a duplicate and
folded here, because this task already carries `RK-007`'s v1 half: the same-company guard, `422
CROSS_COMPANY_TRANSFER` and `WH-SC-319` (note (2) above), and the transfer-price columns the pair is
priced from. Requirement **`FR-465`** moves here. No migration: the demand order, the purchase order and
the transfer-price columns all exist.

**Scope — between two legal entities it is a sale and a purchase, never a transfer (`RK-007`).** Indian
dealer groups run one company per brand or territory and move parts between them. Between two legal
entities that move is a sale from one and a purchase into the other. `FR-001` balances every movement per
company, so a cross-company transfer could never post (`RK-007`). This increment is the v2 half
(`FR-465`): the two documents, created together.

1. **One action, two documents.** *Raise inter-company order* on `WS-090` takes a source site in company
   A, a destination site in company B and the lines. In one transaction it creates:
   - a **demand order** in company A, `demand_type = SALES`, whose customer is company B's counterparty
     (`P2-08`);
   - a **purchase order** in company B, whose supplier is company A's counterparty (`P1-12`).
2. **Cross-referenced in the source quad.** Each document carries the other as its source reference, so
   the lineage `GET` walks from either side to the other and to both sets of movements (`FR-465`).
3. **Priced from the transfer-price columns.** The sale price on A's lines and the purchase price on B's
   are one number, taken from `transfer_price_basis` and `transfer_price` (`FR-244`'s columns, carried by
   `wh_transfer_orders` since this task). `cost_value` stays A's cost relief. There is no new pricing.
4. **Each side then runs its own ordinary flow.** A picks, ships and dispatches (`P2-09`, `P2-10`); B
   receives a GRN against its PO (`P1-13`). Each side hands over to accounting its own envelope (`P2-18`),
   and the invoice and its tax are accounting's (`OD-9`).
5. **No new ledger concept.** Every movement posts in one company. The pair is two documents with a link,
   never a two-company movement.

**Requirement** `FR-465` — owned here now. **Scenario** `WH-SC-322` — company A moves parts to company
B; one action creates A's demand order and B's purchase order, cross-referenced, and no transfer exists.
**Closes** `RK-007` (both halves now). **Depends on** `P2-08`, `P1-12` and `P2-18` — all v1.

**Traps (v2 increment)**

- **Never a transfer, not even an internal one.** The v1 guard in this task stays. This increment adds a
  different action, not an exception to the guard. A transfer order with two companies must still be
  `422 CROSS_COMPANY_TRANSFER` after it ships (`RK-007`).
- **All or nothing.** If the actor cannot create a PO in company B, or B's counterparty for A does not
  exist, the action creates **neither** document. A demand order with no matching PO is exactly the
  double-keying this increment removes.
- **B's PO is not approved on B's behalf.** It is created in its first state and approved by company B's
  own approver, with `FR-408` applying. One user acting in both companies does not collapse two approvals
  into one.
- **Each company sees the other as an ordinary counterparty** (`P1-08`). No company-to-company table is
  added. The mapping is chosen once in the action's setup and kept on the counterparty's external
  reference, never inferred from a name.
- **Cancel is paired, not cascaded silently.** Cancelling A's demand order before dispatch offers to
  cancel B's PO in the same action and shows both outcomes. After A has dispatched, B's PO follows its
  own receiving rules.
