### Rules

- **Subcontract order** (`FR-658`): an order of type `SUBCONTRACT`, BOM usage `SUBCONTRACT` (components = what we
  send, main output = the **processed** item), job worker + a location of base type `JOB_WORKER` (virtual counterparty — the site's `VIRT-JOB_WORKER-<site>`
  or a per-job-worker one; owner unchanged, `L-11`; MD-28),
  optional `service_po_line_ref` + frozen `service_po_ref` (`p9-07`, display back-reference). The processing charge is
  the warehouse-trade subcontract PO's: trade lifts R9 for that service line and lands the charge on the processed item's
  lots as a value-only `LANDED_COST_APPLY` at its service confirmation (FRD-TRADE FR-686); MFG posts none (D-18 item 3,
  R31 F-MF-005). MFG's `PRODUCTION_ORDER` `CostObjectResolver` returns the order's `order_type` and
  `subcontractor_counterparty_id` so trade can validate the link (FRD-TRADE FR-683).
- **Dispatch** (`FR-659`): one `SUBCONTRACT_DISPATCH` movement store → job-worker location, consuming the order's
  component holds (`consumedReservationIds`) and re-reserving nothing — a virtual location admits no hold and is never
  allocatable (MD-31, R29 F3); the store-side lines' relieved value is read back onto `dispatched_value` and enters the
  order's pool (MD-32); outside India or
  intra-GSTIN it creates a core **returnable gate pass** (`p8-03`) `DRAFT`, sourced on the dispatch through MFG's
  `GatePassSourceResolver` for `SUBCONTRACT_DISPATCH` (MD-29; core then approves and gates it out); dispatch note prints components, lots, qty,
  job worker, `expected_return_date`.
- **India path** (`FR-660`, `MD-13`, **SEAM-M5**): this task declares `ai.warehouse.port.jobwork.JobWorkDispatchPort`
  in core (FRD-STORES defines no job-work port; MD-28) — `registerDispatch`, `registerReturn(returnedAsItemId,
  consumedInputQty, scrapQty, scrapRetained)` (receipt; a recall is a return of the input), `reverseReturn(returnRef)`
  (receipt reversal), `registerLoss(dispatchRef, itemId, lotId, qty, reasonCode)` (write-off) (MD-25) — with an optional
  bean; `warehouse-india` implements it — one `JOB_WORK` delivery challan (`INPUTS`/`CAPITAL_GOODS`) and its
  registration, existing clocks (inputs 1 year, capital goods 3 years, moulds/dies/jigs/tools no limit —
  **RE-VERIFY**, `V541053:115,121`). The india return matcher today accepts only same item/lot/UoM
  (same-item/lot/UoM pool at `WhinJobWorkRegistrationValidationService.java:544-560`, records at `:83-93` — re-cited R46 S1) and has no scrap: the india migration adds the
  processed-form return item and scrap (returned / retained) on job-work lines, and the matcher and ITC-04 builder
  accept them (s.143(5), **RE-VERIFY**). **MFG never writes a `whin_` table and never imports `ai.warehouseindia`.**
- **Subcontract receipt** (`FR-661`): through `p10-07`, all components treated as `BACKFLUSH` **from the job-worker
  location**; processed item received at our store (`gate_entry_id`/`gate_entry_ref` on the receipt, MD-26); scrap at the job worker recorded as
  retained (→ `VIRT-SCRAP`) or returned (by-product receipt); `422 OUTPUT_NOT_ON_ORDER` for a foreign item; calls
  `registerReturn` when the bean exists.
- **Valuation without a base change** (MD-32, R29 F6): the subcontract receipt's backflush is virtual → virtual (no
  value); the processed item is received at the absorbed share of the pool (`p10-07`'s absorption, stated
  `extendedCost`) — `WH-SC-647`'s 190 `SHAFT-03P` carry 190/200 of the dispatched value, never ₹0. Job-worker stock is
  the order's WIP, shown in WS-337, not in cost-layer valuation (`warehouse-india` job work has the same gap; not
  changed here).
- **Reconciliation and close**: every component balance at the job-worker location must be zero or resolved by
  *Recall* (posts `SUBCONTRACT_RECALL` back to store at once, each line stating `costSourceLineId` = the dispatch's
  store-side line so the dispatched layers are restored at their cost; the value leaves the pool) or *Write off* (a `REQUESTED` action; a second user with
  `:approve_write_off`, `403 WRITE_OFF_SELF_APPROVAL` on self, posts one `SUBCONTRACT_WRITE_OFF` movement to
  `VIRT-SCRAP`, which carries no value, so MFG moves the written-off dispatched value out of the pool to the
  abnormal-loss record; no `SCRAP` stock status — MD-20, MD-25, MD-32); a pending write-off still blocks close; otherwise close refuses `409 SUBCONTRACT_BALANCE_OPEN` listing the balances.

### WR3-02 · Give moulds, dies, jigs, fixtures and tools a no-limit job-work type (major, PLANNED-WRONG)

> **Statutory content.** Sources are cited under Evidence; anything marked UNVERIFIED there, and every figure, must be confirmed against the law in force at build time.

#### Why this matters

s.143 excludes moulds, dies, jigs, fixtures and tools from the 3-year capital-goods window. Sending a customer-owned or own press die to a component maker for years is normal practice. Clocking it as capital goods raises a false deemed-supply alert and invites a wrong tax invoice under neetub1508/accounting#307.

#### Scope

**R46 ratification F1: reuse the ratified `WHIN_JOB_WORK_TYPE` `TOOLING`** (`V541171:11,33`; already branched in `WhinDeliveryChallanService.java:429`, the registration validation / repository, ITC-04) — **no `MOULDS_DIES_TOOLS` row**, so the WR3-02 part of `V540190` is dropped. The MFG dispatch line picks `INPUTS` / `CAPITAL_GOODS` / `TOOLING` (default `INPUTS`); defaulting from `whm_tools.tool_type` waits for P10-26. Original text: add `job_work_type` `MOULDS_DIES_TOOLS` with a null deemed-supply date. Default it from the item type or `whm_tools.tool_type` (DIE / MOULD / JIG / FIXTURE). Exclude it from ageing alerts but keep it on the open-job-work grid and in ITC-04 (capital goods). Update FRD-MFG R-12.

#### Acceptance

- A die sent on a job-work challan line of type `TOOLING` has `deemed_supply_due_date` null and never raises a deemed-supply alert.
- It still appears in ITC-04 Table 4 as capital goods and on the open job-work grid.
- An INPUTS line on the same challan keeps its 1-year clock.

#### Evidence (classic HEAD 4843b1648d, 2026-11-03)

s.143(1) "(except moulds and dies, jigs and fixtures or tools)", https://www.knowyourgst.com/gstlaw/cgst-act/143-job-work-procedure-143/. FRD-MFG R-12 says "Moulds sent to a job worker travel on the india challan as capital goods". India code has only the settings `return_period_years.inputs` / `.capital_goods` (V541053:113-125) and no `MOULD` / `TOOL` type (grep `mould|jig` in warehouse-india → only class-name false hits). The mfg-subcontracting contract §3.1 says "no time limit", but no code or task builds it.

### WR3-04 · (R46 S1 DECISION: W, **split to its own task after P10-10** — not a close condition of #601; `SUB-OPEN-07` amended to "own task, v1.1") Support inputs shipped direct from the supplier to the job worker (major, MISSING-TASK)

> **Statutory content.** Sources are cited under Evidence; anything marked UNVERIFIED there, and every figure, must be confirmed against the law in force at build time.

#### Why this matters

In auto-component and fabrication clusters a steel mill or coil centre invoices the principal but delivers straight to the press or slitting job worker. The principal takes ITC, must issue the job-work challan, and the clock runs from the job worker's receipt. Refusing this flow forces a fake receipt into our store followed by a fake dispatch, so the stock register, e-way bills and ITC-04 all misstate the real movement.

#### Scope

Add a GRN variant "received at job worker": a PO line whose ship-to is the job worker location. The receipt posts supplier → JOB_WORKER location (owner house), creates the job-work registration with the clock from the JW receipt date, links the supplier invoice, and puts the challan on ITC-04 Table 4. Later processing and return work as in P10-10.

#### Acceptance

- A PO shipped to job worker JW1: posting the receipt creates on-hand stock at JW1's virtual location and a registration with clock start = receipt date.
- ITC-04 Table 4 lists it with the principal's challan.
- Store on-hand is never touched.

#### Evidence (classic HEAD 4843b1648d, 2026-11-03)

s.143(1)(b) and s.19 (CG: 3 years from receipt by JW), https://www.cggst.com/uploads/document/1756724526-section-19.pdf ; Circular 38/2018. Plan: mfg-subcontracting §3.1 "Direct-from-vendor … not supported in v1 (SUB-OPEN-07)" and §6 "v1 not built". There is no task.

### WR3-05 · (R46 S1 DECISION: W, **split to its own task after P10-10 and P10-20** — not a close condition of #601; ITC-04 Tables 5B / 5C) Allow job worker to job worker movement and supply from the job worker's premises (major, MISSING-TASK)

> **Statutory content.** Sources are cited under Evidence; anything marked UNVERIFIED there, and every figure, must be confirmed against the law in force at build time.

#### Why this matters

Multi-stage outside processing is the norm: machining at JW1, then plating at JW2, then heat treatment at JW3, with the goods never coming home. FG is often dispatched to the OEM or exported straight from the last job worker. ITC-04 has dedicated tables (5B, 5C) for both cases. Forcing every leg through our store means fake receipts and e-way bills, and an ITC-04 that does not match the job workers' records.

#### Scope

(a) A JW→JW transfer under the principal's challan: one registration hop, clock unchanged from the original dispatch, Table 5B on receipt home. (b) Shipment from the JW location to a customer through the normal outbound path with an invoice request, closing the registration as "supplied from JW premises" (Table 5C), with an additional-place-of-business warning when the JW is unregistered. (c) Parent job-card stages may chain JW1→JW2.

#### Acceptance

- 100 shafts JW1 → JW2 → home: ITC-04 shows Table 4 once, Table 5B for the return from JW2, and the clock is unchanged.
- 50 finished shafts shipped from JW2 to a customer close that quantity as 5C.
- The open job-work grid shows the current holder.

#### Evidence (classic HEAD 4843b1648d, 2026-11-03)

GST portal ITC-04 manual: Table 5B "received back from a job worker other than the job worker to whom such goods were originally sent", Table 5C "sent to job worker and subsequently supplied from premises of job worker", https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/inputtaxcredit/Manual_itc04.htm. Plan: mfg-subcontracting §6 "job worker → job worker; supply from the job worker's premises — v1 not built". P10-20 stage job work assumes a return home.

### WR3-07 · Raise a supply for scrap left at an unregistered job worker (minor, MISSING-TASK)

> **Statutory content.** Sources are cited under Evidence; anything marked UNVERIFIED there, and every figure, must be confirmed against the law in force at build time.

#### Why this matters

s.143(5): waste and scrap at a registered job worker may be sold by the job worker on payment of tax. If the job worker is unregistered, the principal must supply it. P10-10 books "scrap retained" to VIRT-SCRAP and stops there, so the principal never raises the invoice and tax on the scrap sale leaks.

#### Scope

**R46 S1 smallest form (BUILD here):** the india bean's `registerReturn` refuses `scrapRetained > 0` when the job worker has no GSTIN registration with **`422 SCRAP_RETAINED_JOB_WORKER_UNREGISTERED` · `scrapRetained`** (s.143(5): the principal, not an unregistered job worker, supplies the scrap); the user records the scrap as returned (by-product receipt) instead; no scrap-sale invoice request in v1. P10-10 S2 adds the code to `job-work.contract.md` as a v1.14 row. Original scope: on a subcontract receipt with scrap retained, read the job worker's registration status. If unregistered, require a scrap-sale disposition (buyer, rate) and emit an invoice request from the principal, or hold the scrap as on-hand at the JW location until sold. If registered, record "sold by JW" with its invoice ref.

#### Acceptance

- Retained scrap at an unregistered JW is refused `422 SCRAP_RETAINED_JOB_WORKER_UNREGISTERED`; recording it as returned posts (R46).
- A registered JW case stores the JW's invoice ref and emits nothing.
- ITC-04 waste columns are unchanged.

#### Evidence (classic HEAD 4843b1648d, 2026-11-03)

GST Council flyer https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/e-version-gst-flyers/Job_Work.pdf. P10-10 line 57 "scrap at the job worker recorded as retained (→ `VIRT-SCRAP`)". Accounting #307 covers only deemed supplies.
