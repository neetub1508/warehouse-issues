## Scope

- **`wh_kpi_snapshots`** (`V510106`, `DATA-MODEL.md` §7.3 WH-96).
- **The disputed-but-built `D-9` mitigations.** `accessories` keeps its own 17 tables, its own item
  master and its own 11 reports **permanently** (user decision, 2026-09-01). This design set does not
  argue with that; it **costs** it, and two mitigations are mandatory. This task builds the reporting
  half:
  - **WS-222, a consolidated valuation view spanning both inventories**, over
    `whb_stock_positions` ∪ `whb_external_stock_snapshots`, **tagged by source system**
    (`WAREHOUSE` / `ACCESSORIES`), with the **differing-valuation-method caveat rendered on the
    report itself** rather than in a footnote nobody reads (`FR-397`).
  - **Never a grand total.** Two inventories valued by two methods do not add up, and a single
    number at the bottom of the page is a number a finance director will quote.
  - **The cross-system availability lookup** and **one reporting scope**, so *"do we have it"* is
    answerable across both without a merge.
- The reconciliation half — `whb_item_external_refs` cross-map and category-stocking ownership — is
  `P2-27`'s (WS-066, WS-067, WS-225) and this task consumes it.

## Traps

- **`warehouse` may not read `accessory_stock_levels`.** The quantity arrives by **snapshot** into
  `whb_external_stock_snapshots` (WS-067, `P2-27`), which is what keeps the coupling test green. A
  direct join is a warehouse→accessories dependency and fails
  `WarehouseBaseCouplingTest`/`ArchitectureInvariantsTest`, and `ai.accessories` is on the
  **forbidden-import list** by decision, not by the general rule (`PORT-AND-ADAPTER-CONTRACT.md`
  §9.8).
- **No grand total. Ever.** `FR-397` is explicit and the reason is arithmetic: warehouse values by
  weighted average or FIFO (`OD-6`) and accessories by its own method, so the two columns are not
  addable. The caveat is **rendered on the report**, in the page, not in a footnote and not in the
  export's header row only.
- **The same physical unit counted in both systems is `D-9`'s load-bearing cost and it is
  *undetectable* without the cross-map.** This report is the surface where the double count becomes
  visible; if `whb_item_external_refs` has no `ACCESSORIES` row for a dual-stocked SKU, the report
  shows the same stock twice and says nothing.
- **`asAtDate` is `dateOnly`**, and *as at* is computed **from movements, never from balances**
  (`FR-328`, `E-053`): a balance table answers *now*. Snapshots exist for performance and are
  explicitly a cache under the same `L-4` discipline as positions.
- **`whb_external_stock_snapshots` is `as at` a date and may be stale.** Render the snapshot's
  `asAtDate` and `loadedAt` on the report; a consolidated valuation that mixes today's warehouse
  positions with last Tuesday's accessories snapshot and does not say so is worse than two reports.
- **Export follows the visible columns and honours filters and sort.** Both "export ignores filters"
  and "export ignores grid sort" are recorded live defects across roughly fifty endpoints in this
  repository; do not reproduce them here.
- **Ledger- and report-style grids gain no export-only audit columns** (`BUILD-SPEC-SCREENS.md` §0.5;
  `ExportServiceContractTest.WITHOUT_AUDIT_COLUMNS` is a ratchet that **may shrink, never grow**).
- **`wh_kpi_snapshots` is a cache with a start date that cannot be backdated** (`PNR-3`,
  `IRREVERSIBLE.md` §3.4). The first snapshot's date is the first date any trend exists for.
