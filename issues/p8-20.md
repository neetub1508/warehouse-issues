### Behaviour
- **Calibration status (computed on read, `QIC-T1-07`).** `VALID` (`calibration_due_on` ≥ site-local today + warning days), `DUE_SOON`, `OVERDUE` (< today), `FAILED` (the latest live record's result is `FAIL` — R32-fix F-R32CQ-015: an `ADJUSTED_PASS` with `as_found_in_tolerance = false` is the normal adjust-and-certify case and is **not** `FAILED`; as-found false drives only the `QIC-T4-01` impact list), `NEVER_CALIBRATED`.
- **Record calibration (`QIC-T1-08`).** `:record_calibration` appends a record with its certificate (attachment, subject `QC_INSTRUMENT_CALIBRATION`) and sets `last_calibrated_on`, `calibration_due_on = next_due_on` (default `calibrated_on + calibration_interval_days`, editable); `next_due_on` beyond `calibrated_on + calibration_interval_days` is refused `422 CALIBRATION_DUE_BEYOND_INTERVAL`. A `FAIL` sets `service_status = OUT_OF_SERVICE`. **Back into service (`QIC-T1-11`, `-12`, R32-fix F-R32CQ-004):** a `PASS` / `ADJUSTED_PASS` record sets `service_status = IN_SERVICE` from `OUT_OF_SERVICE` or `OUT_FOR_CALIBRATION` (never from `SCRAPPED`); when the instrument comes back on its `CALIBRATION` RGP (`FR-512` return), it returns `IN_SERVICE` if a passing record citing that `gate_pass_id` is already recorded, else it moves to `OUT_OF_SERVICE` (*awaiting certificate*) until one is.
- **Readings (`FR-701`, `QIC-GRD-07`…`-09`).** A result for a criterion with `instrument_required` and no instrument → `422 INSTRUMENT_REQUIRED` · `results[i].instrumentId`; an instrument of another type → `422 INSTRUMENT_TYPE_MISMATCH`; one not `IN_SERVICE`, `OVERDUE`, `FAILED` or `NEVER_CALIBRATED` → `422 INSTRUMENT_NOT_CALIBRATED` naming its due date — under `instrument_reading_policy = WARN` the reading saves with an acknowledgement and an audit row instead; a reading outside `range_min`…`range_max` → `422 READING_OUTSIDE_INSTRUMENT_RANGE`. The due date at the moment of reading is frozen on the result. The same guards and the same frozen column apply to **per-serial results** (`wh_quality_inspection_serial_results.instrument_id`, `instrument_due_on_at_reading`, created by `P8-06`'s `V511505`, which runs after this task's `V511503`; R32-fix F-R32CQ-026).
- **Failed-calibration impact (`QIC-T4-01`).** A record with `as_found_in_tolerance = false` lists, on WS-695's *Impact* tab, every inspection result **and every per-serial result** (`wh_quality_inspection_serial_results`, `P8-06`; R32-fix F-R32CQ-026) recorded with this instrument since its previous in-tolerance calibration (inspection, item, lot, stage, result, inspector) and emits `qc_instrument.calibration_failed`; the QC in-charge may raise an NCR of source `CALIBRATION_FAILURE` from the list (`P8-17`) — nothing is re-dispositioned automatically.
- **Due clock (`QIC-T3-01`).** Daily per site: instruments entering `DUE_SOON` and those `OVERDUE` notify the custodian and holders of `wh_qc_instruments:record_calibration`, once per due date (`last_due_notified_on`).
- **Mobile:** none (`D-20` item 5).

## Traps
- **Dates are `LocalDate`** — `formatDateSync`, never a timestamp formatter (the date-column rule).
- **The due date moves only by a calibration record**, never by editing the instrument (`QIC-UNR-01`).
- **Not the weighing-instrument register** (`FR-223`): keep the two tables separate; no FK between them.
- Out-of-calibration impact **lists**, it never reverses a posted disposition.
