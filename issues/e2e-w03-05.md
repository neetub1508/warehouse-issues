## Why this task exists

Freight or a duty bill can arrive after part of the lot has gone to a job worker, been issued to production or sent as a sample. Warehouse books that share of the charge to cost of sales. Goods at a job worker are still our stock, and goods issued to production belong in WIP. So the stock-to-GL figure, the margin and the job-work stock statement are all misstated for a manufacturer.

## Scope

Split the consumed quantity by destination. Quantity still owned (JOB_ISSUE to a job worker, TRANSFER in transit) lands on the layer that now holds it. WORK_ORDER/production consumption goes to a consumption-variance or WIP posting purpose. SAMPLE and SCRAP go to an expense purpose. SALES goes to cost of sales. Use one shared definition of "sale" for landed cost and the invoice producer.

## Acceptance

- Landed cost applied after half the lot went on JOB_ISSUE puts that half on the job-work stock layer, not in cost of sales.
- After a WORK_ORDER issue, the share is posted with a WIP/consumption purpose that the accounting envelope can tell apart from COGS.
- After a SALES despatch, the share is posted to cost of sales as today.
- The landed-cost and invoice producers read the same sale/non-sale list.
