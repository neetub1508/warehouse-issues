## Scope

- **`V522000`** — bootstrap: `whb_source_systems` row `ADAPTER_FIELD_SERVICE`, movement types
  `VAN_REPLENISH` / `ISSUE_AT_SITE` / `VAN_RETURN`, and the document types. **Inserted
  idempotently, never `ALTER`ed** (`FR-356`, `D-11`).
- **`V522010`** `whaf_van_stock_assignments` · **`V522011`** `whaf_van_replenishments` ·
  **`V522012`** `whaf_job_consumptions` (`DATA-MODEL.md` §7.4).
- **`V522100`–`V522149`** — permissions, `permission_dependencies` rows, menus with `en`/`fr`/`hi`
  translations, grid configuration, filter scopes.
- **The van is a `whb_locations` row of type `MOBILE`/`VEHICLE` with `assigned_user_id` — not a
  separate table and not a separate reconciliation problem** (`FR-363`, `FR-088`). It works only
  because both columns shipped in **v1** (`P1-05`, `IRR-30`) even though the adapter is v1.1.
- **Replenished by a transfer, consumed at the job, cycle-counted, reconciled at shift end**, and
  **unreturned parts age** — all three are ordinary warehouse operations against an ordinary
  location: a transfer in, an issue out, a count.
- The adapter **reads** `job_trips` for the van's current position —
  `field-service/…/V80007__Create_job_trips_and_track_points.sql:13-48` already knows it.

## Traps

<!-- check-design-set: table-names begin whaf_van_stock — the counter-example this trap forbids: the moment the van becomes a quantity table there are two stock truths. Named so it is not built, never cited as a design object -->

- **The van is a location, full stop.** The moment it becomes a `whaf_van_stock` quantity table there
  are two stock truths and a reconciliation problem that has no owner. `IRR-30` and `WH-SC-272` both
  say so, and `assigned_user_id` was made a **v1 column** precisely to make this possible in v1.1.
- **`field_service`, `services`, `assets` and `field-service` have *no stock capability at all***
  (`C-034`), and `services.parts_used` is free `TEXT`
  (`services/…/V40095__Change_parts_used_from_jsonb_to_text.sql`). There is nothing to migrate from
  and nothing to reconcile against — this adapter is the first stock truth those verticals get.
- **The adapter must not write `whb_`/`wh_` tables directly.** Every movement goes through the port
  (`POST /movements`); the ratchet is `git log --oneline -- warehouse-base/ | wc -l` **unchanged** at
  the commit that merges it.
- **`ai.warehouseadapterfieldservice` is a sibling package, never `ai.warehouse.adapter.fieldservice`**
  (`D-1`) — `@ComponentScan("ai.warehouse")` would load it in installs where field-service is not
  built. Controllers live in `ai.<module>.controller…` or the activity aspect cannot see them.
- **A van is also a vehicle in a future logistics module** (`WH-SC-273`). The link is
  `whb_location_external_refs`, **never an FK from base into another module** — the previous attempt
  at this seam died of exactly that: 84 FK references into `scc_*`, several to tables that never
  existed (`OD-4`, `G-025`/`G-026`).
- **Reconciliation at shift end computes a variance, and the variance posts as a reason-coded
  adjustment** — not as an edit to the van's quantity. `L-2`: the ledger is append-only.
- **Unreturned parts ageing is a dated obligation and needs its job** (`FR-165`), with a
  `whb_job_runs` row (WS-064).
- **`varianceValue` is money and is `DECIMAL(19,4)`**; quantities are `DECIMAL(18,4)`; any per-unit
  figure is `DECIMAL(19,6)` (`DATA-MODEL.md` §5.1).
- **Mobile API services are object literals** (`mobile/src/api/*.ts`, e.g. `serviceJobApi.ts:31`) —
  do not refactor, and do not import the web service.
- **Menu inserts need `WHERE NOT EXISTS`.** `menus` has no unique constraint on
  `(name, parent_id, menu_level)`, so `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` does not stop a duplicate on a Flyway
  retry — `assets/.../V60171:154-155` spells it out.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A technician's van exists as a `whb_locations` row of type `MOBILE` with `assigned_user_id` set, and **no van-stock quantity table exists**
- [ ] Replenishing the van is an ordinary transfer and appears in the movement register as one
- [ ] Consuming a part at a customer site is an ordinary issue, carrying the job reference, the serial and the `device_id`
- [ ] The van is cycle-counted through the ordinary count machinery
- [ ] Shift-end reconciliation posts the variance as a reason-coded adjustment, not an edit
- [ ] Unreturned parts age through a scheduled job with a `whb_job_runs` record
- [ ] The mobile van-stock screen **is** the reconciliation flow, not a read-only mirror
- [ ] The adapter references `job_trips` for the van's position and holds no FK into `field-service`
- [ ] `git log --oneline -- warehouse-base/ | wc -l` is unchanged; `ArchitectureInvariantsTest` passes
- [ ] **Scenarios** `WH-SC-272`'s adapter half
