# R30 — contract open items

> **What this is.** Every `-OPEN-` row across `docs/contracts/*.contract.md` that is still open, in one table, so the
> product owner can decide them in one pass. Collected 2026-10-23 at the R30 contract close-out (`CP-FR-007`, `D-17`
> item a), after the last 17 uncovered FRs got rule rows and `FR-258`, `FR-397`, `FR-471` were withdrawn under `D-16`.
> **Nothing here is decided by this file.** The decision stays on the contract row; this is an index.

**Count: 110 open** — **98** with a proposed default (the contract's *Default* / *Default until decided* /
*Recommendation* cell, or the default the row's status text states), **12** with none. 78 further `-OPEN-` rows are
already closed in place (`RESOLVED`, `CLOSED`, `Settled`, `Ratified`) and are listed at the end, not in the table.

**Method.** A row is collected when its first cell is an `…-OPEN-nn` id. It is *closed* when its status cell — the
`Status` column, or the resolution column of a contradiction table — or its question opens with `RESOLVED`,
`CLOSED`, `Settled`, `Ratified`, `Decided` or `Withdrawn`; a status that opens `OPEN` keeps it open whatever follows.
A placeholder row (*"— · none: every FR in scope states a checkable rule"*) is not an item. **FR** is the row's
`Source` column where it has one, else every `FR-nnn` the row names; `—` means the row names none. A default of `—`
means the contract states none: those rows need a decision before the behaviour can be built or verified.
